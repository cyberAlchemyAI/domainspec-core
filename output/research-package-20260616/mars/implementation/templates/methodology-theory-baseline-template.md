## Pre-Protocol Checklist

1. Domain context is current.
2. Methodology and theory baseline is current.
3. Primary authorities are source-cataloged, ledgered, and inventoried.
4. Known authority ambiguities are explicit.
5. The protocol can justify its metrics and thresholds from these foundations.
