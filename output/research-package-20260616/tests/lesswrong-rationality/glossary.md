# Glossary — *Rationality: A-Z*

Load-bearing vocabulary. Each entry: **source kind**, **local meaning (corpus)**, **Arcanum
reading** (NON-SOURCE, labeled), **promotion status**, **misuse warning**. Arcanum readings are
decided in [bridge-decisions.md](bridge-decisions.md); listing one here is not promotion.

### Map / Territory
- **source kind:** `local-inference` on the corpus's use; **origin is `related-source`** (Korzybski 1931, R11)
- **local meaning:** beliefs are *maps*; reality is the *territory*; a map's only virtue is correspondence.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** spec = map, system = territory; drift = map diverging from territory. `[analogy]`
- **promotion status:** analogy-only (mostly owned by definitions-governance drift checks)
- **misuse warning:** do **not** credit the corpus for the phrase; cite Korzybski.

### Beliefs pay rent (in anticipated experience)
- **source kind:** `primary-source` (B1)
- **local meaning:** a belief that predicts nothing — same anticipations whether true or false — carries zero information and is not a real belief.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** a spec assertion predicting nothing observable is a "floating belief". `[operator-reading]`
- **promotion status:** already-owned (Arcanum P10 *claim ≤ proof* already enforces this) — cite as motivation
- **misuse warning:** the underlying *verificationism* is contested (R1); use as a heuristic gate, not a theory of meaning.

### Semantic stopsign
- **source kind:** `primary-source` (B1)
- **local meaning:** a word/phrase that *halts* inquiry without constraining any prediction ("emergence", "complexity" used as a curiosity-stopper).
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** governance prose words ("robust", "aligned", "best-practice") that constrain no behavior. `[analogy]`
- **promotion status:** promotion-candidate (cleanest of the set — a prose lint)
- **misuse warning:** a term is only a stopsign when it *ends* inquiry; legitimate shorthand isn't.

### The bottom line
- **source kind:** `primary-source` (B2)
- **local meaning:** if the conclusion was written before the evidence was weighed, the arguments are rationalization; only the generating algorithm matters.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** the rationale for pre-registering a dispatch sheet / criterion *before* running. `[operator-reading]`
- **promotion status:** already-owned (experiment-type frozen `criterion.md` at the P2 gate)
- **misuse warning:** don't claim pre-registration is *proven* effective (B8/L5); it's the same caution the corpus over-claims.

### Conservation of Expected Evidence
- **source kind:** `primary-source` (B2)
- **local meaning:** for each anticipated piece of support there must be equal-and-opposite anticipated counter-evidence; no test can expect a net confidence gain before running.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** a review rubric that can only raise confidence (never lower it) is mis-designed. `[operator-reading]`
- **promotion status:** promotion-candidate (scoped to rubric design)
- **misuse warning:** applies to *expected* evidence pre-test, not to actually-observed updates.

### Adaptation-executor (not fitness-maximizer)
- **source kind:** `primary-source` (B3); `related-source` corroborated (R6)
- **local meaning:** organisms run ancestrally-tuned mechanisms; they do not compute current fitness (sugar craving in a calorie-rich world).
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** an agent/skill executes its prompt-shaped behavior, not the goal it was selected for. `[analogy]`
- **promotion status:** local-only
- **misuse warning:** don't extend the "optimization process" framing to a unifying theorem (L3, blocked as B7).

### Terminal vs. instrumental values
- **source kind:** `primary-source` on the distinction; `open-residue` on its **owning essay** (URL 404'd, O1)
- **local meaning:** terminal = valued for itself; instrumental = valued as a means; evolution itself "had" no terminal values.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** goal vs. success-metric; guard against optimizing a metric that detached from the goal. `[analogy]`
- **promotion status:** borrow-carefully (keep analogy-grade — origin unverified)
- **misuse warning:** don't source-attribute to a specific book; origin unconfirmed.

### No Universally Compelling Arguments
- **source kind:** `primary-source` — *via mirror/summary, not a canonical body* (P6, S1)
- **local meaning:** a paperclip maximizer is unmoved by human moral arguments; "morality" names a specific human computation, not a mind-independent absolute.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** not currently bridged — closest is the orthogonality intuition behind anti-bias tensioning. `[analogy]`
- **promotion status:** local-only
- **misuse warning:** the *modest* version (≈ Hume + orthogonality) is sound; the *leap* to morality as one well-defined computation is `[local-inference]`-contested (L4, R9).

### Affective death spiral
- **source kind:** `primary-source` (B2, *Death Spirals* sequence)
- **local meaning:** positive affect toward X biases all evidence about X positive, self-reinforcing past correction.
- **Arcanum reading (NON-SOURCE):** community-level: a governed framework drifting into ritual that suppresses error-correction (B10). `[analogy]`
- **promotion status:** future-work
- **misuse warning:** often mis-placed in B1; the named concept is B2.
