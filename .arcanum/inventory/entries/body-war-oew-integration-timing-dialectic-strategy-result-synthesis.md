# Body War Atlas Into OEW Timing Dialectic Strategy

This entry is a non-authority Inventory read model of a validated dispatch
proposal. It does not decide the timing question, change Body War or OEW,
register the dispatch, establish human comprehension, or admit release or
production readiness.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Dispatch is warranted by parallelism and isolation. The same live evidence can
support the opposed timing claims that the passing Body War Atlas and existing
OEW adapter make a small integration slice valuable now, or that the open
legacy OEW frontier makes that slice duplicative and rework-prone.

## Lanes, Subagents, And Outputs

- Simon, Herbert attacks deferral through empirical integration proof. He must
  distinguish a launcher, shared projection data, focus-scene implementation,
  and static embedding, then identify the smallest changed-file and validation
  boundary.
- Bateson, Gregory attacks immediate integration through adversarial sequencing
  analysis. He must prove concrete dependency, duplication, ownership, or
  rework risk, name the condition that closes it, and admit any no-regret slice.

The agents share one frozen corpus, work independently, and confront the
opposed result once. The parent may select `integrate-now`, `defer`, or
`split-now-and-later`; uncited or missing evidence blocks a decision.

## Dependency Flow

Both attackers run in parallel, join through one bounded confrontation, and
hand their receipts to the parent decision. There are no downstream groups and
no invented repair edge.
