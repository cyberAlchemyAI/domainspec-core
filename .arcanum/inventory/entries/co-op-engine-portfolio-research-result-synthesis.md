## Accepted Product Substrate

The portfolio showcase is not the primary collaboration mechanism. The
accepted Define candidate is a proposed weekly Experiment Exchange:

- all fourteen source-bounded project cards remain available asynchronously;
- experiment and capacity cards are the discovery unit;
- clinics are triggered only by unresolved complementarity, translation,
  access/privacy, scope, or success-test evidence;
- unilateral interest is not a match, an appointment is not a run, and a run
  receipt does not prove market demand or durable recruitment;
- the single browser artifact acts as self-guided recruiter, facilitated
  meeting view, interaction surface, and post-meeting record over one truth
  model with separately owned notes, prompts, authoring metadata, and privacy
  projections.

This composite is `build-from-owned`: Project Pods, Devpost, Open Space,
clinic/office-hours formats, implementation-intention research, Whisper, and
repository-local browser/copy/readability evidence constrain components. They
do not validate the combination.

## Whisper Product Candidate

The user-requested writer/reader product is restored as **Writer Story-State
Studio**, a build-from-owned candidate under Whisper:

- passages yield proposed typed character, place, event, fact, chronology, and
  character-knowledge state;
- every assertion carries source provenance, admission state, temporal scope,
  visibility, and revision lineage;
- machine extraction may propose, but only the author admits canon;
- writers inspect continuity and decide corrections; readers explore an
  admitted spoiler-safe `reader_as_of(scene)` projection;
- the Mira/Ivo and hooded-traveler fixtures distinguish physical encounter
  from identity knowledge and make negation, chronology, admission, and spoiler
  leakage falsifiable.

Current public claims `agentic writer studio`, generic `knowledge graph`, and
`AI collaborator` are typed KILLs. The bounded candidate is specified but not
implemented or run.

## Acceptance-Evidence Boundary

The findings define three proposed contracts:

1. black-box browser behavior over route, DOM, accessibility tree, keyboard,
   state, reciprocal appointment, privacy, console/network, screenshot, and
   trace evidence with known-good, known-bad, and false-positive fixtures;
2. conversational-AI/chat-language and project-voice review using an
   adjudicated corpus rather than unvalidated automatic meaning judgment;
3. ease/coherence checks separating automatable proxies from two-profile human
   retellability and transfer evidence.

UX Evidence Validator is seed-only. None of these contracts is an implemented
audit or current product witness.
