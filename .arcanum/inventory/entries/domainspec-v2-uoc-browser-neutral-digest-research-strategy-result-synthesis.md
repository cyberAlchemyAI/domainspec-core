# DomainSpec v2 Unified Canvas Browser-Neutral Digest Research Strategy

This non-authority Inventory read model records a confirmation-ready research
strategy. It does not decide the implementation, authorize a Work Pack repair,
register or execute the dispatch, or establish browser acceptance, release, or
production readiness.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Dispatch is justified by synthesis, isolation, and parallelism. The question is
not merely whether a local project needs cryptography: it requires separating
security from deterministic identity, tracing the current digest contract, and
comparing removals and browser-neutral replacements against current Node and
browser evidence.

## Research Boundary

Current-source inspection shows `node:crypto` is used for synchronous SHA-256
fingerprints and derived graph/layout identities, not encryption, credentials,
confidentiality, or network trust. Local-only scope therefore weakens security
arguments but does not by itself prove the stable fingerprint contract is
dispensable.

The research must compare complete digest removal, browser-neutral synchronous
SHA-256, asynchronous Web Crypto, a non-cryptographic stable hash, and injected
or precomputed digests. It must identify the smallest repair that restores the
browser without silently weakening replay, source-fingerprint, receipt, or
validator semantics.

## Lanes And Subagents

- Abramsky, Samson traces the exact removal blast radius and migration surface.
- Shannon, Claude compares the five computational alternatives and parity
  evidence.
- Noether, Emmy synthesizes one exact-bound option matrix and recommendation.
- Capucci, Matteo attacks non-vacuity through browser-plus-Node witnesses.
- Gödel, Kurt tests the distinctions between security, fingerprints,
  local-only scope, evidence, and authority.
- Turing, Alan writes findings only after evidence closure.
- Brandenburg, Martin audits the result against the original decision and
  claim ceiling.

The explorer pair varies methodology and source corpus. The reviewer pair uses
different attack vectors. Pair-specific predicted disagreements are frozen in
the admitted sheet.

## Dependency Flow

Explorers run in parallel, then feed one synthesizer. The synthesizer and
reviewers may perform one bounded zig-zag; a missing-evidence route may return
once to explorers. The converged synthesis feeds the writer and then the
auditor, with one bounded writer revision. The parent remains final approver.
