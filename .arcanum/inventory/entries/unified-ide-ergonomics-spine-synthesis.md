# Unified IDE Ergonomics Spine Synthesis

Candidate Inventory synthesis, **not** a canonical product decision. It records
the durable finding of dispatch `2026-06-24-unified-ide-ergonomics-product-view`
(research; closed `resolved`): the IDE-Ergonomics primitive federation has one
**organizing spine**, distilled from four Arcanum dispatch/composition sources
(Necronomicon, Spellcraft, dispatch-spec, the Dispatch Synthesis). Evidence
backing: [`2026-06-24-unified-ide-ergonomics-spine-cards.md`](evidence-cards/2026-06-24-unified-ide-ergonomics-spine-cards.md).

> Thesis: the three source-derived "spines" are **three altitudes of one spine**,
> not rivals — restraint floor (the invariant) / latent dispatch-grammar routing
> (the vocabulary) / lifecycle progression (the position). But — claim ≤ proof —
> they are **not** three co-equal load-bearing axes: only the lifecycle altitude
> does card-level decision work.

> **REVISED 2026-06-24** (review dispatch `2026-06-24-spine-model-premature-lock-review`):
> the "three-altitude **stack**" was found to be a **premature lock** — a sealed
> vertical stack forbids a 4th by construction, with no admission procedure and no
> open/closed declaration. The model was rewritten to an **open, default-deny,
> gated coordinate space** that models its own evolution. The three altitudes
> became three *coordinates* (unordered); "lifecycle" was split into work-arc vs
> session-state clocks; and three further coordinates were surfaced — **evidence/
> receipt** (admitted, load-bearing — the receipt-debt keystone is denominated in
> it), **input-side** (candidate; home `intent-latitude-tags/`), **social/addressee**
> (candidate; home `teammate-handoff-memos/`). The revised model lives in
> `projects/ide-ergonomics/UNIFIED-PRODUCT-VIEW.md`; this entry records the original
> build-dispatch finding for provenance.

## The witness (keystone)

The "evidence rail" span (`PRODUCT-VIEW.md` §The Story, :38-51) routed through the
flat federation yields six **unordered, co-equal peer routes**; routed through the
spine, the identical six get lifecycle tags (side-node=Discover, evidence-rail=Define,
handoff=Design/Plan, route-ambiguity=Decide, two-lane=Research), inducing a partial
order that surfaces **receipt-debt** the flat peer-menu cannot express. That single
observable difference is the spine's irreducible contribution — the proof it is not
a re-skin.

## Adversarial trail (claim ≤ proof)

The original synthesis claimed "three orthogonal load-bearing altitudes, each doing
work the others can't." The `definitional-soundness` skeptic demoted it: the restraint
floor's per-row boundaries already exist in the federation (`PRODUCT-VIEW.md:188-193`),
and the grammar altitude is latent (surfacing it as UI is the "dashboard with no next
action" fail, `:251`). The `precedent` skeptic confirmed **build-from-owned**. Both
demotions are on record in `findings.md` §"Claim ≤ Proof — demotions on record".
