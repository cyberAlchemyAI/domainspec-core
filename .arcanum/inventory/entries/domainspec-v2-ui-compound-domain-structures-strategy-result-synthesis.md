## Accepted Finding

Current evidence does not admit a new compound-structure layer or any of Page,
Layout, Component, Hook, Guard, Binding, Importer, or Processor as a new general
DS-D1 kind. It supports a candidate-only residual-analysis hypothesis for
testing whether a bounded aggregate leaves deterministic obligations after a
finite, preregistered subtraction of existing type, role, projection,
relationship, pattern, aggregation, and named-instance explanations.

Page and Layout currently resolve to contextual roles with unreplicated pattern
pressure. Component, Hook, and Processor are not admitted as general kinds by
current evidence. Guard is a gate role and only a projection when separate Rule
authority exists. Binding must be split by meaning. Importer is a boundary or
workflow role, or a named instance, until stable crossing laws are evidenced.
