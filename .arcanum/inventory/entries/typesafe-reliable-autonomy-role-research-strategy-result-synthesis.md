## Accepted research result

The strongest defensible proposal is **Member of Technical Staff, Agent Task Evals & Environments**, conditional on TypeSafe confirming that one exact component of the task-environment-to-regression-check path is not already owned. The independent final auditor returned `RECOMMEND_ACCEPT` for `findings.md` at SHA-256 `ef2e6709171e3cf34ca01785392ebc9d36a083332429f49744be8b78d8cdd5ab`.

## Selected role hypothesis

The proposed embedded function owns task qualification for one real, recurring, reversible tool-using task: author the environment and runner, freeze the experiment, exercise failure and replay paths, measure operational and economic behavior, and return non-authorizing evidence for a named owner to decide invest, hold, or stop. Conventional aliases are **Research Engineer, Agent Task Evals & Environments** and **Autonomy Task Qualification Engineer**.

## Evidence-backed fit

Repository evidence supports a bounded engineering story: CyberAlchemy v2 contributes authority limits, abstention/escalation, challenges, replay, drift invalidation, and effect boundaries; DomainSpec v2 contributes typed intent, traceability, deterministic source-linked checks, owner gates, blocker/resume state, and experiments that preserve missing evidence; Arcanum contributes runnable task-session, failure-first gate, replay, join, receipt, and lifecycle witnesses. These are strongest as task-environment and runner capabilities around model use, not as model-training or frontier-model-reliability evidence.

Current TypeSafe public evidence captured on 2026-08-01 makes the proposal plausible but not proven: its custom Member of Staff opening invites role creation, while its Synthetic Data, Product, Backend/Platform, and Infrastructure openings define adjacent scopes. Synthetic Data is the largest overlap because it already advertises evaluation frameworks, debugging/data-inspection interfaces, and experiments connecting interventions to model behavior.

## Claim ceiling and kill condition

The evidence does not establish frontier-model training, model architecture, large-scale evaluation infrastructure, deployed-agent reliability, exclusive individual authorship, TypeSafe's internal ownership, the advertised professional-years threshold, or San Francisco logistics. “Member of Technical Staff” is the application target, not verified current seniority.

The separate-role hypothesis is **KILL / absorb into the existing team** if TypeSafe reports that the complete multi-step tool-use path—from environment and grading contract through traces, failure routing, and a regression check—is already owned. It also fails if no real task or actual interfaces are admitted, thresholds cannot be frozen before results, the output is documentation-only, or maintenance costs exceed decision value.
