# Whisper Ontology And Runtime Research Blocked Result

## Direct Result

The two research fronts established an owner-preserving candidate direction but
did not earn exact-binding findings or implementation readiness. Canonical
Whisper composition meaning stays in Arcanum; candidate story-world semantics
remain owner-deferred. A proper runtime requires typed admission, events,
reduction, receipts, human gates, idempotency, recovery, replay, and denial
boundaries that do not currently exist end to end.

Local Python plus SQLite/WAL and content-addressed artifacts is a future
candidate-design selection only, explicitly owner-blocked. The co-op-engine
seam is research-local and zero-authority; the public card and static
deployment were not changed.

## What Survived Review

- OX-01 and RX-01 carry exact repository blobs/selectors and primary external
  precedent with observed versus unverified status.
- Candidate artifacts/entities do not impersonate canonical Whisper concepts.
- Evidence, assessments, receipts, state snapshots, projections, human gates,
  and authority grants remain non-collapsed.
- The proposed fixture covers exact byte rules, mandatory correction, separate
  human evidence, graph validation, stale bindings, crash/retry/replay, and
  denied runtime, ontology, public, and deployment writes.
- Precedent/ownership and definitional/authority reviewer gates passed.

## Blocking Defect

The non-vacuity gate failed at the declared two-loop ceiling. The proposed
sequence uses `pending_gate` and `outcome_ceiling` values outside their declared
enums and resolves correction state without a resolution event or reducer rule.
Therefore `RS26` and live/replay equality are not deterministically defined.

## Route Consequence

The review/evidence-closure gate is blocked. The writer and auditor were not
spawned and `findings.md` was not created. Exactly one dispatch row and one
close row record the six executed agents and `loop_ceiling_reached` exit.

## Claim Ceiling

This is evidence-backed research and a repaired-but-blocked candidate design.
It is not an ontology, runtime implementation, application mutation, deployment,
publication, promotion, release, or production proof. Inventory remains a
non-authority discovery/read projection.
