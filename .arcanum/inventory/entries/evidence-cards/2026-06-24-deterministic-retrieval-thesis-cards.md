## `determinism-is-cost-not-requirement`

- ID: `determinism-is-cost-not-requirement`
- Status: candidate
- Claim or candidate: **determinism is NOT a governance requirement.** The requirement is **provenance
  + a stable citable referent** — auditors attest non-deterministic processes routinely (SOC2 attests
  *that a reviewer approved at T against version V*, not reproducibility); a logged vector pipeline is
  attestable. Determinism's real status = **cost-dominance** (it collapses the attestation/provenance
  surface to ~0), not logical necessity. **REVISES** the earlier `governance-requires-determinism`
  card (which over-claimed "determinism-at-read is a precondition for authority-at-read").
- Source refs: `development/deterministic-retrieval-thesis/findings.md` (§2 Leg 3, §4 A1(c), §6);
  `development/deterministic-retrieval-thesis/research.md` (Z3 self-withdrawal :393-397).

  referents (leg 1), not determinism.
