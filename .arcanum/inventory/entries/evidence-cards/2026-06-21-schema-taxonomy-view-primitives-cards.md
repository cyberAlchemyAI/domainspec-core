## `governed-action-facet-obligations`

- ID: `governed-action-facet-obligations`
- Type: concept
- Status: candidate
- Claim or candidate: `governed action facet obligations` is the corrected
  center for schema/classification/human-view/machine-view lifecycle work. A
  facet is a typed action-obligation surface required for governed
  CyberAlchemy action, not an authority layer, ontology node, or source-of-truth
