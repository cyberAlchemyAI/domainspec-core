# Evidence Cards - Scientific Authority Lifecycle

Each card is a candidate Inventory read model. These cards do not promote a new
definition, ontology schema, constitution, invariant, pattern, or authority
surface. Promotion requires the named downstream owner.

## `scientific-authority-lifecycle-thesis`

- ID: `scientific-authority-lifecycle-thesis`
- Status: candidate
- Claim or candidate: CyberAlchemy can be read as a scientific authority
  lifecycle: operational hypotheses are exercised, observed, challenged,
  replicated across context, and then owner-promoted into reusable patterns,
  invariants, or constitutions only when the evidence supports that movement.
- Source refs:
  `.arcanum/inventory/entries/scientific-authority-lifecycle-synthesis.md`,
  `cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:2766-2802`,
  `projects/ontologize/DAG-AND-METRICS.md:228-251`
- Authority level: synthesis, non-authority
- Promotion owner: definitions-governance if the term becomes canonical;
  constitution-governance if it creates rule pressure
- Trace: synthesized from CAV2-D41/D43, Ontologize DAG, and the
  governed-artifact no-primitive review
- Residue: Name and schema are not canonical.
