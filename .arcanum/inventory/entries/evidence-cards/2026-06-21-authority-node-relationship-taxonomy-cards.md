## `authority-node-deflated-surface-boundary`

- ID: `authority-node-deflated-surface-boundary`
- Status: candidate
- Type: decision
- Claim or candidate: `authority-type` and `authority-posture` are reviewable authority artifacts only through lifecycle envelope fields, owner-profile payloads, PromotionRecord-compatible decision records, ledger evidence, and ontology projections. They are not current ontology-vault `node_type`s, tags, confidence aliases, ledger subgraphs, or movement-graph edges.
