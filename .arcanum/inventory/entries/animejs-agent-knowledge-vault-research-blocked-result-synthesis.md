# Anime.js Agent Knowledge Vault Blocked Research Result

This Inventory entry is a non-authority read model of a closed, blocked
research dispatch. It preserves usable evidence and the exact reason the
candidate ontology was not built.

## Result

The confirmed dispatch ran seven research agents and closed with
`loop_ceiling_reached`. The deterministic reviewer reducer admitted all three
final reviewer receipts but returned `gate_block`; the final split was
precedent `PASS`, non-vacuity `FAIL`, and definitional `PASS`.

## Accepted Evidence

- The official-source lane returned a machine-expandable contract for 418 of
  418 current documentation routes, bound to a semantic JSONL digest. This
  manifest remains returned-not-written, not reproduced package evidence.
- The pinned Anime.js v4.5.0 repository lane returned a 348-row source, type,
  test, example, and package-documentation manifest contract. Source presence
  does not imply test or example execution.
- The recent-model lane classified seven scoped local ontology/model families
  without treating generated projections or proposals as canonical owners.
- The definition repair supplied 21 node contracts and 25 relation contracts;
  precedent and definitional review accepted their ownership and non-collapse
  boundaries.
- Current verified operational Anime.js lessons remain exactly zero because no
  work episode with executed repair evidence was admitted.

## Blocking Evidence

Two final non-vacuity mutants survived:

1. `QRY-003` emits `ABSTAIN_AMBIGUOUS_CHOICE`, while the closed vocabulary
   declares `ABSTAIN_AMBIGUOUS_INTENT`; a tie cannot produce a valid receipt.
2. A relation can name a non-empty consumer without a finite mutation witness
   proving that removing or changing the relation alters retrieval,
   explanation, diagnosis, validation, invalidation, abstention, or next
   action. The consumer rule is therefore still decorative.

## Downstream State

The findings writer and final auditor were not spawned. No `findings.md` was
accepted, and `projects/animejs/ontology/agent-knowledge-vault/` was not
created. No ontology admission, publication, promotion, release, deployment,
or production-readiness decision exists.

## Next Admissible Route

finite behavior-changing witness for every retained relation, then obtain new
confirmation before re-review and any candidate build.
