## Applications & implications (the second question)

- **Applications:** dogfood domainspec-core as ontologize's reflexive proving ground;
  B-op branch as the home for self-governance nodes; a portable kit (authority model +
  default-deny gate owned **out-of-loop** + craft ledger + the affordance-lifecycle engine).
- **Risks:** circular authority (the one irreducible thing reflexivity adds); un-enumerated
  -kind tax; substrate-coupling to cav2's still-candidate apex (`OQ-2`); the **meta-metric
  gap** — gates provably fire, but no measure that self-governance beat plain governance.
