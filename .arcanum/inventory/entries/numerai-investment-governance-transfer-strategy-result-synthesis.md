## Accepted Result

Numerai is implementation precedent, not a missing product layer. The portable
patterns are versioned dataset materialization and ID/time alignment,
config-driven execution and machine-readable result artifacts, time-respecting
validation, and the separation of performance, residual contribution, feature
exposure, and delayed outcomes. These patterns still require local owner
acceptance and prospective definitions before implementation.

The result explicitly rejects exact CORR/MMC/FNC names and forms, Numerai
constants, tournament data/runtime coupling, auxiliary-target ensembles without
validated components, staking/token/stake-weighted authority, live capital,
and the claim that Numerai validates Ontologize. Existing local validation
obligations remain local rather than being re-imported as Numerai novelty.

Ontologize may govern immutable evidence references, denominator semantics,
bounded claims, contradictions, and owner decisions. It may not calculate
financial scores, select models, construct portfolios, trade, or treat monetary
stake as evidence of truth. No local runtime was proven.
