## P1 Trigger Decision

Dispatch is required because current repository authority, algorithm and
program-semantics evidence, and three language-tool ecosystems must be
synthesized; the raw corpus would overfill parent context; external best
practice needs discardable isolation; and both explorer and skeptic work can
run in parallel.

The dispatch extends rather than repeats the auditor-accepted 2026-07-30
implementation-structure result.

## Lanes And Subagents

- Explorers: van Lamsweerde verifies current DomainSpec Operation, Query, Rule,
  binding, effect, and validator ownership; Church tests algorithmic semantics,
  complexity, traversal, loop, fold, and effect claims; Turing verifies
  TypeScript, Python, and Rust enforcement surfaces and limitations.
- Synthesizer: Wiener classifies candidates as portable semantic invariants,
  profile-local policies, heuristics, deferred experiments, or killed folklore.
- Reviewers: Rittel attacks precedent and duplicate ownership; Popper forces
  three-language positive and negative witnesses; Quine attacks definitional
  and authority collapse.
- Writer: Shannon may create only findings.md after exact-binding closure.
- Auditor: Bourbaki audits findings.md; the parent remains final approver.

The proposition “prefer reduce over multiple loops” is an adversarial test case,
not an accepted recommendation.

## Dependency Flow

explorers -> synthesizer -> reviewers -> evidence closure -> writer -> auditor
-> parent

There is one bounded synthesis/review zig-zag, one eligible
synthesizer-to-explorer evidence repair, and one bounded auditor-to-writer
revision. Artifact existence alone does not open a downstream wave.

## Inventory Preflight

The root machine Inventory is lookup-ready. It selected the accepted July
implementation-structure result, the composability/edges/taxonomy synthesis,
and the evolvable-validation framework strategy. It excluded system-integration

therefore requires direct current-source verification and external primary
source evidence. Inventory remains discovery only.

## Candidate Decision Surface

Every pattern must land in exactly one evidence-backed category:

- portable semantic invariant;
- implementation-profile policy;
- advisory heuristic;
- deferred experiment;
- killed folklore.

The research must distinguish query construction, persistence adaptation,
transaction or consistency ownership, business decisions, effects, algorithmic
cost, traversal shape, readability proxies, diagnostics, evidence receipts,
waivers, and promotion authority.

The reduce/loop question must preserve behavior, asymptotic cost, allocation,
ordering, short-circuiting, error propagation, mutation, concurrency,
streaming, and language idiom before any recommendation survives.
