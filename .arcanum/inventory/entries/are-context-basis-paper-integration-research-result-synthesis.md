# ARE Context Basis paper integration research result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the auditor-accepted research
dispatch `2026-09-01-are-context-basis-paper-integration`. It does not promote
definitions, admit an ontology, adopt a Product Capability, select a runtime,
authorize DomainSpec design or implementation, or establish performance or
conformance.

## Result

The two papers strengthen the architecture around the candidate Context Basis
without changing its authority model:

- arXiv:2603.10062v2 supplies useful hierarchy, hybrid local/shared memory,
  access, visibility, ordering, and conflict questions;
- arXiv:2607.21503v1 supplies a useful lifecycle of architecting, ingesting,
  scoping, anticipating, and compacting/consolidating.

The surviving integration candidate is a selector-bound context lifecycle:
owner-controlled sources feed request-relative dependency selection and an
immutable basis; explicit transformations produce consumer-bound views; caches
and shared projections remain disposable or visibility-bearing derivatives,
never routing, admission, decision, continuation, or effect authority.

## Typed outcome

- Candidate: paper vocabulary, operation taxonomy, protocol-field sketches,
  frozen predecision fixture schema, and hand-authored succession plumbing.
- Not evaluable: general definition closure, selector-lock sufficiency, basis
  sufficiency, and dependent semantic benchmarks until OPEN-001 and OPEN-002.
- Killed: Python ontology-runtime enforcement; paper benchmark transfer; and
  all unwitnessed speed, savings, fidelity, consistency, isolation,
  context-rot, warm/cold-equivalence, or readiness claims.
- Owner decision required: OPEN-001, OPEN-002, OPEN-003, projection taxonomy,
  source lifecycle, route projection policies, authority/event vocabulary, and
  experiment thresholds.

## Next route

The next consequential step is not implementation. The ARE and
definitions-governance owners must first resolve OPEN-001, and the ARE and
ontology owners must resolve OPEN-002. OPEN-003 then requires the Codex
integration and Context owners to choose retained view identity plus reuse
receipts or per-turn successor views.

The complete evidence and citations remain in the dispatch `findings.md` and
`research.md` sources above.
