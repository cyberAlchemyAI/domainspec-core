# Ontology Working-Prototype Gap Research Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the proposed research strategy.
It records the exact question, lanes, gate state, evidence leads, and open
residue. It does not establish current ontology capability, authorize the
dispatch, promote ontology meaning, or authorize implementation.

## P1 Trigger

Dispatch is justified by all four Principle-1 triggers:

- synthesis is required across the current ontology tree, authority and
  promotion owners, IOLM/Ponder artifacts, runtime/test surfaces, and prior
  external precedent;
- context protection is required because the current-tree and source evidence
  would be substantially larger than the parent answer;
- isolation is required so speculative prototype boundaries remain
  discardable until attacked by precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional
  skeptics;
- parallelism applies because repository executability, formal contract
  completeness, and user-visible usefulness can be investigated independently.

## Lanes, Groups, And Subagents

- `explorers` runs three agents in parallel under a composite
  methodology/source-corpus tension:
  - `Hewitt, Carl` audits computational repository paths and must return a
    PRESENT/PARTIAL/ABSENT/STALE matrix, concrete commands, and the shortest
    source-to-observable dataflow.
  - `Spivak, David` audits the formal ontology and governance contract and must
    return typed node/relation/view/provenance/promotion obligations plus
    positive and negative finite fixtures.
  - `Simon, Herbert` defines the smallest useful human/agent job and must return
    a scripted scenario with separate machine, human, and authority evidence.
- `synthesizer` is `Mac Lane, Saunders`; it reconciles those three definitions
  of "working" into the lowest sufficient prototype and an ordered
  build-from-owned gap matrix.
- `reviewers` runs three agents in parallel under distinct attack vectors:
  - `Hacking, Ian` applies the precedent gate and classifies each component as
    already deployed, build-from-owned, or precedent-clean.
  - `Popper, Karl` applies the non-vacuity gate and requires one reproducible
    valid/invalid executable witness.
  - `Wittgenstein, Ludwig` applies the definitional-soundness gate and attacks
    collapses among Inventory, ontology, projection, learned scoring,
    hierarchy, validation, and authority.
- `writer` is `Ahrens, Sönke`; it writes `findings.md` only with a complete
  verdict matrix and cited gap/acceptance contract.
- `auditor` is `Loregian, Fosco`; it audits `findings.md` and recommends
  acceptance or one bounded revision. Under the live registrar, the parent
  remains the actual final approver.

## Dependency Flow

`explorers -> synthesizer <-> reviewers -> writer -> auditor` is the sequential
research spine. The synthesizer/reviewer zig-zag has `loop_cap: 2`.
Synthesizer-to-explorer feedback has `loop_cap: 1` and fires only for a
decisive missing fact. Auditor-to-writer revision has `loop_cap: 1`. The whole
dispatch has `max_loops: 1`.
