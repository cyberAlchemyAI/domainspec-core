# Deterministic-Retrieval Thesis — Synthesis (post Two-Lane narrowing)

Candidate Inventory synthesis (not canonical). Records the durable result of dispatch
`2026-06-24-deterministic-retrieval-thesis` (research; Two-Lane Z/A; 3 Lane-Z explorers + writer +
2 Lane-A skeptics + auditor; closed `resolved`). The thesis — *"CyberAlchemy's read path is
deterministic ontology-traversal seeded by typed intent, not vector-RAG"* — survived only in a
**corrected and narrowed** form. Evidence:
[`2026-06-24-deterministic-retrieval-thesis-cards.md`](evidence-cards/2026-06-24-deterministic-retrieval-thesis-cards.md).
**Converges with** [`ontology-operationalization-in-the-machine-synthesis`](ontology-operationalization-in-the-machine-synthesis.md)
(same spine from the VSM/operationalization angle) and **revises two cards** in
[`resonantos-and-ontology-retrieval-synthesis`](resonantos-and-ontology-retrieval-synthesis.md)
(see "Supersession"). Internal read-model — promotes nothing.

## 1. The surviving irreducible object (one line)

**A stable, citable, typed referent + a recorded gate over it, with the query arriving already-typed**
— encodable as a unified graph, a hybrid, OR (dominantly) separate artifacts. A *quarantined
non-deterministic generator* (write-time model-driven extraction) proposes content; a *recorded gate*
(cav2 = the missing S5) confers authority on a frozen typed referent. The genuine contribution is the
**governance + typed-intent provenance WRAPPER around three established patterns**, NOT a novel
retrieval algorithm. "Deterministic retrieval enables governance" overclaims on two counts (findings §1).

## 2. The three legs (deepened, then narrowed by Lane-A)

- **Leg 1 — typed-intent → discrete vector (KEYSTONE, build-from-owned, NOT proven).** The query
  arrives *already a node in a typed graph* (node→pattern, edge→composition; `SEED.md:16-27`) — handed
  a discrete subgraph, not an embedding to denoise. Holds ONLY under a **node-contract boundary** on
  cross-feature edges; else transitive closure re-inflates to whole-graph RAG (Z1 counterexample).
  Build-from-owned of spec-ontology-unification — but the tower is **UNEXECUTED** and the 1:1
  node→pattern assumption is its own open residue.
- **Leg 2 — ingest-time cost relocation (epoch-scoped).** The cheap deterministic read is *purchased*
  at a model-heavy governed write loop (5 line items). Pays cleanly on the **authority dimension** +
  stable-schema/high-read-ratio regimes; **FALSE as unconditional** — silent deterministic-WRONG
  reads on messy input, super-linear reconciliation, and **full-corpus re-ingestion at every ontology
  epoch** (the unbounded tail = view-maintenance-under-schema-change).
- **Leg 3 — governance-needs-determinism (DEMOTED to cost-dominance).** Strong form WITHDRAWN by Z3:
  the requirement is **provenance + a citable referent** (auditors attest non-deterministic processes
  routinely); determinism is a **cost-dominance optimization** (collapses the attestation surface to
  ~0), not a necessity. The ontology is itself non-deterministically authored — "deterministic
  ontology" is a misnomer; only the post-promotion referent + the gate over it are deterministic.

## 3. The two Lane-A corrections (claim ≤ proof)

- **A1(b) — unification is NOT the keystone.** spec-ontology-unification's own Lane A (`SEED.md:48-53`:
  separate artifacts + generated bidirectional mapping + drift-check) delivers the same discrete typed
  seed WITHOUT "same object in two forms," and *dominates* (the drift-check is a detector for the
  unproven 1:1 condition). Keystone = the typed SEED, encoding-agnostic.
- **A1(c) — determinism is NOT a governance requirement.** Provenance + a citable referent is;
  determinism is cost. (Revises the old `governance-requires-determinism` card.)
- **A2 — every leg is build-from-owned of an established pattern** (no terminal KILLs): traversal =
  **GraphRAG / KG-retrieval**; relocation = **CQRS / materialized-view / ETL**; gate = **VSM-S5 /
  symbolic-IR-auditability**. CITE as prior art. The retained novelty is the **two-ontologies**
  distinction (governed hand-typed provenance graph vs GraphRAG's LLM-extracted statistical one) + the
  **already-typed query seed** (which GraphRAG lacks — it still embeds the query to find its entry node).

## 4. Relate to CyberAlchemy as a whole

The honest unifying claim: **CyberAlchemy = a governed typed-referent + recorded-gate wrapper** —
typed seed via DomainSpec (build-from-owned, not-yet-proven) + a recorded gate via cav2-as-S5 (fed by
a quarantined non-deterministic generator) — built on top of GraphRAG-style traversal, CQRS-style
ingest relocation, and VSM-S5 governance, inside a closed loop (Saturn) executed by bounded agents
(Arcanum). The moat is the *wrapper's combination*, demonstrated; the engine algorithm stays
proprietary. cav2-as-S5 attaches to the typed ontology (structural attenuation preserves the referent)
and never to vectors (statistical attenuation dissolves it); vectors, if present, reduce to a thin
governable margin that always terminates in a typed referent.

## 5. Convergence with the operationalization synthesis

Both researches land on the **same spine** from different angles: the operationalization dispatch
(VSM/finishing-and-wiring lens) and this retrieval thesis (RAG/determinism lens) agree that (a) the
read substrate must be a **typed ontology, not vectors** (Conant-Ashby regulator model), (b) **cav2 =
the missing S5** = a recorded gate, never a generator, (c) authority is **conferred at write and
recorded** (the write-authority-proviso = no modeling write-edge to promotion-status ⇔ "content
proposed, authority conferred"), and (d) the highest-leverage move is to **prove the unproven
substrate** (finish+baseline the observer / execute the spec-ontology tower). Two lenses, one machine.
