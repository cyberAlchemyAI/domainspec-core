# Body War Business Ontology Relationship-Coverage Research Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the accepted research. Exact
proof remains in the cited current ontology bytes, governed findings, evidence
closure, auditor return, and validation receipts. Inventory does not create
relations, repair premises, adopt policy, change validation, promote ontology
meaning, or authorize implementation, release, or production.

## Exact Result

The current package has 237 node files after excluding `nodes/manifest.json`,
231 explicit relations, 193 nodes incident to an explicit relation, and 44
explicit zero-degree nodes. All relation endpoints resolve. The graph has 61
weak components: 44 singleton isolates and 17 non-singleton components.

Current node, relation, profile, and validator contracts do not require every
node to participate in an explicit relation.

## Meaning Of Related

Explicit relation degree is calculated only from admitted relation-row
endpoints. Lifecycle-policy membership, source provenance, branch inclusion,
residue impact, challenge metadata, runtime dependency, projection membership,
and authority movement remain separate mechanisms and do not satisfy explicit
degree.

## Complete Classification

The 44 isolates partition exactly into:

- 24 lifecycle-policy members and explicit-relation isolates;
- 10 premise nodes with P0 source-definition drift;
- 6 intentional standalone vocabulary nodes;
- 1 privacy-consent endpoint association requiring owner review with predicate
  unresolved;
- 1 useful deferred city/community operator; and
- 2 useful source-backed concepts with relation decisions still open.

The partition is `24 + 10 + 6 + 1 + 1 + 2 = 44`.

## Decisions

- Reject a universal explicit-incidence rule.
- Keep lifecycle-policy isolates, standalone values, and the deferred operator
  unchanged.
- Reconcile `premise.h-001` through `premise.h-010` before relying on their
  meanings or considering relations.
- Do not add a privacy-consent relation: the source backs an endpoint
  association but does not select a predicate or direction.
- Treat a review-classification completeness registry, diagnostics, and tests
  as unimplemented research recommendations requiring a new
  `body-war.product-ontology-owner` decision.

## Next Owner Route

The product ontology owner should first reconcile the ten premise
definitions. Separately, the owner may decide whether to adopt the
review-classification completeness policy and whether the three open
association/relation questions warrant further evidence. Any accepted
implementation requires a later governed change and failing-then-passing
tests.

## Authority Boundary

The package remains `candidate-local`, with `authority_effect: none` and
`release_admission: not-granted`. The research changed no ontology, schema,
validator, test, application, projection, runtime, release, production, or
authority state.
