# Co-Op Engine Portfolio Research Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model for a resolved research dispatch.
It records the strategy, gates, and close state; it does not promote the
findings into product, implementation, or public authority, and it does not
authorize public disclosure of private project evidence.

## P1 Trigger

Dispatch is justified by all four Principle-1 triggers:

- synthesis across fourteen project corpora, an existing portfolio substrate,
  current external precedent, meeting design, one browser-native
  recruitment/presentation surface, two audience profiles, narrative-preset
  selection, and three validation contracts;
- context protection because raw project and market evidence would overwhelm
  the parent context;
- isolation because speculative product hypotheses must remain discardable
  until skeptic and owner gates admit them;
- parallelism because repository truth, participatory design, external
  precedent, and systems reframing can proceed independently.

## Lanes And Groups

- `lane_z_explorers` runs two parallel explorers with robot talks. It builds the
  portfolio-showcase hypothesis through repository-truth extraction and
  participatory experience construction. Its anti-bias axis is
  `source-corpus + methodology`.
- `lane_a_explorers` runs two parallel explorers with robot talks. It holds the
  collaboration problem fixed while testing external precedents and genuinely
  different service models. Its anti-bias axis is
  `source-corpus + methodology`.
- `synthesizer` reconciles both lanes without averaging away their
  disagreement. It must issue one bridge decision per major mechanism.
- `reviewers` runs three parallel skeptics with robot talks over distinct
  `attack-vector` gates: precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional soundness.
- `writer` produces `findings.md` only.
- `auditor` audits `findings.md` only. The parent remains final approver under
  the live no-self-approval schema.

## Dependency Flow

`lane_z_explorers` and `lane_a_explorers` run in parallel, then both feed
`synthesizer`. Synthesis enters a zig-zag with `reviewers` (`loop_cap: 2`);
accepted reviewer output feeds `writer`, then `auditor`. Auditor-to-writer
feedback has `loop_cap: 1`. The whole dispatch has `max_loops: 2`.
