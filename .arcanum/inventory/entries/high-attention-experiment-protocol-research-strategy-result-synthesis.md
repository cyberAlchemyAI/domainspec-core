# High-Attention Experiment Protocol Research Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model for governed research dispatch
`2026-07-18-high-attention-experiment-protocol-research`. It records the
strategy and auditor-approved result for later specification authoring. It is

## Strategy Summary

**P1 trigger decision:** dispatch was justified by synthesis, context
protection, isolation, and parallelism. A defensible experiment method required
four separately owned lenses: workload/content, execution platform,
internal-validity/inference, and resource-bounded comparison.

**Lanes / groups:**

- `explorers` used a composite
  `methodology + source-corpus + temporal-prior` anti-bias axis. Runtime
  capacity required a documented `3+1` launch, but all four independent returns
  completed before synthesis.
- `synthesizer` reconciled the four ownership surfaces into a candidate
  decision map.
- `reviewers` used the `attack-vector` axis for precedent, non-vacuity, and
  definitional-soundness gates. A two-turn zig-zag repaired the candidate while
  preserving typed KILLs.
- `writer` produced `findings.md` only.
- `auditor` evaluated the findings and returned `APPROVE` without substantive
  revision.

**Gate / ledger state:** the initial check-tension agents both passed the

## Result

The research is sufficient to author a later experiment specification, but the
experiment is not runnable. The surviving contribution is a specification
target plus a closure ledger, not an executable witness.

The findings preserve four design surfaces and repair the central construct:
full context versus a compact evidence-complete control is a retention or
non-inferiority comparison, not a positive-gain test. Any claim that one
checkpoint uses long context better also requires a direct paired checkpoint
contrast and a difference-in-retention contrast. Present tokens, KV-retained
tokens, retrieval-selected tokens, and successfully used evidence remain
separate constructs.

The smallest candidate target is a stack-specific, within-family Llama 1B/3B
comparison over procedural retrieval, two-hop tracing, and contradiction
fixtures, with paired compact/full/evidence-absent controls, exact answer and
provenance scoring, positional rotations, explicit feasibility outcomes, and
separate capability/resource reporting. Initial lengths, margins, sample size,
retrieval role, platform choices, and energy/cost boundary remain unfrozen
human decisions.

## Typed Negatives

- **Runnable-witness claim: KILL.** Generator/data, renderer/scorer, admitted
  checkpoints, frozen runtime, KV-capacity evidence, sample allocation, and
  numeric thresholds do not yet exist as validated artifacts.
- **Prior usable-context formulation: KILL.** Requiring full context to improve
  over a compact prompt containing the same evidence is construct-invalid.
  The later specification must use retention/non-inferiority, added-evidence
  behavior, direct checkpoint comparison, named middle loss, and explicit
  false-support denominators.
