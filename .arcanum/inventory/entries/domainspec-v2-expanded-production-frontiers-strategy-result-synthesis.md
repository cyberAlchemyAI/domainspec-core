# DomainSpec v2 Expanded Production Frontiers Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the resolved research dispatch
`2026-07-13-domainspec-v2-expanded-production-frontiers`. It records the
auditor-approved first-production-release route after the product contract was
expanded to include business ontology/knowledge, architecture and composable
patterns, bounded formal validation, generated executable verification,
structural implementation analysis, and directional cross-graph conformance.

It does not promote definitions, establish profile conformance, prove full
coverage or formal validity, accept Body War, authorize release, or replace the
DomainSpec-v2 Craft ledger.

## Why The Dispatch Reopened The Roadmap

The earlier closed roadmap recommended a narrower validation-toolkit route that
excluded code generation, formal integration, and implementation/spec
equivalence. The accepted product-release contract superseded those capability
exclusions while preserving its strict authority, self-containment, state,
security, operability, CI, clean-candidate, pilot, support, rollback, and release
authorization gates.

All Principle-1 triggers held: formal semantics, executable generation, and the
Body War witness required independent evidence lanes; the evidence corpus was
too large for a single parent pass; the tensions were genuinely independent;
and a final synthesis required precedent, falsifiability, and definitional
review.

  the Body War critical path.

Nine agents ran with no helpers and no explorer feedback rerun. The final
dispatch outcome is `resolved`; the product status remains unauthorized.

## Core Result

The first release is a private repository-local DomainSpec-v2 CLI for one
pinned Linux profile. It must establish one candidate-bound chain:

```text
B=(BO,BK,provenance,authority) → Spec → Sg
Sg → A_req
Sg → F_struct/F_state
Sg → O_spec → T_gen → E_run
Sg → G → Sg_canonical
frozen code roots → I_struct
all applicable planes → cross_graph_conformance_vector
```

`B`, `Sg`, `A_req`, `O_spec`, `T_gen`, and `I_struct` are the contract planes.
`G`, `F_struct`, `F_state`, and `E_run` are internal proof/evidence artifacts
unless a supported profile later exposes them. Generated projections are not
parallel semantic authority, generated tests are not run evidence, and tags are
correspondence claims rather than structural truth.

“Full coverage” is exact equality with an independently owned, nonempty
`O_spec(P)`, every required expected outcome/evidence class, zero skip/todo/
placeholder/duplicate/orphan/absent/unrunnable obligations, and a killed mutant
per obligation key. “Equivalence” is reserved for the bounded `Sg/G` retraction;
the product claim elsewhere is directional preservation and conformance, not
universal code/spec identity.

## Ordered Route

The approved roadmap has `FPR-00` through `FPR-15`:

1. `FPR-00` accepted decision — closed at decision altitude.
2. `FPR-01` authority baseline — first open blocker.
3. `FPR-02` machine product/profile/pilot contract.
4. `FPR-03` governed business plane and `B→Spec` mapping.
5. `FPR-04` total `Spec→Sg` projection and bounded internal retraction.
6. `FPR-05/06/07/08` architecture, formal, generated verification, and
   structural implementation lanes.
7. `FPR-09` directional conformance-vector technical join.
8. `FPR-10/11` exact Body War behavior and release substrate side rails.
9. `FPR-12` clean reproducible release candidate and seeded-red CI.
10. `FPR-13` controlled Body War pilot with an exact `14/14` denominator.
11. `FPR-14` Craft expected-state equality and persistent continuation.
12. `FPR-15` separate claim acceptance, external authorization, and immutable
    private release.

The roadmap is `REPAIR-THEN-GATE`, not roadmap-ready for execution. `FPR-01`
must reconcile the live 13/14, 24/25, and 30/31 authority projections plus
ontology direction before `FPR-02` freezes the supported profile. Parallel
implementation that invents graph schemas or denominators before those two
nodes close is invalid work, not acceleration.

## Body War Witness

The witness is the SuggestedTrack / Parque Taquaral slice, not all TROQAR. The
exact journey authenticates a consenting runner, creates the profile-bound
`volta-do-taquaral` SuggestedTrack, attaches an ActivityEvidenceCandidate, and
permits only `needs_evidence→evidence_submitted`. It must reject unsupported
`source_backed`, verified-geometry, scoring, leaderboard, accepted/rejected, or
pilot-ready promotions. Real-provider callback/origin and allowed-redaction
fields remain blocking frontier decisions.

## Persistent Frontier Strategy

The live continuation surface is `CTX-DSV2-FIRST-PRODUCTION-RELEASE` in
`projects/domainspec-v2/.craft/ledger.yml`. The machine projection under
`craft/expected-node-edge-manifest.yml` records 16 nodes, 38 dependency/evidence
edges, five active frontiers, 24 candidate terms, and the 14 pilot steps.

The stable active frontiers are ontology direction, formal solver profile,
Body War provider/origin/redaction route, implementation-root dispositions,
and APE self-containment. Evidence expires when candidate SHA, profile,
authoritative business/spec sources, mapping, toolchain, solver bounds, source
roots, normalization, or implementation changes. New frontier research still
requires a material dependency, a passed trigger, a tensioned sheet, explicit
human confirmation, registration, and append-only closeout.

Inventory remains a compiled query surface. It must never become the place
where a frontier, owner decision, semantic definition, implementation
acceptance, or release authorization exists exclusively.

## Binding Exclusions

No DomainSpec UI, hosted DomainSpec service, public distribution/SDK,
multi-language implementation, automatic Saturn actuation, arbitrary full-app
generation, universal program proof, proof of undeclared requirements, generic
APE mint/inheritance/cross-project propagation, or automatic promotion/release
authorization is part of this release. Trigger-bound controls such as hermetic
build, SBOM/SLSA, broad attestation, tabletop exercises, and central dashboards
activate only through the bounded threat/distribution decision.

## Current Verdict And Next Move

Production, profile conformance, full coverage, formal validity,
architecture/implementation conformance, cross-graph conformance, clean-candidate
status, Body War acceptance, release readiness, and production authorization are
all unestablished.

Next: execute `FPR-01 AUTHORITY-BASELINE`. Produce one non-contradictory,
owner-ratified authority baseline and a deliberate mutation receipt before
authoring the machine supported profile.
