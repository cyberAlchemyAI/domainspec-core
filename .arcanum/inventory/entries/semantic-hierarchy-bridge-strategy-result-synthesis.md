## Accepted Result

The accepted answer is that the repositories contain useful **contracts and one
narrow projection witness**, not a working hierarchy-discovery engine.

The defensible bridge has five non-collapsing layers:

1. pinned model-neighborhood evidence;
2. explicit typed-relation proposals;
3. separate order-candidate and facet-assignment policies;
4. named-view projection followed by separate componentwise comparison; and
5. validation plus typed residue.

Embedding proximity does not entail semantic relation or parenthood. Projection
does not perform induction. Shared view identities do not prove hierarchy
alignment. Lean preservation taxonomies are not learned semantic ladders.
