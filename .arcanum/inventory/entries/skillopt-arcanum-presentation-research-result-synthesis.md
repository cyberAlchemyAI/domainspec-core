# SkillOpt and Arcanum Presentation Research Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of an auditor-accepted research
artifact whose orchestration evidence closeout failed. It makes the useful
presentation substrate discoverable without converting a semantic audit into
validated run evidence. It does not implement SkillOpt in Arcanum, validate a
live demo, authorize publication, promote a capability, deploy anything, or
establish production readiness.

## Research Outcome

The Portuguese findings package gives a bounded 35-minute presentation spine,
an exact-source claim matrix, a terminology legend, a precedent map, and a
contract simulation. Its thesis is deliberately narrow: SkillOpt reports a
method for proposing and selecting changes to one persistent textual skill
state, while the inspected public Arcanum corpus documents separate contracts
for evidence, observation, reflection, approval, validation, and lifecycle.
Their relationship is limited structural similarity, adjacency, and a proposed
complement—not equivalence or an implemented integration.

SkillOpt results remain paper-reported and were not reproduced. Current Arcanum
claims come from public-source inspection, not runtime execution. The proposed
presentation witness is titled `source trace + SIMULAÇÃO DE CONTRATO`; expected
output, candidate edits, validation, and promotion are explicitly marked
`EXPECTED`, `PROPOSED`, or `NOT RUN`.

## Dispatch Outcome

- Three explorers independently covered SkillOpt primary evidence, current
  public Arcanum state, and presentation non-vacuity.
- One synthesizer reconciled them; three skeptics checked precedent,
  non-vacuity, and definitional non-collapse.
- One writer produced `findings.md`; one auditor requested one exact wording
  repair and then returned `RECOMMEND_ACCEPT` with no semantic blockers.
- One read-only governance helper diagnosed receipt and evidence mechanics.
- The ledger contains one registration row and one terminal close row with
  `exit_reason: error`.

## Evidence Closeout Blocker

The raw 64-record orchestration event log is an observational mixed log, not a
schema-complete causal run-event stream. It lacks direct, correctly ordered
`wait_attempted`, `receipt_joined`, and `gate_decided` evidence. A canonical
passing stream cannot be created from the inspected files without inventing
historical events, so the failed validator receipt and raw log are preserved.

This blocker applies to validated orchestration closeout. It does not erase the
source-bound research text or the independent semantic audit, but neither may
be cited as proof that the native run evidence passed. A future canonical
evidence package needs exact provenance and renewed evidence acceptance.

## Presentation-Use Boundary

Use `findings.md` as research substrate for slide authoring. Do not present the
contract simulation as code, live, runnable, or executed. Keep paper-reported,
locally inspected, proposed, and unverified states visible. The final slides,
rehearsal, executable demo, and audience-comprehension proof remain outside this
dispatch.
