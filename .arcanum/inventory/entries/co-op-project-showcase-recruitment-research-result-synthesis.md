# Co-Op Project Showcase And Recruitment Research Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of accepted research. It does not
admit public project claims, select a final interface, authorize copy,
implementation, or publication, or prove comprehension or recruitment.

## Accepted Product Substrate

The Co-Op Engine remains the existing weekly meeting. Its showcase should be
one browser-native truth surface that supports both live presentation and
self-guided recruitment.

The research retains two genuine comparison candidates:

- `decision-lens-first`: a visitor begins from a domain, problem, skill, or
  contribution lens and then reaches projects;
- `project-index-first`: a visitor begins from a complete project map and then
  reaches each project's opening.

Both candidates must use the same project records, durable project routes,
project-owned previews, adjacent proof and limits, and explicit contributor
states. A live view is a sparse projection of those facts, not a separate deck
with presenter-only truth. Current evidence does not select a winner.

## Content And Trust Contract

The substrate defines:

- a 10-second, 30-second, and deep-detail grammar;
- source-bounded opening-content maps for all fourteen projects;
- stable project truth separated from volatile welcome and contribution state;
- visitor actions separated into inspect or save, non-binding offer, contact,
  human clarification, and commitment;
- a Whisper intake that preserves proof boundaries and project voice rather
  than normalizing every project into generic marketing prose;
- safe and forbidden claim classes, typed negatives, and unresolved owner
  decisions.

## Minimum Comparative Specimen

The smallest next artifact contains three distinct project previews—Agentic
Music Studio, Poker Agent Arena, and Sonar Loop—plus one full Poker Agent Arena
detail route, both candidate entrances, recoverable project-map navigation,
and two cold-reader sessions.

Operational browser evidence, conversational-AI/chat and copy evidence, and
human retellability or contribution-decision evidence remain separate. A
passing layout or route check cannot prove understanding or recruitment.
