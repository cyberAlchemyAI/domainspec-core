## Strategy And Expected Result

The strategy tests whether UX-plan selection should extend to human-operated,
learned, approved, recovered, explained, content-bearing, accessible, and
audited workflows rather than only explicit UI requests. It also tests whether
the architecture companion needs risk-triggered coverage for runtime
enforcement, authority, state, persistence, concurrency, failure, integration,
deployment, data, security, abuse, and quality trade-offs.

The synthesis must disposition security, privacy, data, reliability,
operations, performance, migration, integration, content, accessibility, and
validation into an existing companion, a justified candidate family,
downstream Plan, or no change. It must produce `required`, `recommended`, and
`not-applicable-with-rationale` outcomes so added coverage does not turn every
Design run into every template.

## Result And Claim Boundary

The auditor accepted the converged findings after one synthesis/reviewer loop.
The result retains six views as navigation, not sufficiency; broadens UX-plan
selection only when a changed natural-person actor and changed human-facing
semantic contract both exist; and keeps security, privacy, data, reliability,
operations, performance, migration, integration, content, accessibility, and
validation as risk-triggered architecture/UX extensions or downstream
obligations rather than universal new families.

The key repair is a closed digest-bound `scope_manifest`, an independently
produced denominator receipt, exact concern owners, two-pass fixed-point
selection, and four dispositions: `required`, `recommended`,
`not-applicable-with-rationale`, and `block`. Design planned-witness contracts
remain separate from Plan runnable evidence.

The detector, denominator extractor, expanded Design validator, receipt
schemas, and frozen fixtures do not exist. The current package may claim only
`authored-complete`, not `design-validator-pass` or `plan-evidence-pass`.
`ILP-G01` remains unresolved. See `research.md` and `findings.md`.
