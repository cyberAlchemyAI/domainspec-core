# Arcanum framework method and quality boundary

The CyberAlchemy Method turns bounded intent and discovered evidence into reviewable artifacts through orient, discover, shape, stabilize, and evolve phases. Its recurring controls are the smallest coherent unit, explicit tension, trace preservation, lifecycle routing, and reflection after use.

Quality Bar and Anti-Patterns form a paired execution boundary. The Quality Bar defines observable acceptance evidence; Anti-Patterns define misuse and reject conditions. Both vary by tier: Formulae emphasize deterministic exactness, Transmutations emphasize faithful source-grounded synthesis, and Arcana emphasize role clarity, phase discipline, preserved evidence, and gates.

## Ownership boundary

The method does not collapse lifecycle owners. Invoke may prepare artifacts, Sigil Development owns sigil lifecycle, Spellcraft owns spell lifecycle, and Task Session owns bounded execution. Candidate local vocabulary remains local until its proper governance route promotes it.

This Inventory entry summarizes those rules for lookup. It does not certify any sigil, spell, run, or implementation against them.
