## Accepted Result

The semantic engine is the first major implementation capability, but not the
next safe task. `FPR-01 -> FPR-02 -> FPR-03` must first establish the ratified
baseline, supported profile, source disposition, and precedence contract.
`FPR-04` can then build the first authoritative `Spec -> Sg` walking skeleton.

The research produced 21 live evidence identifiers, not 21 new gaps. They
route into eight existing-owner packages. Only `EGI-005` (deterministic
definition-drift scan missing), `EGI-010` (local IR/authority edge collision),
and `EGI-020` (stale import-manifest destinations) are newly witnessed residue,
and all three remain inside existing owners.

The first safe slices remain:

1. restore the retired-reference guard and close `FPR-01`;
2. freeze a bounded backend profile under `FPR-02`;
3. close the existing precedence frontier under `FPR-03`;
4. build a two-node/one-edge `FPR-04` compiler and independent retraction;
5. admit only the first-witness node pattern and edge composition under
   `FPR-05`.

The interactive ontology implementer is a read-only consumer after the first
four gates. It may traverse generated `Sg`, exercise typed negatives, and route
residue back to FPR-04/05/07. It cannot mint relationships, evidence authority,
lifecycle identifiers, or replacement gap identities.

## Workflow Residue

The runtime had a four-thread ceiling. Eight registered role turns executed,
but only five unique subagent threads were available: completed explorer
threads were reused for the synthesizer and two reviewers. Writer and auditor
threads remained distinct. This weakens the constitution's one-agent/one-role
isolation and means the close row's role-count total must not be interpreted as
eight unique runtime threads. The research result remains evidence-backed and
auditor-accepted, but the run cannot be claimed fully constitution-conformant.

The named `domainspec-research-writing` and `domainspec-findings-writing`
packages were also absent. Their output behavior was followed directly from
the research dispatch contract. This is capability-registration residue, not a
DomainSpec engine finding.
