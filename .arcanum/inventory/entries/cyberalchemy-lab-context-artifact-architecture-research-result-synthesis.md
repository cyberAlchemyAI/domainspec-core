# CyberAlchemy-lab Context And Artifact Architecture Research Result

## P1 Trigger And Strategy

The work required a registered multi-agent research dispatch because the source
inventory, semantic/authority distinctions, recursive ownership model, and
deterministic repository mechanics could not be investigated on one axis
without trading breadth for depth. The confirmed goal was research-only: take
the user's seed tree as a hypothesis, inventory existing relevant surfaces, and
run a three-track Distill Tournament without selecting or implementing an
architecture.

## Lanes, Subagents, And Dependency Flow

- Tarski explored the artifact-centered logical record contract.
- Ashby explored Product/Governance classification and the owner-local
  recursive capability-instance envelope.
- Simon explored the mechanics-only shared contract and deterministic
  projection/source-admission checks.
- Noether synthesized the three units; Lakatos, Popper, and Wittgenstein
  independently attacked precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional collapse.

writer -> auditor -> parent. One synthesizer/reviewer zig-zag and one
auditor/writer revision used the two-loop ceiling. Three one-time helper re-asks
repaired a missing synthesis handoff without changing the material strategy.

## Inventory Preflight

The machine Inventory was lookup-ready before the sheet was drafted. Selected
evidence established that semantic type, authority kind, source direction,
lifecycle, context identity, construction stage, and filesystem layout are
orthogonal; recursion requires altitude, owner, binding, recomposition, and a
stop; and the clean-history Lab has not admitted v2 sources. Nearby proposals
were excluded where they treated a universal governed-artifact primitive,
directory symmetry, or readable projections as authority.

## Direct Research Result

The accepted research result preserves one leading falsifiable option set:

1. an owner-local logical artifact record contract with fail-closed
   `record_kind: artifact_type | artifact_instance` and exact
   `artifact_type_ref` for instances;
2. an owner-local recursive capability-instance envelope, framed by a
   separately identified CAV2 Context and only optionally related to separately
   evidenced DDD Bounded Contexts;
3. a Shared Artifact Mechanics Contract limited to six mechanics: source
   direction, schema binding, generator identity/version/digest, deterministic
   projection comparison, source-admission reference shape, and typed relation
   envelope.

The shared contract is a new unadmitted Lab build-from-owned hypothesis, not a
semantic kernel or authority mechanism. Axiom, Invariant, and Premise remain
distinct semantic types whose current authority-bearing artifacts roll up
through Definition; that rollup does not make them semantic subtypes or create
separate authority kinds. Governance has candidate bounded-context evidence;
Product bounded-context identity remains unverified.

## Residue And Next Human Action

No Lab artifact type, source-admission policy, owner, schema, generator,
validator, receipt contract, negative-test suite, D64 instance binding, D65
construction binding, or architecture decision was created. The next
admissible move is a separately confirmed, non-effectful witness-and-mutant
validation exercise over one proposed local artifact, one typed cross-context
relation, and the eight named failure mutants. Only after that evidence closes
may an authorized owner decide whether to enter design.
