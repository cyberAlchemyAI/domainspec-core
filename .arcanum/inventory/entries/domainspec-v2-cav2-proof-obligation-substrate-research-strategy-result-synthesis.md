# DomainSpec v2 and CyberAlchemy v2 Proof/Obligation Substrate Research Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the closed, auditor-approved
research dispatch. It records a design recommendation and its unresolved owner
and evidence gates. It does not make the substrate canonical or authorize
definitions, constitution, source, implementation, promotion, publication,
release, deployment, or production changes.

## P1 Trigger Decision

All four Principle-1 triggers apply: cross-owner synthesis, parent-context
protection, discardable isolation for competing substrate topologies, and
parallel DomainSpec v2, CyberAlchemy v2, and external-precedent research.

The research may recommend a documentation substrate. It cannot make that
substrate canonical. Canonical adoption remains an owner-routed continuation.

## Decision And Topology

- TOP-03, a shared syntax-only envelope plus owner-local schemas, catalogs, and
  derived projections, is the conditional recommendation.
- TOP-02, two independent owner-local registries plus a derived crosswalk, is
  the automatic authority-correct fallback when the shared syntax owner,
  canonical source, authority ceiling, or exact two-owner adoption acts are not
  ratified.
- TOP-01, one shared cross-project semantic registry, is rejected because it
  would compete with the two current owners.

## Shared Syntax Ceiling

The shared layer may carry identity, version, digest, exact owner-qualified
references, source bindings, and an assertion limit. It may validate syntax and
structural completeness only.

It may not define record kinds, relation meaning, lifecycle transitions,
obligation status or discharge, authority or promotion semantics, attestation
meaning, or publication authorization. Those remain owner-local.

## Evidence And Status Boundaries

Evidence, formal proof objects, checker evidence, receipts, attestation
components, lifecycle decisions, and publication authorization remain distinct.
Materialization origin, authority posture, record currentness, obligation
status, evaluation outcome, projection freshness, and publication authorization
are orthogonal axes and must not be collapsed into one status field.

The current EVF chain is useful candidate evidence, not a complete proof.
Dirty or untracked bytes, planned validators, and version-exact fixture plans do
not establish execution, discharge, adoption, or promotion.

## Public And Authority Boundary

DomainSpec semantics and CyberAlchemy authority remain separately owned.
CyberAlchemy may govern authority kinds, promotion, and claim ceilings without
owning DomainSpec domain meaning. Public Arcanum may receive only separately
authorized public-safe material; this private research strategy authorizes no
such transfer.

Hashes bind bytes, receipts report executions, and attestations prove only
their demonstrated integrity or identity tier. None independently proves
semantic truth or grants canonical status.
