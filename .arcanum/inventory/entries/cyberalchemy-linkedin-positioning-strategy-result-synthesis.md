## Strategy Summary

**P1 trigger:** synthesis, parallelism, isolation, and context protection all
held. The work required separate readings of formal evidence, governance/machine
maturity, and public audience language, followed by two independent claim attacks.

**Groups and angles:**

- three explorers covered category theory/Lean, cybernetics/authority/machine map,
  and LinkedIn audience/company-versus-founder voice;
- one synthesizer reconciled a common positioning spine;
- two skeptics independently attacked non-vacuity and definitional soundness;
- one writer produced cited findings and candidate copy;
- one independent auditor owned final acceptance.

**Dependency flow:** explorers -> synthesizer <-> reviewers -> writer -> auditor.
The single reviewer zig-zag killed the formal-first candidate and repaired the
public category, audience, outputs, authority language, and formal claim scope.
The auditor requested one bounded revision, then returned `ACCEPT`.

**Gate and ledger:** both pre-dispatch tension checkers returned PASS. The user
explicitly confirmed execution. The dispatch row is appended to
`telemetry/agents/subagents-dispatch.yaml`; the close row records the terminal
result separately.

## Result

The selected positioning is an A/C hybrid:

- lead with governed human-AI workflows for teams doing complex knowledge work;
- immediately name reviewable outputs such as decision maps, typed specifications,
  bounded experiments, source-backed briefs, and reviewable artifacts;
- state the authority rule in public terms: AI may surface evidence, run checks,
  and propose changes, while people retain judgment and acceptance authority;
- name CyberAlchemy v2 and DomainSpec v2 as current research and engineering work,
  not as shipped public layers;
- place cybernetics, systems thinking, domain modeling, knowledge/authority
  governance, formal methods, category theory, and software design after concrete
  value;
- restrict Lean/category-theory claims to particular constructions and claims
  about refinement, translation, and information loss.

The company page uses an institutional voice. The founder entry uses the
operator-supplied title `Founder | AI Governance Architect & Researcher`, states
leadership and co-creation, and preserves human collaborators and human review.
