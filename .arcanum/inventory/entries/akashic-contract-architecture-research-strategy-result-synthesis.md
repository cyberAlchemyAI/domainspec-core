# Akashic Contract Architecture Research Strategy

This entry is a non-authority read model of the frozen research proposal. It
does not confirm or register the dispatch, prove a contract, authorize a public
Design edit, bind an owner, or establish runtime behavior.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Synthesis, isolation, and parallelism all apply. The question spans the current
Akashic record and validation surfaces, external contract and provenance
precedents, Phase-1 agent contribution controls, version and compatibility
policy, and later semantic admission. Independent exploration and adversarial
review protect the parent from treating a broad reading as one proved answer.

## Lanes, Subagents, And Output

| Lane | Subagents | Role and expected output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract explorers | Abramsky, Samson; Peirce, Charles Sanders; Ashby, W. Ross | Parallel formal-structural, provenance-lineage, and operational agent-workflow contract matrices |
| Contract synthesizer | Noether, Emmy | Phase-labelled contract catalogue, producer-consumer map, authority non-collapse map, compatibility policy, witnesses, and exact Design-delta candidate |
| Contract reviewers | Popper, Karl; Sattler, Christian; Quine, Willard Van Orman | Parallel precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional-soundness gates |
| Findings writer | Adámek, Jiří | Cited `findings.md` only, including plain-language explanation, verdict matrix, and public-safe Design delta |
| Findings auditor | Brandenburg, Martin | Independent approve-or-revise audit of `findings.md` |

The explorers disagree over minimal structural closure, provenance richness,
and operational contribution safety. The reviewers disagree through three
non-overlapping attack vectors: precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional
soundness.

## Dependency Flow

```text
three explorers in parallel
  -> synthesizer
  <-> three reviewers in parallel (one zig-zag)
  -> writer
  -> auditor
  -> writer if one bounded revision is required
  -> parent final approval
```

The parent, not a working-group agent, owns final approval and any later
curation into the public Akashic Design.

## Inventory Preflight And Design Consequence

The target repository has no local Inventory package. A machine-index-first
lookup of the private parent Inventory selected the prior Akashic architecture
result, Inventory-to-ontology boundary, deterministic-retrieval demotion, and
authority-lifecycle separation. Adjacent reasoning-engine and richer
cross-graph validation entries were excluded as scope expansion.

The lookup changed the strategy from a broad architecture review into a
phase-labelled contract catalogue. Every surviving contract must name its
producer, consumer, inputs, outputs, invariant, failure boundary, authority
effect, version owner, status, and discriminating positive/negative witness.
Phase-0 record and projection contracts remain separate from Phase-1 GitHub and
agent workflow contracts and later graph or Olog admission.
