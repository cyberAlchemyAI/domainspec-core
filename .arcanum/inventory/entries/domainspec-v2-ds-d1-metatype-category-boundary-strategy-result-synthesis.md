## Result

The auditor accepted `findings.md`. The central result is:

> DS-D8 is active relationship-signature authority, while DS-D1 is staged
> type-formation authority: backend `DS-D1.1..14` are schema/fixture-backed
> shape candidates, and UI `DS-D1.15..25` are DS-D8 endpoint-used but DS-D1
> formation-deferred.

The findings reject a silent promotion path: DS-D8 may reference UI endpoint
labels, but it does not confer DS-D1 formation authority on those labels.
`active-harden` is a work posture, not proof.

## Accepted R3 Consequences

- Keep the current phrase **typed composable graph fragment**. Full category
  law is deferred until identity, composition, closure, associativity, and path
  laws are finite validator obligations.
- Normalize relationship signature identity by the full tuple: verb,
  source/target type or type arrays, direction, multiplicity, feature scope,
  lifecycle status, and generated/deferred/deprecated policy.
- Preserve status separation: `owns` and `belongs-to` are active; `owned-by` is
  generated; `depends-on` is deferred; `enforces-cross` is deprecated into
  `enforces`.
- Treat Saga as an explicit fork: preserve active DS-D8
  `Saga --[orchestrates]--> Operation[]` and harden DS-D1 participant,
  compensation, failure/residue, temporal, and cross-owner laws, or owner-route
  collapse/deprecation into Workflow + scope metadata.
- Treat backend DS-D1 rows as shape/fixture-backed candidates until stronger
  semantic obligations become structural and finite.
- Treat UI 11 as `DS-D8 endpoint-used / DS-D1 formation-deferred` unless
  cards, schemas, fixtures, and correction contracts are added.
