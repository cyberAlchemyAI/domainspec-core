# Craft Causal Class Total Refresh Review Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model for the resolved governed review
dispatch `2026-07-10-craft-teaching-total-refresh-review` and the clean-room
teaching package produced from its accepted change requests. It does not replace
the review evidence, the YAML deck authority, or learner validation.

## P1 Trigger

Dispatch was warranted by synthesis, isolation, and parallelism. The target was
one teaching system spread across three essays, two schema views, a rendered
presentation, and prior research/findings. The review needed independent
beginner, narrative, formal, and browser lenses before a new package could be
responsibly authored.

## Lanes And Subagents

The attacker lane ran four agents in parallel with robot-talks:

- `Halford, Graeme`: empirical beginner cognitive walkthrough.
- `Alexander, Christopher`: historical-lineage causal story analysis.
- `Spivak, David`: traceability to the existing Craft teaching definition.
- `Shannon, Claude`: computational and adversarial browser-state audit.

The downstream lanes were:

- `Ahrens, Sonke`, writer: preserved attacker positions and synthesized cited
  change requests.
- `Schon, Donald`, skeptic: vacuity and novice-witness verification.
- `Popper, Karl`, skeptic: definitional, evidence, and scope verification.
- `Brandenburg, Martin`, auditor and dedicated final approver: coverage,
  dropped-finding, zero-findings, and public-boundary audit.

The anti-bias design combined methodology, temporal-prior, source-corpus, and
attack-vector tension. The initialization gate required two independent passes;
the final complete sheet received double PASS before human confirmation.

## Dependency Flow

```text
attackers -> change-request writer <-> verifiers -> coverage auditor
```

The writer/verifier zig-zag used one revision turn. The first final audit
rejected laundering of two dropped process findings and an under-specified
public boundary. After those requirements were removed and the boundary was
made explicit, the same auditor returned APPROVE.

## Accepted Review Result

The accepted review identifies one CRITICAL browser defect and a connected set
of MAJOR teaching defects:

- Space could reveal and navigate in one activation.
- Most slides rendered witness, answer, and term simultaneously.
- The table did not preserve one caused history from choice to wobble.
- Schema validity and plan order were visually conflated.
- Lower-layer formation was taught as complexity rather than an evidence-gated
  boundary with residue reduction, local validation, upper validation, and
  recomposition.
- The essays explained concepts before the learner encountered the failure that
  made each concept useful.

Original findings 4, 6, 16, and 22 were dropped. Failed repair remains one clear
beginner teaching scene, not a universal Craft precondition.

`arcanum/development/craft/teaching/craft-causal-class-2026-07-11/` and contains:

The package uses one persistent desk history: bounded target, learner choice,
artifact, caused wobble, chosen shim witness, candidate lower build, local and
upper checks, recomposition, acceptable-residue stop, and software recognition.
Formal terms arrive only after their witness.
