# Ontological Way Clear Artifact-Readiness Research Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of a confirmation-ready research
proposal. It does not establish a canonical ontology, validate any artifact,
register or run the dispatch, implement Way Clear, alter public Arcanum, or
authorize promotion, publication, release, deployment, or production.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Dispatch is warranted by synthesis, isolation, parallelism, and context
protection across seven artifact families and three evidence traditions,
followed by precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional attacks.

## Core Research Question

Test whether a profile-indexed query of the form
`WayClear(artifact, closureProfile, transition, scopeDeclaration, snapshot)` can
return a reason-complete, digest-bound receipt without collapsing definitions,
criteria, requirements, Definitions of Done, architecture, designs, and UX
plans into a universal checklist.

## Dependency Flow

`explorers -> synthesizer <-> reviewers -> exact-binding closure -> writer -> auditor -> parent`

One synthesizer-to-explorer evidence feedback and one auditor-to-writer revision
are permitted. No undeclared route may be traversed.

## Candidate Ontology Boundary

The proposal tests a shared obligation, decision, claim, evidence, owner,
authority, scope, dependency, closure-profile, reason, and receipt substrate,
while retaining distinct ClosureProfiles for every artifact kind. Candidate
vocabulary is subject to merge, rename, defer, or kill; it is not canonical.

## Finite Witness And Evidence Closure

The candidate must survive a finite seven-artifact graph with a positive chain,
false-clear and false-block mutants, contradictions, stale evidence, conflicting
owners, out-of-scope nodes, and open-world unknowns. Every positive load-bearing
row requires an immutable source identity, exact selector or structured pointer,
explorer return, evidence scope, and explicit proof status.

Way Clear, explicit closure scope, and a finite mutation witness. Inventory is
