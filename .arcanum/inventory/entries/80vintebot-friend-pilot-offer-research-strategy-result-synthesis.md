## Accepted Result

**Interview first. Make no pilot offer now. All three candidate pilots are
`KILL pending discovery`.**

The leading discovery hypothesis is a bounded synthetic configuration review:
compare one current and one proposed AI-agent setup with made-up conversations
and no production activity, solely to inform an authorized 80VinteBot customer
decision.

It is not executable until discovery witnesses:

1. a consequential decision and exact object/version;
2. the authorized decision body and required co-approvals;
3. observable no-production access conditions;
4. a current-configuration comparison baseline;
5. a joined synthetic trace from input through proposed action and escalation;
6. named evidence, burden, metric, retention, deletion, and exit owners.

The onboarding-repeatability and claim-to-evidence candidates are also killed
pending named decisions, owners, artifacts, and witnesses.

## Claim Boundary

The possible CyberAlchemy contribution is a governed decision record and
explicit evidence boundary, not novel evaluation technology. The research
separates contractual delivery, owner use, descriptive operational change,
repeat interest, referral, and signed payment.

A zero-fee friend pilot cannot establish willingness to pay, ROI, demand,
production reliability, product correctness, audit coverage, assurance,
security properties, compliance, certification, legal approval, conversion
impact, revenue impact, or performance outside the tested setup and conditions.
