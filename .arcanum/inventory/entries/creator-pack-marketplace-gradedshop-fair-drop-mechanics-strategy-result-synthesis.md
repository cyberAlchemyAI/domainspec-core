## Result

The strongest conditional v1 candidate is a finite, pre-custodied inventory
drawn without replacement. Quantity-derived tiers are acceptable as a
presentation layer when tier odds equal the sum of remaining item quantities.
The initial manifest and transition rules must be committed before sale; live
state changes append-only after each draw. Arbitrary post-sale odds, unlimited
unique-jackpot replenishment, internal wallet/cash-out, buyback/guaranteed RTP,
and a permanent fixed-price 90% return claim are rejected or deferred.

The R$10,000 witness is arithmetically coherent but only conditionally solvent:
R$20 price, 10,000 items, R$180,000 initial reference value, R$18 reference EV,
90% initial reference-value ratio, 90% reference-value shortfall, and roughly
R$117.87 standard deviation. These are reference estimates, not RTP or cash
guarantees.

The legal gate remains open. No money should be accepted until Brazilian
classification, CDC Article 49 treatment after reveal, licensed PSP/payment
flow, seller/title/custody/authenticity, reserves, and adult age-assurance
controls are cleared in writing.
