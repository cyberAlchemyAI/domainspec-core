## Audited Research Answer

Bolina is a three-band identity, transport, and capability system. Its grant
path binds a human-signed, single-use grant to an exact intent, executor,
resource, action digest, nonce, and expiry. The admitted source archaeology
reports checks 0 through 10, durable grant consumption at check 11, and effect
entry afterward. It also reports that effect and publication tombstone occur
before `beginExecuting`, conflicting with the normative lifecycle and leaving a
separate implementation-conformance blocker.

The minimum formal-method proposal is TLA+/TLC first for transition order,
crash, restart, time, recovery, resource exclusion, and abstract no-bypass
obligations. A separate Zig lane must check CFG reachability, call order,
durability, restart reconstruction, and code-to-model correspondence. A Bolina
Alloy adapter is deferred until an owner approves a distinct structural
obligation that adds discriminating evidence.

## DomainSpec Leverage

The integration is `build-from-owned`, not a new proof framework. Bolina keeps
protocol authority. DomainSpec should reuse and extend the existing
deterministic Obligation IR and keys, TestCase/evaluation patterns, Alloy/TLA
runner and fixture tower, SFOL digest/stale/replay mechanics, code-tag-owned
implementation bindings, and implementation-conformance package.

The new work is bounded to Bolina semantic extraction and projections,
preservation and discrepancy bindings, tool-specific model cases, claim-limited
evaluation receipts, and the separate Zig correspondence layer. Use
`FormalSourceBinding` rather than colliding with the existing private
