# WaveDB For Craft Runtime Research Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the completed research dispatch.
It is discovery evidence only: it does not implement a Craft runtime, change
Craft authority, install or validate WaveDB, or authorize publication,
promotion, deployment, or production use.

## Decision

Advance only a candidate-local, digest-bound, disposable WaveDB projection
experiment. Keep `.craft/ledger.yml` as the sole project-local source of truth.
Hold a transactional sidecar until a separately owned YAML CAS, writer lease,
crash, and recovery contract exists. Kill WaveDB canonical-store replacement
for this route; reconsideration requires a separate Craft-owner authority route.

## Three-Posture Verdict

| Posture | Verdict | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Digest-bound projection/cache | BOUND as an unexecuted candidate experiment | Direct-select a fully verified digest-named isolated generation; YAML remains authority |
| Transactional sidecar with YAML authority | HOLD | No owned CAS, lease, true mid-apply witness, crash contract, or recovery proof |
| WaveDB canonical Craft store | KILL for this route | Changes storage, validation, migration, recovery, schema, and promotion authority |

## Bounded Prototype Shape

The admitted v0 has no active-generation pointer, no database-to-YAML recovery,
no derived-view ingestion, no schema migration, no multi-writer interface, and
no database-resident receipt or receipt reference. Every stored non-manifest
key/value must be enumerated and digested; queries emit zero rows until complete
re-verification against freshly read canonical YAML succeeds.

The finite Work Pack freezes the parser/serializer and complete key/value
manifest, observes exact normal reads and iterator visibility, and adds
non-vacuous wrong-workspace, stale-digest, batch-preflight, derived-view,
crash-phase, corruption, concurrency-observation, and YAML-mutation controls.
These are plans and hypotheses, not executed results.

## Evidence And Closeout

Ten named actions completed: four explorers, one synthesizer, three skeptical
reviewers, one writer, and one auditor. One bounded auditor-to-writer correction
removed a receipt-reference/manifest contradiction. The final audit and writer
binding passed, and the native event stream validated with 70 events and zero
errors.

The governed ledger contains the dispatch row and its paired resolved close row.
Inventory remains a read model and does not become Craft, WaveDB, or dispatch
authority.

## Open Residue

No WaveDB package was installed, built, executed, crash-tested, benchmarked, or
approved for distribution. True mid-apply atomicity, cross-process writer
admission, crash and power durability, schema migration, encryption lifecycle,
native package/source correspondence, platform support, root and dependency
license closure, publication, and production fitness remain open.

## Authority Boundary

Canonical Craft contracts remain under `arcanum/arcana/craft/`.
`arcanum/development/craft/` remains historical evidence only. WaveDB owns its
source claims. The research folder owns the candidate architecture and receipts.
Future implementation, destructive testing, publication, promotion,
deployment, or authority changes require separately admitted owner routes.
