# Ontology Authority and Agent Context Presentation Research Result

This is an accepted non-authority Inventory read model of resolved research
dispatch `2026-08-03-ontology-authority-agent-context-presentation` and its
Whisper-composed presenter candidate. Acceptance applies to the exact-bound
research brief and composition checks, not to a canonical definition change,
ontology admission, agent-memory implementation, authority movement,
publication, release, deployment, or production readiness.

## Accepted Result

The result supports a nominal `14:20` live presentation under a `15:00` hard
limit. It begins in the author's voice with ontology as a bounded knowledge
space or world model, then explicitly distinguishes that framing from the
normative private CAV2-D19 definition. It treats ontology as governed typed
meaning over selected evidence, with relations, time, confidence, provenance,
lifecycle posture, contradiction/drift handling, and use or stop rules.

The research audit returned `RECOMMEND_ACCEPT` after one bounded exact-selector
repair. Whisper's editorial and source gates pass for the presenter-ready
candidate; the candidate live-presentation preset itself remains unpromoted.

## Room Definition and Retell Chain

The stable audience chain is:

`Find candidate evidence. Type meaning. Bound context. Gate use.`

The expanded mechanism names its actors: Inventory or retrieval makes candidate
evidence and selectors findable; explicit selection chooses evidence; an
ontology owner and review route govern the typed meaning contract; a context
builder packages a task-scoped view with warnings; a named owner permits or
denies reliance; and a separate runtime acts, asks, or stops and produces its
own execution evidence.

This avoids making memory, ontology, context, Inventory, or authority sound like
runtime actors. It also states positively that an ontology may include a
glossary or taxonomy and may be represented in a database or graph, while none
of those structures alone supplies the governed meaning contract.

## Agent Context and Authority Boundary

Memory may retain candidate material. A context builder selects the task packet.
The ontology supplies the typed meaning and use contract. A named authority
owner decides permitted reliance. A separate runtime performs any authorized
effect and must provide its own evidence.

The complete authority ladder remains:

`source selector -> Inventory evidence card or EvidenceSet -> ontology candidate -> owner-gated PromotionRecord -> governed ontology node or artifact -> projection or generated read model`

Each transition is an owned review or handoff, not automatic ingestion. A
projection cannot stand in for promoted ontology authority.
