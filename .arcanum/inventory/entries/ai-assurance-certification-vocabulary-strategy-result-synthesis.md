## Strategy Summary

**P1 trigger decision:** dispatch was justified by synthesis, context
protection, isolation, and parallelism. The question crossed repo-local
vocabulary, external standards vocabulary, formal verifier machinery,
operational governance POCs, and investor BP positioning. The core tension was
certification optimism versus assurance-readiness skepticism.

**Lanes / groups:**

- `explorers` ran four parallel, robot-talks-enabled sweeps over standards,
  formal/verifier evidence, operational governance/control evidence, and
  semantic term mining.
- `synthesizer` reconciled explorer returns into a hidden-vocabulary map,
  standards-alignment boundary, evidence ladder, compounding POC thesis, safe
  investor language, forbidden claims, and missing witness queue.
- `reviewers` ran a three-agent robot-talks skeptic group over precedent,
  non-vacuity, and definitional-soundness gates.
- `writer` produced `findings.md`.
- `auditor` accepted `findings.md` with no revision required.

## Accepted Result

The accepted result is a certification/assurance vocabulary addendum for the
investor-BP research base. The strongest safe public vocabulary is **governed
AI work readiness package**. The scoped deliverable phrase is
**receipt-backed assurance case**, but only as a bounded package shape until one
end-to-end specimen exists. The internal architecture terms are `claim <= proof
governance`, `receipt-backed governance proof package`, `receipt bundle`,
`attestation-backed evidence receipts`, `certification-readiness research
substrate`, and `at-scale assurance` as future-only.

the POCs compound because each can leave controlled residue - receipts, gates,
owner decisions, trace links, telemetry rows, readiness verdicts,
formal-methods residue, or verifier outputs - that can later become material
for scoped assurance cases. The findings also foreground the missing witness:
no complete end-to-end receipt-backed assurance case specimen exists yet.
