# Whisper Writing-Framework Integration Research Strategy Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of a confirmation-ready but
unconfirmed research proposal. It records the route and its proof boundary; it
does not authorize registration, target-agent spawn, framework adoption,
Whisper mutation, publication, deployment, or a claim about reader outcomes.

## Preliminary Finding

The four-page Writer Science source is not one indivisible essay template. Its
observed material spans argument movement, introduction problem framing,
recursively nested Index-Discussion units, key-term continuity, old-to-new
information flow, and title or hook guidance. Those responsibilities occur at
different levels. The proposal therefore tests a level-by-responsibility
mapping instead of presuming either one new universal preset or new base-schema
fields.

The current candidate owner surfaces are Whisper's trajectory, technique,
composition, transport or preset, readability, human-review, and conservative
lint seams. That is a hypothesis for research, not an adopted architecture.

## P1 Trigger Decision

A governed research dispatch is warranted by synthesis, isolation, context
protection, and parallelism. Exact PDF reconstruction, external-framework
precedent, and current Whisper owner mapping require distinct corpora and
methods; their returns must be reconciled and independently attacked before a
findings artifact can be admitted.

## Lanes Groups And Subagents

- Aristotle reconstructs the exact PDF with page-bound claims, examples,
  inferences, levels, preconditions, and limitations.
- Gadamer compares a bounded set of established writing frameworks through
  primary or authoritative sources and records attribution or licensing duties.
- Alexander audits current Whisper owners and assigns the smallest placement
  class to each candidate responsibility.
- Simon synthesizes the three returns into a non-collapsed taxonomy, overlap
  matrix, placement decisions, minimal hypothesis, and experiment plan.
- Hacking reviews precedent, ownership, evidence, and attribution.
- Popper reviews non-vacuity, falsifiability, and witnesses.
- Wittgenstein reviews definitions, levels, and owner boundaries.
- Ahrens writes findings only after parent-owned exact-binding evidence closure.
- Brandenburg performs the terminal scope, binding, fixture, safety, and claim
  audit.

The explorer anti-bias axis is source-corpus plus methodology; the reviewer
anti-bias axis is attack vector.

## Dependency Flow

Three isolated explorers run in parallel, then synthesis, then three parallel
reviewers with one bounded zig-zag, then parent-owned evidence closure,
findings, audit, and parent final approval. One source-repair return to an
explorer and one bounded writer repair are available. The global loop cap is
two.

## Inventory Preflight

extend the existing readability owner, retell checks should remain
transport-conditional, semantic quality remains human-reviewed, and recent
Whisper research benefits from isolated candidates, neutral synthesis,
independent semantic and non-vacuity review, and exact evidence closure.

Domain-specific output examples and ontology or runtime entries were excluded
because they do not decide generic framework placement. No indexed item covers

These findings produced three source-isolated explorer lanes and a requirement
that every surviving component receive both one placement decision and one
concrete positive or negative witness.
