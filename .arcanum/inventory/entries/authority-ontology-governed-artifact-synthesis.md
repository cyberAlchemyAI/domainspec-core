# Authority, Ontology, and Governed Artifacts — Synthesis

Candidate Inventory synthesis (conceptual, not a dispatch). Answers: *how do
authority and ontology relate, what is the role of governed artifacts, and is CAV2
a meta-authority?* Grounded in cav2's authority model + definitions + the B-op node
schema. Evidence backing:
[`2026-06-24-authority-ontology-governed-artifact-cards.md`](evidence-cards/2026-06-24-authority-ontology-governed-artifact-cards.md).

> One line: **Authority = who makes it count (decision). Ontology = the map of what
> is and how it relates (description) — it can show authority but cannot perform it.
> A governed artifact = a made thing carrying a kind + provenance + owner route; the
> vehicle where the two meet, which only moves when an owner clears it.**

## The three layers

| | What it is | cav2 anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Authority** | who/what is allowed to make something COUNT, for which purpose — a permission by owner route, not by proximity/usefulness/repetition. The DECISION layer. | `CAV2-D34`; AUTHORITY-MODEL §Authority Rule |
| **Ontology** | the typed node/edge MAP of what is + how it relates. Can model authority (draw `governed_by`, status, level) but **cannot decide it**. The DESCRIPTION layer; the `Projection` kind. | AUTHORITY-MODEL §Non-Collapse rule 2; §Authority Kinds (Projection) |
| **Governed artifact** | NOT a primitive (typed negative) — just `artifact` + the authority model wrapped around it. "Governed" = routed through an owner gate. The VEHICLE / joint where ontology and authority meet. | `2026-06-23` governed-artifact finding; `CAV2-D34/D35/D36/D49` |

## CAV2 as reflexive meta-authority (mold → casting)

- **System/meta authority = CAV2 + the Arcanum method** — authority *about* authority:
  the kinds (`D49`), the promotion lifecycle (`D48`), owner routes, gates (`D37`).
  `D41`: "the traffic map for what is allowed to count, who can make it count, and
  what it must not be confused with."
- **Domain authority = the casting** — authority that decides *within* a domain
  (a client's governed ontology; ontologize's BI governance; our ergonomics model +
  admission gate), manufactured with the CAV2 method.
- **CAV2 is the MOLD; each domain authority is a CASTING.** Reflexive: CAV2 governs
  itself too (the operational-ontology B-op branch = CAV2 modelling its own
  operations). This IS the ontologize product thesis — it sells castings.
- **Honest caveat:** meta-authority in METHOD/POSTURE, not yet ENFORCEMENT (review-mode;
  OQ-1 + OQ-2 open). The mold works; it is hand-operated, not yet a machine.

## B-op encoding + the map/decision split

- In the B-op schema: governed artifact = a **typed node**; ontology = the **whole
  graph** (which "assigns no promotion authority", schema line 8 — Non-Collapse baked
  in); authority = the **status climb** (candidate→premise→promoted, L0-L7) fired by
  the owner gate (ontology-harness + Constitution Governance).
- Mapped onto what we built: **MAP** = the 7 coordinate axes + placement table +
  backlog descriptions + dialectic findings; **DECISION** = the admission gate +
  owner routes + default-deny + restraint floor + status transitions; **VEHICLE** =
  the affordance-lifecycle spell. The harness is **mostly decision + a thin
  taxonomy-map, no typed-graph ontology** → "governance-yes / ontology-no".
