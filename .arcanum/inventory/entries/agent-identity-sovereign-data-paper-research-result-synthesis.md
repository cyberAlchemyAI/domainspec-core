# Agent Identity and Sovereign Data Paper Research Result

This is an accepted non-authority Inventory read model of resolved research
dispatch `2026-08-02-agent-identity-sovereign-data-paper`. Acceptance applies to
the bounded research package and its audit, not to the paper's claims, the
candidate interfaces, an implementation, legal compliance, ontology admission,
authority promotion, publication, deployment, or production readiness.

## Accepted Result

The source DOCX is a conversational concept draft rather than a peer-reviewed
paper or validated framework. Its useful core is a human-autonomy concern:
adaptive AI can add profiling, feedback, personalization, optimization, and
scale to influence, so refusal, withdrawal, correction, revocation, appeal,
non-retaliation, attribution, and accountable review must remain meaningful.

The research rejects equivalence between AI and Romantic literature, belief
installation through dopamine or neuroplasticity, reliable biometric access to
inner emotional truth, reliable burnout prediction or elimination, and claims
that DIDs, credentials, IPFS, blockchain, or ERC-7007 establish truth,
authorization, ownership, persistence, payment, or CAV2 authority.

## Surviving Composition

The smallest candidate composes separately owned layers:

`DataCustodyStore/DataResourceRef -> AgentIdentityEnvelope -> DataUseGrant -> PurposeBoundComputationRequest -> AuthorizationDecision -> mediated computation -> ComputationReceipt -> DerivedClaimSubmission -> private CAV2 Evidence -> ontology candidate -> ChallengeContract -> owner-routed Decision -> authorized projection`

Every arrow is conditional and separately owned. Identity supplies contextual
provenance and authentication, not authorization. Sovereign data remains an
umbrella policy objective rather than a type or ownership proof. Raw biometric,
emotional, conversational, memory, health, and surveillance data stays outside
CAV2; only bounded claims, decisions, provenance references, receipts, and
omissions may reach the private evidence route.

## Killed and Split Candidates

The accepted research kills broad novelty, coequal identity envelope and
attestation types, a delegation-grant alias, a coequal data-use receipt, the
derived-claim envelope carrying CAV2 lifecycle fields, the ontology
candidate/view slash type, and new ChallengeContract or OwnerDecision types.
It retains one identity envelope with revocable attestation members, one direct
grant with a strict attenuated delegation subtype, one authorization decision,
one computation receipt, an external derived-claim submission, and existing
CAV2 challenge and Decision owners.

The combined purpose/audience witness and combined source/deletion/unlearning
witness were also killed and split into independent negative controls.

## Evidence and Execution Boundary

The nine-agent dispatch completed with one reviewer-to-synthesizer feedback
loop. The final auditor returned `ACCEPT` for `findings.md`, whose SHA-256 is
`3c3c07137ed2632e24fb16d9609581da5e0664204f10b709c158d8677d71c388`.
All nine normalized action receipts validate and the native coordinator closed
all five waves.

The non-vacuity execution gate remains failed: zero positive, negative,
leakage, deletion, revocation, or promotion witnesses ran. A generic Lean
default-deny evaluator and soundness theorem exist in the repository, but they
were not rebuilt or mapped to the proposed types and provide no integration or
conformance evidence for this task.

## Next Owner Route

Any implementation effort must first select and pin the external identity and
authorization profiles, author owner-approved candidate schemas, map them to
the generic permission kernel, build a mediated synthetic runtime and
independent leakage observer, and execute the complete witness matrix with
retained receipts. A separate admitted owner Decision is required before any
canonical definition, ontology relation, authority effect, or publication
route.
