# Creator Pack Marketplace Legal And Payment Decision Gate

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of the resolved research dispatch
`2026-07-16-creator-pack-legal-payment-gate`. It preserves the auditor-accepted
result but provides no legal advice, legal clearance, PSP acceptance, tax
advice, route approval, or authorization to accept money.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Dispatch was required because the proposed paid random-card product crossed
Brazilian consumer, gaming/promotion, payment, tax, title, custody, virtual
asset, refund, and chargeback boundaries. These questions required isolated
legal, transaction-structure, and payment-operations evidence lanes followed by
adversarial review.

## Lanes And Subagents

- Three explorers examined Brazilian classification and authority, exact
  transaction and title fact patterns, and PSP/reversal operations.
- One synthesizer reconciled the reports into candidate and authority matrices.
- Three skeptics attacked legal/classification, payments/consumer-remedy, and
  tax/custody/operational claims.
- One writer produced `findings.md` from the accepted synthesis.
- One independent auditor returned `ACCEPT`.

The dependency flow was
`explorers -> synthesizer <-> reviewers -> writer -> auditor`; one bounded
synthesis/reviewer loop was used. No helper roles were used.

## Accepted Result

No researched route is cleared to take money. The current candidate matrix is:

- **A — platform seller:** `HOLD`; the strongest paid candidate, but not
  approved.
- **B — creator seller through a PSP-connected marketplace:** `HOLD`; weaker
  because the creator-seller label must survive the actual ownership, money,
  invoice, remedy, and loss flow or it collapses to A.
- **C — transferable NFT/external claim transfer:** `KILL v1`; the original
  payer can reverse after transfer while another holder retains the redemption
  claim.
- **D — genuinely free allocation before an optional identified-card sale:**
  `HOLD`; the free-offer safe-harbor theory is unowned and fails if the customer
  gives consideration before allocation.
- **E — independently valuable base product plus gratuitous authorized prize:**
  `HOLD`; it exists only inside the exact SPA-authorized plan.
- **F — stored value, cash-out, cash redemption, buyback, or guaranteed RTP:**
  `KILL v1`.

The corrected product invariant is:

> Customers receive no reusable monetary balance and cannot voluntarily
> convert a platform claim into money through platform cash-out, cash
> redemption, resale, or buyback. The exact-card claim is non-transferable.
> This does not restrict mandatory refunds, withdrawal remedies, chargebacks,
> reversals, taxes, creator/vendor payments, or operating expenses. No
> expected-value disclosure is called or guaranteed as RTP.

This replaces the inaccurate phrase “money never leaves the system.” Money
must still leave through legally required remedies and ordinary operations.
No-cash-out, professional custody, everyone receiving a card, and blockchain
transparency are not legal or PSP safe harbors.

## Pre-Money Boundary

No real-money pilot may begin until the chosen exact fact pattern is classified
in writing; seller, merchant, invoice, title, remedy, and loss roles agree; a
licensed PSP/acquirer approves the real UI and flow; tax treatment is fixed;
consumer remedies are designed; exact-card custody and non-transferable claims
are evidenced; cash reserves and physical insurance are separately funded; the
refund-versus-redemption interlock passes; and age, marketing, privacy, KYB/KYC,
and territory controls have named professional approval.
