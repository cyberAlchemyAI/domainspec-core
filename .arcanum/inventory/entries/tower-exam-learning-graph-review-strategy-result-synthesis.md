## Review Result and Evidence Boundary

The seed is repairable and may continue through Refine design/plan, but the current towers do not support doctrinal answer scoring for the possession/ownership/acquisition/protection pilot. The accepted design direction is:

1. five tower-derived node families: `SourceSnapshot`, `TowerSnapshot`, typed `KnowledgeRecord`, `EvidenceAnchor`, and `StudyRoute`;
2. four typed relation families: scope/containment, evidence binding, semantic link, and navigation;
3. separate logical IDs, immutable snapshot IDs, and content digests, with sidecar IDs for heading-derived records;
4. two-layer tower/source provenance with machine-visible evidence taxonomy and closed page anchors for answer support;
5. a learner-session overlay that cannot mutate source knowledge or convert a learner miss into source residue;
6. deterministic abstention and an exact next-question record rather than generic browsing;
7. trace-completion evidence only, never mastery, readiness, comprehension, doctrinal correctness, or exam-effectiveness claims.

The seven-question toy game is usable as a design and later interaction witness. Doctrinal evaluation remains blocked until a separately authorized Research Tower extension binds Cases 6-7 and pilot concepts to verified page-local claims, definitions, and examples. Browser and accessibility controls remain `NOT_RUN`.
