# Learning Graph And Next-Route Guide Research Result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of an executed, accepted research
dispatch. The result is an exact-bound, candidate-local learner projection and
introductory guide. It does not establish a canonical learning-graph ontology,
validate human comprehension or accessibility, prove a real TypeScript adapter,
admit an owner, execute a route, change an ontology or HTML view, or authorize
promotion, release, deployment, or production.

## Result

The final audit accepted a projection over separately owned semantic ontology,
typed conformance evidence, session state, workflow-admission state, and
display-only route suggestions. The learner loop is:

`orient -> focus -> inspect -> identify evidence gap -> revise question -> suggest non-executing owner route`

The proposal KILLs a universal `LearningGraph` ontology, canonical
`QuestionNode` and `UnknownNode`, `understands`, and generic `evidenced_by`,
`routes_to`, `explains`, and `blocks` relations. The surviving candidate records
and relations carry explicit owners, truth conditions, evidence bindings, and
invalidation rules.
