## Accepted review result

Three MAJOR findings survived:

1. The green Key-and-Door walkthrough combines an evidenced `UnlockDoor` formation
   chain, a separate evidenced `HasKeyRule --enforces--> UnlockDoor` edge, and
   unevidenced `DoorUnlocked`/`produces` plus behavioral causality. The fix is to use
   the complete `UnlockDoor` formation chain as the primary live example, keep the edge
   as a separate relationship extension, and call the output path only “not evidenced
   in this x-ray” until literal records exist.
2. The mobile risk matrix hides semantic column headers while retaining auto-placed
   cells, changing the meaning of the matrix. The preferred repair preserves the full
   labelled matrix in a horizontal scroll region.
3. The workday uses present-tense semantic-preservation language for an unimplemented
   BTE path. The accepted replacement is future-tense, structurally bounded, and makes
   relation checking conditional on owner admission plus a frozen causal evaluator.

The review also keeps seven MINOR change-request groups for the plain-language retell
layer, core-receipt terminology, proposal-state copy, process-instance title,
responsive mechanics, heading/label legibility, and the ordinary-language status
legend.

## Copy and example boundary

The review did not sustain “blue fails contrast”: measured foreground contrast on the
dark panels was strong and status was not color-only. It did sustain that load-bearing
labels are mechanically small, mobile structures lose meaning, and the palette carries
too many visual roles for the reader hierarchy. Palette simplification therefore
remains an implementation choice to validate, not a review-proven wholesale replacement.

The hero-order change was downgraded from MAJOR to a usability experiment because no
browser screenshot or timed cold-read evidence exists. Scenario-first hero copy is a
supported change; moving the three on-ramp cards remains something to test.
