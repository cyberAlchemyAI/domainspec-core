## Accepted result

No current repo surface requires an infinity-category architecture or a
general cochain complex. No architecture, authority, canonical vocabulary,
schema, public Arcanum runtime, or implementation change is authorized.

Exactly two current mathematical uses remain justified:

1. `FunctorialResidueStructure.lean` is an ordinary-category, functor,
   Kan-extension, and colimit witness. It shows that morphism-sensitive
   structure can distinguish equal-count cases; it is not Čech or sheaf
   cohomology.
2. `CoupledSkewTower*.lean` is a finite dynamical nonabelian cocycle and
   transfer-equivalence witness. “Non-cohomologous” is allowed only in that
   explicitly defined nonabelian cocycle sense; it is not a cohomology group or
   general cochain complex.

`Para_M(C)` has positive bicategorical/actegorical precedent, but its local
completion and promotion remain killed until a composed-map collision proves
that horizontal composition or interchange changes a checked output.

Čech on real views, Dwyer–Kan localization, and homotopy aggregation remain
witness attempts only. Contextuality and information cohomology remain
prerequisite-gated research. Pipeline, ledger, oracle, residue vector,
Inventory, Ontology, Arcanum, and the evidence loop remain sequences, graphs,
relations, vectors, state machines, or ledgers rather than cochain complexes or
infinity categories.

No novelty is claimed. Mathematical precedent is positive ownership evidence
and never a KILL reason; local applicability is killed only by absent witness,
missing type, failed soundness condition, or collapse to a simpler incumbent.
