# Inventory Runtime Improvement Research Strategy

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of a proposed and unregistered
research strategy. It records the checked runtime-design question, not approval,
execution, implementation, package promotion, or Inventory authority.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Dispatch is justified by synthesis, isolation, and parallelism. The proposed
runtime must reconcile public and installed ownership, executable
warning-and-receipt semantics, and standalone operator portability, then
survive independent precedent, vacuity, and definitional attacks.

## Runtime Hypothesis

The research tests one minimal runtime hypothesis: every Inventory operation
resolves a package-local validator and produces a deterministic non-authority
receipt carrying the package root, validator binding, baseline and candidate
index digests, introduced warnings, inherited-warning count, mutation status,
and observability outcome.

The hypothesis does not authorize historical-tag migration, hide warnings,
weaken projection conformance, or make Inventory an authority.

## Lanes And Subagents

- `runtime-explorers` runs three agents in parallel:
  - Ashby, W. Ross owns formal contract and public/installed lifecycle analysis.
  - Spivak, David owns computational updater, validator, digest, warning-delta,
    idempotence, and receipt witnesses.
  - Wiener, Norbert owns adversarial operator portability across install,
    validate, lookup, append, observe, and failure paths.
- `runtime-synthesizer`: Jacobs, Bart reconciles the three returns into
  candidate runtime contracts, ownership, migration, and test matrices.
- `runtime-skeptics` runs three independent attack vectors in parallel:
  - Rittel, Horst checks actual precedent and owner reuse.
  - Popper, Karl requires closed non-vacuity witnesses.
  - Wittgenstein, Ludwig kills renamed updater, validator, observer, or
    maintenance wrappers without unique responsibility.
- `runtime-writer`: Turing, Alan writes `findings.md` only.
- `runtime-auditor`: Brandenburg, Martin audits the findings as an evidence
  reviewer; the live form owner requires `parent` to remain final approver.

Both fan-out groups carry pair-specific predicted-disagreement sentences in the
admitted sheet.

## Dependency Flow

`runtime-explorers -> runtime-synthesizer <-> runtime-skeptics ->
runtime-writer -> runtime-auditor`, with one bounded synthesizer/skeptic
exchange and one bounded auditor-to-writer feedback revision.
