conformance and adversarial domain/lifecycle sufficiency are independent concerns
that should disagree before the RWO owner accepts a canonical identity contract.

runtime owners to define kernel identity. It challenges the sufficiency of JCS plus
safe integers/NFC, the precision of versions/order/digest/fixture rules, and the
claimed owner separation.
