# Business Plan Build Research Findings

Status: BP-building research substrate, not final business-plan prose. The
controlling rule is `claim <= proof`: every commercial, investor, readiness,
verification, and defensibility claim must stay below the current witness set
and must carry a demotion gate. [Research] [BlindSpot] [Assurance]

## 1. Verdict / what this run establishes

This run establishes how to build the business plan, not the plan itself: write
a full BP as an operating and investor-confidence document; keep the deck as the
meeting-getter; use the memo as the investor decision case; and treat the data
room as a diligence evidence registry, not proof. [Research] [SBA] [Sequoia]
[YC] [Assurance]

The recommended BP spine is narrow: one blocked buyer decision, one paid Metric
Trust / governed AI Work Readiness wedge, one receipt bundle, and one witness
ladder. The broader DomainSpec/CAV2 portfolio belongs behind that spine as
substrate and diligence evidence, not as a front-stage product menu. [Research]
[Portfolio] [BlindSpot]

The front-stage problem should be: some BI, ops, and AI-workflow teams have one
metric or workflow they cannot confidently review, explain, assign to an owner,
or act on without a source chain and readiness gate. That is stronger and safer
than claiming a broad AI governance platform or a system that already builds
systems. [Research] [Portfolio] [BlindSpot]

The sellable offer now is under validation: fixed-scope Metric Trust / AI Work
Readiness engagements with a paid pilot/SOW, named hours, acceptance criteria,
and a stop condition. The productized platform remains a thesis tested through
repeated receipt-backed engagements. [Research] [Portfolio] [BlindSpot] [Moat]

Financials are a skeleton until pricing, delivery hours, direct costs, cash
timing, conversion, repeat work, and runway are validated with buyer evidence.
Use a bottom-up sprint-unit model, not a broad forecast story. [Research]
[HubSpot] [SCORE-Financial]

## 3. Build sequence

1. Build the evidence registry first: source roster, maturity labels, current
   receipts, missing witnesses, non-claims, and accepted prior findings. This
   prevents the BP from turning artifact breadth into traction or platform
   proof. [Research] [Portfolio] [BlindSpot]
2. Write the BP section map second: each section gets a source evidence row, a
   missing witness row, and a falsifier/demotion gate before prose is drafted.
   [Strategy] [Research] [BlindSpot]
3. Build the bottom-up financial model third: sprint price, hours, direct costs,
   founder capacity, cash timing, conversion, retainer attach, runway, and
   sensitivity scenarios. Treat the model as assumptions until validated.
   [Research] [HubSpot] [SCORE-Financial]
4. Draft the operating plan fourth: bind one buyer pain, one metric/workflow,
   one owner, one receipt bundle, one verdict, and one feedback loop into the
   next engagement. [Research] [Portfolio]
5. Draft the investor memo and deck last: the deck compresses; the memo argues;
   the data room substantiates. Do not write front-stage claims that the
   registry cannot support. [Research] [Sequoia] [YC] [Assurance]

## 5. Metric Trust / AI Work Readiness wedge model

| Element | Draft substrate |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Some BI, ops, or AI-workflow owners with one metric/workflow they cannot confidently review, explain, assign, or act on. [Research] [Portfolio] |
| Blocked decision | "Can we use this metric/workflow output for a named business action?" The BP must make the blocked action explicit before claiming urgency. [Research] [BlindSpot] |
| Offer | Fixed-scope Metric Trust / AI Work Readiness engagement: source chain, denominator/confound notes, owner route, readiness scorecard, gate verdict, receipt bundle, and next-action recommendation. [Research] [Portfolio] [Assurance] |
| Validation posture | Under validation; requires 5-10 buyer calls, one paid pilot/SOW, acceptance criteria, hours, and stop condition. [Research] [BlindSpot] |
| Operating loop | Bind named workflow/metric and owner; collect source-linked evidence; record owner decision; issue pass/flag/block/veto verdict; preserve receipt; feed measured residue into next engagement. [Research] |
| Receipt controls | Each receipt needs scope, failure mode, negative control where applicable, integrity specimen where applicable, and explicit non-claims. Receipts make work inspectable; they do not prove truth. [Research] [Assurance] [BlindSpot] |
| Expansion | Adjacent wedges can remain diligence/expansion paths: confidential workflow readiness, guided governance onboarding, and scorecard-style lead/creative evidence. They should not compete with the front-stage offer until the lead wedge is tested. [Research] [Portfolio] |
| Demotion gate | If buyers cannot name an accountable owner, urgent decision, budget path, or paid pilot scope, demote the wedge from lead offer to research hypothesis. [Research] [BlindSpot] |

## 6. Financial model checklist and assumptions

The financial model should start with sprint units, not platform ARR. The base
unit is a fixed-scope paid engagement whose assumptions are visible and
falsifiable. [Research] [HubSpot] [SCORE-Financial]

| Model area | Required assumptions | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue units | Diagnostic, Metric Trust Sprint, advanced readiness package, retainer; platform/license future-only. | Hypothesis ranges only; not validated revenue. [Research] |
| Price | Diagnostic `$2.5k-$7.5k`; sprint `$15k-$35k`; advanced readiness `$25k-$75k`; retainer `$8k-$20k/mo`. | Use as test ranges, not forecast facts. [Research] |
| Delivery hours | Diagnostic `8-20`; sprint `60-100`; advanced `100-180`; retainer `15-40/mo`. | Needs measured pilot hours. [Research] |
| Direct costs | Contractor/analyst/tool costs, data-access friction, legal/security template work, founder delivery time. | Skeleton until paid work records real cost. [Research] [HubSpot] |
| Capacity | Model founder delivery capacity before parallelizing pilots. | Research warns early capacity is closer to one full sprint plus diagnostics/retainer work, not several parallel pilots. [Research] |
| Cash timing | Upfront, milestone, net-30, delayed collection; include runway impact. | Must be modeled before funding ask. [Research] [SCORE-Financial] |
| Conversion | Buyer calls -> diagnostic -> paid sprint -> second workflow/retainer. | Not validated; 30/60/90 gates must collect this. [Research] [BlindSpot] |
| Financial statements | Startup expenses, payroll, sales forecast, operating expenses, cash flow, income statement, balance sheet, break-even, ratios, COGS, depreciation/amortization. | Required for full BP/lender package. [SCORE-Financial] [SBA] |
| Sensitivity | Price, hours, direct costs, cash timing, data delay, retainer attach, close rate, founder bottleneck. | Required before investor confidence language. [Research] [HubSpot] |
| Forecast label | "Bottom-up assumption model" until validated. | Do not call it a validated forecast. [Research] [SCORE-Financial] |

## 7. Diligence evidence registry / data-room checklist

The data room should be a diligence evidence registry: organized for inspection,
review, and assumption testing. It is not proof, certification, audit
acceptance, or external assurance. [Research] [Assurance]

| Registry area | Contents | Demotion / failure rule |
|---|---|---|
| Source registry | Current research, accepted findings, source index references, dispatch state, maturity labels. | Inventory summaries cannot replace source witnesses. [Research] |
| Maturity table | Shipped, runnable, internal, proposal, research, demo, killed, excluded, buyer-tested, paid. | Any unlabeled artifact becomes diligence-only. [Portfolio] [BlindSpot] |
| Receipt bundle | Source chain, confounds, owner decision, readiness scorecard, gate verdict, privacy/reuse boundary. | Receipt without scope/failure mode is weak evidence. [Research] [Assurance] |
| Fresh runnable receipts | `vitest`, `lake build`, `audit_richness.py` batch, missing-obligation fixture, attestation mutation checks where applicable. | Stale or unrunnable claims stay as historical substrate. [Moat] [ModelUse] [BlindSpot] |
| Readiness specimen | Typed spec -> obligations/tests -> validator/richness result -> CAV2 claim classification -> owner decision -> Craft/goal receipt -> L1 seal -> mutation checks -> non-claims. | Without this, do not claim a complete receipt-backed readiness specimen. [Assurance] [Moat] [BlindSpot] |
| Commercial evidence | Buyer-call notes, paid pilot SOW, acceptance criteria, delivery receipt, price tests, rejection notes. | Without paid evidence, "traction," "market pull," and "platform revenue now" are forbidden. [Research] [BlindSpot] |
| Financial model | Bottom-up assumptions, scenarios, sensitivity, runway, use of funds, cash timing. | Without measured pilots, keep financials as skeleton. [Research] [SCORE-Financial] |
| Comparator archive | Dated refresh of spec-first, AI governance, traceability, formal-methods, and security/audit categories. | No unique/first/only claim without refreshed evidence. [Moat] [BlindSpot] |
| Standards notes | NIST-informed, ISO/IEC 42001-aware, EU AI Act-aware vocabulary notes. | No compliance, conformity, certification, or audit-ready language. [Assurance] [NIST] [ISO42001] [EUAIAct] |
| Public/private boundary | Scrubbed front-stage copy; private substrate held for diligence only. | Private mechanisms cannot leak into public Arcanum or public copy. [Portfolio] [BlindSpot] |

## 8. Investor-safe language and forbidden claims

### Use

- "We help teams turn one distrusted metric or AI-assisted workflow into source-linked evidence, owner-routed decisions, readiness gates, and reusable receipts." [Research] [Portfolio] [Assurance]
- "Metric Trust / governed AI Work Readiness is the first paid service hypothesis." [Research] [Portfolio] [BlindSpot]
- "The platform is a thesis under validation through repeated paid receipt-backed engagements." [Research] [Moat] [BlindSpot]
- "Readiness means bounded preparedness against named gates and scope." [Assurance]
- "Auditability means organized for inspection and review, not auditor acceptance." [Assurance]
- "Verifiable only where a named check, validator, receipt with failure mode, integrity specimen, or bounded formal witness exists; provable only where a named formal witness exists." [BlindSpot] [Assurance] [Moat]
- "Standards-informed" or "NIST-informed" only where the BP includes a scoped note or crosswalk. [Assurance] [NIST]

### Demote

| Phrase | Safer replacement |
|---|---|
| "trustworthy/auditable platform" | "source-linked, owner-routed, organized for review" [Assurance] [BlindSpot] |
| "platform" | "platform thesis under validation" unless paid repeatability exists [Research] [Moat] |
| "market" | "bottom-up buyer pocket" until buyer-call and paid-pilot evidence exists [Research] [BlindSpot] |
| "traction" | "buyer calls / paid pilots / LOIs / quotes / repeat pull" only when those exist [BlindSpot] |
| "moat" | "conditional residue hypothesis" until paid reuse and labor-reduction evidence exists [Moat] [BlindSpot] |
| "audit-ready" | "audit-readiness evidence organized for review" only with explicit non-claims [Assurance] |

### Forbid

Do not use: "data room is proof," "audit-ready," "trustworthy/auditable
platform," "proof of a system that builds systems," "provably correct agents,"
"formally verified platform," "receipts prove truth," "platform revenue now,"
"market pull," "traction," "proven moat," "unique," "first," "only," or "many
revenue routes are ready." These phrases exceed current evidence or collapse
terms that must stay separate. [Assurance] [Moat] [BlindSpot]

Do not claim certification, compliance, conformity assessment, auditor approval,
external assurance, ISO/IEC 42001 compliance, EU AI Act compliance, or
third-party audit readiness. External standards can pressure vocabulary and
checklists, but they do not confer status without the required formal route.
[Assurance] [NIST] [ISO42001] [EUAIAct]

## 11. Final bottom line

Build the BP from the wedge outward: one blocked decision, one buyer pocket, one
fixed-scope Metric Trust / AI Work Readiness offer, one receipt bundle, one
bottom-up sprint-unit model, and one witness queue. Treat DomainSpec/CAV2 as the
private substrate that may become leverage only if paid engagements repeatedly
reuse schemas, gates, receipts, owner routes, and bounded witnesses. [Research]
[Portfolio] [Moat] [BlindSpot]

The BP can be credible now as a research-backed operating and
investor-confidence plan. It cannot yet be a claim of platform proof,
certification, compliance, market pull, audit-ready status, provably correct
agents, or proven moat. [Assurance] [BlindSpot] [NIST] [ISO42001] [EUAIAct]
