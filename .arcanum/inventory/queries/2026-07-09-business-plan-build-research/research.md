### Unit Economics / Capacity

Model the sprint as the unit, not "customer" yet.

`Contribution per sprint = price - direct delivery costs - contractor/analyst/tool costs - allocated founder delivery cost`

Track cash gross margin and fully loaded contribution margin. Starting
assumptions to test:

- Diagnostic: `8-20` senior hours.
- Metric Trust Sprint: `60-100` senior hours.
- Advanced readiness package: `100-180` senior hours.
- Retainer: `15-40` hours/month.

If two founders can protect `100-140` true delivery hours/month after
sales/admin, honest starting capacity is about one full sprint plus
diagnostics/retainer work, not four parallel pilots.

### Scenario / Sensitivity Tests

Use SBA break-even structure:

`break-even units = fixed costs / (price - variable cost)`

Minimum model tests:

- Price sensitivity: `$15k / $25k / $35k` sprint.
- Hours sensitivity: `60 / 80 / 120` delivery hours.
- Direct cost sensitivity: `10% / 20% / 35%`.
- Cash timing: upfront, milestone, net-30, delayed collection.
- Data-delay sensitivity: no delay, 2-week delay, 6-week delay.
- Retainer attach rate: `0% / 25% / 50%`.
- Close rate from buyer calls to paid diagnostic, then diagnostic to sprint.
- Founder bottleneck and gross margin after first hire/contractor.

### Use Of Funds

Use funds for buyer calls, 1-3 paid pilots, legal/security/data-boundary
templates, receipt-bundle standardization, one contractor or delivery hire only
after founder bottleneck is measured, and minimal tooling that reduces sprint
hours. Do not use funds for broad platform build before paid repeatability,
certification/compliance/audit claims, multi-wedge GTM, or public verifier /
attestation product as current revenue.

### Investor-Claim Validation Gates

Before investor claims, validate: buyer pays for "one distrusted metric"; named
decision owner, budget path, data access, success threshold, stop condition;
actual delivery hours and delay sources; gross margin including founder labor;
repeatable receipt bundle across at least three paid pilots; retainer or
second-workflow pull; CAC/channel cost and sales cycle; client-permitted proof
artifacts; no certification, compliance, external assurance, truth-by-receipt,
or finished-platform language.

### Pilot Package, Conversion Gates, Buyer Validation, Expansion

Pilot package should be fixed-scope and receipt-backed: source chain/evidence
trail, denominator/confound notes, owner decision, privacy/reuse boundary,
readiness scorecard, optional observed-use receipt. Safe package language is
"governed AI work readiness package."

Buyer-validation plan: run 5-10 calls around "one number you do not trust," then
one paid pilot with stop criteria. Ask what decision is blocked, who owns it,
what happens if it is wrong, what current workaround costs, what artifact would
change behavior, and whether they will pay for a fixed sprint now.

Consulting-to-product path: start manual and founder-led. Fixed scope, fixed
customer, set price, documented limits, and systemization precede software
scalability. Expansion path: Metric Trust Sprint -> AI Work Readiness Diagnostic
-> Confidential AI Workflow Readiness -> Guided Governance Onboarding.

### Adoption Metrics And Proof/Falsifier Gates

Discovery metrics: calls completed, percent with named owner, percent with
urgent decision tied to the metric/workflow, current workaround spend,
willingness to pay, and "next 10 similar customers" density.

Pilot metrics: paid pilot signed, time to produce receipt bundle, buyer accepts
source chain/scorecard, owner makes a decision, privacy/reuse boundary is
explicit, and buyer asks for a second workflow. Local next witness: three paid
pilots with same receipt bundle, reused schema, lower delivery labor, and buyer
pull for a second workflow.

Falsifiers: no paid pilot; buyer cannot name an accountable owner; deliverable
becomes bespoke consulting each time; no repeatable receipt schema; no
second-workflow pull; delivery labor does not decline; buyer asks for
certification/compliance claims the company cannot support; private
CAV2/DomainSpec mechanisms would need to leak to sell the service.

### Future-Only Until Buyer Evidence

Keep future-only: finished platform, deployment-proven platform, broad
moat/compounding, public-ready CAV2/DomainSpec v2, implemented Evidence
Exchange, external-signature attestation, public verifier, complete audit
schema, certification controls mapping, runtime conformance proof, and at-scale
assurance.

### Control-Loop Map

The BP should not present "a system that builds systems" as proven. The
investable operating model is: one paid trust/readiness engagement -> bounded
receipt bundle -> owner decision -> readiness gate -> data-room proof object ->
feedback into product/platform thesis. Accepted substrate already says lead
with paid witnessed trust/readiness engagements, not a finished platform; each
engagement should produce evidence receipts; repeated receipts test a platform
thesis rather than prove it now.

Control spine:

1. Bind the engagement: one buyer pain, named workflow/metric, source authority, owner, privacy/reuse boundary.
2. Run the work as a governed frontier: Craft/goal reads state, classifies risk, routes to owner, joins receipts, audits, stages deltas, and promotes only after approval.
3. Produce the receipt bundle: source chain, denominator/confound notes, owner decision, privacy boundary, readiness scorecard, optional observed-use receipt.
4. Gate the result: pass/flag/block/veto, explicit non-claims, missing witness list.
5. Feed back: update maturity table, pricing model, delivery labor, reusable schema, product roadmap, and data-room proof queue.
