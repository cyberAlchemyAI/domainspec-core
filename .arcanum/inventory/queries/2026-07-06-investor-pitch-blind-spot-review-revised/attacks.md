## Initial Attack: Porter, Michael

### Lens

Breadth is not the pitch. Breadth is credible only if it supports a narrow paid
wedge. The investable story is to sell witnessed trust/readiness engagements
now, then let receipts test the larger platform thesis. The target overreaches
when it treats two-person, three-month reach as proof of buyer pull or a system
that builds systems rather than as impressive internal execution evidence.

### Blind Spots

| Severity | Blind spot | Evidence | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Breadth still reads as sprawl without one lead ICP. | The BP says to sell narrow paid witnessed trust/readiness engagements and names multiple wedges; Metric Trust has no buyer witness yet and needs five buyer calls plus one paid readiness pilot. | Make Metric Trust Sprint the first paid wedge: BI/ops/AI lead, one distrusted operating metric, 2-4 week package, fixed deliverables, explicit price test. |
| Critical | `system that builds systems` outruns proof. | Assurance research says no complete end-to-end receipt-backed assurance-case specimen exists yet; current evidence is strong components, not a fully witnessed assurance-case pipeline. | Demote to governed evidence-to-action method under validation until one specimen connects derivation, validation, CAV2 claim classification, owner decision, Craft/goal receipt, and L1 attestation. |
| High | Five wedges split investor attention. | Market entry lists Metric Trust, AI Work Readiness, Confidential Workflow, Guided Governance, and Lead Evidence. | Lead with one wedge; show the rest as expansion paths sharing the same receipt package. |
| High | Revenue-route optionality is not market proof. | Product/platform language waits for buyer interviews, paid pilots, LOIs, procurement pull, and repeatable specimens. | Replace "several revenue routes" with "one paid wedge now; adjacent wedges staged after buyer validation." |
| High | Compounding/moat is conditional. | Compounding residue thesis is internal until repeated pilots show reuse; "the moat is proven" is forbidden. | Say repeated receipts may compound into governance data and switching cost; require repeated paid deployments, observable reuse, reduced cycle time, and retention. |
| Medium | Buyer urgency is buried under internal vocabulary. | Safe external language is governed AI work readiness package; internal terms like proof package need explicit scope and non-claims. | Start from pain: which AI-assisted decision or metric can you not safely trust today? |
| Medium | Category differentiation is crowded. | Spec-prior tools, traceability systems, AI governance platforms, security/audit platforms, and formal-methods tools already own adjacent language. | Do not pitch spec-first, AI governance platform, first/only. Differentiate on source-linked obligation records, owner authority, claim boundaries, receipts, and scoped witnesses. |
| Medium | Private/public boundary can muddy diligence. | DomainSpec v2/CAV2 are private authority; public derivatives require scrubbing. | Put proprietary substrate in a diligence appendix; front-stage only buyer-safe language and bounded proof objects. |

### Surviving Story

The story survives if narrowed: two people produced an unusually broad,
disciplined evidence corpus in roughly three months, and that corpus shows a
repeatable governance discipline: source links, owner routing, receipts, formal
guardrails, claim boundaries, and staged proof. It does not prove buyer pull, a
finished platform, a certified assurance product, or a durable moat.

Best surviving line: DomainSpec/CAV2 sells governed AI work readiness packages
now; repeated paid engagements produce receipts that test the larger
evidence-to-action platform thesis.

### Proposed Pitch Repairs

- Lead slide: "We help teams trust one AI-assisted workflow or business metric
  by turning it into source-linked evidence, owner decisions, readiness gates,
  and receipts."
- Wedge slide: Metric Trust Sprint first. Buyer: BI/ops/AI lead. Trigger: one
  number no one trusts. Deliverable: source chain, denominator/confound notes,
  confidence grade, owner decision, current-to-target plan, receipt bundle.
- Proof slide: portfolio as substrate, not product menu.
- Demotion rule: "system that builds systems" becomes "governed method for
  producing bounded evidence packages across systems."

### Diligence Questions

Who is the first buyer persona with budget? What exact painful moment makes them
buy now? What is the first paid package, price, duration, and hard stop
criterion? How many buyer calls, LOIs, paid pilots, or procurement
conversations exist today? Which single demo proves the wedge in under 10
minutes? What receipt from one engagement can be reused in the next? What would
falsify the Metric Trust Sprint after five calls or one pilot? Which claims are
excluded from the pitch? What evidence shows breadth reduced delivery cost or
cycle time? What is the expansion path from Metric Trust to AI Work Readiness
without becoming custom consulting forever?

"Two dedicated people, roughly three months" is not yet proof of a governed
system that builds systems. It is an unusually strong productivity signal that
needs receipts, denominators, negative controls, and buyer conversion evidence.

### Blind Spots

| Severity | Blind spot | Evidence | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|

| Critical | Conversion evidence is missing. | Services-led readiness is a hypothesis pending buyer interviews, paid pilots, LOIs, procurement pull, and pricing validation. | Lead with one wedge, probably Metric Trust or AI Work Readiness, and present the rest as substrate/demo. |
| High | Survivorship and portfolio-count inflation. | Portfolio includes Tier 0 substrate, Tier 1 offers, demo surfaces, proposal-only primitives, research assets, and excluded projects. | Add portfolio accounting: shipped, runnable, internal, proposal, killed, excluded, buyer-tested, paid. Include failures and abandoned branches. |
| High | Proof-class mixing. | Trustworthy, auditable, and assurance can be heard as certification or external audit readiness, but assurance findings forbid those claims. | Use "governed AI work readiness package" and a visible non-claims box. |
| High | Hard receipt gap for the two-person/three-month claim. | Required sources do not prove exact dates, labor split, pre-existing substrate, AI assistance level, or net-new artifact status. | Build a diligence appendix: start/end dates, commit chronology, task ledger, artifact age, imported prior work, creator/time attribution, fresh run receipts. |
| Medium | Internal review may not be independent evidence. | Same-lineage model review is not independent evaluation. | Add external or held-out checks: outsider reviewer, fresh build/test runs, negative controls, and buyer-observed specimens. |

### Surviving Story

The story survives if framed as disciplined early evidence, not proof. A small
team rapidly produced a broad, status-labeled body of governed artifacts. The
current proof is not a finished platform; it is a receipt-backed diligence base
for testing whether repeated readiness engagements compound into a platform
thesis.

### Proposed Pitch Repairs

Replace "proof of a system that builds systems" with "early evidence of a
governed artifact-production method, now being tested through paid readiness
engagements." Put breadth after the wedge. Add three slides investors will
trust more than rhetoric: artifact maturity map, receipt chronology for the
three-month build, and next-witness queue.

### Investor Diligence Questions

What exactly was produced between the claimed start and end dates, and what
pre-existed? Which artifacts are runnable today, which are proposals, and which
are research notes? Show commit chronology, task ledger, and creator/time

deferred, or merged? What fresh receipts can be rerun now? Which single wedge
has buyer urgency? How many buyer calls, LOIs, pilots, or paid engagements
exist? Can a non-founder run the method and produce a comparable receipt? What
negative control proves the validator catches real gaps? What would falsify the
claim in 30-60 days? What evidence would show reuse?

`trustable`, `auditable`, `correctness`, and `system that builds systems` slide

compliance posture.

### Blind Spots

| Severity | Blind spot | Evidence | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|

| Critical | `proof` smuggles formal, legal, audit, market, and investor proof. | Assurance findings say no completed assurance-case specimen exists and proof package is internal/scoped. | Replace proof with the evidence class: receipt, bounded witness, source link, owner decision, build/test receipt, buyer proof. |
| High | `reach` treats breadth as compounding evidence. Breadth can also be drift. | Portfolio findings say repeated receipts test a platform thesis, not that a platform is sold; moat findings say compounding is not yet a proven moat. | Count residue types and maturity tiers, not just projects. |
| Critical | `trustable` and `auditable` imply external assurance. | Non-claims exclude certification, compliance, third-party audit readiness, formal correctness, and truth by receipts. | Use inspectable, source-linked, owner-routed, and organized for review. |
| Critical | `correctness` overreaches from bounded witnesses to platform correctness. | Lean/formalization are bounded witnesses, not whole-system verification; model-use findings reject broad formal correctness. | Say bounded correctness witnesses for named properties. |
| High | `several revenue routes` makes options sound validated. | Portfolio verdict keeps platform, ledger, and credit mechanics thesis/prototype status until witnessed runs exist. | Lead with one paid wedge; present the rest as staged options with falsifiers. |

### Surviving Story

The story survives if demoted from proof of a finished system to evidence of a
disciplined substrate. The repo shows a repeatable pattern: authority
boundaries, source links, owner decisions, gates, receipts, deterministic
derivation specimens, bounded formal witnesses, and prototype surfaces. That
supports an investor-safe claim that two people used a governed work system to
generate a broad portfolio faster than ordinary ad hoc consulting would suggest.

### Term Repairs

| Term | Repair |
|---|---|
| `system` | Repo-native governance substrate: DomainSpec v2, CAV2, Craft/goal, Lean discipline, and receipt practices. |
| `proof` | Evidence class tied to scope: receipt, bounded formal witness, deterministic run, owner decision, buyer pilot. |
| `reach` | Maturity-tiered portfolio coverage across POCs, research lanes, service wedges, and demo surfaces. |
| `trustable` | Inspectable and claim-bounded; not truth-guaranteeing. |
| `auditable` | Organized for review or audit preparation; not auditor-approved or third-party audit-ready. |
| `correctness` | Named-property correctness only, under explicit trust borders. |
| `compounding` | Repeated artifacts may leave reusable schemas, gates, receipts, and repair paths; proven only after repeated witnessed reuse. |
| `system builds systems` | The governance substrate helps produce reusable work products and product candidates under human owner routes. |

### Forbidden Phrasing

Forbid: proof of a system that builds systems. Use: evidence that a governance
substrate helped produce many bounded systems and proof surfaces. Forbid:
trustworthy and auditable platform. Use: source-linked, owner-routed,
inspectable readiness packages. Forbid: correctness layer, formally verified
platform, provably correct agents. Use: bounded formal witnesses and
claim-boundary discipline. Forbid: compounding moat is proven. Use: repeated
receipts may become a moat if paid pilots show reuse, reduced cycle time, and
buyer trust. Forbid: public-ready CAV2/DomainSpec or finished platform. Use:
private defensibility substrate; public derivative requires scoped, scrubbed
wording.
