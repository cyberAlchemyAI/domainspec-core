# Investor Pitch Blind-Spot Review Findings

Status: accepted review findings. Final auditor accepted source coverage,
citation precision, and claim discipline. Every surviving pitch claim must stay
at or below its cited proof class.

## 1. Executive Verdict

The pitch is conditionally strong, but only after demotion. The investable claim
is not that the repository already proves a finished "system that builds
systems." The safer claim is that DomainSpec/CAV2 shows a governed,
source-linked work discipline that produced maturity-labeled artifacts,
bounded witnesses, validation specimens, and receipt candidates, and can now be
tested through one paid readiness wedge, preferably Metric Trust Sprint.
Breadth should appear as substrate, not as the opening product claim. Evidence:
`attacks.md:52-56`, `attacks.md:73-81`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-investor-bp-project-portfolio-research/findings.md:22-48`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-ai-assurance-certification-vocabulary-research/findings.md:21-45`.

The core repair is: lead with one buyer pain, one paid wedge, one receipt
bundle, and one proof ladder. Keep platform, moat, assurance, correctness, and
compounding language conditional until buyer pull and bounded proof specimens
exist. Evidence: `attacks.md:284-291`, `attacks.md:348-357`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-spec-driven-completeness-moat-inventory-research/findings.md:9-12`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-novel-model-use-lean-formalization-research/findings.md:13-15`.

## 3. Compounding Story That Survives

DomainSpec/CAV2 can be pitched as a governed work system that helps produce
maturity-labeled evidence packages across different systems. The repo shows a
repeatable pattern: authority boundaries, source links, owner decisions, gates,
receipts, deterministic derivation specimens, bounded formal witnesses, receipt
candidates, and prototype surfaces. Evidence: `attacks.md:220-225`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-spec-driven-completeness-moat-inventory-research/findings.md:9`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-investor-bp-project-portfolio-research/findings.md:50-63`.

The commercial story should be: start with Metric Trust / governed AI work
readiness; each paid sprint emits a standard receipt bundle; repeated paid
receipts test whether the substrate compounds into a platform. Evidence:
`attacks.md:372-376`, `attacks.md:581-588`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-investor-bp-project-portfolio-research/findings.md:303-320`,
`.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-novel-model-use-lean-formalization-research/findings.md:96-106`.

## 7. Proposed Investor-Safe Wording

Primary one-liner:

> We help teams turn one distrusted AI-assisted workflow or business metric into
> source-linked evidence, owner-routed decisions, readiness gates, and reusable
> receipts. The broader DomainSpec/CAV2 portfolio is the governance substrate;
> paid sprints test whether that substrate compounds into a repeatable
> evidence-to-action platform.

Cite: `attacks.md:304-307`, `attacks.md:372-376`,

> platform, certification, compliance, third-party audit readiness, or broad
