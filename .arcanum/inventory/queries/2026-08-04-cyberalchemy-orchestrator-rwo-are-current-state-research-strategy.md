The proposed dispatch will establish the strongest current-state claims direct

## Dependency flow

The three explorers join into synthesis. Synthesis and the three parallel
reviewers have a bounded two-pass zig-zag, with one evidence-only feedback edge
to explorers. Exact-binding closure then gates writer, auditor, and parent
approval. Every downstream step blocks on missing, partial, mismatched, or
timed-out receipts.
