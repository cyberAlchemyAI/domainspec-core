# Findings: Research-Backed Governed Model-Use Discipline

[research] Source packet: `.arcanum/inventory/queries/2026-07-06-novel-model-use-lean-formalization-research/research.md`. Citations below abbreviate it as `research.md`.

## 1. Executive result

[research] The safe result is not broad "novel model use"; it is a **research-backed governed model-use discipline** for **candidate model-use lanes** in source-grounded staged software generation, typed residue evaluation, oracle-gated validation, and bounded readiness packaging. The central owned lane survives only in narrow form: later stages must re-access source grounding, schema and instance residue must remain separate, and stage fidelity must be preserved (`research.md:11`; `domainspec-lean-formalization/research/cyberalchemy-functor-stack-bet/findings.md:147`; `domainspec-lean-formalization/research/cyberalchemy-functor-stack-bet/findings.md:185`).

[implemented L0] The golden-chain package is an executable synthetic mechanics surface: schema, fixtures, finite residue metric, dry-run summary, and claim guard exist, but `claim_allowed` remains false and no real software-stack value is proven (`research.md:13`; `research.md:29`; `domainspec-lean-formalization/research/cyberalchemy-functor-stack-bet/experiment-package/results/dry-run-summary.md:27`; `domainspec-lean-formalization/research/cyberalchemy-functor-stack-bet/experiment-package/results/finite-residue-metrics.md:15`).

[formalized] Lean witnesses support proof boundaries, counterexamples, typed residue separation, and conditional loss-dynamics slack; they do not prove AI-model correctness, model-stack performance, formal completeness, Lean formalization generally, assurance, or certification (`research.md:15`; `research.md:35`; `domainspec-lean-formalization/lean-formalization/F11.lean:11`; `domainspec-lean-formalization/lean-formalization/LossDynamicsPlumbing.lean:27`).

[proposal] The BP-safe commercial wedge is **governed AI work readiness**: bounded engagements that turn model-assisted work into source-linked evidence, owner-routed decisions, readiness gates, bounded evidence receipts, and candidate assurance-case materials. This is a hypothesis pending buyer interviews, paid pilots, LOIs, procurement evidence, and repeatable specimens (`research.md:291`; `research.md:361`; `research.md:443`; `research.md:479`).

[external-reported] Ornith-1 is adjacent precedent for scaffold-aware, self-improving coding-model work, but it is repo-reported and does not validate DomainSpec's approach, local protocols, Lean witnesses, or commercial thesis (`research.md:23`; `research.md:391`; `research.md:393`).
