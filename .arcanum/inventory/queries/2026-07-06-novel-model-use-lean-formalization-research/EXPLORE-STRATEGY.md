# Explore Strategy - Novel Model-Use Research In Lean Formalization

## P1 Trigger Decision

This is a dispatch proposal.

All four Principle-1 triggers hold:

- **Synthesis:** the run must combine the functor-stack bet, Lean architecture,
  research-ai bridges, agent-training bridges, self-harness findings, blind
  recovery/oracle hardening, model-optimization findings, the Ornith-1
  self-improving coding-model case, Kodamai correctness comparison, and the
  prior investor BP / assurance / completeness substrates.
- **Context protection:** the Lean formalization tree and research sidecars are
  too large for the parent chat to carry without flattening live distinctions
  between AI model-use, mathematical model-use, formal proof, experiments, and
  BP narrative.
- **Isolation:** investor-facing "we research novel ways of using models"
  language must be attacked before it enters the business plan.
- **Parallelism:** the model-use evidence sweep, Lean/formal surface audit,
  AI/ML precedent check, commercial/BP framing, and claim-boundary review can
  run independently before synthesis.

The dispatch is **not yet registered or run**. The current gate state is
pending check-tension and explicit human confirmation.

## Goal

Create the research substrate for explaining, in the investor BP, that the
company is researching owned and novel ways of using models. The run must center
`domainspec-lean-formalization/research/cyberalchemy-functor-stack-bet/`, find
more artifacts like it across `domainspec-lean-formalization`, and classify the
full surface into:

- AI model-use experiments or prescriptions;
- formal mathematical modeling / Lean witness surfaces;
- model-evaluation and model-governance methods;
- cross-domain transfer or novelty claims that survived, were demoted, or were
  killed;
- BP-safe language for company reach and compounding research capacity.

The result must not be final BP copy. It should be the evidence map and claim
ladder that lets later BP writing say: we do not merely prompt models; we
research model stacks, source-grounding, dual-residue losses, held-out gates,
independent-evaluator limits, and formal typed residue as a disciplined way to
use, evaluate, and bound models.

### Required External Example Source

- `https://github.com/deepreinforce-ai/Ornith-1`

Use Ornith-1 as an external example/case source, not as proof of our own
claims. Its README describes Ornith-1.0 as an open-source self-improving model
for agentic coding and says the training framework uses RL to generate both
solution rollouts and the scaffold driving those rollouts. The run must check
and date the repository state, record the public claims it actually makes,
compare it to our functor-stack / source-grounding / self-harness / dual-residue
research, and decide whether it supports the BP point that the market is moving
toward scaffold-aware model improvement while our contribution is stronger
governance, typed residue, held-out/non-regression gates, and claim boundaries.
Do not cite Ornith-1 benchmark numbers as verified performance unless the run
also checks the underlying harnesses or treats them as repo-reported claims.

### Exclusions / Claim Guards

- Do not treat "novel to the repo" as "novel to the field."
- Do not call any model-use idea validated unless an experiment or theorem
  actually validates it.
- Do not call Lean artifacts proof of AI-agent correctness unless the proof
  object is directly about that claim.
- Do not claim production model stack, implemented dual-residue training,
  autonomous self-optimization, production correctness layer, shipping
  correctness-by-construction platform, or certification/compliance readiness.
- External precedent checks are required before any public uniqueness,
  "first", "only", or "no one else" language.

## Predicted Disagreement Design

### Model-Use Explorers

- Shannon vs Grothendieck, axis `source-corpus + methodology`: Shannon can
  over-read AI model-use promise from research prose; Grothendieck exposes that
  by separating Lean theorem, formal witness, prose bridge, and ML experiment.
  Shannon exposes Grothendieck's proof-purity bias by identifying commercially
  meaningful model-evaluation methods that are not theorem claims.
- Shannon vs Curie, axis `source-corpus + methodology`: Shannon centers known
  model-use lanes and external examples such as Ornith-1 and can miss hidden
  siblings; Curie exposes that by doing an inventory-style sweep over the full
  Lean formalization tree. Shannon exposes Curie's search-term bias by
  preserving semantic lanes that do not contain the obvious words.
- Grothendieck vs Curie, axis `source-corpus + temporal-prior`: Grothendieck can
  over-center current theorem surfaces; Curie exposes that by surfacing older,
  adjacent, or sidecar research artifacts. Grothendieck exposes Curie's
  artifact-counting bias by marking which hits actually bear a formal or
  definitional load.

### Positioning Explorers

- Porter vs Popper, axis `source-corpus + methodology + temporal-prior`: Porter
  can over-compress Ornith-1 and internal evidence into investor-friendly
  company reach; Popper exposes that by checking novelty, priority, and
  precedent. Porter exposes Popper's over-demotion bias by preserving the
  compounding value of owned POCs even where pieces are build-from-owned.

### Reviewers

- Lakatos vs Taleb, axis `attack-vector`: Lakatos can over-demote because a
  component has precedent; Taleb exposes that by preserving witnessed
  combinations with concrete falsifiable tests. Lakatos exposes Taleb's
  witness-only bias by narrowing claims already owned by external programs.
- Lakatos vs Quine, axis `attack-vector`: Lakatos can import broad market or
  research labels; Quine exposes that by forcing each term to preserve meaning
  across AI, Lean, and BP contexts. Lakatos exposes Quine's local-definition
  bias by testing whether definitions survive external reading.
- Taleb vs Quine, axis `attack-vector`: Taleb can reduce claims to artifact
  counts; Quine exposes that by checking whether the artifact proves the
  defined claim. Taleb exposes Quine's wording-only bias by requiring a
  witness or next experiment for each refined definition.
