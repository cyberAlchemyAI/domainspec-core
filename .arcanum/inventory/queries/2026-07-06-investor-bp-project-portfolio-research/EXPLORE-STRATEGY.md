# Explore Strategy - Investor BP Project Portfolio Research

## Objective

Create the evidence substrate for a later investor business plan by thoroughly
mapping the corrected portfolio universe in `domainspec-core`, plus the
explicitly named owned adjacent repositories that may support training,
implementation, or verticalization offers.

This is not the business plan. It is the source-backed research layer the
business plan should build from. Its job is to prevent the later BP from
overclaiming, missing investor-relevant surfaces, confusing public and private
evidence, or presenting research-only work as shipped product.

The corrected priority order is load-bearing:

1. **Primary spine:** DomainSpec v2 and CyberAlchemy v2 / CAV2.
2. **Core orchestration wedge:** Craft orchestration and the `goal` spell/skill.
3. **Local-node AI / evidence exchange wedge:** the product-research blueprint
   in `proposal-blueprint.md`, especially governed local AI nodes, observed-use
   receipts, and credit-before-money lineage.
4. **Important product surfaces:** IDE Ergonomics, UI Prototyping Studio,
   Whisper/product-research-blueprint composition, User/Translate/Guide, Savvy
   Legal Studio AI, Autoads, and Maestro/TRAMA-style creative operations.
5. **Reach and commercialization candidates:** Ontologize, productized
   consulting/jobs-to-be-done, setup and training packages, and research-repo
   implementation/training offerings built from DomainSpec Lean formalization,
   tower-system-taxonomy, MOGT agentic conversation, and
   `../resonantos-economy-research`.
6. **Explicit exclusions:** `glyph/`, `grain/`, and `projects/goldenquill/`
   are not part of this investor-BP substrate. If encountered while traversing
   broad folders, mark them excluded and do not score them as portfolio
   candidates.

## Whisper Composition Substrate

The research run should treat `whisper` as both a product/capability candidate
and a later BP-writing substrate. The relevant transport is
`product_research_blueprint`: story-first, proof-honest proposal documents with
one-line promise, evidence tiers, load-bearing gaps, verification plan, and next
decisions.

For this dispatch, Whisper does not author the investor BP. It gives the
researchers a composition lens for:

- turning local-node AI and Evidence Exchange into reusable proposal blueprints;
- transferring the same governed author-intent / local-node concept into Savvy
  Legal Studio AI without claiming the transfer is implemented;
- packaging setup/training offers around research towers and governed repos;
- preserving a future fundraising-copy extension boundary without writing the
  fundraising copy yet.

### Reviewers

- Popper vs Taleb: Popper runs precedent/ownership classification, Taleb runs
  witness and downside/non-vacuity attack on the attack-vector axis; a
  precedent-only bias in Popper would be exposed by Taleb's demand for concrete
  survival evidence.
- Popper vs Quine: Popper runs owner/prior-art classification, Quine runs
  definitional-collapse attack on the attack-vector axis; an ownership-map
  bias in Popper would be exposed by Quine's demand that names not hide generic
  concepts or disclosure leaks.
- Taleb vs Quine: Taleb runs no-witness/downside attack, Quine runs
  definitional-soundness/collapse attack on the attack-vector axis; a
  survivorship-risk bias in Taleb would be exposed by Quine's test for whether
  the claim is even a distinct thing.
