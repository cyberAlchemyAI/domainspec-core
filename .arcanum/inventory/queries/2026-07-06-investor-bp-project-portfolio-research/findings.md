# Investor BP Project Portfolio Research Findings

Status: research substrate for business-plan construction. This is not final
investor prose.

## 1. Executive Result And Recommended BP Barbell

The strongest business-plan shape is a barbell:

1. **Sell narrow paid witnessed trust/readiness engagements now.**
   Lead with concrete buyer pain: a business metric no one trusts, an AI
   workflow that cannot be safely reviewed, a confidential document process
   that lacks custody/readiness, or a governed onboarding/setup need. The
   clearest paid wedges are **Metric Trust Sprint**, **AI Work Readiness
   Diagnostic**, **Confidential AI Workflow Readiness**, **Guided Governance
   Onboarding**, and bounded **Lead Evidence / Creative Learning Sprint** when
   framed as a scorecard service, not ad automation. Evidence: Ontologize
   founding-pilot scope and public caution refs; Savvy readiness gaps;
   Porter/Shannon convergence on buyer-first wedges
   (`projects/ontologize/PUBLIC-OFFERING.md:1-9`, `:56-72`, `:120-150`;
   `projects/savvy_legal-studio-ai/.../WORK-PACK.md:16`;
   [research.md](research.md) Agents 2 and 4).

2. **Use each engagement to produce evidence receipts.**
   Each engagement should end with a bounded artifact: source chain,
   denominator/confound notes, owner decision, privacy/reuse boundary,
   readiness scorecard, or observed-use receipt candidate. The BP should say
   the company is testing a governed evidence-to-action platform thesis through
   repeated receipts, not selling a finished platform. Evidence: Craft/goal
   receipt and approval boundaries; ObservedUseReceipt proposal status;
   repeated reviewer warnings not to claim deployment-proven platform
   (`arcanum/spells/goal/README.md:82-96`, `:144-168`;
   `cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-06-24-knowledge-market-product-idea/findings.md:20-35`,
   `:118-132`; [research.md](research.md) Agents 1, 2, and 4).

3. **Protect the long-term thesis with a defensibility substrate.**
   Tier 0 is not a product tier. It is the substrate: **DomainSpec v2**,
   **CAV2/CyberAlchemy v2**, **Craft/goal**, **DomainSpec Lean as
   Formal-Methods Discipline**, and **ResonantOS economy research as
   Anti-Exploitation Research Harness**. These make the company more than an
   agency or agent wrapper, but they should support buyer wedges rather than
   lead the pitch. Evidence: DomainSpec v2 implementation-home refs; CAV2
   authority model; Craft/goal A-grade receipt evidence; Lean proof discipline;
   ResonantOS research-only caution (`projects/domainspec-v2/README.md`,
   `cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:8-16`,
   `arcanum/arcana/craft/SKILL.md:14-25`,
   `domainspec-lean-formalization/LEAN-ARCHITECTURE.md:29-33`,
   `../resonantos-economy-research/README.md:1-8`;
   [research.md](research.md) Agent 4).

Recommended BP headline substrate:

> CyberAlchemy/DomainSpec sells witnessed trust and readiness engagements for
> AI-assisted work and business intelligence. Each engagement produces bounded
> evidence receipts. Repeated receipts test a governed evidence-to-action
> platform thesis backed by authority lifecycle, typed derivation, ledgered
> orchestration, formal-methods discipline, and anti-exploitation research.

Forbidden headline: "a finished autonomous governance platform."

## 3. BP Substrate Outline, Not Final Investor Prose

### A. Market Entry

Start with paid engagements that fit existing buyer budgets:

- Metric trust for BI/ops/AI leaders.
- AI work readiness for CTO/product/engineering teams using agents.
- Confidential workflow readiness for sensitive-document AI teams.
- Guided governance setup for research/protocol/governance teams.
- Lead evidence / creative learning as a bounded scorecard service.

Evidence basis: Porter/Shannon reviewer convergence; Ontologize public
offering; Savvy work-pack; Autoads service-gate research
(`projects/ontologize/PUBLIC-OFFERING.md:1-9`, `:120-150`;
`projects/savvy_legal-studio-ai/.../WORK-PACK.md:16`;
`projects/ontologize/research/2026-06-18-autoads-maestro-smb-marketing-offer/findings.md:7`).

### B. Engagement Receipt

Each paid engagement must produce a receipt bundle:

- Source chain or evidence trail.
- Denominator/confound notes.
- Owner decision.
- Privacy/reuse boundary.
- Readiness scorecard.
- Optional candidate observed-use receipt.

Evidence basis: Craft/goal receipts, ObservedUseReceipt proposal, Ontologize
metric chain (`arcanum/spells/goal/README.md:82-96`; Evidence Exchange
blueprint in [research.md](research.md) Agent 1; `projects/ontologize/SPEC.md:30-45`).

### C. Platform Thesis

Repeated receipts test whether evidence loops can compound into a governed
evidence-to-action platform. The BP should present this as a thesis under
validation, not a shipped platform.

Evidence basis: reviewers converge on "sell wedge first, platform later";
Evidence Exchange marked prototype/concept until W0 -> W1 host-observed receipt
exists ([research.md](research.md) Agents 1, 2, and 4).

### D. Defensibility Substrate

Defensibility comes from five substrate layers:

- Typed derivation and validation discipline: DomainSpec v2.
- Owner-routed authority and promotion gates: CAV2.
- Ledgered orchestration and fail-closed routing: Craft/goal.
- Formal-methods claim discipline: DomainSpec Lean.
- Anti-exploitation/reward-risk stress testing: ResonantOS economy research.

Evidence basis: Agent 4 evidence grades and source refs for DomainSpec v2,
CAV2, Craft/goal, Lean, and ResonantOS.

### E. Demo Surfaces

Use UI Studio, IDE Ergonomics, Whisper, Guide/User/Translate, Savvy, and
Autoads/Maestro as ways to make the substrate visible. They are not all first
products.

Evidence basis: Alexander/Bacon product-card convergence and later downgrade of
UI/IDE/Whisper from leads to demo/composition surfaces
(`projects/ui-prototyping-studio/README.md:28`;
`projects/ide-ergonomics/README.md:13`;
`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:21`; [research.md](research.md) Agent 3).

## 4. Deep Portfolio Sections

### DomainSpec v2

Investor role: defensibility substrate.

DomainSpec v2 should be described as the private implementation and research
surface for typed spec-to-test derivation, evidence governance, deterministic
validation, and drift discipline. It is not the front-door product and not
public-ready platform infrastructure. Evidence: v2 README and import manifest
identify it as an implementation home with migration and reformulation still
active (`projects/domainspec-v2/README.md:7-20`;
`projects/domainspec-v2/IMPORT-MANIFEST.md:22-32`; [research.md](research.md)
Agent 4).

Safe BP use: "the technical spine that lets engagements produce structured
evidence rather than prose-only consulting deliverables."

Next falsifier: one end-to-end witnessed path from typed spec to derived
obligations to validator receipts to an owner decision boundary. Until then,
keep it Tier 0.

### CAV2 / CyberAlchemy v2

Investor role: authority and governance substrate.

CAV2 is the private authority model that prevents evidence, repetition,
proximity, ontology membership, implementation success, credit, or market
attention from becoming authority. Evidence: CAV2 authority and definition refs
(`cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:8-16`, `:75-101`,
`:191-216`; `cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/decisions/README.md:12-17`;

Next falsifier: deterministic source-map/local-link validation plus one
promoted decision record. Public copy must be scrubbed; CAV2 is not yet a public
governance product.

## 5. Local-Node AI To Savvy Transfer With Proof Boundary

The transfer is methodological, not evidentiary.

Local-node AI / Evidence Exchange proposes a host-observed pattern: upstream
work is retrieved or used locally, downstream work is produced, and a receipt
records the relationship with privacy boundaries. Savvy provides a concrete
confidential-document workflow where custody, user approval, artifact
lifecycle, auth, retention, and audit matter. Evidence: local-node blueprint

`frontend/.../savvy-auth.test.ts:73-124`).

Safe transfer claim:

> Savvy is a source case for the kind of confidential workflow where local-node
> receipts may matter: sensitive inputs, authored outputs, reviewer approval,
> and custody boundaries.

Unsafe transfer claim:

> Savvy proves local-node AI, Evidence Exchange, or confidential legal AI is
> ready.

Proof boundary:

- Local-node AI remains proposal-status until W0 -> W1 host-observed receipt
  exists.
- Savvy remains prototype/readiness evidence until one complete confidential
  session passes auth, custody, retention, artifact, and approval checks.
- A future bridge would require a Savvy-like session emitting a redacted
  observed-use receipt without exposing confidential content or promoting
  authority.
