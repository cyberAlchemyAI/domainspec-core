## Plain-Language Model

Arcanum is best understood as a typed network of scoped owners and legal
transitions, not one authority ladder. Definitions govern only terms formally
admitted to the public definitions surface. Constitutions own reusable scoped
rules. Disciplines own recurring-practice descriptions and declared maturity.
Sigils own executable capability contracts, and spells own compositions.
Schemas represent shape; validators emit bounded detection evidence.

Evidence can support a proposal, but only an explicit target-owner decision may
precede canonical mutation. Sigil/spell registry update, generation,
synchronization, installation, and runtime are separate downstream transitions.
Discipline maturity follows a different owner-gated card/catalog mutation path.
