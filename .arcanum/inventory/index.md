## Current Gaps

- Evidence-cards and EvidenceSets are first-pass candidate read models, not
  promoted authority.
- Claim-level branch mapping exists only as candidate ontology-vault condensation.
- Boundary-skeptic residual status is `flag`: owner review is required before
  using the v2 tree as an architecture decision.
- The initial repository map remains a parent-agent install/backfill, while the
  cartographer artifacts are the first completed governed subagent research
  dispatch outputs.
- `projects/domainspec-v2/README.md` was already modified before this inventory
  install; inventory treats it as source evidence but does not claim current git
  cleanliness.
- CAOL ontology lifecycle cards are candidate Inventory read models only. They
  do not authorize ontology, definition, constitution, axiom, ledger, or Context
  Builder promotion.
- Testing-amalgamation cards are candidate Inventory read models only. They do
  not authorize public Arcanum package promotion, generated runtime exposure,
  registry changes, or any private evidence transfer into public fixtures.
- Authority-harness/ledger/ontology cards are candidate read models from an
  audited dispatch. They record that the cycle closes by EXTENDING the existing
  lifecycle envelope (not authoring a new harness); the only net-new identity is
  the typed-hole contract. The ontology-tie cycle has one open link — the
  node→promotion-record bridge edge is KILL-until-amendment and does not
  authorize any ontology, constitution, or schema change until the
  schema-evolution gate selects an amendment. The `ledger_spec_axis` is a 2-of-3
  witnessed claim: the signals JSONL schema is unread and the append-only
  authority-ledger validator does not yet exist.
- Authority-node relationship taxonomy cards are candidate read models only.
  They keep `authority-node` as an inventory/search handle while preserving the
  stricter implementation boundary: authority type/posture material currently
  belongs in envelope fields, owner profiles, PromotionRecord-compatible
  records, ledger evidence, and projections. The relation handles are not vault
  edges, and `authority-type`, `authority-posture`, `promotion-event`, and
  `movement` are not vault `node_type`s until schema evolution explicitly
  promotes that shape.
- Schema/classification/view primitive cards are candidate read models only.
  They preserve the audited repair and the later operator correction:
  `governed action facet obligations` is intended as a basic CyberAlchemy action
  method, not only promotion-candidate readiness and not authority. Generic
  Schema remains reference-only; `classification facet` is preferred over bare
  taxonomy; human-continuation and machine-checkable views must declare source,
  projection, validation boundary, and residue; the action-facet envelope,
