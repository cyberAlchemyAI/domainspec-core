## Key Points

- The owner already separates ProblemFrame admission, semantic evaluation and ReasoningReceipt, exact-effect GateDecision, and effect attempt; the Lean adapter preserves that order instead of treating them as one status.
- Ineligible.Defeated is represented as a completed negative semantic answer. Deferred.Insufficient caused by a missing required fact is represented as an admitted question whose answer is blocked. RecommendedUnderSnapshot is a positive semantic answer even when the later exact-effect gate stops or the binding check rejects the effect.
- SemanticReceipt can carry ExactEffectStage only under a recommendation, so non-recommendation outcomes cannot acquire fabricated downstream effect fields.
- OutOfDomain and the other non-admitted outcomes leave question formulation undetermined. No registered rule for a conclusion domain is not evidence that the surrounding system cannot formulate the question.
- ReceiptCertificate makes the expected evaluator id, schema version, inspected owner revision, and a nonempty external digest machine-visible. JSON decoding, digest computation, production correspondence, and trust in the decoder remain external obligations.
- The narrowest useful pilot is one frozen real ReasoningReceipt decoded into the Lean CertifiedRun shape, with digest and revision validation outside Lean and no owner-schema change unless the fixture exposes a missing field.
- For DomainSpec, keep lifecycle execution outcome separate from semantic result and carry the reasoning receipt as a route-owned artifact rather than expanding the terminal pipeline outcome enum with semantic states.
- For public Arcanum, carry only the generic rule that evidence or a semantic result does not authorize an effect. Do not copy private outcome names, DomainSpec paths, or private prose into the public package.
