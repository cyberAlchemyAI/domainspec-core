# Copy, Conversational-AI, and Coherence Audit

Status: pass for local implementation

## Scope

Reviewed the final browser copy against the accepted research substrate, the
Whisper composition result, the direct user requirements, and the visible
page sequence. This is an expert heuristic audit, not a participant
comprehension study.

## Conversational-AI language

Pass.

- The page never says or implies `you have been matched`, `the AI found`,
  `the agent decided`, or `your collaborator`.
- `Autonomous matching` appears only in explicit negation: the Co-Op Engine is
  a weekly room, not an autonomous matching system.
- AI actions are bounded and attributed. The Whisper vision defines
  `agentic` on first use as AI carrying out bounded, reviewable steps.
- Human action remains visible: visitors choose, facilitators present,
  contributors offer, and project owners confirm welcome and scope.
- Every project says `Possible contribution, not an open role`.

## Terms and jargon

Pass after revision.

- `Knowledge graph` is explained immediately as a map of characters, places,
  encounters, timelines, and connections.
- Internal shorthand such as `governed`, `witness`, `substrate`,
  `claim ceiling`, and generic `artifact` language was removed from the
  visible page or replaced with reviewable, demonstration, output, or plain
  evidence language.
- Necessary project/domain names remain intact, including Agentic Music
  Studio, L0, TypeScript, Kuhn poker, Leduc, MOGT, and Strava. Each sits in a
  concrete project context rather than carrying the main explanation.

## Ease of understanding

Pass at the intended heuristic level.

- The opening starts from a felt problem: unfinished projects become hard to
  enter.
- Two clear entrances follow: choose by desired activity or inspect all
  fourteen.
- Every card repeats the same question → summary → exists pattern.
- Every detail repeats vision → now → limit → next experiment → possible
  collaborators.
- Ambition is adjacent to present proof and limitation, reducing the need to
  infer project maturity.
- The contribution prompt asks for one uncertainty and one bounded act rather
  than a vague commitment.

## Coherence and presentation continuity

Pass.

- The self-guided and presentation modes use one semantic project record
  spine; there is no presenter-only project copy.
- The twenty-stage presentation follows hook → problem → entrance → fourteen
  projects → meeting → contribution → evidence boundary.
- Functional page cues use orienting, choosing, opening, testing, inviting,
  and closing language to state what the current region is doing.
- The existing 45-minute meeting remains the destination. The page does not
  invent a replacement workflow.

## Evidence ceiling and residue

The audit supports clarity and coherence as expert judgments only. Cold-read,
teach-back, and meeting-use observation are still required to claim actual
understanding or recruitment effectiveness. Current project welcome remains a
human-owner decision.
