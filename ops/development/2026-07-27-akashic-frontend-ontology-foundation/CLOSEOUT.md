same-machine adapter between the browser and the current Akashic read models,
and treat the richer Akashic-owned graph profile as a candidate contract for

live Git, and agent-execution capability. The research supports a first
implementation direction; it does not prove implementation, ontology
promotion, release, deployment, production readiness, or human usability.
