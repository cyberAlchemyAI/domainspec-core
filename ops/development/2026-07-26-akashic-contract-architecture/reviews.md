## Quine — Definitional-Soundness Gate

Verdict: REVISE

Authority ceilings are sound, but lifecycle state, phase placement, and naming
must be repaired:

1. Say the public Design **self-identifies** as an authored-complete candidate,
   while it remains owner-blocked, unaccepted, unimplemented, and
   contract-incomplete at selector/serialization/diagnostic/version seams.
2. Classify separately:
   - current boundary/requirement;
   - missing normative profile;
   - planned witness;
   - no execution.
3. Keep only external/deferred, non-normative Phase-1 interface stubs in the
   Phase-0 Design. A normative Phase-1 suite belongs in a separate future
   artifact.
4. Use one naming hierarchy:
   - `ValidationFact`: predicate result over bound inputs;
   - `ValidationReceipt`: immutable serialized envelope for facts/provenance;
   - `ValidationReport`: Phase-1 aggregation for one candidate revision;
   - `ProjectionReceipt` and `RegenerationReceipt`: distinct operation receipts.
5. Replace generic `OwnerDecision` in flows with typed decision kinds or a
   closed `decision_kind`: merge authorization, rights disposition,
   publication, and ontology admission remain different.
6. A remote observer, not the authorization decision, produces `MergeReceipt`;
   authorization and repository observation are joined inputs.
7. Replace adjectival “trusted” with predicates for base ref, producer identity,
   tool/version, policy digest, and verification result.
8. Define `CapabilityProfileRef` as routing identity only; packet content and
   selected Inventory handles cannot mutate or establish capability.
9. `OntologyCandidate` is a separately identified derived candidate referencing
   authored origins; it never reuses authored record identity. Candidate
   derivation and ontology admission are separate.
10. Split first-class evidence records from assessment records.
11. Split current supported-tuple rejection from future deprecation/migration.
12. Do not publish multiple research/synthesis ID families as independent
    contract identities. Use temporary aliases or existing names only.
13. Keep `EvidenceValue`, `SourceSelector`, and `SelectedSourceSegment`
    distinct.
14. Say “currently described ownership roles; no bound owners.”

Definitional KILLs that survive:

- Phase-0 first-class `EvidenceAssertion` → merge current behavior into embedded
  `EvidenceValue`;
- content-bearing capability profile → content-free `CapabilityProfileRef` plus
  separate `ContextPacket`;
- prose-only `CONTRACT-CATALOG.md` → merge useful summary into current Design;
- raw ambiguous relations and stored inverses → existing typed embedded relation
  and generated inverse view;
- self-authored lifecycle/authority fields → later separate assessment and typed
  owner-effect contracts.

Do not KILL solely for lack of a witness:

- deprecation, RDF/SHACL, Olog, semantic graph and ontology admission remain
  deferred/excluded from Phase 0 unless a duplicate or collapse is actually
  identified.
