# RG2b — Minimal Surface for "Who We Are" (the restraint voice)

Role: the brake. A separate agent is maximizing what to pull from the private corpus.
My job is to argue for cutting. Calibrated against the live public pages
(`arcanum/docs/index.html`, `system.html`): tight lead, 3–4 sections, panels that
**link** rather than re-explain, one LinkedIn CTA in the footer, "scoped to what exists"
honesty. The About page must match that register, not exceed it.

---

## 1. The minimal section set (and the explicit leave-out list)

A "Who We Are" page does one job no other page does: **tell a stranger who is behind
this and why to trust/contact them.** That needs four sections, no more:

1. **Lead (hero).** Two or three sentences: who we are (two people building
   CyberAlchemy in the open), and the conviction that drives it (agents are fast but
   not reproducible — so we build governed, legible tools). Mirror the existing lead
   length exactly.
2. **The people.** Vladimir Rondelli and Victor Boscaro — already the two names in
   every footer. A short panel each: what they own / point of view. One line, not a CV.
3. **Why we build this way (the conviction).** One short section — the belief that
   makes the work coherent: build in the open, keep claims tied to evidence, absorb
   complexity into structure so people don't carry it. This is the "why," stated once,
   pointing outward to the pages that show the "what."
4. **Work with us (CTA).** One section, one action: LinkedIn.

**Leave out (filler, redundancy, or over-share):**
- ❌ **Any re-explanation of DomainSpec / Saturn / Craft / the loop.** They have pages.
  Name once, link, move on.
- ❌ **The 25 meta-types / 29 relationships / `T = f(C,R,Δ)` / 95 tests / 96% / 38.75%→54.37%.**
  All of that lives on System and Paper. Repeating metrics here dilutes both pages.
- ❌ **A roadmap / "what's planned."** System already carries the honest roadmap.
- ❌ **A timeline, origin story, or "our journey."** Over-share; nobody arrives at an
  About page for a founding narrative. One sentence of conviction beats a saga.
- ❌ **A values/principles list (3–6 bullets).** Generic. The conviction paragraph
  carries the only value that matters: evidence-tied, built-in-the-open.
- ❌ **A pricing / "what's free vs. product" block.** That's Saturn's `#offer`. Link if needed.
- ❌ **Private-corpus detail** — internal process, dispatch ledgers, telemetry, client
  names, unpublished experiments, anything not already on a public page. Default: out.
- ❌ **Photos/bios beyond one line each** unless the operator already wants headshots.

## 2. Relationship to existing pages (redundancy risks)

The page is the **human anchor** of a site that is otherwise about the system. It should
say *who* and *why*, then hand off:
- "what is meaningful" → **System** / **Paper** (DomainSpec). Do not restate.
- "how the work is done" → **Arcanum repo** / **loop guide**. Do not restate.
- "evidence / what's free" → **Saturn**. Do not restate.
- "the building philosophy" → **Method**. About may echo one sentence of stance, but
  Method owns the full treatment — link it.

**Top redundancy risk:** drifting into a second System page. If a sentence explains the
product rather than the people or their conviction, it belongs on another page. Cut it.

## 3. Length / scannability target

Match the existing pages, do not exceed them. System has ~5 sections; index ~6 short ones.
About should be the **shortest page on the site**: hero lead + 3 sections, ~250–400 words
of prose total. One screen of intent, then links. No wall of text, no metrics dump, no
loop diagram. Reuse the same CSS, two-author footer, and `eyebrow → h2 → lead → panels`
rhythm so it reads as part of the set.

## 4. The one job + the one CTA

- **One job no other page does:** put a credible human face on the system — make a
  stranger believe two named people stand behind these claims and can be reached. Every
  other page argues the *work*; this page vouches for the *people*.
- **One CTA:** **Work with me → LinkedIn** (`https://www.linkedin.com/in/vladimir-rondelli/`),
  the same action already in both footers. Primary button in the CTA section + repeat in
  footer. Resist adding "Open Arcanum / Read the paper / Saturn" as competing buttons —
  those are inline links in the prose; the page closes on **one** ask.
