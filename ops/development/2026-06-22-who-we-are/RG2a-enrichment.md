## 1. Positioning / What CyberAlchemy is

- `[public-safe]` **Identity line.** "A research and engineering group at the intersection of
  autonomous systems, formal governance, and applied cybernetics." (main.html — already-published
  "Who We Are" framing.)
- `[public-safe]` **What we make.** Not software that automates tasks, but architectures that
  understand and steer complex environments. (main.html)
- `[public-safe]` **Intent / execution / evidence frame.** A system that keeps three things in
  contact — *intent* (what we meant to build), *execution* (what agents and code actually did),
  *evidence* (what tests, telemetry, and source show) — and names the drift when they diverge.
  (README.md "Core Idea"; deep-dive "System Thesis".) This is the cleanest public articulation of
  the "operating system for humans and agents" idea.
- `[public-safe]` **Code as probe.** "Code is not the end product; it is the observational probe
  we send into a domain to chart its territory and return empirical evidence." (main.html "What We
  Believe".) Strong, quotable, fully public.
- `[public-safe]` **Cybernetics² principle.** We research cybernetic theory (feedback, regulation,
  control) and apply those same principles to the tools we build — systems that act as cybernetic
  loops on human activity: track intent, interpret reality, verify alignment, feed it back to human
  operators. (main.html "How We Work".)
- `[public-safe]` **Self-defending software thesis.** The shift from describing how an architecture
  *should* work to ensuring it *performs* — embedding a control loop so the system self-corrects on
  empirical evidence. (shareable deck / index.html "Software That Defends Itself".) Publishable as
  vision language; keep it generic.
- `[private-keep-internal]` **Named internal projects.** Saturn, MARS, MOGT, Necronomicon,
  Arcanum, DomainSpec — and their architectures (convergence loop, operational ontology branch,
  authority ladder, promotion gates, layer stack). These are internal/proprietary. Public copy may
  describe *capabilities* in the abstract but should not enumerate project names or the layer map.
- `[private-keep-internal]` **Internal artifact corpus.** definition/architecture/ontology docs,
  source ledgers, work slices, telemetry schemas. Internal-only.

## 2. What we value / how we work (recurring themes)

These themes recur across BOTH the corpus and the persona — strongest candidates for a
"what we value" section. All `[public-safe]` as general philosophy.

- `[public-safe]` **Transparency as architecture.** A system that can't explain itself is a
  concentration of power; clarity is a structural choice, not a communication one. The black box is
  the enemy. (persona "Philosophical Core" #3; main.html cybernetic feedback to human operators.)
- `[public-safe]` **Nudge, don't cage.** Rigid rules break under reality's weight; design the
  conditions and let correct behavior emerge. The anti-authoritarian design move. (persona "Core"
  #2 + "What He Dislikes".)
- `[public-safe]` **Decompose to find emergence.** Break a system into its smallest honest parts to
  find where the interactions produce behavior the parts can't — the insight is in the interaction.
  (persona "Core" #1.)
- `[public-safe]` **Evidence over vibes.** We don't prove models, we falsify them; measure reality
  against intent rather than trusting the blueprint. (persona Popper/Taleb foundation; corpus
  drift/convergence + "evidence flows up".)
- `[public-safe]` **AI as symbiosis, not replacement.** Systems that feed alignment back to human
  operators and keep intent and observed operation mutually traceable — humans steer, agents
  execute, evidence closes the loop. (main.html "human operators"; README intent/execution/evidence.)
- `[public-safe]` **Self-consistency as aesthetic.** The goal is structure clean enough that
  exceptions and if/else workarounds disappear into it. (persona "What He Enjoys".)
