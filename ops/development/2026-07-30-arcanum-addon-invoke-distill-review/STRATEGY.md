# Arcanum Add-on Invoke Distill Review Strategy

## P1 Trigger

Dispatch is required by parallelism and isolation. The high-complexity
cross-owner Design and Plan need two independent full-corpus judgments whose
raw objections remain separable until the parent joins them.

## Lanes and Subagents

One group runs two read-only agents in parallel:

| Lane | Pooled identity | Runtime role | Lens | Expected output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proposer | Abramsky, Samson | explorer | formal methodology: smallest coherent unit, layer/dependency closure, and owner-preserving recomposition | exact current-artifact receipt with PASS or bounded objections |
| Balancer | Ashby, W. Ross | explorer | adversarial methodology: authority collapse, runtime/state coupling, SWU atomicity, evidence inflation, degraded modes, and gate completeness | exact current-artifact receipt with PASS or bounded objections |

The anti-bias axis is methodology: constructive formal recomposition versus
adversarial operational falsification. The pair is expected to disagree where
the Proposer accepts a minimal composition that the Balancer can break, or
where the Balancer demands machinery that the Proposer can prove unnecessary.

## Dependency Flow

The two lanes are independent and parallel. There is no inter-agent feedback
edge. The parent:

1. joins and closes both;
2. preserves their raw returns in the private review folder;
3. assembles `attacks.md`;
4. writes the verified change-request synthesis to `findings.md`;
5. reconciles public-safe Distill receipts and the Invoke gates in the product
   worktree without implementing fixes or selecting an SWU.

## Inventory Preflight

`STRATEGY-INVENTORY-PACKET.md` records an owner-index lookup pass and a target
machine-index gap. Selected evidence forces exact joined Distill receipts,
full-corpus attack, current-byte verification, and retention of the accepted
three-owner topology only as a hypothesis to test.

## Claim Ceiling

The dispatch is read-only. Parent reconciliation may change planning artifacts
only. It cannot implement the add-on, refresh Bootstrap packages, install or
grant the add-on, run providers, validate the browser witness, select an SWU,
or decide readiness, promotion, deployment, or release.

## Result and Next Route

The review is closed with verified change requests in `findings.md`. The
product-side Distill gate remains block because both independent receipts
validated the bytes they reviewed as block and the reconciled bytes have not
received a second independent validation.

A revalidation would be a new, separately shown and confirmed dispatch over
the changed-byte digest. Governance, Bootstrap, owner, adapter, retention, and
execution gates remain independently blocked.
