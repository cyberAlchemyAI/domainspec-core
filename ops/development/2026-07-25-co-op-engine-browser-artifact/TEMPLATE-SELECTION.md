# Define Template Selection

## Inventory Result

The prebuilt `domainspec-spec` family is eligible because the target has:

- owned states and transitions;
- operations and queries;
- multiple human and machine projections;
- privacy and authority boundaries;
- acceptance-critical invariants;
- a later architecture and execution-pack route.

## Selection

| Candidate | Eligibility | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `domainspec-spec` | exact fit for a stateful browser product with source, state, projection, and acceptance contracts | selected |
| `ux-plan` | required at Design because natural people read, decide, act, navigate, and recover through the rendered semantic contract | defer to Design companion |
| `architecture` | useful, but the DomainSpec architecture bundle already supplies the six-view baseline | not selected separately |
| `research` | accepted governed research already supplies the discovery baseline | not selected |
| `spell` / `sigil` | the target is a product artifact, not a reusable Arcanum capability | not applicable |

No eligible tie exists.

## Discovery Gate

Discovery is satisfied by:

- `../2026-07-25-co-op-engine-portfolio-research/research.md`;
- `../2026-07-25-co-op-engine-portfolio-research/findings.md`;
- its closed append-only dispatch evidence.

No discovery waiver is required.

## Authority Boundary

Template selection establishes an authoring shape only. It does not promote a
template, approve the product mechanism, validate Design, authorize
implementation, or prove public safety.
