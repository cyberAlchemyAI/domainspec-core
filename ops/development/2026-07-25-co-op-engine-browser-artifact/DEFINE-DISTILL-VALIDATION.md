## Role Conversation Trace

### Round 1

- Proposer claim: define the complete one-artifact system—truth record,
  self-guided projection, live projection, appointment, receipt, fourteen
  cards, audits, and Whisper copy.
- Evidence: accepted findings and user-confirmed lifecycle route.
- Balancer objection — responsibility overload: final copy and executable audits
  are later lifecycle products; including them as Define outputs would hide
  evidence gaps.
- Reconciliation: retain their contracts and boundaries, defer implementation,
  witnessed validation, and composition.

### Round 2

- Proposer claim: the smallest Define unit is one admitted project record plus
  its self-guided and facilitated projections.
- Balancer objection — requisite variety: a single record alone would omit the
  cross-project catalog, meeting mechanism, privacy, and audience obligations
  that determine the artifact's meaning.
- Reconciliation: the smallest coherent Define unit is the **one-artifact
  product contract**—shared truth record, projection rules, collaboration state
  distinctions, project-card admission boundary, and proposed acceptance
  obligations. It does not contain stack, components, tasks, copy, or tests.
