## Fixed Constraints

- One application and one truth model; no separate slide deck.
- Fourteen project records eventually render from owner-admitted facts.
- The first falsifying fixture is Poker Agent Arena.
- Interest, candidate pair, bilateral appointment, and terminal receipt remain
  distinct states.
- Every field has one minimum visibility class; unclassified fields fail
  closed on every observable surface.
- Initial participant fixtures are synthetic or explicitly consented and
  low-risk.
- The first production deployment is access-restricted.
- Public access requires the independent leakage audit, black-box UI evidence,
  both comprehension profiles, coherence and conversational-language/copy
  review, and D-005 publication/naming approval.
- Veritasium is internal explanatory lineage only.
- Copy must avoid unnecessary meta-narration about what the page, section,
  slide, filter, or interaction is doing.
- Whisper composition follows stable truth, projection, and acceptance
  contracts.

## Design Scope

Design owns:

- architecture and component boundaries;
- typed record, projection, state, and visibility contracts;
- self-guided, facilitated, presenter, authoring, and interaction surfaces;
- browser route and state semantics;
- persistence and external-effect boundaries;
- restricted hosting and eventual public-release controls;
- planned fixtures, validator contracts, and UX acceptance signals.

Design does not own:

- implementation files under `projects/co-op-engine/`;
- creation or deployment of a Sites project;
- final project copy;
- execution of audits;
- public naming approval;
- work-pack tasks, waves, or mutation-ready SWUs.
