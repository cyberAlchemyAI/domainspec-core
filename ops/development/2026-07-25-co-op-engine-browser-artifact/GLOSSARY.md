# Candidate Glossary

These terms are local to the Co-Op Engine product definition. They are not
promoted Arcanum or cross-project definitions.

| Term | Plain bridge | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Co-Op Engine | Proper name of the weekly meeting. | Not an autonomous engine or AI matcher. |
| Experiment Exchange | The selected weekly mechanism for inspecting small experiments and making reciprocal appointments. | Define selection only; not yet implemented or validated. |
| Eligible experiment | A bounded opportunity with owner, evidence, limit, ask, SLA, expiry, privacy, stop rule, and expected witness. | Eligibility does not mean quality or fit. |
| Response overdue | The owner did not answer a new offer within 72 hours. | The card may be inspected but cannot enter a new pair or clinic. |
| Stale card | A card more than thirty days beyond owner verification or explicitly withdrawn during review. | Historical record only; not eligible or clinic-ready. |
| Energy mark | A signal such as can-help, want-to-learn, clarify, or refer. | Interest, not a match or commitment. |
| Evidence-triggered clinic | Live attention justified by a recorded translation, access, privacy, complementarity, or success-test problem. | Not default airtime or curator preference. |
| Candidate pair | Two parties for whom a possible contribution has been proposed. | Not reciprocal intent. |
| Bilateral appointment | Both parties confirm who, what, when, where, how, effort, boundary, stop rule, and receipt date. | Intent, not completed action. |
| Receipt | A dated record of ran, not-started, blocked, declined, revised, or stopped, plus evidence and owner decision. | Historical evidence, not mechanism efficacy. |
| Claim ceiling | The strongest wording current evidence supports. | A limit, not a target for optimistic copy. |
| Stop rule | The condition that ends or revises a small experiment. | Not a failure stigma. |
| Mapping limit | The point where an analogy or explanation stops being reliable. | Must remain visible when a bridge is used. |
| Truth record | The admitted source, claim, state, experiment, and visibility fields behind every projection. | Does not replace project authority. |
| Projection | A permitted view of the same admitted record for one audience or task. | May change disclosure and pacing, not truth. |
| Visibility class | The least-restrictive audience allowed to observe one field: public, meeting-visible, assigned collaborator, participant-private, or owner-private. | An unclassified field is blocked everywhere; a class is not proof that implementation cannot leak it. |
| Policy version | The exact admission, response, expiry, and refresh rule attached to a historical card or receipt. | Later policy changes do not reinterpret past evidence. |
| Presenter notes | Timing, boundaries, cues, and warnings for the facilitator. | Must not leak into audience-visible surfaces. |
| Meta-narration | Copy that tells readers what the page, section, slide, filter, or interaction is doing instead of communicating the substance. | Necessary orientation, state, accessibility, and recovery instructions remain allowed. |
| Writer Story-State Studio | Candidate writing workspace for author-reviewed story facts and temporal/epistemic projections. | Not a current Whisper feature or agentic writer studio. |
| Canon admission | An author accepts a proposed story assertion as part of the story's admitted state. | Machine extraction may propose but cannot silently admit. |
| `reader_as_of` | What a reader may know at one story point without future disclosure. | A spoiler-safe projection, not a summary of all canon. |
| `character_as_of` | What a character may know at one story point with a stated basis. | Meeting someone does not necessarily establish identity knowledge. |
| Internal explanatory lineage | Private inspiration for a misconception-to-reveal movement. | No public Veritasium branding, imitation, endorsement, or affiliation claim. |
