## Closure and recomposition proof

Every candidate owns the same responsibility: prepare a beginner to distinguish work shape, evidence state, and next-owner action through one need-before-name movement. Inputs are a concrete or abstract premise plus frozen controls; outputs are an attempt, explanation, exact reveal, transfer, and bounded repository connection. Nothing outside the candidate supplies hidden semantic glue. Splitting below three routes loses continuity and transfer evidence; expanding to seven lessons adds unapproved implementation scope.

The architecture recomposes upward by applying its guide-wide pattern to one lesson at a time after operator selection. Runtime controls remain unchanged. Source HTML, accessibility, and deployment evidence remain separate downstream responsibilities.

## Evolution profile and deferred complexity

Expected evolution pressures are additional lesson topics, readers with less software familiarity, and possible alternate domains. The smallest extension boundary is the six-beat audience sequence plus a story-spine/method choice per lesson. Adaptive story selection, personalization, analytics, a global character bible, automated comprehension scoring, and a reusable analogy registry are deferred because no current evidence justifies their complexity.

## Premortem

The most likely failure is that the operator selects vivid copy, implementation expands every lesson, and readers remember characters or metaphors while confusing joined evidence with truth or gate passage with execution. Guardrails: one anchor budget, exact non-implications, analogy drop points, protected controls, mobile/accessibility validation after selection, and intended-reader review before any comprehension claim.

## Cycle guards

The two-round budget is exhausted. The candidate field did not grow after scoring began. Recurrent tensions—continuity versus lore, analogy recall versus load, dialogue resonance versus cueing—produced no new evidence in a third conceptual pass, so further author-only reduction would rename rather than resolve them.

## Stable tension ledger

1. Recurring product cast improves causal continuity but requires lore recall.
2. Casebook independence lowers lore but increases domain reorientation.
3. Analogy can improve memory while adding a second representation that may drift.
4. Dialogue adds human tension while speaker status can leak correctness and increase assistive-technology verbosity.
5. Community examples broaden beginner access while adding a transfer step back to software.
6. Minimum concreteness may be sufficient, but author review cannot establish that without intended-reader evidence.

## Frame-expiry conditions

Re-run the tournament if the target audience is no longer beginner, the transport stops being an interactive guided class, protected IDs or lifecycle ontology change, lessons no longer use attempt-before-reveal, the guide's primary objective becomes reference scanning rather than causal learning, or intended-reader evidence contradicts the author scores.
