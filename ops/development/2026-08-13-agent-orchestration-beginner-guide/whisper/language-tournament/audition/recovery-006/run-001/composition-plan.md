# Composition plan — Recovery 006 twelve-entry tournament

## Objective and finite budget

Distill a guide-wide teaching architecture, not merely a Route 1 rewrite. The comparison unit is one candidate containing complete audience-copy samples for Route 1, Route 3, and Route 7. Tournament mode is fixed at twelve candidates, two recursive rounds, one reconciliation pass, twelve score dimensions, and one non-weighted Pareto calculation. No candidate may be added after scoring begins.

The output remains a flagged audition. Intent is volatile, the transport is candidate/unproven, the human gate is pending, and authority effect is none.

## Broad layer and smallest coherent unit

The broad layer is the seven-lesson guided-class teaching architecture. The selected optimization point is a three-route architecture spanning:

- entry: choose a work shape from the safeguard the problem needs;
- causal middle: trace exactly how far evidence moved;
- final transfer: name present state, missing action, next owner, and residue.

An isolated opening cannot test continuity or transfer. A seven-lesson rewrite would smuggle implementation and inflate the volatile intent. Three full route samples are therefore the smallest unit that closes and recomposes.

## Frozen field

The factorial field crosses three story spines (`product`, `community`, `casebook`) with three methods (`consequence`, `analogy`, `dialogue`). Three controls retain the current abstract baseline, minimum concreteness, and deliberate overstory excess. Recovery 005 is evidence about one Route 1 possibility only and receives no default selection status.

Each factorial proposal must actually demonstrate its story spine and teaching method. Each control must demonstrate its control property while retaining every required moment.

## Round 2 — Balancer pressure and revision

The Balancer checks every proposal against named objection categories:

- abstraction-level mismatch;
- completeness and protected-binding fidelity;
- consistency across three routes;
- causal clarity and need-before-name;
- authority and claim boundary;
- cue safety and option parity;
- cognitive load and decorative lore;
- requisite variety across entry, lifecycle, and transfer;
- boundary-object quality of stories and analogies;
- implementation and accessibility disruption risk;
- premortem failure mode;
- guide-wide extensibility.

Reconciliation may revise candidate wording or its score evidence. It may not add candidates, change a hidden value or expected answer, reorder a formal sequence, alter a repository path, widen a claim, or turn an audition into implementation.

## Shared example budget

Viable candidates get at most one anchor episode, two contrast objects, one analogy, and one transfer check per route. Story details must establish cause, consequence, responsibility, or recall. Analogy candidates must state mapping, limit, and drop condition. `C03-overstory-stressor` is deliberately exempt and labels the excess so it can test cognitive-load failure rather than masquerade as preferred copy.

## Scoring and hard elimination

After all twelve files are frozen, score each 1–5 on resonance, beginner relevance, reader trajectory, concreteness, analogy accuracy, cue safety, cross-lesson continuity, cognitive load, binding fidelity, claim boundary, implementation cost, and guide extensibility. Higher is always stronger or safer. Every score requires a candidate-specific observation.

Eliminate before Pareto calculation only for:

- a protected binding failure;
- a widened claim ceiling;
- an omitted required route moment;
- an analogy that materially misstates authority, evidence, governance, or execution.

Do not calculate a weighted total. Candidate A dominates B only when A is at least as strong on all twelve dimensions and strictly stronger on at least one. Publish every non-dominated survivor with its trade-offs and stable disagreements.

## Distill technique plan

- **Abstraction-level guard:** reject isolated sentence polish and seven-lesson implementation as the wrong comparison level.
- **Recomposition proof:** require every candidate's entry, middle, and transfer to form one guide architecture.
- **Evolution profile:** test whether the pattern can vary stories and methods without changing controls.
- **Frame-expiry condition:** invalidate the optimization if the target audience, interaction contract, lifecycle ontology, or transport changes materially.
- **Navigable result:** give the parent a start path through contract, field, scores, front, and trace.
- **Cognitive-load check:** count characters, objects, analogies, dialogue turns, and formal terms competing in working memory.
- **Requisite-variety check:** ensure the architecture handles shape choice, temporal evidence reasoning, and owner transfer rather than one repeated quiz pattern.
- **Boundary-object check:** stories and analogies must stay legible to beginners while mapping back to exact repository semantics.
- **Premortem:** ask how the candidate would fail in a mobile lesson or be remembered incorrectly.
- **Set-based tournament:** preserve non-dominated trade-offs instead of selecting by weighted total.
