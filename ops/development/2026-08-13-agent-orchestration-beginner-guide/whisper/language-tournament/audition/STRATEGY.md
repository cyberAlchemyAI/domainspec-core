This proposal prepares a small Whisper language audition. It does not rewrite
any of the seven course routes. It tests three teaching movements on one moment
from Route 1, one from Route 3, and one from Route 7, then stops at Human Gate A
so the operator can select, blend, reject, or revise the language direction.

## P1 Trigger Decision

P1 is `dispatch`. Three language candidates must be produced independently,
compared neutrally, and attacked through two distinct review lenses before a
later writer can report the non-dominated set. One helper cannot preserve those
independence and dependency boundaries. More agents grant no additional
authority: the current HTML, deployment, canonical vocabulary, and private
authority sources remain immutable.

## Objective And Evidence Boundary

The objective is to produce and independently review three small
beginner-language auditions for the Route 1 opening, Route 3 lifecycle tension,
and Route 7 transfer reveal. Every candidate must preserve the current IDs,
answers, commands, paths, ownership boundaries, lifecycle order, and evidence
claim ceiling. The result may improve editorial confidence; it cannot prove
human comprehension, browser transport, publication, deployment, or production
readiness.

`baseline_extractor` → `candidate_writers` → `audition_synthesizer` ⇄
`audition_reviewers` → `findings_writer` → `audition_auditor`.

zig-zag and auditor/writer feedback edges each have a loop cap of one. Every
cross-wave dependency is receipt-typed. The parent owns evidence closure and
final approval; Human Gate A follows the audit.
