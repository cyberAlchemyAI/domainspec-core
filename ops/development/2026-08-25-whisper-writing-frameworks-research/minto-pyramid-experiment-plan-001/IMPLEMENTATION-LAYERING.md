# Implementation Layering: Minto Pyramid experiment

## Purpose

Sequence one experiment-local Minto Pyramid test from the smallest non-vacuity witness through repeatability and governance hardening without turning experiment evidence into Whisper adoption authority.

## Target and scope

- Target: `minto-pyramid-experiment-001`
- Scope: research workflow
- Current state: accepted research and candidate selection exist; no fixture, validation result, human review, integration, or reader evidence exists.
- Boundary heuristic: each layer ends when it unlocks one new decision with less scope than the next decision would require.

## L0 minimum working unit proof

The L0 unit is not one file. It is the smallest meaningful end-to-end witness composed from three atomic SWUs:

1. a source-bound controlled case;
2. a mechanics-only validation receipt; and
3. a precommitted role-label-blind human semantic review chain that issues the bounded witness verdict.

Removing any one loses source/provenance closure, declared mechanical evidence, or the human judgment required by the accepted research. Combining them would hide independently failing boundaries.

## Non-regression guardrails

- Later layers must preserve the exact L0 source selectors, whole-document unit, executive transport, counterexample, and claim ceiling.
- Mechanical validation never decides truth, semantic equivalence, genre fit, hierarchy plausibility, or reader outcomes.
- L0's plan-owned validator recomputes actual-byte bindings and closed structural invariants only; it is not a promoted or reusable Whisper validator.
- Human review never creates canonical integration, promotion, publication, or reader-effect authority.
- Any change to unit level, transport, changed decision, prevented failure, or boundary case creates a new experiment candidate.
- The experiment must use original synthetic prose and must not copy protected examples or reveal private source locators.

## Recommended next layer

- Next layer: `L0`
- Key decision unlocked: whether one complete Minto-specific non-vacuity witness can be produced under the accepted boundaries.
- Major deferred scope: repeatability, validator packaging, reader outcomes, and every canonical Whisper change.
