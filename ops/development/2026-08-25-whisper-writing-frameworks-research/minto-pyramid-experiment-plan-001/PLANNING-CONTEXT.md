## Bound design decisions

- Candidate: `minto-pyramid`.
- Transport: `executive-brief`.
- Unit level: `whole-document`.
- Current owner: `composition_plan` within the transport side of `transport_or_preset_profile`; transport and preset remain distinct.
- Narrow sourced relation: one governing point with supporting ideas organized beneath it.
- Experiment-only operationalization: compare identical span content under a flat evidence-led composition plan versus `declared-governing-point-support-hierarchy`. The single changed decision is the composition-plan selection; governing-point order, support groups, and review-block segmentation are its specified structural consequences, not separate scored interventions. This is not a claim that the bound public source establishes a complete answer-first method.
- Intended prevented failure: `support-association-not-declared`. This is one exhibited structural failure, not a disjunction and not a claim about clarity or persuasion.
- Boundary case: a discovery-led composition whose intended form requires withholding a governing point; Minto activation must remain false.
- Reader study: excluded.

## Assumptions

- Synthetic original prose will be used so the experiment does not copy protected examples.
- One role-label-blind human editorial reviewer with an exact pre-review commitment and post-response reveal is sufficient for L0 feasibility; repeat reviewers and inter-rater evidence belong to L1.
- No network retrieval is required during execution because all source claims are bound to accepted repository evidence.
