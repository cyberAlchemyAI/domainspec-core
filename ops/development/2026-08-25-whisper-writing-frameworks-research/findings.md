## Validation and experiment plan

1. **Bind inputs.** Record source identity, digest or version, exact selector, attribution, component, unit level, applicability, counterexample, proof status, and current owner before evaluating a candidate. Decomposition never removes provenance. [FS:/artifacts/validation_experiment_plan/0; RP:/artifacts/findings/1]
2. **Run mechanical validation only on declared mechanics.** Eligible checks are schema shape and required source bindings; declared unit-level and owner references; ordered part and candidate references; transport/preset completeness as separately defined; configured lexical presence or duplication; and positive/negative fixture conformance. [FS:/artifacts/executable_checks; RD:/artifacts/corrected_definitions/executable_lint]
3. **Run blinded human semantic review separately.** Humans assess truth and fairness, genre fit, inference and warrant adequacy, RST plausibility, conceptual cohesion, pronoun resolution, synonym drift, given/new movement, opening promises, repetition, point placement, and hierarchy. [FS:/artifacts/human_checks; RD:/artifacts/corrected_definitions/human_check]
4. **Do not infer reader outcomes from lint or editorial review.** Trust, clarity, coherence, persuasion, retention, comprehension, model update, and semantic correctness are forbidden executable claims. [FS:/artifacts/forbidden_executable_claims; RN:/validation/semantic_lint_boundary]
5. **Measure reader outcomes only in a separate preregistered experiment.** Declare population, baseline, intervention, metric, sample, and the transport-bounded claim before data collection. [RP:/artifacts/findings/3; RN:/artifacts/reader_outcome_boundary]
6. **Require candidate-specific non-vacuity before reopening integration.** A generic multi-framework test plan is insufficient; each candidate needs its exact paired composition, changed decision, prevented failure, and counterexample. [RN:/blockers; RN:/artifacts/witness_verdicts]
7. **Require separate authority after evidence.** Repeated observations remain experiment evidence or learning residue until an owner separately approves any schema, preset, canonical, promotion, or publication proposal. [RD:/artifacts/corrected_definitions/experiment_evidence; RD:/artifacts/corrected_definitions/learning_residue; EC:/authority]
