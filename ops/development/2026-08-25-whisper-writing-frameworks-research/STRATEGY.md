## Preliminary Finding

The Writer Science PDF is not one indivisible essay template. It mixes at least
four responsibilities at different levels: argument movement, introduction
problem framing, recursively nested discourse structure, and clause-level
cohesion/information flow. Treating all of them as one Whisper preset would
collapse responsibilities; promoting all of them into the base schema would
overfit a single source.

Current Whisper already provides plausible owner surfaces: `trajectory_core`
for movement, `technique_stack` for reusable moves, `composition_plan` for
ordered parts, transport or preset contracts for genre-specific requirements,
and `readability_dynamics` plus human editorial checks for delivery and
cohesion. The research must test that preliminary mapping rather than assume it.

## P1 Trigger Decision

This is a governed research dispatch. Synthesis, isolation, context protection,
and parallelism all apply: the named PDF, external writing-framework precedent,
and current Whisper architecture require different source corpora and methods;
their returns must be reconciled and attacked before any integration direction
is credible.

## Objective And Evidence Boundary

Produce an exact-source-bound answer to two questions:

1. Which distinct writing responsibilities in the PDF and a bounded set of
   established frameworks are worth making available to Whisper?
2. Where does each surviving responsibility belong in Whisper without
   duplicating or overruling an existing owner?

The run may recommend a candidate architecture and fixtures. It may not edit
`arcanum/`, adopt or promote a framework, change a schema or validator, publish,
deploy, or claim that a technique improves real reader outcomes.

## Dependency Flow

```text
three explorers in parallel
  -> synthesizer
  <-> three reviewers in parallel (zig-zag cap 1)
  -> exact-binding evidence closure owned by parent
  -> findings writer
  -> auditor
  -> parent final approval
```

One synthesizer-to-explorer feedback edge is reserved for missing source,
selector, or ownership evidence. One auditor-to-writer feedback edge is
reserved for a bounded findings repair. The whole dispatch has `max_loops: 2`.
