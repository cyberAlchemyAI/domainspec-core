# Design Distill Validation

- Budget: Standard.
- Smallest coherent architecture: provenance gate, fresh-YAML compiler,
  digest-addressed unserved generation, complete verifier, admitted query, and
  external quarantine/rebuild evidence.
- Recomposition: all selected extensions attach to the six baseline views and
  reduce to the bounded hypothesis without adding a canonical writer, active
  pointer, rollout, migration, or publication path.
- Balancer evidence: the read-only Invoke validator helper required the exact
  deterministic Design receipts and flagged runtime-readiness gaps; those gaps
  remain outside the Design evidence state.
- Objections preserved: registry/source contradiction, native build unknowns,
  true mid-apply atomicity, multi-writer admission, power durability,
  migration, encryption lifecycle, and dependency/license closure.
- False-success checks: planned witnesses are not executed evidence; process
  crash is not power-loss proof; batch rejection is not mid-apply rollback;
  content similarity is not exact provenance.
- Verdict: `pass` for architecture completeness and recomposition.
