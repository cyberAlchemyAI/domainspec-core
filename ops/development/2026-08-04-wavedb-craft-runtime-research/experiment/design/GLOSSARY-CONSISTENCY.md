# Glossary Consistency

- Define glossary compared: `../define/GLOSSARY.md`
- Canonical Craft terms compared: `arcanum/arcana/craft/SKILL.md`
- Result: `pass`

The Design uses `canonical YAML`, `projection generation`, `closed manifest`,
`fresh admission`, `zero-output failure`, `quarantine`, `rebuild`, `exact
provenance`, `observation receipt`, and `candidate-local` with the same meanings
as Define. `authority`, `receipt`, `validation`, `promotion`, and `deployment`
retain their owner contracts. No Invoke-local term is promoted to canonical
Arcanum vocabulary.

`generation`, `manifest`, and `quarantine` describe the candidate adapter only;
they do not change Craft's canonical storage or lifecycle model.
