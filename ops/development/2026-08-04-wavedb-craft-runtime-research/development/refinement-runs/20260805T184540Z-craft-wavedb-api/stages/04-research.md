# Stage 04 — Research Decision

## Decision

No external research is triggered.

The current question is architectural and contract-bound. Local evidence is
sufficient to select a safe first unit and identify what remains unproven. The
missing `0.1` adapter fixture is implementation input, not a question that web
research can safely answer for this repository. WaveDB limitations are already
captured by direct runnable receipts and outrank general external descriptions.

## Evidence precedence

1. Craft canonical schemas own source and row semantics.
2. The successful diagnostic receipt proves only its exact snapshot route.
3. Failed diagnostic receipts constrain the adapter.
4. README/package claims may inform future work but cannot override local
   observed behavior.

## Research trigger retained

External research would require a named design gap whose answer cannot be
obtained from local source or fixtures, plus separate operator confirmation.
None exists at this stage.

## Verdict

`pass`; continue to Distill using local evidence only.
