# Implementation layering

## Purpose

Prove a fresh public-safe resolver at the narrowest boundary, then admit
canonical source, generated runtime parity, consumers, and private composition
only when their independent owners accept the evidence.

## Layer decision table

| Layer | Decision unlocked | Minimum working unit | Included | Explicitly deferred | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 owner gate | May a public-safe candidate be authored, and under which API/license/owner bindings? | one durable no-code decision record using `.arcanum/profiles/decision-profile.yml` | extraction boundary, canonical name/API/license/compatibility, catalog/normalization/ACI/disposable-consumer owners | all source mutation | signed/digest-bound decision receipt or blocker |
| L1 candidate sigil | Does a sanitized project-agnostic closure reproduce the declared relation safely? | candidate-local sigil support closure outside `arcanum/**` | schemas, core, JSON port, manifest, synthetic fixtures, scans, core/browser witness | canonical mutation, generation, consumer install | frozen core/public-check cases and real-browser portable receipt |
| L2 canonical and profiles | Can accepted source generate behaviorally equal Claude/Codex packages and roll back? | explicit canonical acceptance plus isolated two-profile generation | public Arcanum source, selective Bootstrap, parity, disable, rollback | real consumer/product/private integration | public owner receipt plus wrapper matrices and exact rollback digest |
| L3 consumer composition | Does one runtime consume advice without taking or bypassing authority? | disposable consumer then independently owned plan refresh/adapters | generic install, ResonantOS plan refresh, orchestrator/Augmentor contracts | private ACI admission and rollout | install/rollback, zero-authority adapter, consumer-owner receipts |
| L4 private admission | Does the generic entry boundary compose with CAV2/ACI without moving private authority public? | private compatibility adapter and content-addressed ACI references | private conformance and promotion gate | whole-engine, release, production claims | private authority receipt and negative boundary tests |

## Active layer window

- Selected: L0 only.
- First unit: `SWU-IRO-001`.
- Stop: after a durable pass/block decision receipt and Invoke Refresh.
- Next owner on pass: `sigil-development` for L1 candidate staging.

## Non-regression guardrails

- `authority_effect: none` is invariant at every layer.
- Generated packages remain projections of one canonical closure.
- Consumer adapters never repair, infer, rank, select, authorize, persist
  independently, execute, or claim effects.
- A candidate never becomes a confirmed route without an existing canonical
  Dispatch/group/seat.
- Private concepts, fixtures, imports, and authority do not enter public source.
- Passing browser cases proves portable execution only.
