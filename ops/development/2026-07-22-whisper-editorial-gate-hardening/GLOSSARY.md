# Working Glossary

These terms are local to this work pack. They do not add canonical Arcanum definitions.

| Term | Meaning here | Not the same as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| evidence axis | One independently recorded kind of proof: source/structure, editorial language, comprehension, or implementation/render. | A score whose successes can compensate for another missing axis. |
| structural validation | A deterministic check of format, required fields, provenance, configured phrases, duplicate blocks, or schema constraints. | Proof that people understand or prefer the language. |
| editorial language audition | Human review of a bounded sample or composed surface for meaning order, voice, audience fit, and avoidable duplication. | Transport promotion or browser validation. |
| composed surface | The ordered group of audience-visible blocks that a reader encounters together and that must be reviewed as one unit. | A single replacement sentence removed from its neighbors. |
| first surface | The transport-defined opening role set that teaches the human situation before machine details. It may be source-order based; it does not claim exact viewport geometry without render evidence. | Merely the first DOM node or first sentence. |
| surface manifest | A machine-readable ordered list of reviewed blocks, source selectors, roles, and content digests. | The rendered artifact itself. |
| comprehension record | A human-provided result for required questions, bound to the reviewed surface digest. | Machine semantic scoring. |
| explicit approval binding | An approval record that names its kind, target transport/audience, artifact digest, surface digest, prompt, and approver. | Ambient words such as `confirmed` or approval of a different plan. |
| intent state | `forming`, `volatile`, or `frozen`, controlling which derivative work is allowed. | General project status. |
| derivative | A full audience-facing output produced from a composition plan, such as HTML, slides, video, or a review projection. | The small audition or intent substrate. |
| generation permission | The pre-generation decision that says whether a full derivative may be produced. | Final editorial readiness. |
| status ceiling | The highest `draft_status` allowed by the applicable evidence and transport gates. | A suggested status the producer may override upward. |
| candidate transport | A transport contract available for trials but not yet backed by promotion evidence. | A proven transport. |
| post-apply editorial verification | A separate review of the composed artifact after the approved change has been applied. | Reuse of the pre-apply phrase audition. |
| correction event | An append-only record that a prior result failed operator or reader review, including whether meaning cores changed and which prior receipt is invalidated. | Silent replacement of old telemetry. |
| false-positive readiness | A prior `pass` or readiness claim later rejected for the same target surface. | An ordinary improvement request after an accepted result. |
| retell chain | A validation prompt that asks someone to restate a mechanism or lifecycle. Rendering it is exceptional and must have a distinct audience job. | A second automatic explanation section. |
| surface delta budget | A manifest rule requiring each added audience block to replace/consolidate an existing block or declare a distinct necessary job. | A word-count quota or proof of semantic non-duplication. |
| run receipt | The typed evidence envelope for one Whisper composition run. | Lifecycle promotion evidence by itself. |
| local preset | Repository-owned Whisper configuration under `.arcanum/spells/whisper/presets/`. | Public canonical Whisper content or generated native skill text. |
