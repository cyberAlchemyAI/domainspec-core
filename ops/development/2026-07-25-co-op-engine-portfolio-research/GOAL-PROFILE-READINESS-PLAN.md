# Co-Op Engine Browser Artifact — Goal-Profile Readiness Plan

## Status and Authority

- Status: evidence-backed readiness roadmap
- Current lifecycle state: research accepted; Invoke Define not yet run
- Authority: planning handoff only
- Source of research claims: [findings.md](./findings.md)
- Not established: approved specification, validated design, executable work-pack, selected SWU, implementation, audit pass, public copy, publication, or runtime readiness

This document plans how to earn a runnable Codex Goal Profile. It is not an
Invoke Plan artifact and does not authorize implementation.

## Outcome

Produce one browser artifact for the Co-Op Engine weekly meeting that:

1. works as a self-guided recruitment and explanation page;
2. works as the facilitated presentation for the 45-minute meeting;
3. uses one structured truth record for both projections;
4. explains all fourteen projects within their verified claim ceilings;
5. helps a participant move from inspection to a reciprocal seven-day
   appointment and later receipt without calling unilateral interest a match;
6. serves technical builders without internal-framework context and domain
   practitioners—such as writers and musicians—who want to build with AI while
   retaining their professional judgment;
7. passes black-box browser, conversational-language/copy, ease/coherence, and
   privacy-boundary acceptance gates before public use.

The browser artifact is the page and the presentation. Presentation mode may
change pacing and disclosure, but not facts, sources, maturity, claim ceilings,
or state.

## Fixed Product Baseline

The accepted research fixes these constraints for Define:

- **Public event name:** Co-Op Engine may remain the meeting proper name.
- **Public mechanism wording:** proposed weekly Experiment Exchange or session
  and record; no autonomous-matching interpretation.
- **Primary mechanism:** experiment and capacity exchange, not a portfolio
  showcase.
- **Portfolio role:** all fourteen projects remain browsable as orientation and
  recruitment context.
- **Live attention:** clinics are evidence-triggered, not selected by maturity,
  popularity, or curator preference.
- **Valid progress:** energy mark, candidate pair, reciprocal appointment, and
  terminal receipt are different states.
- **Whisper product candidate:** Writer Story-State Studio is build-from-owned,
  not a current feature or shipped product.
- **Internal explanatory lineage:** the Veritasium-inspired
  misconception-to-reveal movement is an internal candidate preset only.
  Public branding, endorsement, or imitation claims are forbidden.
- **Forbidden umbrella language:** AI matcher, autonomous matching, finished
  marketplace, proven recruitment system, AI collaborator, generic knowledge
  graph, and agentic writer studio.

## Why a Runnable Goal Is Blocked

The Codex Goal Profile contract requires a selected task or SWU, bounded write
scope, concrete done criteria, an available verification surface, and a strict
runtime handoff pack in both Markdown and JSON/index form. None can be admitted
before the product definition, design selectors, acceptance ownership, stack,
and work-pack are approved.

The lifecycle therefore remains:

`research → Invoke Define → blocker decisions → Invoke Design → acceptance-contract review → Invoke Plan + Distill → select one SWU → Context Builder runtime handoff pack → Codex Goal Profile → bounded execution`

Public copy remains later:

`bounded implementation → witnessed audits → Whisper composition/revision → publication decision`

### Gate 3 — Acceptance-Contract Review

The following are specifications until executable witnesses exist.

#### Black-box browser contract

Observe only public routes, DOM, accessibility tree, interaction, history,
network, storage, screenshots, and rendered behavior. Cover:

- desktop and mobile;
- keyboard traversal, focus, Escape, back, refresh, and direct links;
- self-guided and facilitated projections from the same record;
- filters, selection, appointment, and receipt transitions;
- no premature `matched` state;
- presenter/private data absent from disallowed surfaces;
- console/network cleanliness;
- semantic headings, landmarks, names, announcements, dialog focus, contrast,
  and reduced motion;
- known-good, known-bad, and false-positive Poker and Writer fixtures.

Visual similarity cannot pass when DOM state, network state, keyboard behavior,
or privacy projection disagrees.

#### Conversational-language and copy contract

Create an adjudicated corpus across the fourteen project voices before setting
thresholds. Automated checks flag:

- canned assistant transitions;
- praise, reassurance, and fake intimacy;
- meta-narration about what the page, presentation, section, filter, or
  interaction is doing—for example, “this page will guide you,” “in this
  section we explore,” or “the next slide shows”—when the interface or content
  can communicate that substance directly;
- answer-bearing questions and repeated `you can`;
- anthropomorphic understanding, intention, identity, or partnership;
- unearned agentic, intelligent, copilot, knowledge graph, or AI collaborator
  wording;
- unsupported superlatives;
- future work described as current;
- hidden claim ceilings;
- template repetition that erases domain voice.

Human adjudication decides whether warmth is attached to a real invitation,
questions are genuine, human judgment remains explicit, capability and limit
are concrete, and unnamed cards remain distinguishable. Necessary orientation,
state, accessibility, and recovery instructions are not meta-narration: they
remain when they help a person decide or act and cannot be carried reliably by
structure, labels, or controls alone.

#### Ease and coherence contract

Automated proxies check first-use bridges, heading and paragraph structure,
problem/witness/limitation/ask/action presence, reference clarity,
status/source consistency, mobile meaning, CTA preservation, and cross-mode
claim consistency.

Cold readers then test understanding:

- technical builder: `5/5 facts + correct transfer + zero forbidden claims +
  zero facilitator repairs + zero leakage`;
- writer or musician: `5/5 facts + correct transfer + zero forbidden claims +
  zero facilitator repairs + zero leakage`.

Readability scores, screenshots, navigation success, and facilitator rescue do
not substitute for retellability and transfer.

**Gate to Plan:** every acceptance obligation has an owner, fixture, evidence
shape, and planned verification surface.

### Gate 6 — Runtime Handoff Pack

After one SWU is selected, run Context Builder in strict runtime-handoff mode.
Persist, as session evidence rather than canonical planning truth:

- a Markdown handoff pack;
- a structured JSON/index handoff pack;
- obligation-to-source coverage;
- exact write scope;
- exact validation command or reviewable evidence;
- named uncovered gaps, if any.

Any uncovered, contradictory, stale, unsafe, write-scope-missing, or
validation-missing obligation blocks the runtime handoff.

### Gate 7 — Codex Goal Profile

Only then run `codex-goal-profile` for the selected SWU.

The profile must contain:

- outcome;
- verification surface;
- constraints;
- boundaries;
- iteration policy;
- blocked stop condition;
- work-pack and SWU links;
- Markdown and JSON/index handoff-pack links;
- pack-first execution;
- named-gap-only fallback exploration;
- required reporting for every extra source.

The goal is complete only when the selected SWU's acceptance evidence passes
and its Task Session closeout synchronization contract is satisfied. It must
not claim the whole browser artifact is complete.

## Overall Completion Evidence

The full artifact is eligible for publication review only when:

- one deployed browser artifact supports self-guided and facilitated use from
  one admitted truth model;
- all fourteen cards remain inside their source-backed ceilings;
- appointment and receipt transitions are witnessed without premature match
  language;
- presenter/private data does not leak through any tested surface;
- black-box browser tests pass on the agreed browser/device matrix;
- conversational-language/copy audit passes its adjudicated corpus;
- both audience profiles pass the exact ease/coherence threshold;
- Writer Story-State Mira/Ivo and hooded-traveler fixtures pass without silent
  canon mutation or spoiler leakage;
- Whisper's operator audition and owner review pass;
- the publication owner approves names, citations, and non-endorsement
  boundaries.

Meeting attendance, screenshots, green unit tests, or polished copy alone do
not establish this outcome.
