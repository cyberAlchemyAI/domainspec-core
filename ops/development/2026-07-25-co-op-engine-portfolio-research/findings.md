## Goal

Produce an evidence-bounded product, recruitment, and explanatory substrate for a single browser-native Co-Op Engine artifact that works self-guided and live, explains all named projects honestly, and supports a 45-minute weekly collaboration session without treating presentation, interest, or polish as collaboration evidence.

## TL;DR

The executive decision is to stop treating the portfolio showcase as the primary mechanism. Promote for Define consideration a **proposed weekly Experiment Exchange with evidence-triggered clinics**: all fourteen projects remain self-guided; live attention follows eligible cards and participant signals; a valid outcome requires reciprocal appointment and a next-week receipt. “Co-Op Engine” is the event proper name, not a claim of autonomous matching or an implemented engine.

This is cited research substrate, not final public copy and not a witnessed product. The composite is build-from-owned: its components have owners and precedents, but their combination is unvalidated. Current KILLs remain visible: public `agentic writer studio`; generic `knowledge graph` or unresolved `Epistemic Graph`; `AI collaborator`; public `Agentic Music Studio` absent a delegated musical-behavior witness; and any autonomous-matching meaning for Co-Op Engine.

## Context

Fourteen unevenly mature projects must be understandable to technical builders without internal-framework context and to domain practitioners—writers, musicians, runners, designers, and BI practitioners—who want to build with AI without being reduced to prompt users. A page-plus-deck split would create truth drift, while a dense page projected wholesale would fail in the room. The dispatch therefore tested repository truth against participatory service design (Lane Z), then tested the proposed showcase against external precedents and a solution-independent feedback-loop redesign (Lane A).

Agent 1 established project-by-project claim ceilings and accepted Agent 2’s correction that fourteen pitches cannot fit the meeting ([research, Agent 1 §§1–4](./research.md#agent-1--repository-truth-audit-and-project-cards)). Agent 2 made observable acts, domain breadth, and separate projections decision-bearing ([research, Agent 2 §§1–3](./research.md#agent-2--participatory-service-and-meeting-design)). Agent 3 found owned mechanism precedents but no precedent for this composite ([research, Agent 3 §§1–4](./research.md#agent-3--external-precedent-and-jobs-to-be-done-comparison)). Agent 4 turned discovery into an explicit state machine whose outcome is a receipt, not a view, energy mark, clinic, or appointment ([research, Agent 4 §§1–5](./research.md#agent-4--systems-reframing-and-feedback-loop-design)).

## Executive Product Decision

**Solution-independent problem:** capable makers with unevenly mature projects and useful skills lack a low-risk way to inspect a concrete opportunity, decide whether a contribution fits, make a bilateral seven-day appointment, and return evidence of what happened.

**Decision:** advance an expiring **Experiment Exchange with evidence-triggered clinics** into Invoke Define. The opportunity/experiment—not the project, presenter, or maturity rank—is the unit of recruitment. All fourteen projects are available asynchronously. A clinic is justified only by exchange evidence such as unresolved complementarity, cross-domain translation, access/privacy negotiation, or disagreement about the success test. Maturity limits claims; it does not allocate airtime.

The service chain is:

`eligible experiment → energy mark → optional clinic → candidate pair → bilateral appointment → ran|not_started|blocked|declined|revised|stopped receipt → keep|revise|close`

No shortcut may label a unilateral offer “matched.” An appointment witnesses reciprocal intent, not action. A run receipt witnesses an action under stated boundaries, not market demand, general efficacy, durable recruitment, or the validity of the full Co-Op mechanism.

## Shared Human/Machine Record and State

One structured record drives both self-guided and facilitated projections:

```text
project: id, display_name, domain, project_voice, canonical_root,
  authority_status, owner, verification_date, expiry, maturity_class
truth: problem, for_whom, what_exists, witness[], mapping_limit,
  claim_ceiling, forbidden_claims[], source_selectors[]
explanation: resonance, relevance, trajectory, observable_act,
  capability, limitation, human_judgment, term_bridges[]
experiment: id, uncertainty, contribution_options[], skill_ask,
  recruitment_ask, effort_ceiling, stop_rule, expected_witness,
  owner_response_sla, check_in
capacity: participant, offer, evidence, availability, learning_interest,
  privacy_limit, modes[]
surfaces: audience_visible, speaker_notes, interaction_prompts,
  authoring_metadata
state: eligibility, energy_marks[], candidate_pairs[],
  bilateral_appointments[], receipts[]
visibility: public, meeting_visible, assigned_collaborator,
  participant_private, owner_private
```

Required singular fields are project ID/display name/owner/authority/maturity/problem, claim ceiling, current witness, experiment uncertainty, owner SLA, expiry, privacy class, and check-in. Sources, forbidden claims, contribution options, term bridges, and expected witnesses require at least one entry unless explicitly blocked. An eligible experiment has exactly one accountable owner and one or more contribution options. An appointment requires reciprocal confirmation by at least two parties and one receipt date.

Valid transitions are:

`draft→eligible→energy_marked→clinic_selected?→candidate_pair→bilateral_appointment→ran|not_started|blocked|declined|revised|stopped→keep|revise|close`

Expiry moves a non-terminal record to `stale`; refresh returns it to `draft`. `energy_marked→matched` and unilateral `candidate_pair→appointment` are invalid and fail closed.

Surface ownership remains separate even inside one artifact:

- **Audience-visible:** problem, witness, limit, observable act, bounded ask, safe source links.
- **Facilitated meeting:** room roles, energy marks, clinic queue, appointment read-back.
- **Presenter notes:** pacing, evidence boundary, transition cue, operator warnings; never audience DOM leakage.
- **Interaction:** filters, decisions, negotiation, reciprocal confirmation, receipt capture.
- **Authoring metadata:** authority, source selectors/digests, verification, expiry, forbidden claims.
- **Privacy:** field-level public, meeting-visible, assigned-collaborator, participant-private, and owner-private projections, preserved across routes, DOM, APIs, exports, screenshots, and traces.

Modes change pacing and disclosure, never truth, maturity, source binding, mapping limit, or ceiling.

## Self-Guided and Live Routes

**Self-guided recruitment route:** orient to the decision → filter eligible experiments by domain, contribution, time, maturity, and boundary → open problem/witness/limitation/act/ask → choose `join`, `offer`, `follow/refer`, or `decline` → negotiate availability, privacy, reuse, and effort → candidate pair → reciprocal appointment → seven-day receipt. Early/domain-led cards are included when eligible; maturity does not suppress them.

**Forty-five-minute live route:**

- **0–5:** orient to “Which seven-day experiment, if any, will you join or help?”
- **5–12:** silent scan of all eligible cards; mark `can help`, `want to learn`, `need clarification`, or `refer`.
- **12–29:** two or three evidence-triggered clinics covering problem, witness/limit, observable act, contribution, and smallest experiment.
- **29–39:** negotiate bilateral who/what/when/where/how, access/privacy, effort ceiling, stop rule, and receipt.
- **39–45:** reciprocal confirmation and receipt-date read-back; unresolved interest becomes follow/referral.

Clinics are triggered by evidence, never curator preference or maturity rank. All fourteen projects remain self-guided even when they receive no clinic.

**Genuine alternative—Clinic-only Cohort:** for a small recurring high-trust room or scarce eligible-card supply, a facilitator selects two or three already eligible experiments; the artifact is pre-read, live controller, and post-record, with no open exchange. This sacrifices asynchronous discovery and can privilege live attendees, so it is a comparison condition, not a renamed Exchange.

## Whisper Explanatory and Voice Contract

The internal Veritasium lineage supplies a candidate movement, not audience-visible branding:

`misconception → reveal → concrete instrument → honesty boundary → zoom-out → ask → offer → scope → reciprocal appointment → receipt`

The misconception hypothesis is: “This is a gallery of finished AI products, and collaboration means joining one.” It remains a hypothesis until audience evidence admits it. The reveal is that claim ceilings differ and the collaboration unit is an observable act plus a falsifiable seven-day experiment. The instrument is project-specific: play a hand, annotate UI, operate a bounded workbench route, retell an explainer, trace a metric, author a musical rule, or map a run. The honesty boundary is that an act may expose mechanics, understanding, or friction; it does not establish market demand, generalized efficacy, readiness, or composite validity. The zoom-out is that inspectable acts and owner-routed receipts let a mixed portfolio learn without pretending each project is a product.

Whisper’s operator audition still gates the opening, tension, and reveal. Internal lineage never substitutes for the three-moment voice check.

Two audience profiles govern explanation:

1. **Technical builder without internal-framework context:** needs an inspectable mechanism, interface/boundary, current witness, mapping limit, and exact build/test ask.
2. **Domain practitioner building with AI:** needs a recognizable craft act, retained human judgment, concrete capability and limit, and a contribution route that does not reduce expertise to prompting.

Terms are earned on first use through concrete need. Meeting vocabulary—Experiment Exchange, energy mark, candidate pair, bilateral appointment, receipt, claim ceiling, and stop rule—must receive plain bridges. Preserve project voice: Poker uses hands, seats, hidden information, and replay; TROQAR uses routes, zones, safety, and fair play; Music uses rules, gesture, scene, and listening; writing uses passages, canon, character knowledge, and spoilers. A uniform “governed AI” template fails.

Conversational warmth is permitted when attached to a real invitation. Forbidden residue includes assistant coaching, canned transitions, praise/reassurance, fake intimacy, meta-narration, answer-bearing questions, repeated “you can,” anthropomorphic understanding/intention/partnership, and unearned “agentic,” “intelligent,” “copilot,” “knowledge graph,” or “AI collaborator.”

### Copy and chat-language audit

Build an adjudicated corpus across all project voices with accepted, rejected, and borderline examples before setting thresholds. Automated rules flag the forbidden residue above, unsupported superlatives, future-as-current, hidden ceilings, and repeated templates that erase domain voice. Human adjudication asks whether warmth is attached to a real invitation, capability/limit and retained judgment are concrete, questions are genuine, and unnamed cards remain distinguishable. Do not ban first/second person or warmth categorically.

### Ease and coherence audit

Automatable proxies cover first-use bridges, sentence/paragraph and heading structure, presence of problem/witness/limitation/ask/action, reference clarity, status/source consistency, mobile meaning/CTA preservation, and absence of cross-mode claim conflict. These proxies cannot establish understanding.

Each profile receives only the rendered candidate, with no source inspection or facilitator:

- **Technical builder:** retells candidate versus admitted canon, exact provenance, temporal/as-of scope, privacy/visibility boundary, and what a receipt proves. Transfer: explain why `character_met_character` does not entail identity knowledge and what evidence admits `character_knows_fact`.
- **Writer or musician:** retells scene, location, encounter, author admission authority, and spoiler boundary. Transfer: given a flashback that changes encounter order, choose `accept|edit|reject` and explain the effect without future-state leakage.

The exact pass threshold for each profile is `5/5 facts + correct transfer + zero forbidden claims + zero facilitator repairs + zero spoiler leakage`. Record misconceptions and interventions. Readability, navigation, screenshot quality, and facilitator rescue do not substitute for retellability or transfer.

Safe umbrella claims are: “proposed weekly Experiment Exchange,” “one browser artifact with self-guided and facilitated projections,” “evidence-triggered clinics,” “bounded seven-day experiment,” and each project’s specific ceiling above. “Build-from-owned” means precedents exist; it does not mean deployed, novel, effective, or public-safe.
