## Final decision

Do not kill the combined decision-first, map-recoverable, route-durable
architecture. Do not pass it either.

It remains a disciplined, hand-buildable hypothesis. The synthesis names the
right minimum classes of evidence, but it has not run the browser witness or
the two cold-reader decision test. Therefore its candidate mechanisms may move
forward only as comparison candidates, not as admitted design or evidence of
comprehension, recruitment, welcome, or superiority.

The non-vacuity questions remain:

1. What does the visitor accurately understand?
2. What do they choose next because of this surface?
3. Which material claim remains bounded after that choice?

The final gate is **REVISE** because the synthesis can answer these questions
with one small witness after the exact revisions below. `KILL` applies only to
the mechanisms that cannot change a responsible visitor decision while
preserving project truth and authority.

## Reviewer-exchange agreements

### With Popper's precedent gate

- The combined architecture and the stable-truth/volatile-welcome contract are
  local `build-from-owned` compositions. Deployed components make them
  plausible to test; no cited precedent proves the local ordering, result
  cardinality, comprehension, recruitment effect, or welcome.
- Decision lenses have deployed act/skill-entry relatives, but local labels,
  inclusion explanations, and the `1–3` result rule require local evidence.
- Presentation controls are deployed; “same governed facts under two view
  policies” is still a local contract requiring a parity witness.
- The project-index-first alternative must remain a genuine control. The
  witness must not install decision-first as the default merely because the
  prose prefers it.
- Inventory no-match and the Experiment Exchange typed negative cannot do
  non-vacuity work. Neither changes what a cold reader understands or chooses.
- Precedent never supplies local welcome. A participation precedent cannot
  populate an owner, recipient, current ask, freshness rule, or response
  capacity.

### With Quine's definitional-soundness gate

- “Map” must earn the word through corpus completeness, current position,
  direct jumps, return to prior state, and interruption recovery. Otherwise the
  specimen has an index, not a map.
- One preview is inspected at 10- and 30-second depths; these are human test
  conditions, not separately authored content species.
- The CTA ladder must be split into project-side
  `none | possibility | open ask` and visitor-side
  `inspect/save | non-binding offer | contact | human clarification |
  commitment`. Interest is private and must not become an observed rung.
- Ask and offer have different owners. A project publishes an ask; a visitor
  authors an offer. Contact proves transmission only; commitment happens
  outside the showcase after human clarification.
- Live mode must preserve stable identity and factual parity. A claim carried
  only by presenter notes or spoken transition makes a mechanically working
  page non-vacuous only as a slide deck, not as the required dual-use artifact.
- The name-substitution, sentence-deletion, and clever-phrase-removal tests are
  necessary adversarial checks. Memorable but inaccurate retelling fails.

## Reviewer-exchange disagreements and boundaries

1. **A definitional PASS is not a non-vacuity PASS.** Quine establishes that
   the combined architecture need not collapse into a renamed portfolio or
   deck. That is necessary, but an operational distinction can still change no
   real reader decision. The architecture remains `REVISE` until observed.

2. **A precedent-legible candidate is not witnessed locally.** Popper's
   conditional precedent pass establishes ownership and claim bounds, not
   outcome. Conformance to APG, History API behavior, or a reveal.js-like
   control does not raise the non-vacuity verdict.

3. **Removal is not enough for the optional reveal.** The synthesis currently
   says removing the reveal leaves orientation and truth intact. That only
   proves it is nonessential. Under my gate, a candidate reveal must improve
   correct route choice or accurate proof/limit retelling against the resolved
   static control. Otherwise kill the reveal as ornamental, even if it remains
   definitionally sound.

4. **A full map is not valuable merely because all projects are locatable.**
   It survives only if it changes recovery: after an interruption, the visitor
   can identify current position, jump deliberately, and resume without
   inferring rank, maturity, or welcome from layout.

5. **An open ask is not required for the minimum witness.** Ownership findings
   show that welcome is unbound across the selected fixture. The smallest
   honest contribution witness is therefore a `possibility` or `none` state
   with inspect/save affordances. Inventing an open ask to test recruitment
   would corrupt the test.

Each preview must state its act, present proof/maturity, one important limit,
contribution state, why it appeared under the selected lens, and one durable
detail link. If swapping two project names leaves either preview plausible,
kill and rewrite both previews.

Use one complete Poker Agent Arena detail with addressable `evidence`,
`limits`, and `contribute` sections. Use placeholders for the other eleven
projects only to prove corpus completeness in the map; placeholders do not
count as validated previews.

### Comparative entrances

Expose both without scrolling:

- `Find by what I want to do`;
- `See all projects`.

Counterbalance which entrance each cold reader uses first. Both entrances must
operate on the same records. The act lens must explain each inclusion, make no
fit claim, preserve `See all projects`, and reset reversibly. The all-project
control is not a strawman; it is the project-index-first control.

### Self-guided path

1. Open cold.
2. Choose an entrance and select one of the three previews.
3. Open Poker detail.
4. Direct-load and refresh its `limits` section.
5. Use browser Back/Forward and recover prior lens/index state, result order,
   focus target, and usable scroll position.
6. Reset to all projects.
7. Inspect the contribution state and either copy/inspect the current artifact
   or leave. Leaving is not a failed conversion and generates no “interest”
   state.

### Projected path

Use the same records:

1. portfolio opening;
2. complete map with current position;
3. the three contrasting previews;
4. Poker detail;
5. interruption and direct jump to Sonar Loop;
6. one action back to the map and one labeled action back to Poker;
7. the same Poker contribution state and durable URL.

Run one projected pass without presenter explanation. No material fact may
exist only in speech or notes. At actual room distance and browser zoom, the
name, act, proof, limit, and contribution state must remain readable.

### Browser evidence

Record separate pass/fail results for:

- direct load and refresh of project and section URLs;
- Back/Forward restoration of lens/index, result order, focus, and scroll;
- visible, operable keyboard focus and comprehensible order;
- narrow-viewport reflow without horizontal comparison dependency;
- reduced-motion and script/enhancement-loss fallback without lost truth or
  route;
- lens inclusion explanation, reset, and complete-map escape;
- shared-record parity across live and self-guided views;
- one-action map recovery and labeled direct jump;
- projection zoom/clipping and room-distance readability;
- absence of private paths, presenter-only facts, inferred welcome, generic
  `Join`, commitment, or analytics-authored “interest.”

These results prove mechanics and factual parity only.

### Human evidence

Use exactly two cold readers for the minimum witness:

- one technical builder unfamiliar with the internal framework;
- one domain practitioner, preferably a musician working with AI but not
  required to write framework code.

Do not coach or repair answers. After the opening and preview scan, ask:

1. “Which project would you inspect next, and what on this page caused that
   choice?”
2. “What exists now?”
3. “What important thing does it not yet claim?”
4. “How does it differ from one adjacent preview?”

After detail and contribution state, ask:

> “What, if anything, can you responsibly do next from this page, and what
> would still require an owner or human to confirm?”

Pass requires each reader independently to choose a route for a content-bearing
reason; retell the project's native act, present proof/maturity, and one limit;
distinguish it from an adjacent project; and avoid converting possibility into
welcome, contact into response, or contact into commitment. Different choices
are acceptable. An inaccurate retelling after perfect browser behavior fails.

## Known-bad and false-positive fixtures

### Known-bad: impressive equal-card gallery

Render fourteen equal cards with interchangeable “AI-powered” summaries, green
`Active` badges, repository links, modal details, one `Join us` button, and
auto-advancing live mode.

It must fail if either reader cannot distinguish Music Studio, Poker Arena, and
Sonar Loop by native act; treats runnable code, research, and specification as
equal maturity; infers welcome from repository visibility; loses direct
route/Back context; or cannot name a responsible next action. Visual quality is
irrelevant.

### False positive A: mechanics pass, decision fails

Use semantic headings, stable URLs, correct history, keyboard operation,
responsive layout, reduced motion, and polished live projection. Populate
valid records but label all three “working AI tools” and derive a common
`Contribute` control from each next witness.

Automation passes. The gate fails if either reader says Music Studio is usable,
Sonar Loop is implemented, or Poker accepts contributions.

### False positive B: live parity appears to pass

Render the same project fields in both modes, but let presenter notes explain
that Poker is only Kuhn and that its contribution route is unbound. Browser
field parity can appear green if notes are excluded from comparison. The gate
fails because the audience's bounded decision depends on presenter-only truth.

### False positive C: cleverness improves recall, harms truth

Use a memorable reveal that causes both readers to remember Poker but retell it
as a hosted competitive arena. The reveal wins unaided name recall and loses
proof/limit accuracy. Kill it.
