## Final position

The synthesis contains a definitionally meaningful candidate, not a new name
for a portfolio. It remains meaningful only as a **local, comparative
hypothesis**:

> reversible editorial entrance → complete recoverable corpus → durable
> project truth → independently governed welcome record

Live use is a projection policy over that same structure, not another content
type. Popper is right that precedent establishes component plausibility and
obligations, not the superiority of this composition. Taleb is right that the
composition remains verbal until one small specimen shows what two different
visitors understand, choose, and refuse to overclaim. These findings do not
weaken the definitional distinction; they prevent the distinction from being
borrowed from labels alone.

The architecture is not killed. The current synthesis requires finite
terminology and verdict revisions before it can govern the writer. In
particular, “opening,” “map,” “interest,” “ask,” and “live mode” still admit
reskinned readings, and the candidate matrix does not yet preserve the three
reviewer gates without category leakage.

## Agreements from the reviewer exchange

1. The combined ordering is `build-from-owned`; no cited owner establishes
   local comprehension, recruitment, welcome, the 1–3 result count, or
   decision-first superiority.
2. Decision lenses borrow a deployed family but their labels and inclusion
   rules are local editorial claims. “Match,” “fit,” and “recommendation” are
   forbidden unless a different product and evidence base are admitted.
3. reveal.js owns presentation controls, not this artifact's factual identity,
   route semantics, or comprehension.
4. WAI patterns specify operability; they do not themselves call a dialog or
   accordion a suitable content container. The local durable-route and
   recovery obligations do that work.
5. Recruitment precedent cannot populate a local welcome record. A visible
   repository, possible task, or precedent for volunteering is not owner
   evidence that this project wants help now.
6. Project-index-first must remain a genuine control. Calling
   decision-first “recommended” is premature before the local comparison.

### With Taleb

1. “Different from a portfolio” must be witnessed through changed

2. The minimum specimen should fix three deliberately unlike previews:
   Agentic Music Studio, Poker Agent Arena, and Sonar Loop; one full Poker

   marketing fields.
6. Generic `Join`, truth-bearing fragments, route-hostile long dialogs, and

## Disagreements and reconciliations

1. **Precedent does not emit KILL, but this gate does.** Popper correctly
   reclassifies generic `Join` as an unsupported local claim under his gate. I
   retain KILL under definitional soundness because `Join` collapses offer,
   contact, acceptance, and commitment into one undefined verb.
2. **A reveal does not earn architectural status from memorability.** I agree
   with Taleb that a promoted reveal mechanism must improve a bounded decision
   or accurate retelling against a resolved-static control. If it does not, KILL
   it as an architectural candidate. A removable, statically resolved contrast
   may still survive as sentence-level craft under the “clever” test; it must
   not be described as a navigation or comprehension mechanism.
3. **The three-project witness does not validate the fourteen-project
   corpus.** It can establish that the grammar is non-vacuous for three unlike
   cases. The remaining eleven records stay placeholders until separately
   admitted and composed; they cannot inherit validation from the specimen.
4. **“Map” is not guaranteed by completeness.** Taleb's recovery path is
   necessary, but the term also requires visible location or traversal
   context. A complete list with links is an index. Use the weaker, accurate
   name unless current position, return context, and interruption recovery are
   actually represented.

## Repaired terms that must govern synthesis and writing

| Term | Final definition | Proof/claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Portfolio opening** | One collection-level tension plus simultaneous, visible exits to a reversible editorial lens and the complete corpus. | It claims neither personal fit nor a hidden reveal. If reusable unchanged for an unrelated AI gallery, reject it. |
| **Project opening** | The first project-specific statement of felt friction, native verb-object act, current maturity, and adjacent limit. | It is not a slogan, preview synonym, assistant coaching, or miniature specification. |
| **Preview** | One bounded comparison object containing recognizable act, current artifact/status, important limit, contribution state, inclusion reason when lensed, and a durable detail link. | “10 seconds” and “30 seconds” are comprehension depths over one preview hierarchy, not separate authored objects or guaranteed reading times. |
| **Detail** | The canonical addressable project account that expands mechanism, current artifacts, dated proof, limits, authority conflicts, trajectory, distinctions, and conditional welcome evidence. | Depth means inspectability, not length. A repository dump or animated specification is not detail. |
| **Corpus index** | A complete, directly navigable representation of admitted projects. | It makes no stronger orientation claim than completeness and addressability. |
| **Project map** | A corpus index that additionally represents current position or lens context, direct jumps, return state, and interruption recovery. | If those properties are absent, call it an index. Spatial card arrangement alone is not a map. |
| **Decision lens** | A reversible editorial projection whose inclusions are traceable to exact project language and which keeps the complete corpus one action away. | It is not matching, recommendation, ranking, assessment, or canonical taxonomy. Result count is a test parameter, not a fact. |
| **Live mode** | A reversible projection-density and traversal policy over the same canonical routes and records. | It may alter size, density, order, and controls only. No live-only fact, spoken truth dependency, second document, or presenter authority. |
| **Contribution state** | Project-side `none`, `possibility`, or `open ask`. | `Possibility` names useful conceivable work but provides no invitation. `Open ask` requires current owner, artifact, scope, recipient, public route, freshness/expiry, visibility, and supportable response expectation. |
| **Interest** | A visitor's private or weakly expressed inclination to continue considering a project. | The page can preserve attention through inspect/save/follow affordances but cannot infer or certify interest from clicks. Interest is not a workflow state. |
| **Offer** | A visitor-authored, non-binding statement that they may contribute a named act or artifact through an authorized route. | It is not the owner's ask and does not imply receipt, fit, acceptance, or commitment. |
| **Contact** | A transmitted message to a bound recipient through a disclosed route and visibility policy. | It proves outreach only, not response or welcome. |
| **Commitment** | A mutually confirmed bounded agreement after human clarification of scope, ownership, artifact, review, and next action. | It occurs outside the showcase and cannot be inferred or emitted by it. |
| **Brief** | Every retained sentence changes recognition, comparison, trust, or responsible next action. | Shortness may not remove maturity, proof, or limit. Delete a sentence if its removal changes no such decision. |
| **Substantial** | A cold reader can accurately retell concrete act, present reality, important limit, and distinction from an adjacent project. | Word count and detail density are not substitutes for this retell. |
| **Clever** | Optional precision, contrast, or rhythm that makes a true project-owned act memorable without adding or hiding a claim. | Remove it and retest. If accuracy or navigation depends on it, it was concealment; if memorability rises while retell accuracy falls, reject it. |

KILL would exceed the gate. Revision is mandatory because the present
synthesis can still be implemented under ordinary meanings of “map,”

writer treats the synthesis as governing substrate.
