# INITIAL REVIEWER POSITION — Taleb, Nassim Nicholas

Dispatch: `2026-07-26-co-op-project-showcase-recruitment-research`  
Role: skeptic  
Gate: **non-vacuity only**  
Position: initial, before reviewer exchange

## Gate decision

The synthesis has a hand-buildable core, but no architecture candidate passes
yet. It describes the right witness class without fixing a sufficiently exact
decision-changing specimen.

My gate asks only:

1. What does a visitor understand?
2. What do they choose next?
3. Which claim remains bounded after that choice?

A screenshot, valid schema, complete content record, successful click path, or
polished presentation cannot answer those questions. They can prove that a
surface rendered and behaved, not that it helped either target visitor make a
sound decision.

`KILL_CANDIDATE` is reserved here for a mechanism that cannot produce a
concrete decision-changing witness. `CHALLENGE` means the mechanism may survive
if the exact witness below passes. I make no precedent or definitional verdict.

## Smallest admissible witness

Hand-build one static-data browser specimen. Do not build all fourteen detail
pages, matching, intake, accounts, or a presentation framework.

### Fixed content

- One landing opening: one concrete sentence that earns the choice between
  `Find by what I want to do` and `See all projects`. Both controls are visible
  without scrolling and neither claims personal fit.
- Three previews whose nouns, acts, evidence, and limits cannot be swapped:
  - **Agentic Music Studio** — edit a shared musical specification; research
    tower exists; no working studio or validated perception claim.
  - **Poker Agent Arena** — submit and inspect one constrained poker decision;
    runnable Kuhn CLI exists; no Hold'em, hosting, money, or final leaderboard.
  - **Sonar Loop** — inspect or challenge one governed quality-loop rule;
    docs-first specification exists; no implementation-completeness claim.
- Each preview visibly states: recognizable act, current artifact/status, one
  important limit, contribution state, and `View project`.
- One full detail route for **Poker Agent Arena**, with addressable `evidence`,
  `limits`, and `contribute` sections.
- One honest contribution state. Because the synthesis reports welcome as
  unbound, use `contribution possibility`, not an open volunteer CTA: “A
  Leduc/reveal fixture is a possible next witness; no public contribution route
  is currently bound.” The available action is `Copy project link` or
  `Inspect the current artifact`, not `Join`.
- One persistent route back to the prior lens result and one route to all
  projects.

The witness may use placeholder records for the other eleven projects only to
show that the complete map remains one action away. Those placeholders must
not be counted as validated previews.

### Required self-guided path

1. Open the landing page cold.
2. Choose the plain-language act lens that returns all three fixtures and says
   why each appeared.
3. Open Poker Agent Arena.
4. Deep-link directly to `limits`; refresh there.
5. use browser Back and recover the prior lens, result order, focus target, and
   readable position;
6. reset to all projects;
7. inspect the honest contribution state and choose either to inspect/copy the
   link or to leave without being treated as a failed conversion.

### Required projected path

Use the same records in a low-density live view:

1. opening;
2. complete map;
3. the three contrasting previews;
4. Poker detail;
5. interruption: jump to Sonar Loop;
6. return to map, then back to Poker;
7. end on the same unbound contribution state and durable Poker URL.

No factual sentence may exist only in presenter notes. At the room's actual
browser zoom and projector size, a person at the back must be able to read the
project name, concrete act, present proof, important limit, and contribution
state.

## Observable black-box checks

Record pass/fail evidence for:

- both landing exits work without presenter explanation;
- each preview remains distinguishable with images, color, animation, and card
  borders disabled;
- lens result explains its inclusion and exposes `All projects` plus reset;
- project, `evidence`, `limits`, and `contribute` URLs survive direct load and
  refresh;
- Back/Forward restores the prior lens, result order, focus, and usable scroll
  position;
- keyboard-only operation reaches every control in a comprehensible order with
  visible focus;
- narrow viewport reflows without horizontal comparison dependency;
- reduced-motion mode loses no content or route;
- live mode and self-guided mode render the same project facts and claim
  ceiling;
- live interruption recovery takes at most one action to reach the map and one
  labeled action to reach either project;
- loss of script or presentation enhancement leaves headings, links, proof,
  limit, and contribution state readable;
- no private path, presenter-only fact, generic `Join us`, inferred welcome, or
  commitment state appears.

These checks prove mechanics and factual parity only.

## Human non-vacuity check

Recruit exactly two cold readers for the minimum witness:

- one technical builder unfamiliar with the internal framework;
- one domain practitioner, preferably a musician for this fixture, who works
  with AI but does not need to write framework code.

Do not coach, define internal terms, or repair an answer during the task. Give
each person both opening controls in counterbalanced order.

After the 10-second opening and three-preview scan, require each person to
answer:

1. “Which project would you inspect next, and what on this page caused that
   choice?”
2. “What exists now for that project?”
3. “What important thing does it *not* yet claim?”
4. “How is it different from one adjacent preview?”

After the detail and contribution state, ask the decision question:

> “What, if anything, can you responsibly do next from this page, and what
> would still require a human or owner to confirm?”

Minimum pass: each reader independently chooses a route; accurately retells
the selected project's concrete act, current proof, and one important limit;
distinguishes it from another preview; and does not turn `contribution
possibility` into welcome, acceptance, or commitment. A disagreement about
which project is most interesting is not failure. A mechanically successful
path with an inaccurate retelling is failure.

## Fixtures

### Known-bad fixture: “Fourteen impressive AI projects”

Render fourteen equal cards with interchangeable “AI-powered” summaries, green
`Active` badges, repository links, and one `Join us` button. Open details in a
modal. In live mode, auto-advance through the same cards.

Expected mechanical behavior may include successful rendering and clickable
controls. Expected gate failure:

- neither profile can distinguish Music Studio, Poker Arena, and Sonar Loop by
  domain act;
- runnable code, research, and draft specification appear equally mature;
- repository visibility is retold as public welcome;
- Back/direct-link behavior does not preserve project context;
- `Join us` gives no responsible next action or bounded recipient.

This fixture must fail even if it is visually excellent.

### False-positive fixture: “Everything passes except the decision”

Use semantic headings, stable URLs, perfect Back/Forward restoration, keyboard
operation, responsive layout, reduced-motion behavior, and a polished live
view. Populate all three previews from valid records, but label each “working
AI tool” and show a common `Contribute` control derived from its next witness.

Expected automation result: all browser checks pass. Expected human result:
one reader says Music Studio is already usable, or says Sonar Loop is
implemented, or assumes Poker accepts their contribution. The artifact
therefore fails the gate. Mechanics cannot launder a false maturity or welcome
decision.

## Initial position

The architecture candidate is worth one small falsification attempt, not a
fourteen-project build. Promotion requires the fixed witness to change a real
next choice for both audience profiles while preserving an accurate retelling
of proof, limit, and contribution authority. Until that happens, the synthesis
is a disciplined hypothesis. The optional reveal, generic CTA, and
route-hostile containers are killed unless someone supplies the concrete
decision-changing counter-witness specified above.
