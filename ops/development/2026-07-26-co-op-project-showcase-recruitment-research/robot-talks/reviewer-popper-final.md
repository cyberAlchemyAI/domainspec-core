## Final position

The synthesis is precedent-legible but not yet precedent-clean in its labels.
Current primary owners establish that cards, collection filters, durable
browser history, accessible accordions/dialogs, presentation overviews and
speaker views, contribution-by-act discovery, participation guidance, mentored
trial contributions, and responsiveness guidance are deployed mechanisms.
They do not establish that the local combination improves comprehension,
recruitment, or welcome.

The combined
`decision lens → recoverable map → durable project truth → conditional welcome`
architecture and the stable-truth/volatile-welcome record split are
`build-from-owned`. They remain legitimate candidates only because the
synthesis already proposes local comparison and falsification. No current
mechanism earns `precedent-clean/novel-attempt`; Inventory's
`no_inventory_match` is discovery residue, not an exhaustive absence result.

Taleb and Quine strengthen, rather than displace, this conclusion. Ownership
cannot rescue a mechanism that does not change a visitor decision, and deployed
precedent cannot rescue a term that collapses into a grid, deck, jobs board, or
generic gallery. Conversely, those failures are not precedent verdicts.
Precedent never emits `KILL`. It classifies what is borrowed, bounds the
analogy, and identifies which local witness remains necessary.

The old Experiment Exchange remains correctly used as a **typed negative for
this corrected goal**. It is evidence that matching, admission, reservation,
SLA, appointment, application, and automated commitment constitute a different
operational product. It is not evidence that those mechanisms are universally
bad, and it is not positive authority for the showcase.

   mode`, `recruitment ask`, `offer`, `contact`, and `commitment` require the
   operational separations Quine supplies.

   those invariants.

   project-specific.
