# Refine Run Strategy

Status: awaiting-confirmation

## P1 Trigger

A governed delegated wave is recommended because the candidate must combine
three independently falsifiable lenses: semantic schema closure, human
authoring/projection behavior, and governance/migration safety. Each specialist
must be able to reject the others' assumptions without editing shared sources.

## Group And Lanes

One parallel, read-only specialist group is proposed after the first three
inline Refine stages.

| Lane | Role | Angle | Expected output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | semantic-source-schema-architect | Closed model, canonicalization, stable identity, modular schema, and version compatibility. | Receipt with representation comparison, candidate modules, rejected alternatives, and structural witnesses. |
| S2 | projection-authoring-architect | Deterministic compiler, projection receipts, semantic diffs, correction workflow, and no-writeback behavior. | Receipt with source-to-view contract, authoring flow, drift checks, and usability failures. |
| S3 | governance-migration-adversary | Authority non-collapse, public/private boundary, legacy migration, rollback, and promotion gates. | Receipt with failure matrix, migration constraints, typed residue, and forbidden inferences. |

All three run in parallel and join with policy all. The parent writes the joined
candidate matrix only after every role is terminal, joined, closed, and
receipt-complete.

## Anti-Bias Axis And Predicted Disagreements

Primary axis: structural closure versus authoring ergonomics versus governance
safety.

- S1 versus S2: S1 may prefer the most closed representation; S2 must expose
  when that choice makes ordinary authoring, review, and semantic diffs
  impractical.
- S1 versus S3: S1 may treat a valid package as sufficient identity; S3 must
  expose where owner admission, migration, or authority evidence is missing.
- S2 versus S3: S2 may prefer convenient round-trip behavior; S3 must reject
  any path that gives a generated view source authority or bypasses gates.
