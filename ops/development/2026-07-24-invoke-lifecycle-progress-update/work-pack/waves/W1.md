# W1: Deterministic Protocol

- Layer: L1
- Goal: reject false epochs, invalid transitions, and incompatible readiness
  before capability integration.
- Entry gate: passing SWU-ILP-001 owner/source decision.
- Included: SWU-ILP-002 -> SWU-ILP-003 -> SWU-ILP-004.
- Parallelization: none by default; source paths and fixture runners are likely
  shared.
- Exit evidence:
  - epoch/control-graph validator receipt;
  - transition/supersession/churn validator receipt;
  - mode/readiness validator receipt;
  - all negative and positive controls causal.
- Stop: exact-digest weakening, role collapse, incomplete control graph, or
  source placement outside the owner decision.
