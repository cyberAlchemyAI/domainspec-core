## Gaps left for research

- The current Inventory does not provide an independent denominator of recent
  Invoke Design packages that should have selected a UX or architecture
  companion.
- It does not decide whether security, operations, data lifecycle, migration,
  content, or validation need new companion families or stronger sections in
  existing families.
- It does not prove a generic UX trigger beyond the recorded product and
  operator cases.
- It does not establish which architecture quality attributes are universally
  required versus risk-triggered.

## Strategy consequence

Use four independent explorer lenses: human experience and accessibility;
architecture quality and runtime structure; resilience, security, data, and
operations; and empirical sampling of existing Invoke Design packages. Require
the synthesis to produce a trigger matrix with `required`, `recommended`, and
`not-applicable-with-rationale` outcomes, and prefer extending owned
companions before proposing a new template family.

This packet is a discovery read model. It does not prove precedent, approve the
dispatch, select a canonical template, or authorize mutation.
