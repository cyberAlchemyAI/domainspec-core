# Arcanum Structural Governance Research Strategy

## P1 Trigger Decision

This is a governed research dispatch. It satisfies all four Principle-1 triggers:

- synthesis: the answer must reconcile definitions, constitutions, disciplines, methods, schemas, validators, lifecycle, promotion, registries, generation, installation, observability, and the already mapped build protocol;
- context protection: current public sources, private comparator boundaries, history, validators, catalogs, and generated projections are too broad for one parent context;
- isolation: structural hypotheses and recent-change diagnoses must remain discardable until independently checked;
- parallelism: constitution, discipline, and lifecycle/enforcement lanes can run independently before synthesis.

## Objective And Evidence Boundary

Produce one current, exact-bound view of Arcanum's structural governance: what each structural artifact or process means, which owner controls it, how it is added or changed, where it composes, what validates or enforces it, how it becomes available at runtime, and how it constrains the previously mapped Invoke–Task Session–Craft building protocol.

The target is public Arcanum. Private DomainSpec and cyberAlchemy-v2 sources may be used only as labelled comparator or upstream-owner evidence and may not be copied into public outputs as public doctrine. Generated packages, registries, catalogs, Inventory, ontology, telemetry, receipts, and runtime installation are bounded evidence, not semantic or promotion authority. Evidence closure is `exact-binding`: each load-bearing claim needs direct source identity, repository-relative path, source-local selector, explorer reference, evidence scope, public/private class, and observed/executed/unverified proof status.

## Structural Hypothesis To Test

The research starts with a candidate stack, not a conclusion:

1. definitions own canonical meaning;
2. constitutions own modular structural rules and invariants;
3. disciplines own recurring practices and route recommendations;
4. methods and process documents explain procedures;
5. schemas and validators provide deterministic checks;
6. lifecycle and promotion capabilities govern creation, revision, validation, canonicalization, deprecation, and retirement;
7. registry, generation, installation, and runtime packages project availability;
8. Inventory, ontology, Craft, observability, and receipts expose state or evidence without inheriting semantic authority;
9. Dispatch Spec and Orchestrate coordinate delegated execution without becoming the structural owner.

Every lane must try to falsify this taxonomy and report better owner-grounded boundaries.

## Lanes, Groups, And Subagents

### Explorers — parallel

Anti-bias axis: composite of `methodology` and `source-corpus`.

1. **Alexander, Christopher — constitutions and axioms.** Extracts current constitution namespaces, modular-law contracts, axiom relations, composition, precedence, conflict handling, versioning, validators, lifecycle, and authority boundaries.
2. **Beer, Stafford — disciplines and practices.** Maps the discipline catalog and representative cards, their meaning, relationship to methods and capabilities, and their proposal, validation, selection, composition, growth, promotion, deprecation, and retirement paths.
3. **Simon, Herbert — lifecycle and enforcement.** Traces definitions, constitutions, disciplines, sigils, spells, schemas, validators, promotion, registry, generation, installation, runtime parity, observability, reflection, and recent structural changes.

### Synthesizer — sequential after all explorers

**Noether, Emmy** joins the three returns without averaging away contradictions. The synthesis must make category boundaries, owner transitions, precedence, enforcement, public/private status, and proof ceilings explicit.

### Skeptics — parallel after synthesis

Anti-bias axis: `attack-vector`.

1. **Russell, Bertrand — precedent/owner gate.** Rejects duplicate structural layers, processes, validators, and promotion routes; found ownership means build-from-owned.
2. **Popper, Karl — non-vacuity gate.** Requires finite add/change/conflict/promotion/drift traces and exact first-stop behavior.
3. **Quine, Willard Van Orman — definitional-soundness gate.** Rejects collapse among meaning, law, practice, procedure, validation, lifecycle, projection, evidence, and authority.

### Writer And Auditor

- **Bourbaki, Nicolas** writes `findings.md` only after exact-binding evidence closure returns `ready`.
- **Brandenburg, Martin** audits `findings.md` only and may request one bounded writer revision.
- The parent remains final approver and mechanically appends complete explorer returns to `research.md`.
