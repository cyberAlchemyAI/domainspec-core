Produce one current, holistic, exact-bound view of how Arcanum turns intent into governed implementation: owners, stages, artifacts, gates, receipts, integration points, failure/continuation paths, Craft participation, and the additional skills needed for ordinary features, sigils, spells, and bounded multi-SWU execution.

The primary evidence is the live August 5 repository state, including tracked history and uncommitted canonical Arcanum changes. Generated `.agents` and `.claude` packages are parity evidence, not canonical authority. Prior Inventory entries are source leads only. The result may diagnose contradictions and propose owner-routed repairs, but it cannot edit, promote, publish, deploy, or establish readiness for any capability.

Evidence closure uses `exact-binding`: every load-bearing integration or gate claim must include immutable source identity, repository-relative path, source-local selector or JSON Pointer, explorer return reference, evidence scope, and proof status. Current dirty bytes must be distinguished from committed behavior and from generated mirrors.
