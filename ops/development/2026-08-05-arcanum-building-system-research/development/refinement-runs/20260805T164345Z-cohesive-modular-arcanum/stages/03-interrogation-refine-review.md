## Highest-discrimination question

**Could the proposed shared protocol become a second source of operational truth even if its prose calls it pure?**

No operator question was required at this stage because the approved constraints answer the safe default: the protocol may validate a supplied owner transition but may not select a unit, mint an owner receipt, write owner state, dispatch work, or choose a successor.
