# Podcast-Voice Revision Plan

Status: executed  
Intent state: frozen  
Full presentation and HTML revision: completed after explicit operator voice approval

## Correction diagnosis

The prior language is structurally clear but sounds like compressed systems documentation: nominalized headings, schema-shaped lists, declarative slogans, and too little lived narrative between ideas. The requested direction is a thoughtful solo-podcast voice: intimate, curious, plainspoken, and willing to stay with one concrete example long enough for meaning to accumulate.

## Distill frame

- Target context: a fifteen-minute live presentation for a mixed business, product, and technical audience.
- Objective: make governed ontologies meaningful enough that the audience can understand why agent memory requires typed context and separate authority.
- Output artifact for this phase: exactly three language-audition moments.
- Mode: Standard by assumption.
- Role path: inline Proposer and Balancer simulation because multi-agent dispatch was not requested.
- Budget: one proposal track, two recursive rounds, one reconciliation.

## SCU cores

### Resonance

- Desired voice: reflective podcast host, conversational intelligence, genuine curiosity, humane precision.
- Emotional residue: the audience should feel that “memory without a world around it” is both powerful and dangerous.
- Forbidden feel: corporate keynote slogans, architecture-document narration, glossary recitation, or theatrical certainty.

### Relevance

- Begin with what an agent can retrieve but still fail to understand.
- Earn formal vocabulary through one old approval record.
- Preserve the distinction between retained material, bounded context, ontology meaning, owner authority, and runtime action.

### Trajectory

One narrative thread carries the presentation:

1. An ontology is introduced as the world around remembered information.
2. An agent retrieves a genuine approval and appears to have succeeded.
3. Missing time, scope, supersession, and owner state turn retrieval into uncertainty.
4. The ontology makes those missing premises explicit without deciding authority.
5. Augmentor becomes the concrete design for bounded context and owner-retained promotion.

## Distill role trace

### Round 1

- Proposer claim: replace twelve explanation blocks with one host-led investigation of an old approval record.
- Evidence or assumption: the existing opening question and later reveal already supply a genuine narrative anchor.
- Balancer objection — source-fidelity risk: a podcast metaphor can personify the ontology or imply that it decides permission.
- Reconciliation: the ontology supplies a meaning contract; a context builder packages a task view; a named owner decides reliance; a separate runtime acts.

### Round 2

- Proposer claim: let spoken copy carry the story while projected copy holds one quiet thought.
- Evidence or assumption: the current imagery already carries system structure, so the screen does not need schema-like labels.
- Balancer objection — cognitive-load risk: conversational language can become long, repetitive, or vague.
- Reconciliation: use short paragraphs, one concrete image per beat, deliberate questions, and a stable distinction at every transition.

## Smallest coherent unit

The smallest coherent voice unit is: one concrete remembered record, surrounded progressively by the world needed to interpret and use it.

This unit recomposes into the full deck because it can introduce type, relation, time, confidence, provenance, lifecycle, authority, bounded context, and Augmentor without switching narrative anchors.

## Surface map

- Projected copy: one thought, usually one sentence.
- Spoken copy: story, pauses, questions, and meaning.
- Speaker notes: evidence, timing, and claim boundaries.
- Interaction prompt: a real decision before the reveal.
- Authoring metadata: correction and construction logic; never projected.
