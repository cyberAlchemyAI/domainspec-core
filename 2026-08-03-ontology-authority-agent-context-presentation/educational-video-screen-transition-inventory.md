## Sound palette

- **Owned source:** warm, stable, lightly textured tone.
- **Retrieval/selection:** short cobalt scan notes; functional, not futuristic spectacle.
- **Typed meaning:** precise cyan drawing tones.
- **Candidate or unresolved state:** amber interval that does not fully resolve.
- **Owner authority:** low violet tone with surrounding silence.
- **Runtime action:** distinct pulse appearing only after a conditional owner signal.
- **Forbidden transition:** motion stops and sound falls away; avoid buzzers and alarm sounds.
- **Closing:** the opening harmonic motif returns with fewer layers and ends in room tone.

## Negative animation rules

- Never animate the old approval becoming false.
- Never use green to imply current permission before the owner decision.
- Never collapse confidence, commitment, promotion, and authority into one meter.
- Never let a candidate, request, temporary index, or context packet cross the owner gate automatically.
- Never show Augmentor mutating governed meaning or producing a release state.
- Never use generic AI-brain imagery, talking-head avatars, code rain, or decorative motion unrelated to the spoken claim.
- Never hold an unchanged full image beneath a new explanatory paragraph; reframe, isolate, connect, or deliberately hold the consequence.
