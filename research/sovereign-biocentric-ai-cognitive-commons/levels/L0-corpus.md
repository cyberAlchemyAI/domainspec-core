## Corpus Shape

The corpus begins with belief shaping through logic and emotion, then moves into
empathetic productivity, surveillance risk, a human-agency manifesto, cognitive
authorship, cryptographic provenance, shared value, a cognitive commons, and
learning from negative outcomes. See [`../sources/source-map.md`](../sources/source-map.md).

## Evidence Failure Modes

1. Numeric citations restart in different assistant responses.
2. Several citations are domain roots rather than exact supporting pages.
3. Social posts and vendor materials are mixed with standards and scholarship.
4. Repeated response text can look like independent corroboration when it is not.
5. Example JSON and Python contain placeholders presented with stronger language
   than their mechanics support.
6. Metaphors such as “high mind,” “spirit,” “exoskeleton,” and “eternal” are not
   operational definitions.
7. Legal, medical, and ownership conclusions cross domains without jurisdiction,
   validation, or professional review.

## L0 Exit

The source is legible, identified by hash, segmented by voice and stage, and
bounded as a design corpus. L1 may adjudicate its claims without treating the
document as a paper or authority.
