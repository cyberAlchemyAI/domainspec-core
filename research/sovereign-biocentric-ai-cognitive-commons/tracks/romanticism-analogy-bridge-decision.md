# Bridge Decision: Romantic Literature and AI

Status: `analogy-only`

## What The Analogy Notices

Literature can organize attention, emotion, imagery, interpretation, and
story-consistent belief. Adaptive AI-mediated systems can select information,
respond to observed behavior, and change a person's information environment.

## Why Equivalence Is Blocked

Romanticism was not a single controlled framework. Historical accounts describe
varied practices involving imagination, nature, feeling, skepticism, dissent,
politics, the sublime, formal experimentation, and tension between passion and
reason. A printed poem or novel does not ordinarily observe a reader, update a
policy from clicks, or optimize a platform metric in real time.

Narrative-transportation research supports a general relationship between
absorption and story-consistent attitudes. It does not establish that Romantic
literature uniquely bypasses logic or shares a demonstrated dopamine mechanism
with recommender systems.

## Decision

- Retain the comparison as the corpus's originating question.
- Do not use Romanticism as evidence for the Sovereign Biocentric architecture.
- Do not describe Romantic literature as manipulation by default.
- Require a named work, author, passage, interpretive question, and literary
  scholarship before a future period-specific claim.
