# Bridge Decision: Sovereign Biocentric AI

Status: `borrow-carefully`

Promotion boundary: `local-only`

## Decision

Borrow the framework as a set of falsifiable design constraints, not as a legal,
clinical, economic, or technical architecture.

## Borrow Carefully

- minimize sensitive collection and prefer local processing where justified;
- preserve meaningful refusal, override, correction, and remedy;
- keep human and organizational responsibility explicit across the lifecycle;
- protect exploration, dissent, rest, and non-optimized work;
- capture contribution provenance without collapsing it into ownership;
- treat adverse outcomes as safety-learning evidence under strict access and
  retention rules;
- make attribution and benefit allocation governed and appealable.

## Keep Analogy-Only

- “Human-Spirit Exoskeleton”;
- “machine as floor, human spirit as sky”;
- high/low vibration language;
- chaos as the source of genius;
- “Sustainable Human Renaissance” equation.

## Block

- default collection of biometrics, emotion, keystrokes, or exact location;
- immutable public personal records;
- automatic truth, authorship, ownership, or value from cryptographic integrity;
- automated workforce routing by inferred mental state without necessity,
  validation, consent, safeguards, and review;
- deterministic wellbeing, productivity, or innovation claims;
- attribution or remuneration claims based on the cited ERCs.

## Promotion Decision

No promotion candidate is listed. The model needs a bounded use case, threat
model, data and decision-rights map, measures, affected-party review, and an
evidence harness before architecture or governance promotion can be considered.
