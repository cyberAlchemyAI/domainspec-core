# Local Glossary

Status: `local-research-only`

## Sovereign Biocentric AI

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: The corpus's proposed orientation in which AI protects human
  agency, privacy, health, creativity, and meaningful choice rather than
  maximizing behavioral predictability.
- Arcanum reading: A design constraint bundle, not a finished architecture.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: “Biocentric” does not establish clinical benefit, ecological
  validity, or legal compliance.
- Evidence: corpus stages S04-S07; [`sources/source-map.md`](sources/source-map.md).

## Human-Spirit Exoskeleton

- Source kind: `analogy`
- Local meaning: AI absorbs administrative and informational friction while a
  person retains high-consequence judgment and action.
- Arcanum reading: A memorable human-AI responsibility boundary.
- Promotion status: `analogy-only`
- Misuse warning: It must not imply that creativity is uniquely mystical, that
  AI cannot contribute creatively, or that automation is always low-value.
- Evidence: corpus stage S06.

## Cognitive Act

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: A bounded, contextual human contribution to sensemaking,
  creation, empathy, decision, or action that can be described and evidenced.
- Arcanum reading: A candidate contribution unit that needs provenance and
  review before recognition or reward.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: A recorded input is not automatically original, valuable,
  copyrightable, owned, or causally responsible for an outcome.
- Evidence: corpus stage S07; [`tracks/cognitive-act-definition-card.md`](tracks/cognitive-act-definition-card.md).

## Cognitive Artifact

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: A record produced during or after a Cognitive Act, such as
  text, code, a decision rationale, or a trace.
- Arcanum reading: The inspectable artifact is distinct from the human event.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: Telemetry and model weights need separate rights, consent, and
  provenance analysis; they are not automatically the creator's property.
- Evidence: corpus stage S07.

## Data Sanctuary

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: Sensitive raw data remains under the person's control with
  local processing preferred and disclosure minimized.
- Arcanum reading: A privacy architecture objective.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: Local execution does not alone supply security, informed
  consent, deletion, correctness, or freedom from employer coercion.
- Evidence: corpus stages S04 and S07; UNESCO neurotechnology recommendation.

## Creative Dissonance

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: Deliberately preserving unoptimized time, dissent, uncertainty,
  and exploration so optimization does not erase divergent paths.
- Arcanum reading: A constraint against closing the decision frontier too early.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: Disorder, overload, unsafe working conditions, and crisis are
  not inherently creative or beneficial.
- Evidence: corpus stages S05-S07.

## Agency Sovereignty

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: A person retains informed override and meaningful authority
  over decisions that materially affect them.
- Arcanum reading: Decision rights must be explicit at consequential gates.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: A nominal opt-out is not sovereign if refusal carries hidden
  penalty, surveillance, or loss of essential access.
- Evidence: corpus stages S04-S07; UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation.

## Cognitive Commons

- Source kind: `primary-source`
- Local meaning: A governed library where people or communities contribute
  contextual knowledge under explicit attribution, access, benefit, and remedy
  rules.
- Arcanum reading: A contribution-governance problem, not merely a database.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: “Commons” does not mean public-domain, unrestricted training,
  equal ownership, or automatic tokenization.
- Evidence: corpus stage S10.

## Negative Outcome Record

- Source kind: `local-inference`
- Local meaning: A consent- and safety-reviewed record of an adverse outcome,
  precursor, response, and lesson retained for prevention.
- Arcanum reading: Preserve failure evidence without converting it into blame.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: This is not “negative reinforcement,” a negative ML example,
  permanent public shaming, or proof of an individual's sole responsibility.
- Evidence: corrected reading of corpus stage S11.

## Provenance Attestation

- Source kind: `operator-reading`
- Local meaning: A signed assertion linking identified evidence and contextual
  claims to an issuer under a verification policy.
- Arcanum reading: Evidence about who asserted what, when, and under which rule.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: Signature validity does not establish truth, originality,
  legal authorship, ownership, or value.
- Evidence: W3C DID Core and VC Data Model 2.0.

## Tamper Evidence

- Source kind: `related-source`
- Local meaning: A digest or signature makes later byte changes detectable
  under specified assumptions.
- Arcanum reading: Integrity evidence for an artifact.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: It is not permanence, availability, secrecy, attribution, or
  deletion resistance.
- Evidence: IPFS CID documentation; W3C VC Data Model 2.0.

## Contribution Review

- Source kind: `local-inference`
- Local meaning: A human-governed process that evaluates evidence of source,
  originality, relevance, causal contribution, consent, and conflicts.
- Arcanum reading: The missing bridge between provenance and recognition.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: It cannot be replaced by a token mint, hash, timestamp, or
  engagement score.
- Evidence: `R7` and `R9` synthesis.

## Value Return

- Source kind: `operator-reading`
- Local meaning: A governed benefit—credit, access, money, reciprocity, or
  community resource—returned under an agreed contribution policy.
- Arcanum reading: Allocation is a policy decision with evidence and appeal.
- Promotion status: `local-only`
- Misuse warning: Economic value is not inherent in every trace and cannot be
  calculated fairly without a stated model and dispute route.
- Evidence: corpus stages S07 and S10; open residue R7/R9.
