## What To Borrow Carefully

- Human-facing AI should preserve understandable role boundaries and meaningful
  refusal or override.
- Sensitive behavioral and mental-state inference demands data minimization,
  strong consent, security, oversight, and remedy; local processing may help but
  is not a guarantee.
- Contribution records can support attribution if they remain assertions under
  a trust and review policy.
- Creative exploration and dissent should not be optimized away; neither should
  unsafe chaos be romanticized.
- A commons is legitimate only through contribution, access, consent,
  attribution, benefit, dispute, withdrawal, and harm-governance rules.
- Negative outcomes can teach when records are minimized, contextualized,
  reviewable, and protected from blame misuse.

## What To Keep Analogy-Only

- Romantic literature as a precursor image for emotion, imagination, and belief.
- Human-Spirit Exoskeleton.

- Chronotopic seal.

## What To Block

- Treating the dialogue as a peer-reviewed paper.
- Treating repeated generated prose as corroboration.
- Treating cryptographic control or integrity as truth or ownership.
- Treating a public immutable record as privacy by default.
- Treating an ERC as a training-data attribution or compulsory-payment system.
- Treating CAS as law or open source.
- Treating surveillance outcomes as deterministic.
- Treating an individual user as the only accountable AI actor.
- Treating all Romantic literature as emotional manipulation.
