# Distilled Knowledge

Status: `pass`

## One Sentence

The corpus's durable contribution is not an “eternal ownership” technology but
a demand that human-AI systems keep agency, sensitive data, contribution
recognition, collective learning, and remedy visible as separate design duties.

## Compositional Spine

```text
intent
  -> bounded contribution
  -> consented record
  -> tamper-evident provenance assertion
  -> independent validity and contribution review
  -> governed use and attribution
  -> benefit, appeal, withdrawal, and harm learning
```

## What To Borrow Carefully

1. AI should reduce external friction without silently taking meaningful
   decision rights.
2. Sensitive emotional, biometric, and inferred mental-state data deserve
   minimization, local-first processing where appropriate, and strong consent.
3. Contribution provenance is valuable when it supports review, attribution,
   dispute, and repair.
4. Unoptimized time, dissent, ambiguity, and rest can be protected design
   conditions rather than treated as inefficiency.
5. Failure evidence should be retained in a safety-governed form so systems can
   learn without shaming or exposing people.
6. A cognitive commons requires contribution, access, attribution, allocation,
   privacy, appeal, and remedy rules.

## What To Keep Analogy-Only

- The Human-Spirit Exoskeleton.
- “The machine is the floor; the human spirit is the sky.”
- Romantic literature and adaptive AI as two versions of one framework.
- “Chronotopic seal” as more than a contextual provenance metaphor.
- “Sustainable Human Renaissance” as an outcome equation.

## What To Block

- A hash, DID, VC, timestamp, token, or location automatically proving truth,
  originality, authorship, ownership, value, or causal contribution.
- The sample `Qm` plus truncated hexadecimal digest as a valid IPFS CID.
- A placeholder JWS as a digital signature.
- ERC-7007 or ERC-7857 forcing arbitrary neural networks to cite or reward
  human contributors.
- An immutable public location/biometric record as a privacy-preserving default.
- The proposed CAS text being described as an enforceable or OSI-compatible
  open-source license without legal review.
- AI surveillance being described as either guaranteed to eliminate burnout or
  guaranteed to produce specific diseases.
- Romanticism being reduced to bypassing critical thought.
- All AI harms being attributed only to individual user intent; responsibility
  also belongs to organizations and other actors across the lifecycle.

## Operator Test

Before adopting any component, ask:

1. What exact assertion is being made?
2. Which bytes or event does the evidence identify?
3. Who issued the assertion, and under what policy?
4. What does verification establish—and what does it not establish?
5. Which law, agreement, or governance process determines rights?
6. Can the affected person refuse, correct, withdraw, appeal, and obtain remedy?
7. What empirical evidence would justify the claimed human outcome?
