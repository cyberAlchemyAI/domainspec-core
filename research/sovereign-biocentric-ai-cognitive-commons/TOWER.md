# Tower Model

Target: `Sovereign Biocentric AI and Cognitive Commons`

Depth: `standard`

Status: `closed`

Promotion boundary: `local-research-only`

## One-Sentence Model

Preserve the corpus's demand that AI support rather than absorb human agency,
but replace its automatic ownership and permanence claims with a consent-aware
provenance system whose assertions, integrity, truth, authorship, rights, and
value are evaluated separately.

## Layers

| Level | Question | Artifact | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | What is actually in the corpus, and who says it? | [`levels/L0-corpus.md`](levels/L0-corpus.md) | Source identity, stages, voices, and limits recorded |
| L1 | Which ideas are durable, overstated, or unsafe? | [`levels/L1-residue-map.md`](levels/L1-residue-map.md) | Every named residue is closed or explicitly deferred |
| L2 | What can an operator responsibly borrow? | [`levels/L2-closure-plan.md`](levels/L2-closure-plan.md) | Definitions, decisions, learning pack, and validation agree |

## Compositional Spine

```text
human intent and contextual contribution
  -> consent and provenance capture
  -> integrity and source attestation
  -> independent claim and contribution review
  -> bounded disclosure or collective use
  -> attribution, remedy, and revocation controls
  -> learning from both successful and harmful outcomes
```

## Gate Order

1. Corpus identity and voice separation.
2. Source-kind and truth-status separation.
3. Technical, legal, health, and literary adjudication.
4. Operational definitions and worked example.
5. Borrow, analogy-only, block, and future-work decisions.
6. Helper closeout, link validation, and final learning pack.

## Claim Ceiling

This tower can establish a source-backed design interpretation. It cannot
establish legal enforceability, mental-health efficacy, fair economic valuation,
cryptographic provenance in a live system, or community legitimacy without the
future work named in [`residue/open-residue.md`](residue/open-residue.md).
