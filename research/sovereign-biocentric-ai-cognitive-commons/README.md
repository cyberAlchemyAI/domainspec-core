# Sovereign Biocentric AI and Cognitive Commons Research Tower

Status: `closed`

Promotion scope: `local-research-only`

## Research Question

What durable, defensible design can be recovered from a human-AI dialogue that
moves from an analogy between AI and Romantic literature toward a
privacy-preserving, human-agency-centered cognitive commons?

## Source Boundary

- Primary corpus: the DOCX recorded in [`sources/source-record.md`](sources/source-record.md).
- Human prompts are primary ideation evidence, not proof that the proposed
  legal, technical, psychological, or literary claims are true.
- Assistant responses are statements in the corpus. They require independent
  support before this tower accepts them as related-source claims.
- Related sources are included only when they close a named residue or prevent
  a dangerous collapse.
- No corpus vocabulary is promoted into Inventory, Ontology, canonical
  definitions, sigils, spells, or runtime contracts.
