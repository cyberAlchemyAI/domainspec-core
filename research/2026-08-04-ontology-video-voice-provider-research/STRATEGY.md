12:30 ontology educational video. The result will compare at most eight
providers, identify hard exclusions, shortlist at most three providers for an

## Exact evidence boundary

Every load-bearing provider row must carry:

- provider and claim or fixture ID;
- current first-party URL and page title;
- exact heading, selector, anchor, or structured pointer;
- retrieval date and scope, including plan, region, or jurisdiction when stated;
- explorer-return reference;
- evidence scope;
- `observed`, `executed`, or `unverified` proof status.

Pricing is volatile and must remain time-scoped. Absence of a documented right,
privacy control, retention term, consent rule, or technical capability is
`unknown`, never permission.

## Stop conditions

Stop or block when:

- a load-bearing claim lacks a current official source and exact selector;
- pricing or policy scope cannot be stated without inference;
- the route requires an account, payment, credential, upload, API call, synthesis,
  cloning, or other external action;
- no provider survives the hard requirements for a reproducible audition;
- an existing owner decision is found but its scope cannot be resolved;
- the comparison implies that a shortlist is a final choice or that a written
  protocol proves audio quality.
