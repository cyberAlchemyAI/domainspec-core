>

>

>

## Reproducible blind-audition protocol

1. **Admit nothing yet.** First record the owner accent/locale choice, named terminal operator, closed legal/data decisions, executable tooling, and a complete immutable manifest for each admitted provider–model–stock-voice configuration. Use no custom or cloned voice.
2. **Lock generation.** Submit the whole exact excerpt once per render, without chunking/stitching, and make two scored repetitions with identical supported settings. Use seed `8042026` only where documented, with documented best-effort semantics. Unsupported seed, instruction, rate, pitch, gain, quality, latency, pronunciation, or timing controls are recorded `unsupported/not exposed` and omitted. No music, EQ, compression, denoising, reverb, or time-stretching.
3. **Lock delivery where supported.** Use this non-spoken instruction only on a route that documents free-form instructions: `Reflective solo narrator. Read as a thoughtful investigation: intimate and warm, never a keynote or advertisement. Aim for 120–130 words per minute. Let punctuation create short reflective holds. Give “approved” genuine reassurance before the question introduces doubt. Keep time, confidence, and authority distinct. Deliver “ask the responsible owner—or stop” with restraint, not triumph. Do not add, omit, or paraphrase words.` Never add instruction words to spoken text.
4. **Test pronunciation.** First render unchanged text without a dictionary; expect ordinary English “Body War,” stressed on “Body.” If the selected route documents lexicon/phoneme correction, allow exactly one correction, retain the failed take, lock the correction, and create two replacement repetitions. Failure after correction—or failure where correction is unsupported—disqualifies the configuration.
5. **Preserve raw evidence.** Store immutable UUID-named raw bytes, SHA-256, complete redacted request, request/response ID, UTC times, billed units, native format/duration, and native timing metadata. Never overwrite a regeneration.
6. **Normalize reproducibly.** Freeze tool names, versions, commands, and tolerances. Decode once to mono 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV; apply only two-pass BS.1770 normalization to `-16 LUFS-I`, true peak ≤ `-1 dBTP`; do not trim or repair. Record input/output hashes, loudness, peak, and gain. Raw masters remain decisive.
7. **Verify independently.** Freeze forced-alignment and independent-fidelity tools, versions, commands, thresholds, VTT mapping, and manual-review owner. Alignment supplies timing only. Independently transcribe without the reference or perform a separate token-by-token listening audit; compare for every addition, omission, substitution, or reorder. Route all mismatches/low-confidence tokens to manual review; unresolved differences disqualify. Inspect “approved,” the question, all three terms, “Body War,” and “ask the responsible owner—or stop.” Build waveform-derived, ordered, non-overlapping VTT cues with complete coverage; provider timestamps remain diagnostic, not caption authority.
8. **Blind and randomize.** Assign opaque files such as `B001.wav`; protect the provider key until score sheets lock. Freeze the randomization command and listener-order manifests after artifacts freeze. Use separately balanced orders for at least three listeners, never placing a candidate's repetitions consecutively. Fix headphones, player, output level, quiet room, and one replay.
9. **Score only sound.** Score 1–5 with anchors `1 = unacceptable/clear failure`, `3 = adequate`, `5 = excellent`: warmth/intimacy 20%; naturalness/artifact freedom 20%; reassurance-to-doubt arc 20%; time/confidence/authority distinction 15%; pacing/breaths/ask-or-stop cadence 15%; pronunciation 10%. Compute `Σ(weight_percent × score / 5)` on 0–100; report median and listener/repetition spread. An absolute difference below 3.0 points is a tie requiring a longer-sequence test.
10. **Decide fail-closed.** Disqualify altered words, uncorrected key terms, cloning, clipping/truncation/non-speech artifacts, duration outside 75–120 seconds, missing master/reproducibility evidence, or failed rights/privacy review. Keep editability, observed latency, actual billed cost, rights, privacy, retention, security, and sound scores separate. After gates, unblinding, and any longer prototype, the named operator must listen and record dated `approve`, `reject`, or `defer` with governing hashes. Scores never auto-select.
