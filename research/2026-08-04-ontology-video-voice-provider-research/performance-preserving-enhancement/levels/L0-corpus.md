# L0 — corpus

## Included source families

1. The locally approved narration contract.
2. Local surgical restoration: iZotope RX 12.
3. Automatic cloud enhancement: Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech v2.
4. Automatic post-production/mastering: Auphonic singletrack.
5. Open local neural enhancement: Resemble Enhance.
6. Speech-to-speech conversion: ElevenLabs Voice Changer.

## Why this corpus is sufficient for the architectural question

The corpus spans the material distinctions needed for the decision: manual versus automatic, local versus uploaded, waveform-oriented cleanup versus generative reconstruction, and restoration versus voice conversion.

It is not sufficient to rank audible quality. That requires one real recording, frozen settings, rendered derivatives, and blind listening.

## Deliberate exclusions

- Live-call noise suppression, because the target is recorded narration.
- Text-to-speech providers, already covered by the parent study.
- Voice-clone enrollment vendors as a separate class; cloning becomes relevant only inside the optional speech-to-speech lane.
- User reviews and unsourced “best tool” lists.
