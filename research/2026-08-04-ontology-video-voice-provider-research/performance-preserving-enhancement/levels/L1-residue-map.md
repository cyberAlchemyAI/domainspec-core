# L1 — residue map

| Residue | Why it matters | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|
| No narrator recording | There is no material on which to judge preservation or improvement | Blocks quality selection |
| Recording environment and microphone unknown | The best repair chain depends on the defects actually present | Record a controlled short sample first |
| Tool availability/licensing unknown | RX may not be installed or licensed; local neural tools may not fit the hardware | Preflight before audition |
| Cloud data terms not bound to an account | Voice recordings can be sensitive identity data | Blocks upload until reviewed and authorized |
| No frozen settings | Automatic systems can change and strong settings can overprocess | Capture version, settings, date, and output hash |
| No blind identity test | “Cleaner” can still mean “less like me” | Score cleanliness and identity separately |
| No edit/repair policy | Re-recording a weak line may be better than algorithmic reconstruction | Prefer a new human take for semantic or emotional failures |
| No accepted production chain | Restoration and mastering order affects results | Empirical comparison required |

The core unresolved proposition is: **which lightest intervention improves intelligibility and consistency while leaving this narrator recognizably and meaningfully intact?**
