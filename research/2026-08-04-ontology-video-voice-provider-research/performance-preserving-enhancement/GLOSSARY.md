# Glossary

- **Immutable raw** — the untouched recording retained with a content hash. It is never overwritten by cleanup or mastering.
- **Restoration** — removal or reduction of defects such as noise, reverb, clicks, plosives, clipping, or hum while attempting to preserve the recorded performance.
- **Mastering** — final control of tonal balance, dynamics, loudness, and peaks for consistent delivery.
- **Generative enhancement** — reconstruction of missing or degraded speech information. It may improve intelligibility while changing timbre or inventing spectral detail.
- **Speech-to-speech conversion** — re-synthesis in a target voice using a source recording to drive words, timing, and delivery.
- **Performance** — the narrator's actual timing, rhythm, breath, stress, hesitation, emotion, and phrasing.
- **Voice identity drift** — a processed result sounding less recognizably like the recorded speaker.
- **Derivative** — any output made from the immutable raw, stored separately with its tool and parameter manifest.
- **Overprocessing** — processing strong enough to create artifacts, erase useful breaths/ambience, flatten expression, or change identity.
