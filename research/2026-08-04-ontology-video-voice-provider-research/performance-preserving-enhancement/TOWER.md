|---|---|

## Research controls

- Every claim is labeled by source kind in [claims.md](claims.md).
- Provider capability is not audible proof.
- The untouched recording remains the source of truth.
- Every processed output must be a new derivative with a reproducible manifest.
- Cloud upload, provider enrollment, spend, cloning, and voice conversion remain unauthorized.
- Empirical conclusions must be earned through the proposed listening test.
