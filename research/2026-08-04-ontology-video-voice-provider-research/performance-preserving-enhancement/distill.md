# Distillation

## One-sentence model

**Let the human create the meaning; let the tools remove what prevents the listener from hearing it.**

## Compositional spine

`performance authority -> immutable capture -> defect-specific restoration -> gentle mastering -> identity-aware audition -> human acceptance`

## Governing distinction

Restoration may improve access to the recorded performance. Voice conversion creates a new performance-bearing signal. They can both be useful, but they are not the same claim and must not share one acceptance label.
