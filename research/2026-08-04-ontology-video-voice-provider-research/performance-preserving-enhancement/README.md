# Performance-preserving voice enhancement

## Result

For this film, the strongest next route is **record the real human performance, then restore and master it conservatively**. That preserves the narrator's timing, hesitation, emphasis, accent, and emotional arc instead of asking text-to-speech to invent them.

Speech-to-speech voice conversion is a separate, optional derivative. It can retain timing and delivery better than text-to-speech, but it re-synthesizes the voice and adds identity, privacy, and artifact risks.

No recording was uploaded, processed, or evaluated in this research. The recommendation is architectural; audible quality remains unproved.
