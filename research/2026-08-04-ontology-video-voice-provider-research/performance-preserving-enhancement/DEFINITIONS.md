# Local research definitions

These definitions are explanatory and local. They do not amend canonical vocabulary.

## Performance-preserving enhancement

A post-recording process whose acceptance condition is not merely cleaner speech, but cleaner speech **without losing the narrator's meaningful timing, emotion, accent, phrasing, and recognizable identity**.

## Source-of-truth recording

The immutable raw recording that establishes what the narrator actually said and how it was performed. Processed files are evidence-linked derivatives, never replacements for this source.

## Identity-preservation gate

A listening decision that rejects a derivative when cleanup changes the narrator's recognizable voice, emotional meaning, pronunciation, or intended pauses beyond the declared tolerance.

## Conversion boundary

The point at which a process stops repairing captured audio and begins synthesizing a new voice signal. Outputs beyond this boundary must be labeled `voice-converted`, not `restored`.
