# Stage 01 — Context Baseline

## Evidence frame

The current object is a validated text-and-picture production package, not a video. It has 12 ordered sequences from `00:00` through `12:30`, 1,543 narration words, captions covering the same duration, eight named still-image worlds, and a picture score. Current validation explicitly records that no video, voice recording, animation, mix, or final render exists, and that the stills have not been separated into motion layers or tested for parallax.

AutoAds supplies one useful design precedent: a research output is a candidate with sources, rationale, a contradiction path, allowed uses, review state, and residue. Its project is explicitly not executed, so this run may adapt the contract but cannot invoke an AutoAds runtime.

The Anime.js vault currently supplies an implemented, transport-neutral consultation core and a neutral knowledge projection over official documentation and pinned repository artifacts. It may provide evidence-bound task recipes and topic identities. It does not prove HTTP integration or that any planned motion works in a browser; those require a later executed witness.

## Source and owner frame

| AutoAds seed and auto-research design | read-only precedent | adapt candidate/review/residue semantics | treating AutoAds as a running service or importing advertising metrics as animation truth |

| Voice-provider and performance research | separate gate | preserve its timing and voice-following boundary | uploading, synthesizing, selecting, or spending on voice |

## Non-collapse obligations

1. `source_observed` is not `documented_capability`.
2. `documented_capability` is not `planned_motion`.
3. `planned_motion` is not `executed_witness`.
4. `executed_witness` is not a positive `subjective_evaluation`.
5. A positive evaluation is not an `owner_decision` or a verified `Lesson`.
