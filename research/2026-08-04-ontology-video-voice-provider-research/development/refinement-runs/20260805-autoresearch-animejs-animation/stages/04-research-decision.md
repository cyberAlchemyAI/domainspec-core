# Stage 04 — Research Decision

## Decision

Use local research only. No external browsing is needed for this design frontier.

## Why local evidence is sufficient

- The source video package already fixes the narration, sequence order, image worlds, caption fixture, picture score, and semantic boundaries.
- AutoAds already documents the candidate, contradiction, review, decay, and residue pattern that must be adapted.
- The live Anime.js knowledge vault already contains the relevant official-documentation projection, pinned repository evidence, task recipes, and a query surface.
- The missing proof is runtime animation behavior. Browsing cannot supply that proof; it belongs to a later local implementation and browser witness.

## External-research trigger

Stop and request a new confirmation only if the later plan requires a capability absent or stale in the pinned vault, a volatile browser/accessibility fact that the local evidence cannot bind, or a third-party rendering/provider integration. None is required for the current design.

## Claim ceiling

This stage selects sources for design. It does not validate Anime.js runtime behavior, visual quality, accessibility in a browser, performance, voice timing, or production suitability.
