## Boundary heuristic

- L1 value: eliminates supply-chain and identity drift before any scene depends on the runtime.
- L1 cost: three small consumer-owned files plus deterministic local tests.
- Boundary: scene structure and animation add a different decision and can fail independently, so they begin after OAVA-003.

## Non-regression rules

- OAVA-003 cannot weaken source hashes, proof-state separation, contradiction gates, reduced-motion equivalence, or owner gates established by L0.
- The vendor runtime and license are immutable inputs, not material writes.
- A different runtime byte, version, license, path, package identity, provenance receipt, browser, image, timing fixture, or candidate fingerprint creates a new witness identity.
- Static source inspection is not runtime conformance and cannot emit `executed_witness`.
- No layer edits AutoAds, the Anime.js knowledge vault, the source video package, or public Arcanum.

Recommended next layer: L1, restricted to `SWU-OAVA-003`. Major deferred scope: all scene, timeline, browser, visual, voice, episode, release, and promotion work.
