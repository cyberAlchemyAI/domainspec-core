# TypeSafe Custom Role — Synthesized Candidate Package

## Synthesis verdict

**Provisional winner: Member of Technical Staff, Reliable Autonomy Systems.**

This is not a “perfect-role” verdict. It is the strongest surviving hypothesis because it has a falsifiable first-90-day witness, uses evidenced system/workflow-reliability capabilities, and does not require unproved frontier-model training expertise.

It remains conditional: if TypeSafe’s Synthetic Data or Product teams already own end-to-end autonomy-task qualification, the standalone custom role should be killed or absorbed into one of those teams.

## Evidence separation

### Current TypeSafe facts, dated 2026-08-01

- TypeSafe publicly frames itself as a frontier-model lab focused on reliable, general AI systems, real-world decision-making, and production autonomy.
- Its live Ashby board listed nine openings, including Member of Staff/Create Your Own Role, MTS/Synthetic Data, Product Engineer, Backend/Platform, Infrastructure/Kubernetes, and GPU Kernel Engineer.
- Synthetic Data explicitly owns datasets, internal evaluation/debugging tooling, evaluation frameworks across model iterations, and experiments relating data/training interventions to behavior.
- Product Engineering explicitly owns customer-facing features from prototype to production plus product quality, reliability, performance, and instrumentation.
- Platform and Infrastructure own serving, APIs, availability, clusters, telemetry, and incident response.
- The Member of Staff opening expects five or more years of professional expertise in high-performing teams and is fully in-person in San Francisco.
- Public pages do not establish TypeSafe’s private roadmap or whether task-level autonomy qualification is currently unowned.

These facts were live-checked by Explorer 3, but the parent still needs immutable page captures before public writing.

### Inferred TypeSafe need

TypeSafe may need a hands-on owner for the seam where model-level evaluation ends and a multi-step, tool-using task becomes an inspectable real-world trial: explicit task contract, effect boundaries, runnable harness, replayable trace, cross-layer failure classification, and decision-ready go/hold/kill evidence.

This is an inference, not a discovered roadmap item. It survives only if TypeSafe confirms that neither Synthetic Data nor Product already owns the seam.

### Verified candidate evidence

- Public Arcanum contracts support bounded definition, planning, execution, validation, handoff, fail-closed gating, multi-agent joins, replay, and repository-local observability.
- A recent deterministic governance-runner prototype passed seven bounded units in synthetic repositories, including executor joins, replay, reconciliation, interruption recovery, atomic apply, and owner-hook paths; the final unit remained correctly blocked by missing external-owner readiness.
- A private finite semantic evaluator has a promoted L0 witness with typed recommendation, abstention, defeat, invalidation, escalation, counterfactual, replay, and persistence-before-effect behavior. It does not exercise real effects or a production actor runtime.
- A private intent router has a 34/34 local test witness for deterministic, non-authorizing routing from structured input. It does not establish natural-language understanding or production integration.
- Private DomainSpec evidence includes a current 240/240 test-derivation-engine run, typed traceability specimens, owner decision gates, blocker-preserving continuation, negative controls, and explicit residue.
- Small formal witnesses test bounded structural claims and counterexamples. They do not prove model safety, neural semantics, or production reliability.

### Accepted or canonical, but non-operational evidence

- The broad reasoning-engine specification exceeds the implemented finite evaluator slice.
- Arcanum’s dispatch package is still labeled draft despite implemented deterministic routing and join contracts.

### Capability and logistics gaps

- No established ownership of frontier-model training, model architecture, distributed training, GPU kernels, synthetic-data pipelines, causal model-level reliability, production inference, or large-scale eval infrastructure.
- No established production customer, deployment, on-call, or incident-response history.
- Python, TypeScript, statistical experiment design, and LLM implementation depth need interview evidence beyond repository artifacts.
- The repository more strongly proves team/project artifacts than exclusive individual authorship.

## Useful lane disagreement

- **Wiener/CAV2:** the differentiator is decision infrastructure around AI—authority, challenge, abstention, replay, drift, and effects—not model reliability.
- **Spivak/DomainSpec:** the differentiator is the traceable bridge from ambiguous intent to typed contracts, checks, decisions, residue, and continuation. Its private and partially provisional posture limits public claims.
- **Simon/Arcanum and TypeSafe:** the employer-useful unit is an end-to-end autonomy qualification trial. It is the most operational framing, but it has the sharpest Synthetic Data/Product overlap risk.

The provisional winner uses Simon’s concrete ownership unit, Wiener’s claim ceiling and effect boundaries, and Spivak’s traceability discipline. It does not erase their disagreement by claiming these are one proven production capability.

## Rank 1 — Member of Technical Staff, Reliable Autonomy Systems

**Mandate:** Own the qualification path from one promising model capability to one inspectable, repeatable, decision-ready real-world autonomy trial.

**Lab problem owned:** Model, data, product, runtime, and infrastructure owners can each improve their layer while no one owns an end-to-end answer to why a consequential multi-step task passed, failed, abstained, or should advance.

**Why it does not duplicate current openings:** Synthetic Data retains datasets, model-evaluation frameworks, and training-intervention analysis; Product retains workflow selection and shipped behavior; Platform and Infrastructure retain runtime availability and operations. This role owns only the cross-layer task contract, complete trial harness, replayable evidence, failure routing, and terminal qualification decision package. That boundary must be confirmed internally.

### Five responsibilities

1. Convert one economically valuable task into an explicit contract covering inputs, environment, success, abstention, escalation, tool/effect permissions, and owners.
2. Build and run the complete model-and-tool qualification harness rather than only writing a process or framework.
3. Capture replayable evidence and distinguish model, specification, product, runtime, infrastructure, and authority failures.
4. Test negative paths such as stale input, missing telemetry, unauthorized effects, partial completion, persistence failure, and nondeterministic replay.
5. Return a go/hold/kill package and land one reusable regression gate in an owning team’s workflow.

### Interfaces

- **Model research:** supplies checkpoints and hypotheses; receives task-level behavioral failures; retains model-quality authority.
- **Synthetic Data/evals:** co-designs fixtures and interprets model/data failures; retains datasets, general eval frameworks, and training-intervention analysis.
- **Product/design:** selects the valuable workflow and acceptance surface; retains user experience and release authority.
- **Platform/infrastructure:** supplies inference/runtime interfaces and telemetry; retains serving, SLO, cluster, and incident ownership.
- **Leadership/human owner:** selects the pilot and makes risk and go/hold/kill decisions.

**Explicit non-scope:** Model training, synthetic-data ownership, general model evaluation, model architecture, GPU kernels, inference serving, cluster operations, customer feature ownership, release authority, developer advocacy, compliance, or a general governance platform.

### 30/60/90-day outcomes

- **30 days:** Select one reversible, multi-step task; freeze its contract and owner map; baseline it with existing instrumentation; deliver a runnable thin slice and cross-layer failure taxonomy.
- **60 days:** Run the frozen task against available versioned candidates; capture model calls, tools/effects, state, interventions, terminal state, and invalid/incomplete evidence; prove that a broken trace cannot silently pass.
- **90 days:** Complete one cross-functional pilot; reproduce principal failures; test at least one bounded intervention; route each failure to its owner; issue a go/hold/kill package; land one reusable regression gate.

**Concrete witness:** An inspector can replay the controlled evidence, explain why the task passed or failed, and determine whether the next change belongs to model research, data/evals, product, platform, or infrastructure.

### Evidence-to-responsibility binding

| Responsibility | Evidence claims | Bound use |
|---|---|---|
| Explicit task and effect contract | ARC-01, CAV2-01 | Contract and claim-boundary design, not model correctness |
| Runnable qualification harness | ARC-02, ARC-03 | Bounded synthetic/local prototypes, not production |
| Typed outcomes and replay | CAV2-02, CAV2-03 | Finite evaluator/router witnesses only |
| Negative-path and owner routing | ARC-04, DS-03 | Local fail-closed fixtures and owner-decision evidence |
| Traceable regression gate | DS-01, DS-02 | Deterministic checks and conformance evidence, not whole-system semantics |

**Public-safe language:** “I build repeatable, inspectable systems that turn ambiguous AI-assisted work into explicit task contracts, executable checks, replayable evidence, and owner-routed decisions. At TypeSafe, I would propose applying that practice to one falsifiable real-world autonomy trial.”

**Disqualifying gaps:** Kill or absorb the role if Synthetic Data/Product already owns the complete task loop; if no real task/checkpoint/tool trace is available within 90 days; if the role is expected to own training, infrastructure, or production release; or if the candidate cannot demonstrate sufficient hands-on implementation and experiment-design depth.

## Rank 2 — Research Engineer, Traceable Task Reliability

**Mandate:** Build deterministic specification, traceability, and evaluation seams that expose how task intent becomes model-and-tool behavior and where evidence becomes incomplete.

**Lab problem owned:** Reliability experiments become difficult to interpret when requirements, fixtures, transformations, outcomes, unresolved decisions, and evidence are not linked through stable identities.

**Why it does not duplicate current openings:** This role would not own datasets or model-iteration evaluation, and it would not own shipped product behavior. It would own source-to-check traceability and task-level evidence semantics. The distinction is defensible, but narrower and more likely to be absorbed by Synthetic Data or Product.

### Five responsibilities

1. Translate task intent into typed obligations, stable source handles, and explicit uncovered residue.
2. Derive deterministic checks and mutation controls from those obligations.
3. Preserve traceability across task inputs, transformations, model/tool outputs, and decision owners.
4. Design experiments that distinguish support, existing explanation, failure, and missing evidence.
5. Maintain blocker, continuation, and replay artifacts without treating generated output as authority.

### Interfaces

- **Model research:** uses the trace matrix to connect observed behavior to a stated obligation.
- **Synthetic Data/evals:** owns examples, datasets, and model-comparison methodology; consumes source-linked task obligations.
- **Product/design:** owns user intent and acceptance; resolves ambiguous or non-executable obligations.
- **Infrastructure:** exposes stable execution and telemetry identifiers.
- **Leadership:** accepts unresolved residue and prioritizes closure.

**Explicit non-scope:** General eval-platform ownership, training intervention analysis, customer product, production orchestration, model interpretability, formal completeness, or automated human judgment.

### 30/60/90-day outcomes

- **30 days:** Select one task and map sources, obligations, outcomes, gaps, and owners.
- **60 days:** Implement a two-corpus check-generation and mutation pilot with replayable outputs and explicit uncovered obligations.
- **90 days:** Integrate the trace contract into one existing evaluation workflow, demonstrate at least one defect or ambiguity it exposes, and obtain an owner decision on whether the added signal justifies its cost.

**Concrete witness:** A changed requirement or task result can be traced to affected checks and owners; a missing relationship remains explicit instead of being silently inferred.

### Evidence-to-responsibility binding

| Responsibility | Evidence claims | Bound use |
|---|---|---|
| Typed task obligations | DS-02 | Bounded private conformance evidence |
| Deterministic checks | DS-01 | Structural/check derivation only |
| Traceable transformations | DS-02, CAV2-03 | Stable local artifacts, not model interpretability |
| Typed experiments and gaps | DS-04 | Provisional experiment; current drift must remain disclosed |
| Blocker/decision continuity | DS-03, ARC-01 | Local owner-routing evidence |

**Public-safe language:** “I build deterministic pipelines that connect ambiguous requirements to source-linked checks, explicit failure cases, unresolved decisions, and inspectable handoffs.”

**Disqualifying gaps:** Kill as a standalone role if TypeSafe considers this ordinary Synthetic Data or Product Engineering work; if it produces only schemas and documents; if it cannot expose a measurable failure within 90 days; or if the candidate cannot implement inside TypeSafe’s language and evaluation stack.

## Rank 3 — Member of Staff, Autonomy Qualification

**Mandate:** Lead and implement a cross-functional qualification discipline that decides when a model-enabled workflow is sufficiently evidenced to advance.

**Lab problem owned:** Cross-functional autonomy work can progress through partial successes without a single decision package joining behavioral, tool, runtime, product, and owner evidence.

**Why it does not duplicate current openings:** The role would own the terminal cross-layer qualification decision package, not any underlying functional layer. This is also its weakness: without substantial code and experiment ownership, it collapses into program management, governance consulting, or a Product responsibility.

### Five responsibilities

1. Select qualification pilots with leadership and functional owners.
2. Define evidence, failure, escalation, and terminal-decision contracts.
3. Implement the thin-slice runner and evidence join.
4. Reconcile contradictory or incomplete evidence without manufacturing closure.
5. Facilitate a go/hold/kill decision and assign each next action to its actual owner.

### Interfaces

The functional boundaries match Rank 1, but this role emphasizes decision integration and leadership coordination. Functional teams retain all technical authority over models, data, product, runtime, and infrastructure.

**Explicit non-scope:** Portfolio program management, policy governance, release management, model safety branding, research direction, or substituting process for experiments.

### 30/60/90-day outcomes

- **30 days:** Obtain one pilot mandate, owners, and falsifiers.
- **60 days:** Implement the evidence join and run failure-injection rehearsals.
- **90 days:** Deliver one inspectable qualification decision and reusable owner-routing contract, backed by running code and trial evidence.

**Concrete witness:** A previously ambiguous advance/hold decision becomes falsifiable and owner-routed through an executable pilot—not merely a review meeting or framework.

### Evidence-to-responsibility binding

| Responsibility | Evidence claims | Bound use |
|---|---|---|
| Select and bound pilots | ARC-01 | Repository-local contract |
| Evidence/failure contract | CAV2-01, CAV2-02 | Private bounded design and witness |
| Implement runner/join | ARC-02, ARC-03 | Synthetic/local evidence only |
| Reconcile contradictions | DS-05 | Non-authority staging result |
| Owner decision and routing | DS-03, ARC-04 | Local decision and gating evidence |

**Public-safe language:** “I lead by building the experiment and evidence path needed for cross-functional teams to make an explicit advance, hold, or stop decision.”

**Disqualifying gaps:** Kill if the role is mainly coordination; if five-plus professional years at Staff level cannot be established; if it lacks direct implementation ownership; or if leadership expects a proven production-autonomy, frontier-model, or research-operations background.

## KILL option — No standalone custom role

**Mandate:** Do not manufacture a title around responsibilities already owned by Synthetic Data or Product.

**Lab problem owned:** None separately; qualification work stays with its current functional owner.

**Why this may be correct:** Public postings already give Synthetic Data substantial evaluation ownership and Product end-to-end product reliability ownership. The supposed gap is inferred from public boundaries, not confirmed internally.

**Responsibilities:** No new role. Synthetic Data owns model/data evaluation; Product owns the real workflow and shipped behavior; Platform/Infrastructure own runtime operation. The candidate may pursue a conventional opening only if its requirements and evidence align.

**Interfaces:** Existing team interfaces only.

**Explicit non-scope:** No vanity role, “responsible AI” layer, governance office, or cross-functional process inserted merely to preserve novelty.

**30/60/90:** No custom-role plan should be promised until TypeSafe confirms an unowned task-qualification seam. If it is already owned, redirect the candidate package to a conventional role or stop.

### Evidence-to-decision rows

| Kill trigger | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Synthetic Data owns end-to-end task/tool qualification | TS-03 plus TypeSafe interview confirmation |
| Product owns the qualification harness and terminal decision | TS-04 plus TypeSafe interview confirmation |
| Candidate cannot evidence Staff-level experience | PERS-01 and independent employment evidence |
| Pilot requires frontier-model training or production-infrastructure ownership | GAP-01 |
| No inspectable 90-day task is available | TypeSafe interview confirmation |

**Public-safe language:** “I would first test whether this responsibility is genuinely unowned. If it already sits in Synthetic Data or Product, I would not propose a separate role.”

**Disqualifying gaps:** Any one of the kill triggers above.

## Selection rule

Advance Rank 1 provisionally. Prefer Rank 2 only if TypeSafe wants the work embedded in evaluation engineering and explicitly values specification-to-check traceability. Rank 3 survives only with hands-on coding and independently established Staff-level experience. Activate KILL if TypeSafe confirms existing ownership or cannot supply a concrete 90-day trial.

System/workflow reliability must remain distinct from model reliability:

> The candidate evidence supports controlling how model output becomes inspectable work. It does not show improvements to model weights, frontier-model behavior, or general model reliability.
