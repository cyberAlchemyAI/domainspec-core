## Lanes, groups, and subagents

### Group 1 — explorers, parallel

Purpose: produce three exact-bound evidence-to-role matrices. Anti-bias is a composite methodology and source-corpus axis.

1. **Wiener, Norbert — CyberAlchemy v2 reliability explorer.** Formal authority, challenge, decision, evidence, failure, and bounded-reasoning mapping. Expected output: exact source selectors, proof posture, TypeSafe relevance, first-90-day responsibilities, safe wording, and forbidden overclaims.
2. **Spivak, David — DomainSpec v2 traceability explorer.** Empirical audit of typed intent, relationships, validators, tests, task receipts, owner decisions, and residue. Expected output: evidence-backed delivered capabilities and explicit implementation gaps.
3. **Simon, Herbert — Arcanum and TypeSafe-fit explorer.** Repository-wide operational evidence plus current TypeSafe mission, team, and role overlap. Expected output: live-source fit map, non-duplication analysis, interfaces, logistics/capability gaps, and ranked role candidates.

The sheet contains one predicted-disagreement record for every explorer pair.

### Group 2 — synthesizer, dependent singleton

**Runtime-neutral synthesizer.** Reconciles explorer evidence into three ranked role hypotheses and one KILL option. It must separate current TypeSafe facts, inferred needs, verified candidate evidence, accepted-but-non-operational research, and gaps. It writes no file.

### Group 3 — reviewers, parallel after synthesis

Purpose: attack the candidate roles along the attack-vector axis.

1. **Lakatos, Imre — precedent skeptic.** Tests existing TypeSafe and industry ownership; a found owner becomes BUILD-FROM-OWNED or ALREADY-DEPLOYED, never an automatic kill.
2. **Popper, Karl — non-vacuity skeptic.** Requires a falsifiable, economically useful 30/60/90-day witness that does not depend on unproved frontier-model credentials.
3. **Quine, Willard Van Orman — definitional/claim skeptic.** Rejects vague governance branding, role collapse, workflow-to-model inflation, and private-to-public leakage.

The sheet contains one predicted-disagreement record for every reviewer pair.

### Group 4 — writer, dependent singleton

**Shannon, Claude.** After exact-binding evidence closure returns `ready`, writes `findings.md` only. The deliverable must include one selected role or typed KILL, aliases, mandate, responsibilities, interfaces, non-scope, 30/60/90-day plan, success measures, evidence matrix, overlap analysis, candid gaps, questions, and pasteable public-safe application language.

### Group 5 — auditor, dependent singleton

**Brandenburg, Martin.** Audits only the final findings and evidence package. It rejects vanity titles, process-only value, unsupported model claims, hidden logistics assumptions, private leakage, or application language that reads as governance consulting rather than builder ownership.

## Dependency flow

```text
explorers --sequential--> synthesizer --zig-zag(2)--> reviewers
                              |
                              +--feedback(1)--> explorers, only for missing evidence

reviewers --exact-binding evidence closure--> writer --sequential--> auditor
                                                        ^                |
                                                        +--feedback(1)---+

auditor recommendation --> parent final approval
```

Every consuming wave remains blocked until its type-owner gate returns `ready`. Artifact existence alone does not open a gate.
