## TypeSafe problem proposed for ownership

The proposed function owns **agent task qualification**: the narrow technical seam between a model capability and a falsifiable task-level experiment. It authors the runnable task environment, runner, replay path, failure injections, measurement bundle, and provisional routing evidence for one task. It does not presume that this seam is currently unowned; public sources cannot establish TypeSafe's internal roadmap or ownership. The hypothesis is timely because, as captured on **2026-08-01**, TypeSafe publicly described economically valuable automation and real-world decision-making as company aims, advertised a “Create your own role” Member of Staff opening, and simultaneously advertised adjacent Synthetic Data, Product, Backend/Platform, and Infrastructure scopes. ## Why this is strongest, but not “perfect”

“Perfect” cannot be proved. TypeSafe's actual ownership, roadmap, first task, numerical thresholds, and willingness to create this interface are private and unknown. The supplied evidence also does not independently establish individual contribution, the advertised professional-years threshold, deep deployed-system experience, or San Francisco logistics. A role can therefore be tested only as a falsifiable hiring and operating hypothesis—not certified as a perfect fit. ([remaining uncertainties and SENIORITY-UNKNOWN](receipts/evidence-closure.json))

It is nevertheless the strongest surviving hypothesis for four reasons:

- It maps the repository evidence to bounded environment-and-runner engineering instead of inflating it into frontier-model, general evaluation, or deployed-agent expertise. ([claim ceiling](receipts/evidence-closure.json))
- It uses conventional, searchable `Evals & Environments` language while making the task-level boundary explicit. ([title reconciliation](review-round-2.md#parent-closure-disposition))
- Its first 90 days require candidate-authored running code, real model/tool interfaces, precommitted thresholds, failure injections, economics, and an owner-recorded decision; documentation alone fails. ([non-vacuity verdict](review-round-2.md#non-vacuity))
- It has an honest absorption path when the work is already covered, so novelty is never used to preserve an unnecessary title. ([kill and absorb conditions](synthesis-revision.md#7-kill-and-absorb-conditions))

## Responsibilities

These are proposed responsibilities, not claims about current TypeSafe ownership. ([organizational posture](receipts/evidence-closure.json))

1. **Encode the real task and its baseline.** Implement the environment, inputs, owner-defined task permissions, grading contract, terminal states, reversible test effects, and economic baseline. The role tests those permissions; it grants no access and authorizes no effects.
2. **Author and operate the task runner.** Connect the controlled trial to the actual versioned model and tool interfaces, persist versioned inputs and outputs, and distinguish incomplete runs from observed task failures.
3. **Precommit the experiment.** Before seeing results, record the sample size, repetition policy, primary metric and threshold, critical failure conditions, review limit, decision rule, and maintenance ceiling with designated owners.
4. **Exercise failure and replay paths.** Inject stale input, partial completion, denied tool use, interrupted execution, missing trace, and changed-result replay; preserve an explicit unknown category.
5. **Produce provisional cross-interface evidence.** Route observations to the likely functional interface without asserting an exact cause. Model, product, and runtime specialists determine causes within their domains.
6. **Deliver a non-authorizing decision package.** Report task outcomes, economic signal, review burden, costs, failure visibility, uncertainty, and projected upkeep. The designated owner records invest, hold, or stop; a reusable check is proposed only if the precommitted conditions pass.

These responsibilities are bound to the reviewed technical and economic witness. ([responsibility repair](synthesis-revision.md#4-five-technical-responsibilities-and-boundaries), [WITNESS-90](receipts/evidence-closure.json))

## Explicit non-scope

The role does **not** own or claim:

- frontier-model architecture or training;
- synthetic-data pipelines, general model evaluation, or causal interpretation of training interventions;
- product roadmap, customer-facing feature ownership, serving uptime, cluster operations, or incident remediation;
- broad assurance, compliance, formal proof of AI correctness, or exact cross-layer cause determination;
- access grants, effect approval, risk acceptance, deployment, release, or a claim that an agent can operate independently in a live environment;
- a general reasoning engine, general-purpose orchestration platform, or verified business value from repository artifacts.

These exclusions implement the binding claim ceiling; the proposal is task-environment engineering around model use and nothing broader. ([claim ceiling and writer constraints](receipts/evidence-closure.json), [definition and claim boundary](review-round-2.md#definition-and-claim-boundary))

## Falsifiable 30/60/90-day plan

### First 30 days — admission and frozen baseline

- Product and the designated decision owner supply one existing, recurring, economically meaningful, reversible task, the actual model/tool interfaces, a measurable baseline, and functional collaborators.
- The candidate authors and runs a thin task adapter, environment, runner, frozen fixtures, failure injector, replay command, and inspectable run bundle.
- Before results, the relevant owners freeze sample size `N`, repetitions, primary metric and threshold `T`, critical failures, replay threshold, review limit, net-task-value calculation, and monthly maintenance ceiling `M`.
- **Falsifier:** if the task, interfaces, baseline, collaborators, decision owner, or candidate-authored running code is absent—or another team already owns the complete loop—stop or absorb the proposal.

### By 60 days — measured task evidence

- Run the precommitted sample against the baseline and agreed candidate configuration.
- Exercise stale input, partial completion, denied tool use, interrupted execution, missing trace, and nondeterministic retry.
- Measure accepted completion, net task value, human review, false passes, false blocks, replay completeness, interface cost, latency, candidate engineering time, and projected upkeep.
- Return provisional routing with an explicit unknown category; functional specialists determine cause.
- **Falsifier:** framework-only output, threshold changes after results, silent incomplete traces, or unmeasured upkeep fail the trial.

### By 90 days — one recorded economic decision

- Repeat the task on a time-separated or otherwise precommitted evaluation slice.
- Deliver running code, inspectable traces, injected-failure results, economic comparison, uncertainty, and maintenance estimate.
- The named owner records **invest**, **hold**, or **stop**. Hold or stop is a valid witness.
- A reusable check is proposed only if the primary metric clears `T`, no critical effect-boundary failure occurs, replay clears its threshold, net decision value is positive, and upkeep is at or below `M`.
- **Falsifier:** if upkeep exceeds the value of the decisions improved, do not retain the separate function or check.

No numerical threshold is invented here; TypeSafe owners must supply and freeze the values before results. ([precise witness](synthesis-revision.md#5-precise-306090-economic-witness), [WITNESS-90 and remaining uncertainty](receipts/evidence-closure.json))

## Success measures

Success is a decision-quality witness, not a promised positive outcome:

- accepted completion rate against the frozen task contract;
- net task value per attempt: accepted-output value minus interface cost, human review/rework, and expected failure loss;
- human-review minutes per accepted outcome;
- false-pass and false-block counts;
- trace and replay completeness;
- task/interface cost and latency;
- candidate engineering time and projected monthly upkeep;
- failure visibility, including the rate of unresolved cases;
- a named owner's recorded invest, hold, or stop decision;
- retained check only when its measured decision value exceeds maintenance cost.

These measures come from the admitted non-vacuity witness and remain future, precommitted pilot measures. ([non-vacuity requirements](reviews.md#non-vacuity-reviewer), [WITNESS-90 proof posture](receipts/evidence-closure.json))

## Public-safe role charter

### Member of Technical Staff, Agent Task Evals & Environments

**Status:** Proposed embedded role, conditional on TypeSafe confirming that a distinct technical component exists.

**Purpose:** Build the evaluation environment and runner for one real, recurring, reversible tool-using task, then measure its operational and economic behavior so designated owners can decide invest, hold, or stop.

**Core outputs:** A versioned task environment, candidate-authored runner, frozen experiment plan, failure and replay tests, inspectable evidence bundle, economic comparison, uncertainty statement, upkeep estimate, and owner-recorded decision.

**Working interfaces:** Model research supplies model interfaces and hypotheses; evaluation specialists shape fixtures and interpretation; Product and Design define task value and human experience; Platform and Infrastructure supply approved interfaces and telemetry; leadership names the decision owner.

**Boundaries:** The role does not train models, own general model evaluation, set the product roadmap, diagnose every technical cause, operate infrastructure, grant access, approve effects, or make deployment and release decisions. It provides task-level evidence; the responsible specialists and designated owners decide and act.

**Admission test:** Start only when TypeSafe supplies a real task, actual interfaces, a measurable baseline, precommitted thresholds, functional collaborators, and a named decision owner, and expects the candidate to author running code.

**Stop or absorb:** Drop the standalone title if an existing team already covers the complete path, the work becomes documentation-only, the required inputs cannot be frozen, or measured decision value does not justify ongoing upkeep.

This charter contains only public-safe proposal language.
