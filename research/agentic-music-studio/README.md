# Research Tower — Agentic Music Studio

**Target:** Communication interfaces and processes for an *agentic* (not generative-model-reliant)
music studio built on specs, voice control, and live-coding languages — with ergonomics as a
first principle and craft/artisanship preserved.

**Seed question (verbatim from operator):**
> develop an agentic music studio, using specs, human voice controlling, and languages like
> TidalCycles. The idea is **not relying on models, but agentic engineering to make good music,
> without losing the craft/artisan/artistic part** of music making. Ergonomics should be a first
> principle. What I envisioned is something along the lines *"create some dark violin stochastic
> synth, then use some dissonance scale to bring a strangeness to the listener"* — **what other
> types of interfaces, as communication processes, could this studio have?**

**Output root:** `research/agentic-music-studio/`
**Depth:** full
**Status:** closed (local research artifact; promotion-candidates listed, not promoted)
**Date checked:** 2026-06-16

## What this tower is

A layered, source-backed scaffold that answers the operator's question — *what communication
processes could the studio support* — by (1) grounding the operator's own worked example in real
research domains, and (2) deriving a **taxonomy of ten communication processes** plus the
architectural spine (the spec as a shared lingua franca) that makes them cohere.

It is **local research**, not a build spec. It teaches, compares, and recommends. It does not
promote vocabulary or contracts into Inventory / Ontology / sigils / runtime without a separate
governed promotion decision (see `bridge-decisions.md`).
