## 1. Operator model (one paragraph)

*Rationality: A-Z* is one escalating argument: **the quality of a mind's map of reality bounds
everything that mind can do or protect.** Make beliefs track reality and "pay rent" in
predictions (**B1**); catch the human algorithm that writes its conclusion first and rationalizes
after (**B2**); understand that minds are *adaptation-executors* shaped by blind evolution, not
truth-trackers (**B3**); accept the reductionist, Bayesian world the true map converges on
(**B4**); protect fragile human values against proxy-optimization (**B5**); and institutionalize
the art in error-correcting communities with precise words and "something to protect" (**B6**).

## 2. Source-backed core claims (with source kinds — full table in [claim-ledger.md](claim-ledger.md))

- `[primary-source]` Beliefs should **pay rent in anticipated experience**; evidence is an event
  causally **entangled** with its target (B1).
- `[primary-source]` **The bottom line**: only the generating algorithm counts, not post-hoc
  arguments; **Conservation of Expected Evidence** forbids a test expected to only confirm (B2).
- `[primary-source]` Organisms are **adaptation-executors, not fitness-maximizers** (B3;
  `[related-source]`-corroborated by Williams, Tooby & Cosmides).
- `[primary-source]` **No Universally Compelling Arguments**; morality names a human computation
  (B5) *— via mirror/summary, not a canonical body.*
- **Author leaps the literature does NOT license** (`[related-source]`/`[local-inference]`):
  Many-Worlds "wins outright" (physicists ~15%, R7); Solomonoff as a usable razor (R2);
  verificationism as fresh (R1); morality as one well-defined computation (L4, R9); strong
  trainability of rationality (L5, R10). **Structural fault line:** B1's rent-criterion
  contradicts B4's MWI verdict (L2).
