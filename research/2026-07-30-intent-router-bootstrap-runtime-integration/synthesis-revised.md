`receipt_digest` is retained only as the current wire name for the **disposition integrity digest**: a checksum over disposition material. It is not support evidence, confidence, approval, a reasoning receipt, a lifecycle receipt, or authority.

Evidence vocabulary:

- **Support evidence:** provenance-bearing references supporting request discriminator posture; admissibility remains with its source/ACI owner.
- **Disposition integrity digest:** the current wire `receipt_digest`; proves only disposition-byte integrity.
- **Reasoning/lifecycle receipt:** an ACI-owned record that an admitted command/event or reasoning stage occurred under its governing contract.
- **Authority evidence:** canonical proof that a principal confirmed the Dispatch/group/seat or an authorization owner admitted an action.

No evidence reference, digest, or receipt upgrades confidence or authority by existing alone.

## Orchestrator seam, lifecycle, and owner sequence

1. A source adapter constructs the request; normalization ownership must be bound before implementation.
2. The catalog projector reads named canonical capability and confirmed Dispatch sources and emits or rejects a frozen digest-bound projection.
3. The thin adapter invokes one pinned local JSON-port process and validates bytes, protocol, versions, digests, limits, and `authority_effect: none`.
4. ACI stores request and disposition only as content-addressed artifacts referenced by ACI-owned command/event records.
5. `candidate` is advisory evidence for human consideration. `ambiguous`, `no-match`, and `invalid` do not cross the workflow-binding seam.
6. The human confirms or authors an existing canonical Dispatch/group/seat. No `selection_id`, selection table, or parallel selection lifecycle is created.
7. Only the confirmed canonical Dispatch/group/seat may be passed to `RuntimeService.bind_host_workflow_turn()`.
8. Existing runtime owners independently validate binding, authorize execution, attempt execution, observe effects, and record terminal evidence.

[Simon receipt; `host_dispatch_hook.py:373-427`, `service.py:5192-5761`, `migrations/009_host_workflow_binding.sql:1-30`]

Selection, Dispatch binding, authorization, execution attempt, and observed effect plus receipt are non-substitutable:

- human choice does not create a binding;
- binding does not authorize execution;
- authorization does not prove an attempt;
- an attempt does not prove an effect;
- an observed effect without its required receipt does not prove governed completion.

Each boundary independently fails closed.

`dispatch_open_or_appender_calls`, `aci_journal_dispatch_open_calls`, and `compatibility_fallback_calls` must always remain zero in this adapter suite: the adapter may consume only an already confirmed canonical Dispatch/group/seat and must never open a second Dispatch, append another ledger row, synthesize ACI `dispatch-open`, or invoke compatibility fallback.

The proposed `IR_*` codes and runners are future acceptance-contract names, not claims about current executable vocabulary.
