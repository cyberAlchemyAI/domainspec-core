# W4 — Closeout And Optional Extension Decision

- Layer: L3.
- Layer question: Is the L2 result aligned, publishable within its evidence, and
  worth exactly one new extension boundary?
- Entry gate: W3 phase decision and complete receipt inventory.
- Task: [VERIFY](../tasks/TASK-HALP-VERIFY.md).
- SWU: closure-only exemption.

## Review Scope

- Spec/architecture/plan/result alignment.
- Receipt completeness and reproducibility.
- License, evidence retention, and publication boundary.
- Claim language bounded to measured checkpoints/stacks/workloads.
- Non-regression against L0–L2 guarantees.
- Value of one candidate extension: external comparator, realistic corpus,
  retrieval, 128K, or independent stack.

## Exit Evidence

One of:

- audited closeout and stop;
- audited package and bounded publication;
- one approved extension with owner, budget, claim, matrix, and kill rule;
- repair route with the failed alignment/receipt gate.
