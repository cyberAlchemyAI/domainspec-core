# Closure Gates

| Gate | Pass condition | Current state | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0 — source identity | Every load-bearing statement resolves to a named source and source kind | pass | Corpus may enter synthesis |
| G1 — horizontal invariance | The instrument names the claim, limits, governance, and approval invariants shared by all views | pass as design | May define representation contract; no comprehension claim |
| G2 — vertical necessity | A climb requires typed missing-vocabulary residue after local repair fails | pass as method | May define promotion decision; no cognitive mechanism claim |
| G3 — formal boundary | Formal results are separated from operational and psychological bridges | pass | Analogy may motivate structure |
| G4 — protocol validity | Baseline/treatment tasks are fixed, blindness and contamination are recorded, and metrics are machine-checkable | open | Route to `research-evidence-harness` |
| G5 — treatment evidence | Clean baseline/treatment results support the preregistered direction | open | No effectiveness claim |
| G6 — human evidence | Human arm supports a human-comprehension result | open | No human-cognition claim |
| G7 — public boundary | Private evidence has a public-safe derivation and source-owner review | open | No public paper insertion |
| G8 — paper authority | Paper owner accepts wording, outline placement, and result status | open | No paper mutation or publication |

## Gate policy

Passing G0–G3 closes the **definition of the lane**, not its empirical claims. G4–G6 are evidence gates. G7–G8 are publication and authority gates. No lower gate implies a higher one.
