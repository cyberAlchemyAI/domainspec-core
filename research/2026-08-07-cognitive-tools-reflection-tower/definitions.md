# Candidate Definitions

These are tower-local definitions. They do not alter canonical Arcanum or cyberAlchemy-v2 vocabulary.

## Cognitive Navigation Instrument

A bounded set of representations, invariants, tasks, and residue classes used to test whether a reader can traverse an explanation without enlarging its claim or authority.

## Horizontal Representation Set

A collection of audience-specific expressions of one claim whose design contract requires preservation of the same evidential ceiling, interpretation limits, governance route, and approval boundary.

## Reflective Escalation

A gated move to a richer explanatory layer after an observed mismatch has been typed, local repair has failed, and the current vocabulary cannot name a distinction required for recomposition.

## Round-Trip Comprehension Protocol

A baseline/treatment procedure in which a participant interprets a concrete case through one or more representations, enters any required richer layer, and returns to reconstruct and transfer the case under fixed scoring rules.

## Return Fidelity

The measured preservation of the original case's distinctions, claim ceiling, interpretation limits, and authority boundary after a round trip.

## Formal-Analogy Boundary

The rule that formal preservation or tower-assembly results may motivate the structure of a cognitive instrument but cannot count as evidence that human or model cognition obeys that structure.
