# Tower Structure

## Construction

```text
bounded claim or concrete case at L_n
          |
          v
horizontal expression set H_n
          |
          v
fixed comprehension task P
          |
          v
typed residue R_n
     / local repair closes it --------> remain at L_n
    /
   +-- missing vocabulary persists ---> seed L_(n+1)
                                         |
                                         v
                               return to concrete case
                                         |
                                         v
                              empirical comparison + gate
```

The construction separates representation design from evidence of comprehension. The first can be specified from repository sources. The second remains open until a controlled protocol is executed.

## Levels

| Level | Purpose | Output | Claim ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | Establish the exact source corpus and statuses | [`levels/L0-corpus.md`](levels/L0-corpus.md) | What the sources explicitly define or report |
| L1 | Classify mismatches, overclaims, and missing bridges | [`levels/L1-residue-map.md`](levels/L1-residue-map.md) | Local synthesis and analogy, visibly labeled |
| L2 | Define closure actions and the paper lane | [`levels/L2-closure-plan.md`](levels/L2-closure-plan.md), [`PAPER-LANE.md`](PAPER-LANE.md) | Candidate proposal, not cognitive result |
| L3 | Execute and audit a comprehension protocol | [`EVIDENCE-HANDOFF.md`](EVIDENCE-HANDOFF.md) | Unentered; requires a separate governed evidence route |

## Three axes, kept distinct

- **Horizontal (`H`)**: alternative expressions of the same claim for different readers or decisions. Success means the claim ceiling and governed meaning remain invariant.
- **Vertical (`V`)**: a richer layer created because the current layer cannot name an observed distinction. Success means prior distinctions are preserved and the missing vocabulary is added.
- **Diagonal/metacognitive (`D`)**: a reader's evaluation of their own reasoning and confidence. This is a conditional research bridge, not part of the paper's supported result set.

## Stop rule

Stop climbing when local repair closes the residue, the remaining residue is acceptable and recorded, the next layer would add more ambiguity than it resolves, an owner boundary is reached, or empirical evidence is required. For this tower, the last condition is decisive: the paper lane can be defined, but cognitive effectiveness cannot be closed from formal analogy.

## Gates

The operative gates are in [`gates/closure-gates.md`](gates/closure-gates.md). Passing this tower means only that the candidate lane is source-grounded, bounded, and ready for an evidence-harness design. It does not mean publication, promotion, deployment, or experimental validation.
