# Related Work Inside the Repository

## Optimization chains

Supply the horizontal representation contract: one claim, multiple expression forms, explicit limits, governance, and approval. They do not provide evidence that the forms improve comprehension.

## Craft and CAV2 D17

Supply the vertical method: classify residue, attempt local repair, promote only missing-schema residue, specify the next layer, and stop under bounded conditions. They do not turn every confusion into a new level.

## Lean reflection tower

Supplies a formal precedent for faithful promotion, vocabulary extension, and non-total closure. The framework itself states that the operational-audit bridge is separate and may fail.

## Reflection Tower–Arcanum mapping

Surfaces navigability as a genuine gap, but remains a draft partial mapping. Its own judgment is that “isomorphism” is too strong.

## Psychology and neuroscience bridges

Offer candidate interpretations of tower depth and nested metacognition. They remain conjectural or conditional until non-circular tasks and human evidence exist.

## Novice comprehension test

Supplies the closest existing protocol shape: preregistered claim, fixed baseline/treatment comparison, stall classification, invalidation rules, and separated agent and human arms. The baseline also teaches that the instrument itself can leak answers and that predicted stall classes can be falsified.

## DCB saga review

Supplies an adversarial case where a compelling mechanism label exceeded the actual executable witness. It is valuable for transfer testing precisely because the correct answer must resist the label and preserve the narrower claim.

## Relationship claim

This tower does not claim that these works are equivalent. It uses each for a separate role—representation, escalation, formal limit, protocol, or calibration—and records the bridge type in [`bridge-decisions.md`](bridge-decisions.md).
