# Cognitive Tools Reflection Tower

## Research target

Define a paper lane that joins three distinct operations without turning their formal resemblance into cognitive proof:

1. **Horizontal representations** carry one bounded claim through audience-appropriate forms.
2. **Vertical residue promotion** adds a richer explanatory layer only when the existing forms cannot name a required distinction and local repair fails.
3. **Round-trip comprehension** tests whether a reader can enter the richer representation and return to a concrete case without losing distinctions, inventing authority, or exceeding the evidence.

The lane is named **Cognitive Tools: Representation, Reflection, and Round-Trip Comprehension**.

## Safe paper claim

> We define a source-grounded cognitive instrument and a falsifiable comprehension protocol. Formal reflection-tower results motivate the instrument's structure; they do not prove that the instrument improves human comprehension or models human cognition.

## Central distinction

Horizontal views are peers, not tower rungs. A vertical climb is justified only by typed residue that cannot be repaired inside the current representational vocabulary. Neither operation establishes cognitive effectiveness. That requires a controlled comprehension experiment, including the deferred human arm for any human-cognition claim.
