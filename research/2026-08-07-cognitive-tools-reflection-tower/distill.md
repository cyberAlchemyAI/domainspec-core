# Distillation

## In one sentence

Expose one bounded claim through several peer representations, climb to a richer vocabulary only when those representations cannot name an observed mismatch, and test comprehension by requiring a faithful return to the concrete case.

## What is established

- The repository separately defines multi-form expression, residue-driven layer growth, and a falsifiable baseline/treatment protocol.
- The formal sources explicitly distinguish their mathematical result from operational and cognitive bridges.
- These pieces support a bounded paper proposal.

## What is not established

- That the instrument improves comprehension.
- That formal reflection is a model of human cognition.
- That agent stalls generalize to humans.
- That the proposed metacognitive `D` axis is distinct or validated.

## The most useful test

Give a participant a case whose attractive mechanism label exceeds the executable evidence. After using the instrument, ask them to return to the concrete witness and state exactly what was demonstrated, what remains unshown, and who could approve a stronger claim.
