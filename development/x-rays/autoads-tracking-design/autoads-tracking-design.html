autoads Tracks Creative Outcomes As Governed Evidence

The design is not a normal ad dashboard. It is a Creative Learning Cycle where a variant is dispatched, outcomes are captured, metrics are normalized with evidence, a grader checks confidence and confounds, and only then can an owner decide whether the lesson should shape future creatives.

What is being tracked

Creative variants, ontology card targets, hypothesis refs, network outcomes, KPI observations, confound flags, owner decisions, and feedback residue.

Why tracking matters

It keeps "better creative" from becoming opinion-led or metric-led. A metric must keep its source, denominator, response window, and allowed interpretation.

Where it stops

It stops on missing provenance, unclassified network fields, PII escape, incomplete windows, low confidence, unresolved confounds, or missing owner decision.

Gate Board

This is the tracking logic in checklist form. Each row prevents one kind of false learning, especially the tempting shortcut where a good-looking metric becomes a reusable creative lesson without context.

Tracking question Pass condition Stop condition Why it matters
Is the variant tied to an ontology card and hypothesis? Yes: generation has lineage and target context. No: log but do not measure as a trusted run. Prevents concept erasure: the system knows which audience, family, and objective the metric belongs to.
Did the Network Adapter provide source provenance? Yes: source, endpoint or locator, API context, and captured_at exist. No: reject at ingest. An unsourced outcome cannot become evidence.
Are fields classified by authority class? Aggregate fields compute locally; private fields stay redacted. PII, identifiers, spend, or network-specific metrics escape policy. Separates usable tracking signal from sensitive or unclassified material.
Does the KPI preserve denominator and response window? MetricObservation includes numerator, denominator, period, evidence_refs, and interpretation limit. Window, denominator, or evidence chain is missing. Prevents comparing metrics that only look similar.
Did the Grader resolve confidence and confounds? veracidade is declared and confound flags are absent, resolved, or bounded. Low veracidade plus unresolved confounds does not advance. Keeps metric movement from masquerading as reliable creative learning.
Can a metric promote itself? No: a candidate must carry context, contradiction path, allowed uses, and review. Direct N3 MetricObservation to N4/N6 shortcut is blocked. This is the core metric != authority rule.
Did an owner approve reuse? OwnerDecision records approved allowed uses. No owner record, no KnowledgeProjection. Tracking can recommend, but it does not grant itself authority.

Field Authority Map

The field map is the data-governance part of tracking. It decides which network fields can become aggregate KPI evidence, which fields stay private, and which fields block until a network-specific policy exists.

impressions clicks conversions ctr

Aggregate signal. Compute locally and share only as aggregate KPI evidence.

spend_usd cpc cpa

Business-sensitive financial fields. Store locally; audit access; avoid cloud transit.

audience_target

PII-adjacent audience field. Store locally and redact before promotion.

network_metrics

Network-specific and still hypothesis-bound. Block at dispatch until classified per network.

click_id conversion_event_id

PII-bearing identifiers. Use only inside campaign scope; never export into shared knowledge.

Dependency Boundary

autoads owns the seed design and stubbed proof path. It depends on Ontologize governance rules, future ad-network API research, privacy routing, and human owner decisions before tracking can safely influence real spend or reusable creative knowledge.

Inside autoads tracking design

  • Creative variant shape and lineage.
  • Ontology card target: audience, family, objective.
  • System-agnostic Network Adapter contract.
  • Outcome Measurement and KPI normalization.
  • Grader gates for veracidade, evidence_refs, and confounds.
  • Feedback loop into generator cards and auto-research residue.

Outside or not yet proven

  • One real ad-network outcome API and attribution model.
  • Network-specific response windows and metric semantics.
  • PII and privacy policy decisions for live data.
  • Confound thresholds tuned on real traffic.
  • Owner approval records and allowed-use decisions.
  • Cross-network generalization after a first pilot.

Tracking Maturity Lifecycle

The seed is intentionally not live. The plan makes tracking prove itself in layers so the first working slice can validate the whole loop without live spend, credentials, or PII.

First proof

One ontology card, control plus challenger, stub outcomes, KPI normalization, grader gate, owner decision, and feedback residue.

Validation backlog

H1 schema/windows, H2 confound thresholds, H3 candidate-to-card contract, H4 ads generalization.

Live pilot condition

Run bounded research on one target network's outcome API and attribution model before replacing the stub adapter.

Reader Terms

Reader terms are explanatory, not new canonical definitions. Each term says why it matters and what mistake to avoid while reading the tracking design.

Creative Learning Cycle

Meaning: one loop from generation to tracked outcome to owner-governed feedback.

Why it matters: the tracking design only works if the whole loop closes.

OutcomeEvent

Meaning: a sourced event from dispatch or network outcome capture.

Why it matters: no source, no evidence.

MetricObservation

Meaning: a KPI with denominator, window, veracidade, and evidence refs.

Why it matters: it is evidence, not final authority.

veracidade

Meaning: confidence grade based on evidence outside the metric itself.

Why it matters: high performance is not automatically high trust.

Authority-class register

Meaning: routing policy for fields such as aggregate signals, spend, audience data, and identifiers.

Why it matters: tracking can compute without leaking sensitive source fields.

OwnerDecision

Meaning: human approval for allowed reuse.

Why it matters: tracking may propose a lesson, but ownership decides whether it becomes knowledge.
