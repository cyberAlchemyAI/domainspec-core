# Dual-residue experiment feasibility review

## Conclusion

**NOT-YET-FEASIBLE.** Operationally, this means the experiment is repairable but not presently capable of issuing the verdict its prose and E1 contract promise. It does not mean `MISFIT`: that conclusion would require the repository's purpose or ownership boundary itself to be inappropriate. The current claim contract conflates two comparators, the runnable path does not faithfully implement A or D, the evaluator/reporter cannot enforce the paired statistical gates, and no proposal is actually frozen.

**Contextual/architectural fit: FIT, as this review's architectural assessment of a candidate-local evidence harness.** A separate repository is an appropriate isolation boundary for model/data/runtime evidence and falsification artifacts. It is not DomainSpec authority, theorem authority, or a promotion source. Keeping it adjacent is sensible if every result remains pinned, local, and separately accepted by the canonical owner.

Inventory was used only for discovery and claim ceilings; every disposition below is grounded in current target bytes or the attack command receipts.

## Defect classes and ceilings

- **Conceptual/claim-contract:** comparator identity, `grad_indep` promotion contradiction, undocumented formal-to-empirical bridge assumptions, and ambiguous verdict meaning.
- **Executable/mechanics:** missing DPO, zeroed D weights/no grid search, incorrect joint selector, corpus bypass, denominator mismatch, and unstable loss scaling.
- **Evidence/lifecycle:** absent paired vectors/statistics, fail-open reporter, stale README, absent split files, and no actually frozen source of truth.
- **Authority/promotion:** candidate-local probes, tests, or a future run do not alter DomainSpec definitions, promote an axiom, establish theorem objects, or demonstrate release/production readiness. Upstream acceptance is a separate owner action.

## Dependency-ordered change requests

1. **Select the claim.** Freeze the distinction between primary D-vs-A and secondary D-vs-specialists; remove `grad_indep` and categorical promotion effects. **Gate R1:** an independent reader maps every headline to exactly one comparator, metric, direction, and allowed conclusion.
2. **Select one canonical protocol and verdict algebra.** Resolve top-level/E1/constitution drift, model scale, upstream bindings, PASS/FLAG/BLOCK exhaustiveness, and lifecycle metadata. **Gate R2:** validator plus human review identifies one immutable complete contract and no authoritative mirror.
3. **Repair treatment fidelity.** Implement A's DPO or re-scope it; implement D grid search and fail on null weights; replace best-axis selection; test config-to-objective parity. **Gate R3:** no-training unit/integration witnesses prove each arm activates exactly its declared objective.
4. **Repair corpus custody.** Generate and retain train/dev/test files, verify hashes, prohibit regeneration, and prove dev/test isolation. **Gate R4:** all arm entry points reject missing or mismatched manifest bytes.
5. **Repair metrics and statistics.** Choose the instruction denominator, emit paired per-example vectors, bind negative direction and one-sided bootstrap, and fail closed. **Gate R5:** negative-control fixtures cannot emit PASS; malformed/missing evidence blocks adjudication.
6. **Repair optimization validity.** Verify tokenizer/label/mask alignment and stabilize the sparse, imbalanced instruction-gradient estimator; pre-register zero-gradient and weight-calibration rules. **Gate R6:** focused tests plus a no-network local smoke witness pass in a pinned environment.
7. **Freeze, then run.** Record repository/model/data/runtime hashes, chosen D weights, untouched test boundary, and complete command receipts. **Acceptance gate A1:** four faithful arms finish under the same frozen contract and reporter output is reproducible from raw paired evidence.
8. **Adjudicate locally, then request owner review.** Apply only the frozen rule and publish limitations. **Acceptance gate A2:** canonical DomainSpec owners may accept the result as candidate evidence; no automatic promotion occurs.

## What a repaired run could establish

A repaired, frozen run could establish a local treatment-effect verdict for the pinned Qwen-0.5B model, generated corpus, shallow-schema regime, implementation, seeds, metrics, weights, and chosen comparator. With separate direct probes it could also report gradient geometry at sampled operating points.

It could not establish general scalar-score blindness, categorical irreducibility, universal gradient independence, production rich-schema behavior, 8B replication, DomainSpec-wide validity, theorem promotion, release, deployment, or production readiness. Those require different objects, successor experiments, replications, and explicit canonical-owner gates.
