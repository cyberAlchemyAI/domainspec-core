Who We Are

We are a research and engineering group focused on the intersection of autonomous systems, formal governance, and applied cybernetics. At CyberAlchemy AI, we don't just build software to automate tasks—we build architectures designed to understand and steer complex environments.

What We Believe

We believe that research is fundamentally a method for discovering and mapping unknown domains. In this paradigm, any software constructed is just a tool for this discovery.

Code is not the end product; it is the observational probe we send into a domain to chart its territory, measure its dynamics, and return empirical evidence.

How We Work: Cybernetics²

We operate under a principle we call Cybernetics².

Because we deeply harness and research cybernetic theory—the study of regulatory systems, feedback loops, and control—we apply those same principles to the very tools we build. We engineer systems that act as cybernetic loops to human economic activity: tracking intent, interpreting reality, verifying alignment, and feeding that data back to the human operators.
