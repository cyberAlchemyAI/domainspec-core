# Body War OIDC session policy
Finite witnesses for active, idle, absolute and ended session boundaries and fresh authentication timestamps. The provider runs the compiled pure policy imported by the real OIDC boundary. It does not certify OIDC protocol or provider linking; those have separate real-browser tests. A compiled mutation accepting equality at both deadlines must fail exactly two cases.
