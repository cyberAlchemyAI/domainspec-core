## Definition baseline

### Problem

The accepted foundation defines a candidate Tower Evidence Navigator but leaves the first buildable boundary broad. The immediate problem is to plan a private, falsifiable research package that converts one exact tower manifest into a disposable candidate projection without claiming complete corpus coverage, semantic authority, representative selection, or an IDE implementation.

### First Work Pack objective

Author a non-executed plan for candidate path `projects/cyberalchemy-lab/research/tower-evidence-navigator/` that:

1. preserves the Research Tower package spine;
2. defines machine-readable snapshot, claim, anchor, ontology, relation, validation, privacy, and blocked-promotion records;
3. proves the contract with one explicitly synthetic, complete, mechanics-only toy tower and deterministic failure mutants;
4. plans the exact owner prerequisite for one real, complete selected tower;
5. plans a deterministic privacy-filtered projection compiler;
6. reserves, but does not implement, a direct-GET interface for a later IDE-owner Work Pack.

The synthetic fixture is test evidence only. It cannot establish corpus coverage, representativeness, source usefulness, or readiness for the real one-tower witness.

- No claim to combine or cover all repository research.
- No conversion of every research directory into a tower.

- No T04 selection and no T26 representativeness claim.

## Representative-tower decision packet

The future corpus owner must choose exactly one disposition:

- `select-t04`: provide owner identity, complete ordered manifest, byte sizes and digests, tower logical ID, privacy class, exclusions, and why the tower is representative for the bounded witness;
- `retain-t26-mechanics-only`: preserve all provisional/nonrepresentative labels, supply any additional complete manifest material only for mechanics, and expressly deny representative status;
- `select-other`: identify the eligible tower and provide the same complete manifest, identity, privacy, and rationale fields.

The decision must bind exact bytes and state expiry/reselection conditions. Silence, earlier preference, digest equality, or a synthetic passing fixture cannot select a tower.
