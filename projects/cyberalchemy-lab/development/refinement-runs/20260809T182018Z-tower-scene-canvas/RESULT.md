# Tower Scene Canvas Refine result

## Decision

Plan the CyberAlchemy Lab as an open **evidence scene canvas**, starting with the existing one synthetic tower rather than a game engine or a simulated research runtime. The first implementation unit is a source-bound scene manifest; scenes come after the manifest passes negative validation.

## Design that survived review

- `tower:toy` is the only initial tower.
- Evidence progression scenes: `evidence-l0-corpus`, `evidence-l1-residue`, `evidence-l2-closure`.
- Tower System architecture descriptors are separately namespaced (`system-l1-*` through `system-l4-*`) and visibly `realized`, `candidate`, or `unproven`; unavailable ones are explanatory, never simulated.
- Native tower/level buttons, detail dock, status/refusal panels, explicit focus/reset controls, mobile semantic strip, and reduced-motion semantic parity are core. The animated lab/robots remain decor.
- Existing HTML/CSS/JS remains sufficient for the planned first slice. No engine is selected.

## Evidence and validation

Inventory lookup readiness passed. The registered two-role local review dispatch completed read-only:

1. `receipts/tower-semantics-mapper.md` mapped allowed and forbidden scenes, found label-axis collision risk, and required a manifest.
2. `receipts/spatial-prototype-critic.md` supplied P0 accessibility/interaction constraints and future browser acceptance criteria.

`REFINE-DISPATCH.json` passed Dispatch Spec validation. No external research, product mutation, source/fixture mutation, authority change, promotion, publication, deployment, commit, push, or other Git-history mutation occurred. The only non-run-local change is the required open/close pair appended to `telemetry/agents/subagents-dispatch.yaml` for the completed read-only review dispatch.

## Complete residue / blockers

- A multi-tower manifest and its loader do not exist yet.
- The current projection is flat synthetic L0 and cannot select level scenes.
- Representative real-tower selection remains open; `promotion:toy` is blocked.
- Persona-as-artifact, runtime-queryable graph, live MOGT use, and ELO/oracle-consensus are not present as Lab runtime capabilities.
- Browser accessibility and responsive acceptance remain future validation, not established by static review.
- A later engine decision is deferred until the DOM/CSS work pack demonstrably fails a defined requirement.
