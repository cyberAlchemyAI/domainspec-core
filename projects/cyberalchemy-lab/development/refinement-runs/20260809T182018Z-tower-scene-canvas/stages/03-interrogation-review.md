# 03 — Interrogation refine review

## Review synthesis

The two source-backed reviews agree on the selected first slice:

- make one synthetic tower expose three evidence-level scenes;
- give the evidence progression and Tower System architecture different namespace-qualified IDs;
- bind every selectable scene to sources, state, ceiling, and an unavailable/refusal presentation;
- preserve native controls and a text-order interaction surface alongside the visual canvas.

## Stable disagreements retained as residue

The semantic reviewer classifies current Tower System L2 construction and parts of L3/L4 as prior-work/governance evidence. The interaction reviewer correctly insists that no such evidence makes them live features of the Lab canvas. The plan resolves this by using `implementation_status` per architecture descriptor and rendering every unimplemented system layer as unavailable, read-only explanatory content.

## Design requirements accepted

1. A manifest is required before scene implementation.
2. `tower:toy` is the only initially selectable tower.
3. All level and camera controls are native buttons; neither robots nor canvas position navigate semantic content.
4. Decoration is paused/hidden independently from semantic state.
5. Missing/unproven content is visible as unavailable rather than silently omitted or simulated.

No new external research is needed; no product implementation is authorized by this review.
