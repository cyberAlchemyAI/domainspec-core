# 08 — Distill repair

## Repaired contract rules

1. `semantic_axis` is mandatory and closed: `evidence_progression` or `tower_system_architecture`.
2. `level_id` is prefixed by its axis: `evidence-*` or `system-*`.
3. Evidence scenes require `source_refs`, `evidence_state`, and `claim_ceiling`.
4. Architecture scenes require `implementation_status` and `unavailable_reason`; only explicitly current local capabilities may be marked realized, and none becomes an active Lab runtime.
5. Any violation fails the manifest build and produces a visible unavailable panel. It cannot fall back to decorative art.
6. Page controls are DOM buttons first; canvas placement is a view enhancement.

## Future validation matrix

- loader unit test: missing source ref, collision, bare numeric level, and authority-effect violations reject;
- page integration: one tower, three evidence levels, selected-state behavior, and refusal behavior;
- browser: keyboard, mobile, reduced-motion, focus/reset orientation, no page errors;
- scoped check: current synthetic-only claim ceiling and no new dependency/import.
