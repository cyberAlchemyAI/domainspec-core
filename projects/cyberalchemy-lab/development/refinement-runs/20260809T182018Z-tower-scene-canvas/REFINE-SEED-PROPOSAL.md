# Refine seed — open tower scene canvas

## Intent

Turn the existing one-tower, synthetic Research Tower Explorer into a planned open-canvas direction: many tower landmarks can be navigated, and each selected evidence level becomes a scene rather than another dashboard card.

## Bounded first witness

`tower:toy` remains the only current selectable tower. Its three evidence scenes are:

| Evidence progression | Scene | What it may show | What it must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 Corpus | Corpus Observatory | source snapshots, locks, provenance, evidence anchors | representative coverage or a real corpus |
| L1 Residue | Residue Conservatory | open questions, blocked claims, scope | a resolved decision or an agent action |
| L2 Closure | Closure Workshop | named next-owner route and validation requirements | that closure, promotion, or implementation occurred |

The Tower System stack is a separate, future map. It may be rendered only with explicit `realized`, `candidate`, or `unproven` labels: L1 substrate; L2 construction; L3 application; Lphi decision policy; L4 adjudication.

## Success criterion

Produce a compact plan and scene-contract proposal that allows one synthetic, evidence-bound vertical slice to grow into multiple towers without inventing personas, runtime graphs, verdicts, or authority. The plan must name data contracts, navigation, motion/accessibility behavior, tests, and refusal states.
