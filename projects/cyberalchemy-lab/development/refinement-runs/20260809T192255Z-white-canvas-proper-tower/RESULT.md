### Result

The selected direction is one **White Tower View**: a warm-white architectural workspace with a single vertical **Synthetic Evidence Tower**, three evidence floors, an anchored detail surface, and a separate Tower System reference. The tower is a navigation model over one locked synthetic fixture—not a representative corpus, live research runtime, game world, or authority surface.
