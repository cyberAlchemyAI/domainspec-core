# CyberAlchemy Lab Foundation Research Strategy

## Project Decision Before Research

CyberAlchemy Lab is proposed as a governed local research workbench, not as a
new authority system. Its intended job is to make source-backed Research Tower
knowledge navigable through a candidate ontology projection and the existing
IDE workbench while keeping the canonical research files, evidence, approvals,
receipts, and promotion owners distinct.

The phrase "all research in the repository" is a coverage objective, not a
permission to turn every file into a node. A raw preflight found 41 `TOWER.md`
paths only when ignored/generated paths are included. That set mixes live
candidates with worktree copies, output/test fixtures, handoff copies, nested
repository surfaces, and possible content duplicates. The research must define
eligibility, identity, version, duplicate, supersession, privacy, and residue
rules before it can make a completeness statement.

The smallest defensible initial witness is:

```text
one existing Research Tower
  -> one candidate-local ontology projection
  -> one read-only Tower Navigator in the IDE Canvas
  -> source path + digest + selector evidence
```

It excludes personas, scoring, automatic promotion, bulk ingestion, provider
execution, remote/multi-user behavior, publication, deployment, and claims of
human usefulness.

## P1 Trigger Decision

`dispatch` is justified by all four triggers:

- **Synthesis:** Tower System, Research Tower, repository corpus, IDE workbench,
  Inventory/Ontology, and external precedent must be reconciled.
- **Context protection:** repository-wide manifests and source traces would be
  much larger than the parent context.
- **Isolation:** corpus, runtime, and ontology hypotheses must be independently
  attackable and discardable.
- **Parallelism:** the three explorer angles and three skeptic gates are
  independently executable within their groups.

Dispatch type is live `research`; evidence contract is `exact-binding` because
path, digest, selector, version, graph identity, and workbench revision are
load-bearing.

## Dependency Flow

```text
explorers (parallel)
  -> synthesizer
  <-> reviewers (zig-zag cap 1)
  -> exact-binding evidence closure
  -> writer
  -> auditor
  -> parent final approval
```

Repair edges:

- synthesizer -> explorers: feedback cap 1 for missing source/provenance/
  selector/runtime evidence;
- auditor -> writer: feedback cap 1 for citation, structure, wording, or verdict
  presentation when evidence already exists.

Whole-dispatch `max_loops` is 2. Missing evidence with no eligible edge or
remaining capacity blocks the writer; no `findings.md` is manufactured.

## Research Tower Composition

`research-tower` shapes the next durable foundation package after accepted
findings. The findings must define the inputs for:

- source record and exact source boundary;
- `README.md` and `TOWER.md`;
- L0 corpus, L1 residue map, and L2 closure plan;
- closure gates;
- notation bridge where required;
- claim ledger with source-kind labels;
- glossary and governed local definitions;
- bridge decisions (`borrow-carefully`, `analogy-only`, `block`,
  `promotion-candidate`, `future-work`);
- final learning pack;
- explicit subagent closeout ledger.

That later package remains local research. It does not itself implement the IDE
adapter, create canonical ontology authority, or promote definitions.

## What Confirmation Does And Does Not Authorize

Replying `confirmed` authorizes registration and execution of the exact
research dispatch bound to the digest above. It authorizes source reads and the
declared bounded external precedent research, plus creation of `research.md`,
`findings.md`, receipts, and closeout evidence in the working folder.

It does **not** authorize the initial product implementation, ontology
promotion, bulk ingestion, public Arcanum extraction, publication, deployment,
release, credentials, or production changes. Accepted findings will expose the
smallest candidate-local creation Work Pack; execution of that Work Pack stays
a separate gate because a research result is not implementation authority.
