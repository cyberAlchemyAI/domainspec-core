A **Research Tower package** is rooted at `TOWER.md` and is eligible when it has a coherent research target and a navigational or contractual spine. Eligibility is independent of current scaffold conformance, closure, canonical ownership, representativeness, and publication class. A **research artifact** is a bounded, addressable output with declared producer, scope, provenance, content identity, status, and claim ceiling; existence does not prove truth, closure, usefulness, or authority. ## Candidate ontology contract

All records below are candidate-local and carry `authority_effect: none`.

- `SourceSnapshot`: logical source ID, content digest, path/locator, bytes, media type, capture context, owner, privacy class, and revision relations.
- `TowerPackageSnapshot`: tower logical ID plus canonical ordered artifact manifest and source bindings. A path or `TOWER.md` digest alone is incomplete.
- `Claim`: one explicit falsifiable proposition with owner-preserved logical ID, scope, status, and claim ceiling.
- `EvidenceAnchor`: selector resolving against exactly one immutable source snapshot, with evidentiary role; digest mismatch or selector failure invalidates current use.
- `OntologyCandidate`: proposed meaning-bearing record with scope, provenance, explicit evidence ceiling, review state, contradiction path, and no authority effect.
- `ValidationRecord`: immutable exact-input witness binding validator/profile/version, environment, command, result, violations, scope, and time. Passing proves only that check.
- `PromotionRecord`: named owner's disposition over one identified primary candidate claim and exact evidence, including gate result, use scope, contradiction path, expiry, rollback/retirement, and decision reference; it documents but does not generate authority.

A `Relation` is typed, directed or explicitly symmetric, and records admitted endpoint types, provenance, cardinality, cycle policy, and status. Required candidate relations are `package_contains`, `anchored_by`, `supports`, `references_definition`, projection `derived_from` every projected snapshot ID, and promotion `blocked_by` open residue. Co-occurrence and equal bytes create no semantic relation. [research.md@sha256:29d7a80d1ceeb1823b7bbd74579bf167167d4a66e4b6aa7f04ef6b8139e021e6#L537-L570]

## Identity, version, provenance, contradiction, and authority rules

1. Logical identity and snapshot identity are separate: `source_snapshot_id = H(logical_source_id, content_digest)`, `tower_snapshot_id = H(tower_logical_id, canonical ordered artifact manifest)`, and projection revision binds the complete input snapshot set, projection rules, and generator contract.
2. Digest equality proves byte duplication, not semantic identity, canonical ownership, lineage, or supersession. Preserve durable owner IDs; otherwise use an explicit sidecar registry.
3. A changed source creates a new immutable snapshot. Historical anchors remain historical; current dependent claims become challenged, promotion is blocked pending review, and projections are regenerated or invalidated. Never rewrite history.
4. Contradiction is an explicit relation between scoped claims with provenance and status. It blocks dependent promotion until adjudicated; it does not silently delete either claim.
5. Missing selectors, digest drift, dangling relations, privacy-filter failure, stale projection revisions, or changed validator inputs invalidate current use, not prior records.
6. Residue is first-class and must remain addressable, scoped, and linked to the claim or gate it blocks.
7. Evidence volume, validation, telemetry, Inventory records, projections, Canvas events, silence, and earlier preference cannot create authority. Only the named owner can issue an authority decision over exact candidate bytes.
8. Promotion remains blocked until exact inputs, owner, scope, contradiction handling, expiry/rollback, privacy/publication boundary, and a separate decision are present. [research.md@sha256:29d7a80d1ceeb1823b7bbd74579bf167167d4a66e4b6aa7f04ef6b8139e021e6#L470-L485,L509-L521]

## One-tower read-only witness contract

The next witness is **specified but unimplemented**:

1. The corpus owner supplies exactly one complete ordered tower manifest and immutable snapshot ID; initially T26 may exercise mechanics only.
2. A deterministic compiler accepts the allowlisted manifest, locked source snapshots, one source-backed claim, one definition reference, one explicit residue record, selector-bound anchors, one claim candidate, and a blocked `PromotionRecord`.
3. It emits a disposable, privacy-filtered projection with forward/reverse adjacency and revision `H(inputs, rules, generator contract)`.
4. A fixed trusted shell obtains that exact projection by direct GET and renders navigation only. It performs no persistence, Canvas publication, source write, Git action, external request, validation approval, or promotion.
5. The shell displays source digest, selector, owner, privacy class, claim ceiling, contradiction/residue status, projection revision, and stale/invalid state. Unknown kinds or relations are rejected.

Deterministic negative tests must reject: changed source digest; missing selector; duplicate path with same content treated as identity; dangling relation; unsupported record kind; private-source exposure; stale projection revision; validation without exact bound inputs; projection-attempted promotion; POST/publication; non-allowlisted tower; manifest reorder without new snapshot ID; and contradiction or open residue presented as cleared. All remain **not executed**. [research.md@sha256:29d7a80d1ceeb1823b7bbd74579bf167167d4a66e4b6aa7f04ef6b8139e021e6#L537-L574]
