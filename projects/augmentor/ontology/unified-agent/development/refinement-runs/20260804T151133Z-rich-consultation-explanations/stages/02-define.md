# Stage 02: Invoke Define

- Capability: `invoke define`
- Verdict: pass
- Target owner cycle: Augmentor consultation and answering-agent integration

## Problem

The current consultation path is safe and source-linked, but an agent receives too little structured help to explain the system deeply. The richest beginner fields never cross the projection. A caller must reconstruct current state, examples, analogies, and vocabulary by itself, which encourages dry answers or unsupported bridging claims.

## Definition

`Rich explanation support` is a closed, read-only, source-bound addition to a consultation result. It gives the answering agent enough structured material to explain one supported subject in `brief`, `guided`, or `deep` form. It never generates the final prose and never changes the status, authority, or meaning of the evidence.

## Required User Outcome

An ordinary user receives:

1. a direct answer;
2. a truthful account of what exists now;
3. a process they can retell;
4. one clearly labeled scenario when useful;
5. one analogy with an immediate limit when useful;
6. two to five definitions for terms actually used;
7. the important boundary and source handles.

## Requirements

- `currentState` must state its scope and cannot turn ontology posture into live product truth.
- Example kind is structural: `illustrative` or `source-derived`.
- Every analogy has a separate non-empty limitation.
- Glossary entries are plain-first, evidence-linked, usage-minimal, and `authorityEffect:none`.
- Projection, glossary, and explanation support share one bundle digest and atomic install.
- Retrieved text remains non-instructional evidence.
- Profiles change depth and payload only; they cannot change truth, status, or authority.
- Truncation removes whole explanation units and reports omissions.
- Final prose remains agent-owned.

## Non-Goals

- Building or claiming an integrated reasoning engine.
- Changing routing, memory, decision, or effect authority.
- Adding model calls to the consultation service.
- Creating a canonical definitions source.
- Deploying or publishing the change.
- Proving human comprehension without a later measured study.

## Dialectic Result

The explanation-depth advocate requested state, process, examples, analogies, and glossary support. The authority guardian required closed fields, explicit scopes, source handles, non-instruction roles, no final prose, and semantic truncation. They converge on a typed explanation-support bundle rather than more free-form prose.
