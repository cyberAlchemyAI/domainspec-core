Craft is a way to make outputs answer back.

The simple version: a model does not merely obey a prompt. It translates an intention through a schema into an artifact. Craft keeps the residue visible, then decides whether to repair, stop, route, or open another layer.

Ledger Bridge

The philosophy becomes useful only when it has rows.

The current canonical Craft package keeps the runtime architecture intentionally small: a project-local `.craft/ledger.yml` is source of truth; `CRAFT.md` and `.craft/index.json` are derived views. That is why the mental model lands in these row families.

contexts Where the making is happening Scopes the local aim, child contexts, stage, gate, and current next move.
typed_items Where blockers get shape Turns a vague stop into base type, lane, closure condition, evidence, and role hints.
decisions Where authority enters Keeps question, options, status, rationale, selected option, impact, and evidence visible.
recomposition Where local work returns Prevents a child proof from disappearing without changing the parent context.

Philosophical Aspect

Residue is not waste. It is evidence.

The deeper Craft thesis is that making is a general transformation pattern. But the page keeps the honesty boundary: the universal physics language is an inference and research horizon, not a proven property of every run.

1. Operational Track schema, artifact, validation, residue, decisions, blockers, and next moves.
2. Formal Model work as recursive structure-preserving translation with entropy and recomposition.
3. Philosophical Understand making as possible form becoming manifested instance under constraint.
4. Horizon Treat a universal physics of craft as a research program that must earn force through local evidence.

Reader Terms

Plain-language anchors before local jargon.

This prototype is for someone learning Craft. These terms are explanatory, not canonical definitions. They keep the mental model readable while pointing back to the source-backed architecture and initial formal model.

reader terms Translation The whole act of turning intention and schema into an artifact through a model, tools, context, and validation path.
why it matters Residue The meaningful remainder after the output exists: drift, missing structure, ambiguity, contradiction, or validation gap.
reader outcomes Smallest Coherent Unit The smallest piece of work that still has enough meaning, local validation, and a return path into the larger work.

Evidence Boundary

What this page claims, and what it only suggests.

The artifact uses source-backed material from the two Craft development HTML files and the canonical Craft README/architecture. The interaction design and teaching sequence are inference, meant to make the model learnable.

Source-backed

  • Craft uses `.craft/ledger.yml` as source of truth and derives `CRAFT.md` plus optional indexes.
  • The formal tuple is C = (I, S, F, E, D, R, G, V).
  • Residue can lead to repair, stop criteria, routing, or reflection tower work.
  • Current canonical Craft architecture is intentionally small and does not ship a public CLI or renderer.

Inference

  • The best first teaching surface is an interactive mental model rather than a document outline.
  • Showing philosophical claims as a ladder reduces overclaiming while preserving ambition.
  • Clicking tuple elements helps readers connect the formal model to ledger rows.
  • The visual style borrows the Studio prototype posture but is purpose-built for Craft.
