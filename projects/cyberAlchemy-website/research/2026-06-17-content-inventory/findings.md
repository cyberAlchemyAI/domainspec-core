# CyberAlchemy Website Selection Findings

Dispatch id: `2026-06-17-cyberalchemy-website-content-intake`

Final verdict: `resolved`.

## Editorial Law

CyberAlchemy can be published now as a capability-foundry method and trust
discipline for transforming intent into evidenced artifacts. It must not be
published as a shipped end-to-end runtime, security system, or universal product
capability unless each claim is separately source-backed.

Every page must preserve:

- authority type
- claim type
- source anchors
- public-boundary status
- proof label or built/designed/planned/open status
- website role
- explanation method
- transformation need

HTML and PDF artifacts are form evidence unless factual claims are re-anchored
to owned sources. Private/session/project material may guide strategy, but public
copy must be abstracted, anonymized, re-anchored, and constrained by
claim <= proof.

## Authority Types

- `canonical source`: source can support public factual claims after normal
  writing review.
- `public documentation`: public-safe docs or pages that can support public
  claims, with line/source checks.
- `internal evidence`: useful for strategy, but not public copy without
  abstraction and re-anchoring.
- `presentation artifact`: useful for page shape, rhythm, visuals, or rhetoric;
  factual claims require independent anchors.
- `analogy pattern`: useful for offer/page structure; not CyberAlchemy factual
  authority.

## Claim Status Labels

- `Built`: verified by owned source evidence as implemented or operating.
  Documentation alone is not enough.
- `Designed`: an architecture, method, or model is specified, but operating
  implementation is not established.
- `Planned`: roadmap, intended pilot, or next capability.
- `Open`: unresolved proof boundary or active research question.

If a claim spans labels, use the weaker label.

## Rejected Or Deferred

- Raw session text: internal evidence only.
- Raw dispatch mechanics: internal operation only; public wording is structured
  critique loops.
- Broad shipped-system claims: defer until separately backed.
- General security claims: replace with scoped decision-boundary discipline.
- Full operational ontology runtime authority: defer or heavily caveat.
- MARS/MOGT/Saturn-style research claims: defer until source-audited.
- PDF factual claims: defer until extraction and audit.
- CyberAlchemy HTML factual claims: defer until claim audit against owned
  sources.
