# Hellbender Offering-Start Summary

Status: internal candidate summary for starting an offering. Public-source only.

## Executive Read

Hellbender is a strong strategic fit for CyberAlchemy/Ontologize because its public process is already
a closed physical AI learning loop: architecture decisions, prototypes, productization, DFM/DVT,
manufacturing, QA/calibration, deployment, CVaaS/RaaS management, and field-driven evolution.

The right sale is not "AI strategy." It is **governed process intelligence for physical AI scale**:
one metric Hellbender already cares about, rebuilt from source events with denominator semantics,
evidence references, confidence, data boundaries, and an owner-approved path from operational evidence
to reusable knowledge.

## Recommended First Offer

**Physical AI Lifecycle Intelligence Pilot**

For one Hellbender product line, production loop, or CVaaS deployment, CyberAlchemy maps the lifecycle
ontology, installs a data boundary register, rebuilds one priority metric from source evidence, and
creates a promotion graph that turns manufacturing, field, or service evidence into owner-approved
reusable knowledge.

Best first wedge: **EOL QA/calibration to field performance to sustaining engineering**.

Why this wedge:

- It connects manufacturing, product quality, customer-visible reliability, and the Evolve support
  promise.
- It can start from structured metadata rather than raw images, customer IP, or sensitive design
  files.
- It naturally produces evidence-linked metrics: calibration yield, EOL escape rate, rework
  recurrence, time to root cause, field incident resolution, and value-engineering savings.
- It has a clear owner-decision path: quality/manufacturing/product/sustaining engineering approve
  what becomes a reusable rule, what stays local, and what is rejected.

## Offer Wedges

1. **Production Truth Layer**
   Rebuild one manufacturing metric: first-pass yield, calibration pass rate, rework recurrence, EOL
   escape rate, or root-cause cycle time. Buyer: COO, VP Manufacturing, Head of Quality.

2. **DFM/DVT Learning Loop**
   Trace prototype-to-productize evidence into approved design/manufacturing lessons. Buyer: CTO, VP
   Engineering, Director of Productization.

3. **Evolve Feedback Register**
   Map field issues into candidate changes, owner decisions, and approved/rejected/private residue.
   Buyer: COO, Head of Product, Sustaining Engineering.

4. **CVaaS Data Boundary and Trust Layer**
   Define what telemetry is private, internal, shareable, reusable, or restricted for service
   improvement and model/product updates. Buyer: Chief Growth Officer, Head of Product, security
   owner.

5. **Custom-to-Platform Knowledge Transfer**
   Turn repeated custom-project lessons into evidence-backed platform assumptions without leaking
   customer-specific knowledge. Buyer: CEO, CTO, Chief Growth Officer.

## Pitch Narrative

Hellbender already knows how to build physical AI systems. CyberAlchemy is for the next scaling
problem: making the intelligence behind that full-lifecycle process explicit, defensible, and
reusable.

We start with one metric your team already cares about. We trace it to source events, define what it
counts and excludes, assign confidence, set the privacy boundary, and create the owner-reviewed path
for lessons to become reusable knowledge. The first pilot is deliberately small: one product line or
production loop, one lifecycle stage, one proof metric, one governed learning loop.

## Discovery Questions

1. Where in Architect, Prototype, Productize, Manufacture, Evolve, or CVaaS do decisions most often
   become hard to reconstruct later?
2. Which number does leadership or a customer already use but not fully trust?
3. What real-world event creates the metric record, and what is the denominator?
4. Which systems contain the source events: MES, ERP, manufacturing DB, QA/EOL logs, calibration logs,
   field tickets, model registry, CRM, or customer success tooling?
5. Which data can be used in a metadata-first pilot, and which must stay restricted?
6. Who can approve a lesson as reusable across product lines or customers?
7. What decision improves if the metric becomes auditable?

## Risks And Guardrails

| Risk | Guardrail |
| --- | --- |
| Data/IP access blocked by client contracts, customer IP, regulated data, or security review. | Metadata-first pilot; no raw images or design files; fail-closed boundary register; local/on-prem option. |
| Compliance overclaim. | Position as operational evidence governance only; do not claim compliance, certification, audit substitution, or security certification. |
| Wrong proof metric. | Start from a sponsor-owned number tied to DFM, DVT, QA, calibration, field reliability, service uptime, or root cause. |
| Run boundary ambiguity. | Decide product line, lot, device, fleet, customer deployment, or retrain window before metric work. |
| Cross-client leakage. | Tenant/customer boundary in ontology; no cross-client promotion without explicit owner approval and reuse rights. |
| Raw vision/model data scope explosion. | Start on structured operational metadata, QA/calibration outputs, failure tags, and approved logs. |
| Delivery lane mismatch. | Default Hybrid: data and dashboards stay in Hellbender tools; governed knowledge/promotion receipts live in the CyberAlchemy layer. |
| Cross-vertical proof gap. | Label as founding pilot; do not claim repeatable product proof until a non-grant pilot validates it. |
