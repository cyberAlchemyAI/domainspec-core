# Operational-ontology definitions (candidate)

Step `define-operational-branch` (`definitions-governance`). Candidate definitions; none promoted.
Each marks status: **SB** source-backed · **H** hypothesis. Link: [CARRY-SET.md](CARRY-SET.md).

- **Operational ontology branch** — the branch of knowledge about *agent operation*: how routes,
  capabilities, prompts, disciplines, and decisions are run and improved over time. Distinct from
  the per-run `run-context` branch (B3). A **candidate extension**, not a promoted branch. [SB
  ONTOLOGY-ARCHITECTURE.md; RECONCILIATION.md §1]

- **Operational axiom (L7)** — a load-bearing invariant of how the system must operate (the "why"),
  on which operational governance depends. Promoted only via PromotionRecord with strong evidence,
  dependency + contradiction review, and explicit commitment. [SB BUSINESS-ONTOLOGY.md; PROMOTION-LIFECYCLE.md:99]

- **Operational constitution (L6)** — enforceable governance of operating form/model/transformations
  and review gates that preserve operational axioms (the "how"). [SB BUSINESS-ONTOLOGY.md; PROMOTION-LIFECYCLE.md:98]

- **Operational discipline** — a recurring cross-capability operating practice catalogued in
  `DISCIPLINES.md`. It is the **discovery/evidence layer** that *feeds* operational policy/
  constitution; it cannot itself claim promotion authority. [SB DISCIPLINES.md:53-55, README:28-33]

- **Operational policy (L5)** — a promoted operating rule within a defined use scope, with owner and
  rollback path. The first promoted tier above candidate. [SB PROMOTION-LIFECYCLE.md]

- **Candidate operational node (L3)** — a telemetry- or literature-seeded operating fact with branch,
  scope, evidence, confidence, and visible candidate status. The ceiling for telemetry-only evidence. [SB]

- **Operational mutation** — a recorded change in operating state (route run, capability invoked,
  decision gated, reflection triggered, gap found; and the not-yet-instrumented "discipline hardened"
  and "lesson promoted"). [SB / H]

- **Telemetry (operational)** — non-authoritative L0–L2 evidence (signals, envelopes, receipts) that
  may seed candidate nodes but may not be promoted without Constitution Governance + ontology-harness
  review. [SB ONTOLOGY-ARCHITECTURE.md:323]

## The disciplines-as-axiom relationship (definitional)

> An operational **discipline** *feeds* → an operational **policy** *hardens into* → an operational
> **constitution** (the "how") which *is justified by* → an operational **axiom** (the "why").

`DISCIPLINES.md` is the **discipline** node, not the apex. The apex (constitution + axiom) is the
operational branch's highest knowledge category, owned by Constitution Governance. [verdict:
[decisions/disciplines-as-axiom.md](decisions/disciplines-as-axiom.md)]
