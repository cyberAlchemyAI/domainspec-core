# Decision: Is DISCIPLINES.md the highest knowledge category for the operational branch?

Step `adjudicate-disciplines-as-axiom` (`decision-gate`, dialectic). Owner of record for any
promotion: **Constitution Governance + ontology-harness** (this gate assigns no authority).
Evidence: [sessions/operational-analysis-receipts.md](../sessions/operational-analysis-receipts.md),
[levels/L1-authority-map.md](../levels/L1-authority-map.md).

## Question

H0: should `arcanum/disciplines/DISCIPLINES.md` be the **highest category of knowledge** for the
operational ontology branch, on the same tier as **axioms (L7)** and **constitutions (L6)**?

## Proponent position

The operational branch needs a stable top layer of "how we operate" truths; DISCIPLINES.md is the
one place that already names cross-capability operating practices. Some are already `canonical`.
Treating that catalog as the branch's apex would give agents a single highest-authority reference.

## Skeptic position

Disciplines are explicitly **forbidden** from claiming promotion authority and **cannot directly
promote ontology knowledge** [DISCIPLINES.md:55]; they sit on a separate maturity ladder, not the
L0–L7 authority ladder. Axioms/constitutions are *load-bearing authority with rollback paths and
contradiction review*; disciplines are a *discovery/evidence layer*. Equating them collapses
L1–L3 into L7 and creates **circular governance** (the `artifact-constitution` discipline already
governs `disciplines/**`). The operational branch is itself only a **candidate extension** pending
user/ontology-harness review.

## Verdict — H0 is REFUTED as stated; REFRAMED and ACCEPTED in its intent

**The highest category of the operational branch is an operational _constitution + axiom_ set
(L6/L7). `DISCIPLINES.md` is the disciplined _source / discovery layer_ that feeds that set — it
is not, and must not be, the authority tier itself.**

This honors the user's intent ("a highest category of knowledge for operations, like axioms and
constitutions") while staying inside the boundaries the literature already enforces. It is
**precedent-backed**: `schema` and `artifact-constitution` are canonical disciplines *backed by*
the Schema and Artifact Constitutions — disciplines that became/required constitutions. The same
path applies to operational practices: a recurring operational discipline → PromotionRecord →
operational policy → operational constitution → (rarely) operational axiom.

```
operational telemetry (L0-L2)  →  operational discipline (catalog, evidence)
        →  PromotionRecord  →  operational policy (L5)
        →  operational CONSTITUTION (L6, "how")  ⟵ derived from  →  operational AXIOM (L7, "why")
```

`DISCIPLINES.md` lives at the *discipline* node of that chain. The **apex** is the operational
constitution/axiom set, owned by Constitution Governance.

## Conditions attached to the verdict (binding on the seed)

1. The seeded operational branch is a **candidate extension**, labeled pending user/ontology-harness
   review — not a promoted fourth branch. [ONTOLOGY-ARCHITECTURE.md:79,139]
2. Telemetry seeds **candidate (L3) nodes only**; nothing promotes past L3 without Constitution
   Governance + ontology-harness review.
3. Avoid the **circular-governance trap**: an operational constitution that governs disciplines may
   not take a governed discipline as its sole authority source; authority flows through PromotionRecord.
4. Disciplines may **recommend routes / catalog patterns**, never promote ontology, constitution, or
   axiom knowledge directly.
