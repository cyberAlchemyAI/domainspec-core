## Decision Verdict

Proceed as a buyer-discovery and readiness experiment that can become a manual-first, evidence-bounded service only when the buyer passes strict gates. Do not present Ontologize, Autoads, or Maestro-Trama as a finished marketing platform, autonomous ad buyer, or proven performance system. The credible commercial shape is a **One-Offer Lead Evidence Sprint**: one offer, one buyer segment, one conversion action, one primary channel execution path, an optional second-channel brief/readiness pass, one capped response window, and one owner-reviewed scorecard. [R1][R3][R5][R6]

The offer should sell disciplined uncertainty reduction, not software or growth certainty. The usable outcome is what available evidence supports keeping, changing, pausing, retesting, or marking inconclusive. That framing fits the substrate because Ontologize contributes metric-trust discipline, Autoads contributes the experiment-loop concept, and Maestro-Trama contributes creative-performance vocabulary and historical creative-learning concepts, while the combined system remains pre-commercial. [R1][R2][R3]

The no-go boundary is equally important: do not claim guaranteed leads, CPA/ROAS improvement, SEO ranking lift, autonomous campaign management, cross-channel attribution truth, or proven buyer demand. Those claims are not supported by the research substrate, and platform documentation reinforces that TikTok and Google require proper event/conversion setup before performance interpretation, while Google Search does not guarantee crawling, indexing, serving, or ranking. [R3][R4][Parent Official/Public Source Verification]

## Buyer-Facing Offer

**Offer name:** One-Offer Lead Evidence Sprint. Alternative buyer-safe name: Decision Scorecard Sprint. [R5][R3][R6]

**Buyer-facing sentence:** We help you turn one offer into a small TikTok or Google/SEO evidence test, then give you an owner-reviewed scorecard showing what the available evidence supports keeping, changing, pausing, retesting, or calling inconclusive. [R2][R5][R6]

**Buyer promise:** Help the owner choose one offer, define one conversion action, package it for TikTok creative response or Google intent capture, inspect evidence after a capped window, and make one recommendation without pretending the result proves long-term channel performance. A second channel can be prepared as a brief/readiness path, but execution should stay one-channel until operator time, tracking, and budget tolerance are witnessed. [R2][R5][R6]

**The buyer is not buying:** AI ads, Ontologize, Autoads, Maestro-Trama, a reporting dashboard, an autonomous media buyer, or an SEO guarantee. The buyer is buying a bounded way to stop guessing which offer, creative angle, or demand-capture path deserves the next dollar or next iteration. [R4][R6]

**Candidate first buyer segments, not an ICP yet:** local appointment businesses, owner-led ecommerce or retail with one hero SKU or seasonal offer, and SMBs already spending on ads but unsure what is working. These are plausible starting segments from the channel-market explorer, not proof of demand, willingness to pay, or repeatability. [R5][R4][R6]

**Avoid first buyers:** businesses with no offer clarity, no conversion action, no follow-up owner, no budget category, generic brand-awareness goals, or a desire for guaranteed leads and set-and-forget automation. [R5][R6]

## Source Integration Map

**Ontologize role: metric trust and decision governance.** Ontologize supplies the discipline for one business slice, one priority metric, source events, denominator rules, evidence references, confidence/veracidade, owner review, and bounded client-facing claims. [R1][R3]

**Autoads role: experiment-loop shell.** Autoads supplies the conceptual campaign loop: candidate, variant, network outcome, normalized KPI, grader, promotion DAG, owner decision, and feedback. It should remain internal substrate because the research says it is seed-stage and not executed with live spend, real clients, credentials, pricing, or production network integration. [R1][R3]

**Maestro-Trama role: creative-performance intelligence.** Maestro-Trama supplies creative metrics, winner semantics, retrieval/analysis surfaces, and creative-learning vocabulary, but current evidence is not sale-grade causal proof and has limitations around attribution, grain, channel scope, and feedback-system maturity. [R1][R3]

**Combined service role:** the three sources combine into a metric-trust plus owner-reviewed creative evidence loop, not a platform. The durable artifact is an evidence bundle and reusable creative/keyword/content learning, not an automated campaign machine or causal proof engine. [R1][R4][R3]

## TikTok Track

TikTok should be positioned as the **creative response and demand-expression track**: test whether strangers respond to the offer when it is expressed as native short-form hooks, objections, proof, and direct-response creative. [R5]

The TikTok deliverable should be a brief and scorecard, not a promise that the service can beat TikTok optimization. TikTok provides Pixel, Events API, standard events, lead generation paths, and Creative Center resources, but those platform capabilities do not prove the service can generate leads or optimize campaigns autonomously. [Parent Official/Public Source Verification][R4]

Minimum TikTok scope should include one offer, one buyer profile, one conversion action, a small set of creative angles, a response window, and an owner decision. If the buyer cannot set up or export the relevant lead/conversion evidence, the TikTok track should stop at readiness or brief preparation. [R2][R5][R6]

TikTok claims must separate **platform metric** from **reusable creative lesson**. A high CTR, low-cost lead, or apparent winner is only a provisional candidate unless denominator rules, spend context, lead quality, confound checks, owner review, and follow-up behavior support the lesson. [R1][R3][R4]

## Google Ads/SEO Track

Google Ads should be positioned as the **intent-capture and conversion-measurement track**: inspect whether existing demand can find the offer and whether the business can measure calls, forms, bookings, leads, or other conversion actions. [R5][Parent Official/Public Source Verification]

Performance Max, Smart Campaigns, lead form assets, conversion measurement, and conversion value rules can support the track, but they do not justify claiming direct control over Google AI optimization or guaranteed lead economics. The service should feed better inputs and inspect outputs, not claim to outperform the platform. [Parent Official/Public Source Verification][R4]

SEO should be positioned as **findability and source-backed content improvement**, not ranking lift. Google frames SEO as helping search engines understand content and users find a site, while also stating that payment does not buy ranking and that crawling, indexing, and serving are not guaranteed. [Parent Official/Public Source Verification]

The Google/SEO deliverable should be an intent brief and evidence review: offer page or Business Profile path, conversion event, keyword/intent assumptions, source content, Search Console or account exports when available, and one recommended next change. SEO belongs in findability/content readiness unless a near-term proxy metric and time horizon are defined. [R4][R5][Parent Official/Public Source Verification]

## Service Package Ladder

**1. Readiness Check**
A short paid or credited diagnostic that confirms one metric or lead source, one source system, one owner, one channel path, one budget cap, and one kill rule. If these are missing, the deliverable is a no-go memo rather than a sprint. [R6][R3]

**2. One-Offer Lead Evidence Sprint**
A capped, pricing-hypothesis sprint with one offer, one conversion action, one primary execution channel, an optional second-channel brief/readiness pass, 3-6 creative or message variants where appropriate, a predeclared response window, and an evidence bundle that supports keep/change/pause/retest/inconclusive. The sprint may include client-paid ad spend, but the claim remains decision support, not guaranteed performance. Timeline, price bands, and willingness to pay remain hypotheses until buyer witness exists. [R5][R6][R3][R2]

**3. Post-Launch Decision Review**
After the client runs ads or publishes changes in their normal account, the service normalizes outcomes, checks confounds and minimum confidence, then routes the result to promote, retire, keep private, defer, or mark inconclusive. [R2][R3][R6]

**4. Creative Learning Cadence**
A retainer or recurring cadence is only a future package hypothesis until paid discovery or pilot evidence shows owners will pay for repeated scorecards. If it survives, it should remain focused on reusable evidence, creative lessons, and decision governance rather than platform automation. [R4][R6][R3]

## Evidence Gates and No-Sale Gates

**Evidence gate: buyer clarity.** The buyer must name one offer, one buyer segment, one conversion event, one source system, one owner, one decision that changes, one budget category, and one stop condition. [R2][R3][R6]

**Evidence gate: measurement readiness.** TikTok and Google claims require event or conversion evidence before performance interpretation; without Pixel, Events API, conversion measurement, lead form export, Search Console, Business Profile, CRM, or equivalent source evidence, the service should remain in readiness mode. [Parent Official/Public Source Verification][R2]

**Evidence gate: predeclared evidence rules.** The sprint should define denominator rules, exclusions, negative cases, response window, lead-quality definition, kill condition, and what counts as inconclusive before analysis. Numeric thresholds, spend floors, sample-size rules, and confound catalogs are still missing implementation work. [R2][R3][R6][R4]

**Evidence gate: owner review and behavior change.** No creative, keyword, SEO, or channel recommendation should be promoted as reusable learning until the owner reviews the evidence and approves a real decision. Owner review proves governance occurred; it does not prove causality, buyer willingness to pay, or durable channel fit. [R1][R3]

**No-sale gate: performance guarantees.** Reject or reframe buyers who want guaranteed leads, guaranteed CPA/ROAS, SEO ranking promises, set-and-forget automation, generic AI strategy, broad multi-channel orchestration, or platform migration first. [R4][R6]

**No-sale gate: weak source access.** Do not sell a performance sprint if the buyer cannot provide exports, screenshots, asset links, conversion-event evidence, or a minimum source chain for the metric being judged. [R2][R3]

## Differentiation and Future Moat Hypothesis

The current differentiation is **governed interpretation of client/platform evidence**. TikTok and Google already automate large parts of targeting, bidding, placement, campaign assembly, and optimization, so the differentiated service should not be "we run ads with AI." It should be "we help you know what your ads and offer test actually taught you, including when the evidence is inconclusive." [R4][Parent Official/Public Source Verification][R6]

The initial advantage is not a moat or technology lock-in. It is operating discipline: source-linked metrics, denominator rules, response windows, confound checks, owner decisions, and reusable creative/keyword/content learning. [R1][R3][R4]

The future moat hypothesis can become a proprietary learning loop only after repeated paid tests produce comparable evidence bundles with lineage from offer, creative, channel, source event, metric, decision, and next action. That future moat is not proven by the current substrate. [R3][R4]

## Roadmap

**Phase 0: Readiness script and no-go memo.** Build the service intake around offer, conversion action, source system, owner, budget cap, channel choice, response window, and stop rule. This supports the strongest proof gate before any campaign work. [R2][R3][R6]

**Phase 1: Manual evidence bundle.** Deliver the first sprint through client tools, exports, spreadsheets, screenshots, and owner review. Do not ask for ad-account credentials at the first service level if exports and asset links are enough. [R2][R6]

**Phase 2: TikTok and Google/SEO brief templates.** Create repeatable templates for TikTok creative hypotheses and Google/SEO intent capture, with explicit evidence references and claim boundaries. [R4][R5]

**Phase 3: Post-launch scorecard.** Normalize spend, impressions, clicks, leads, lead quality, response window, denominator, confound notes, inconclusive criteria, and owner decision into one inspectable scorecard. [R5][R1][R3]

**Phase 4: Internal substrate use.** Use Maestro-Trama retrieval and creative-performance vocabulary, Autoads grading/promotion concepts, and Ontologize trust discipline internally, while keeping buyer-facing language service-oriented and non-technical. [R1][R2]

**Phase 5: Automation research only after paid proof.** Live ad-network integration, automated recommendation, spend prediction, and retainer cadence should wait until bounded network/API research and repeated paid evidence show that the manual loop reliably produces owner decisions. [R2][R3][R6]

## Claim Discipline

Allowed claims:

- We help make one marketing decision inspectable. [R2][R6]
- We use your own ad, lead, content, or search evidence to create an owner-reviewed scorecard. [R2][R5]
- We separate platform metrics from reusable business learning. [R1][R4]
- We can prepare TikTok creative briefs and Google/SEO intent briefs around one offer, with one-channel execution until tracking and operator capacity are witnessed. [R5][R4][R2]
- We stop or downgrade the engagement when source evidence, owner commitment, or conversion definition is missing. [R3][R6]
- We treat inconclusive outcomes as valid results, not failed sales. [R3][R4][R6]

Disallowed claims:

- We guarantee leads, CPA, ROAS, rankings, or revenue lift. [R3][R4][Parent Official/Public Source Verification]
- We autonomously manage TikTok or Google Ads. [R3][R4]
- We have proven SMB buyer demand for this offer. [R3][R4]
- We can replace platform optimization, BI, agencies, or attribution systems. [R4]
- We have cross-channel attribution truth or causal proof from current Maestro-Trama winner data. [R3]
- SEO work will produce ranking lift or guaranteed indexing. [Parent Official/Public Source Verification]
- A buyer-reviewed scorecard proves causality, repeatable performance, or willingness to pay. [R1][R3]

Preferred language:

- Use "client-inspectable" instead of "auditable" for SMB trust. [R6]
- Use "decision-grade" only when paired with bounded response window, source chain, denominator, confound checks, inconclusive criteria, and owner review. Prefer "evidence-bounded" when numeric thresholds are not yet specified. [R2][R3]
- Use "readiness," "sprint," and "scorecard" before "platform," "automation," or "AI." [R4][R6]
