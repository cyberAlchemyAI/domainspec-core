# Autoads x Maestro-Trama x Ontologize SMB Marketing Offer Research

Dispatch: `2026-06-18-autoads-maestro-smb-marketing-offer`
Date: 2026-06-18
Status: collected research substrate with reviewed synthesis
Question: how can `../maestro-trama` and `projects/autoads/` help package Ontologize as marketing strategy/service offerings for small-to-middle business owners, first focused on TikTok and Google Ads/SEO?

## Working Answer

The buyer-facing wedge is not "Ontologize," "autoads," "Maestro-Trama," "AI ads," or a finished marketing platform. The most credible offer is a manual-first **One-Offer Lead Evidence Sprint** or **Decision Scorecard Sprint**: one offer, one buyer segment, one conversion action, one primary channel execution path, optional second-channel brief/readiness, one capped response window, and one owner-reviewed scorecard.

The internal substrate is useful but pre-commercial:

- Ontologize supplies metric trust discipline: source chain, denominator, evidence grade, owner decision, and claim boundaries.
- Autoads supplies the ad-learning loop: candidate -> variant -> network outcome -> KPI -> grader -> promotion DAG -> owner decision -> feedback.
- Maestro-Trama supplies creative-performance vocabulary, metrics, winners, retrieval, spend prediction, and TRAMA/Creative DNA materials.

The service must not claim autonomous media buying, guaranteed CPA/ROAS, SEO ranking lift, full platform readiness, proven buyer demand, or lead proof. Inconclusive evidence is a valid outcome.

## Explorer Returns

### R1 - Forrester / Substrate Mapper

The existing loop stack is coherent, but still pre-commercial. Ontologize/CyberAlchemy is the "make one operating metric trustworthy" loop: one business slice, one priority metric, source events, denominator rules, evidence references, confidence/veracidade, owner review, and a promotion graph from run evidence into reusable knowledge. Its optimization-chain method turns that into client language: action pattern -> metric window -> analysis method -> bounded BI optimization -> owner decision -> next-run routing.

Autoads is the ads-specific verticalization of that same loop: auto-research proposes candidates, a generator emits variants, a network adapter returns outcomes, measurement normalizes KPIs, a grader blocks weak/confounded evidence, the promotion DAG requires owner decision, and feedback re-parameterizes the next generation. It is explicitly a seed, not executed; the first planned layer is a stub network adapter with fixture outcomes and no live spend, PII, credentials, pricing, or clients.

Maestro-Trama contributes the mature creative-performance substrate: domain vocabulary for creative entities, sourcing, labels, lifecycle, attribution, TRAMA, CapoMastro, Maestro, and winner semantics; metric formulas and windows for spend, impressions, clicks, orders, revenue, ROAS, CPA, CAC, CTR, conversion rate, and lifecycle windows; and a concrete creative ops path from approved creative -> CapoMastro -> Maestro -> campaign -> removal/evaluation.

The performance loop is already framed as closed-loop marketing: performance marketing optimizes business objectives, creatives affect click, conversion, ticket, and cost terms, and the learning loop is produce -> rollout -> measure -> learn -> feed back into production. The Criativo Vencedor constitution and winners data product supply a labeled positive class for what worked, while performance data supplies the flat per-creative measurement surface.

Artifact roles:

- Ontologize is the trust/governance layer: assessment, source inventory, metric semantics, privacy boundary, owner decision, and bounded claim discipline.
- Autoads is the campaign experiment shell: creative candidate generation, ad-network outcome ingestion, KPI grading, promotion/retirement, and feedback into research/generation memory.
- Maestro-Trama is the creative intelligence substrate: Creative DNA separates operational facts, genotype extraction, and phenotype rules so creative meaning can be versioned and tested against performance without circularly using performance to define labels.

A credible SMB service substrate would be a "Marketing Metric Trust + Creative Learning Sprint," not an autonomous ads platform. The source-backed offer is: pick one SMB growth metric, one campaign/channel, one decision owner, one evidence chain, and one next creative/action slate; then deliver an inspectable metric plus governed recommendations.

Main caveat: SMB is a packaging inference from these substrates, not a proven buyer segment. The hard gates remain buyer pain, budget category, data access, metric owner, success threshold, and kill criteria; autoads is not executed, several Maestro-Trama apps are draft/spec-stage, and current performance data is Insider/Meta-specific rather than cross-SMB proof.

### R2 - Simon / Substrate Mapper

The feasible SMB offer is an operator-assisted **Creative Decision Sprint**, not an AutoAds platform. The buyer pays to make one marketing decision inspectable: "which creative pattern should we reuse, pause, remix, or test next?" Delivery should start from exported ad/account data and existing creative assets, with manual interpretation and owner approval before any live spend changes.

Feasible workflows:

1. Historical Creative Evidence Review: client exports recent creative performance and provides creative assets or links. The operator clusters/retrieves analogous creatives, joins performance evidence, flags low-spend or noisy winners, and returns a ranked reuse/remix/retire recommendation set.
2. Next-Brief Pack: client gives product, audience, objective, constraints, and examples of winners/losers. The service turns those into creative directions with lineage, hypothesis, and evidence references. Generation may be template-first and LLM-assisted internally, but the buyer receives creative briefs and rationale, not generative autonomy claims.
3. Post-Launch Decision Review: after the client runs ads in their normal account, they export outcomes for a fixed response window. The service normalizes KPIs, checks confounds/minimum confidence, and routes the result to owner decision: promote, retire, keep private, or defer.
4. Spend/Cohort Inspection Add-On: spend prediction is internal calibration and exploratory inspection, not "we predict your winners."

Decision points:

- Qualify only if the buyer can name a metric owner, source system, decision that changes, budget category, and reason existing BI/agency workflows are insufficient.
- Choose one objective and one response window before analysis, then pre-register denominator rules, exclusions, negative cases, and stop condition.
- Start with historical exports and client-tool delivery.
- Do not offer live ad-network integration until a bounded network/API research pass exists.

Client inputs:

- one business goal;
- one product/audience/objective;
- recent creative performance export;
- asset links/files;
- known winners and failures;
- campaign constraints;
- budget/test tolerance;
- accountable decision owner.

Do not ask for ad-account credentials at the first service level. CSV exports, screenshots, and asset links are enough for the manual evidence review.

Buyer-facing sentence:

> We help you use your own ad history to choose the next creative move with evidence, not vibes.

Internal sentence:

> Manual Ontologize-style metric trust sprint over creative-performance data, optionally using Maestro retrieval/analysis surfaces and Autoads governance concepts.

### R3 - Goldratt / Substrate Mapper

The constraint is buyer-grade proof, not whether we can generate ads or whether metrics exist. Autoads is explicitly non-executed, starts with a stub adapter, and excludes live spend, pricing, contracts, named clients, and executed pipeline work. Maestro/TRAMA has strong internal measurement vocabulary, but the learning system needed to prove improvement is still missing feedback capture, lineage-to-performance binding, and an improvement mechanism.

Bottlenecks:

1. No paid offer until the claim is narrowed. The honest shape is buyer discovery or paid readiness around one distrusted metric, not a platform, compliance offer, autonomous marketing service, or guaranteed improvement.
2. The proof spine is missing. TRAMA can produce creatives, but current docs say runs disappear without persistent record of trigger, output, or survival past day 10; v1 needs lineage, operator review, telemetry joins, cohort analysis, and schema gates.
3. Lineage is a hard dependency. Auto-generated creatives must carry parent creative IDs, references, and agent-run metadata.
4. Current Maestro evidence is useful but not sale-grade causal proof. Winner classification is causal-heuristic, thresholds are tuned on historical data, external events are not modeled, and current winner data lacks held-out validation plus attribution-version tags.
5. The flat performance table cannot carry channel proof alone. It is lifetime-aggregated and lacks daily grain, `ad_id`, consistent conversion-rate scaling, and attribution-version tags.
6. The recommender/closed-loop surface is not yet dependable product surface.

Dependency order:

1. Buyer gate first: budget or paid time, data access, owner time, metric, success threshold, and stop condition.
2. Define one workflow/run boundary: metric, owner group, evidence chain, denominator, exclusions, negative cases, stop condition.
3. Build the source-linked metric substrate: one KPI with `veracidade`, evidence refs, response window.
4. Add lineage/provenance.
5. Grade before recommending: confidence/confound gates must block promotion.
6. Owner decision last.

Minimum paid-readiness scope:

Sell only a **Marketing Metric Trust Sprint / paid readiness pilot**: one SMB, one channel, one operating marketing metric, one source chain, one baseline/response-window definition, at least two creative variants, manual/client-tool delivery, owner review, and a final evidence bundle. The allowed deliverable is client-inspectable evidence chain and owner-reviewed metric, not "we improved your ads."

### R4 - Porter / Channel Market Explorer

Package this as a **marketing metric trust + creative learning service**, not as an AI ads agency or finished automation platform. The strongest wedge is: "we help an SMB make one TikTok/Google/SEO growth loop inspectable: what was tried, what metric moved, what denominator/source evidence supports it, what creative lesson is safe to reuse, and who approved it."

Market shape:

- TikTok is commoditizing setup and optimization through platform automation. Therefore "we run TikTok ads with AI" is weak positioning. The buyer pain left over is creative fatigue, evidence quality, and knowing what to learn from tests.
- Google Ads is similar. Performance Max and Google AI optimize bids, placements, and campaign delivery; the differentiated service should feed better inputs and inspect outputs, not pretend to beat platform automation directly.
- SEO is a trust trap. Google frames SEO as helping users and search engines understand content, but there is no ranking guarantee. Packaging should be "source-backed content/landing-page evidence and Search Console-informed improvement," not guaranteed AI/SEO visibility.

Owned substrate:

- Ontologize gives paid readiness or discovery around one distrusted metric with budget/data/owner/success threshold/stop condition committed before stronger claims.
- Autoads gives the service spine, but is unexecuted: no live network integration, real spend, model training, or executed pipeline.
- Maestro-Trama adds creative-performance intuition, but local docs caution that TRAMA's learning system is not complete and Google/TikTok are out of current v1 scope.

Differentiation:

The differentiated claim is not "better ads." It is **platform automation plus governed learning**. TikTok/Google decide delivery; this service decides what the business can safely learn from delivery. The durable artifact is a source-linked creative/keyword/content learning loop with denominator rules, response windows, confound checks, owner decisions, and reusable brief inputs.

Best first packages:

1. Marketing Metric Trust Sprint: one channel, one campaign/workflow, one distrusted metric, one source chain, one owner decision.
2. TikTok Creative Evidence Sprint: map audience x creative-family x objective, run or inspect a bounded creative batch, classify what can be reused, and separate platform metric from reusable creative lesson.
3. Google Ads + SEO Demand Evidence Sprint: inspect PMax/Search/landing-page inputs, first-party signals, conversion events, source content, and Search Console evidence; deliver a "what to change next and why" pack.
4. Creative Learning Retainer only after a first paid proof.

Commodity risks:

- Sounding like every small agency: TikTok ads, Google Ads, SEO, AI creatives, reporting dashboards.
- Platform-native tools already automate targeting, bidding, campaign assembly, creative generation, budget pacing, and reporting.
- Evidence fragility: attribution windows, conversion tracking quality, platform black boxes, objective mismatch, seasonality, creative fatigue, and small-sample SMB budgets.

Do not claim proven buyer demand, proven Google/TikTok execution, SEO ranking lift, autonomous campaign management, cross-channel attribution truth, TRAMA parity, cross-vertical proof, guaranteed CPA/ROAS improvement, or BI/platform replacement.

Safe claims: source-linked marketing metric, owner-reviewed creative learning, platform automation input audit, governed creative test loop, founding pilot/readiness sprint, and client-inspectable evidence chain.

### R5 - Christensen / Channel Market Explorer

The first job an SMB owner would hire is:

> Turn one offer into a lead-producing channel test I can trust enough to decide what to scale, fix, or stop.

The buyer-visible wedge is **One-Offer Lead Proof Sprint**: one product/service, one buyer segment, one conversion action, two first channels, and one owner-reviewed scorecard.

Buyer promise:

> We will help you choose one offer, package it for TikTok and Google intent, launch or prepare the smallest measurable test, and give you a decision-grade answer: keep, change, scale, or stop.

Minimum scope:

- one offer: service, booking, consultation, high-margin product, or local promotion;
- one buyer profile;
- one conversion action: call, form, booking, WhatsApp/message, quote request, or store visit proxy;
- two channel briefs: TikTok creative/lead-gen brief plus Google Search/GBP/SEO intent brief;
- one evidence scorecard: spend, impressions, clicks, lead/form/call count, lead quality, response window, denominator, and owner decision.

Best first profiles:

- local appointment businesses with high value per lead: medspa, dental, clinic, fitness, legal, home services, aesthetic services;
- owner-led ecommerce or local retail with one hero SKU or seasonal offer;
- SMBs already spending on ads but unsure what is working.

Avoid first:

- buyers with no offer clarity;
- no conversion action;
- no owner for follow-up;
- no budget category;
- generic brand-awareness goals.

Channel jobs:

- TikTok: find whether strangers respond to the offer when expressed as native short-form creative. Use TikTok for hooks, objections, social proof, direct response, and fast qualitative signal.
- Google Ads/SEO: capture existing intent and make the offer findable when someone is already looking. Use Search/Smart Campaign/lead forms for demand capture, Business Profile/local SEO for local discoverability, and one offer page or GBP path with clean tracking.
- Cross-channel: compare TikTok demand creation against Google demand capture using the same offer and lead-quality definition. Do not compare by CTR alone.

Sell the decision. Hide the machinery until repeated paid tests prove the loop.

### R6 - Thaler / Channel Market Explorer

SMBs will not buy "Ontologize/autoads." They may buy a bounded way to stop guessing which ads to keep, kill, or scale.

Offer shape:

Lead with **Ad Decision Sprint** or **Creative Learning Sprint**, not a platform. Promise one channel, one offer, one conversion event, one capped test window, and one owner decision. Keep delivery manual/client-tool first: existing ad account, spreadsheet evidence bundle, no new login, no subscription required.

Package ladder:

1. Readiness Check: one metric/lead source, source system, budget cap, owner, channel choice, kill rule.
2. Sprint plus client-paid ad spend: 2-3 weeks, 3-6 variants, predeclared response window, evidence bundle, keep/kill/scale decision.
3. Review Cadence only after the sprint produces a real decision.

The service cannot out-control platforms; it must out-interpret them.

Friction hypotheses:

- Trust: SMBs distrust agencies and dashboards. Use "client-inspectable," not "auditable."
- Attention: assume low attention. Cap buyer lift to two calls plus one owner review.
- Budget: sell a capped experiment, not a retainer first.
- Proof anxiety: pre-register denominator, window, exclusions, and stop condition.
- Channel confusion: do not sell multi-channel orchestration. Pick one channel by buyer job.

Risk reversals:

- client owns ad accounts and spend;
- no credentials retained;
- no long contract;
- readiness fee credits into sprint;
- if readiness fails, deliver no-go memo and stop;
- spend cap and kill rule signed before launch;
- "no AI needed" is allowed;
- require a buyer-supplied bad case.

Disqualification gates:

No sale if the buyer cannot name one conversion event, one source system, one decision owner, one decision that changes, one budget category, and one reason existing ads/BI/agency work is not enough. Reject if they want guaranteed leads, generic AI strategy, set-and-forget, multi-channel magic, or platform migration first.

Behavioral failure modes:

- Daily-dashboard addiction: buyer reacts to early CTR.
- Authority bias: platform metric becomes truth.
- Choice overload: too many channels.
- Loss aversion: fear of wasted spend.
- Agency lock-in anxiety.

## Reviewer Returns and Resolution

### Taleb / Fragility Review

Taleb accepted viability only as a constrained experiment, not as a growth promise. Main objections: the offer can collapse into commodity agency language; TikTok and Google control delivery, optimization, attribution surfaces, and platform feedback; attribution and small samples can manufacture false confidence; buyer-side execution can break the sprint; SEO is especially dangerous as a buyer promise; and the internal tech is not a commercial proof engine yet. Required narrowing: replace "Lead Proof" with "Lead Evidence," make "inconclusive" a valid outcome, soften "scale" to "increase/revise/pause/retest," and sell the no-go memo as proudly as the sprint.

### Kahneman / Buyer-Evidence Review

Kahneman objected that "proceed" was stronger than evidence permits because SMB demand and willingness to pay are not witnessed. Critical downgrades: treat the offer as buyer-discovery/readiness until there is paid discovery, signed pilot intent, or budget-confirming interviews; avoid two-channel execution until operator time/cost is proven; mark price bands, retainers, and package ladder as hypotheses; treat candidate segments as plausible starting segments rather than ICP; and require a metric spec before using stronger decision-grade language.

### Quine / Definition Review

Quine collapsed broad terms into distinct behavior. "Marketing strategy" becomes a bounded one-offer/channel test plan. TikTok is the creative response/demand-expression track. Google Ads is the intent-capture and conversion-measurement track. SEO is findability and source-backed content improvement. Ontologize remains the internal metric-trust and decision-governance substrate; Autoads remains the internal ad-experiment loop shell; Maestro-Trama remains the internal creative-performance evidence substrate. "Moat" is reserved for a future moat hypothesis after repeated paid comparable evidence bundles.

### Resolution Applied In `findings.md`

- Renamed the buyer-facing offer to **One-Offer Lead Evidence Sprint**, with **Decision Scorecard Sprint** as a buyer-safe alternative.
- Downgraded verdict to buyer-discovery/readiness experiment that can become a manual service under strict gates.
- Made one primary channel execution the default; TikTok plus Google/SEO can both be prepared as briefs/readiness paths, but not both executed until tracking, budget, and operator capacity are witnessed.
- Added inconclusive as a first-class result.
- Replaced moat language with current differentiation plus future moat hypothesis.
- Kept pricing, package ladder, retainer, and segment claims explicitly hypothetical until buyer witness exists.
