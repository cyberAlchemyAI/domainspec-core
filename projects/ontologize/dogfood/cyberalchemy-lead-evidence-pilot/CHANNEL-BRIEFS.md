Status: draft

## Channel Policy

Execute one primary channel first. A second channel may be prepared as a readiness brief, but should not be executed until tracking, owner attention, and operator capacity are clear.

## Google / SEO / Findability Brief

Use this track to test whether existing demand can understand and find the CyberAlchemy offer.

Readiness checks:

- offer page exists;
- conversion event exists;
- analytics or source evidence exists;
- Search Console or equivalent evidence source is available, if applicable;
- page title, heading, and summary match the offer;
- no ranking, indexing, traffic, or lead guarantees are implied.

Candidate evidence:

- impressions;
- clicks;
- click-through rate;
- conversion event count;
- qualified lead count;
- query or intent cluster;
- page engagement;
- owner review of lead quality.

Deliverable:

- intent brief;
- page improvement notes;
- scorecard entry;
- decision: keep, revise, pause, retest, or inconclusive.

## TikTok Creative Response Brief

Use this track to test whether the CyberAlchemy offer can be expressed as short-form creative or founder-led content.

Readiness checks:

- offer can be said plainly in one sentence;
- 3-6 hooks exist;
- conversion action is defined;
- response window is defined;
- evidence source is available;
- no autonomous optimization or lead guarantee is implied.

Candidate creative angles:

- "AI code is easy; trusting AI code is hard."
- "Your agent did the work. Can you prove why it was right?"
- "Stop reviewing AI output from scratch. Review the trail."
- "Intent, evidence, owner decision: the missing loop in AI development."
- "Governed AI development without pretending humans disappear."

Candidate evidence:

- views or exposures;
- profile/page visits;
- click-through;
- form starts;
- qualified replies;
- owner review of reply quality.

Deliverable:

- creative-response brief;
- message variant scorecard;
- decision: keep, revise, pause, retest, or inconclusive.

## Direct Distribution Fallback

If paid/organic channel readiness is weak, run the first evidence pass through direct distribution:

- send the offer to trusted operators;
- ask for one concrete response: confusing, useful, urgent, irrelevant, or worth a call;
- record objections and language;
- avoid treating positive reactions as purchase proof.

This can validate the scorecard mechanics before channel execution.
