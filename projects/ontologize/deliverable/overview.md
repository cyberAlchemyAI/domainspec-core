## The offer, in one line

**CyberAlchemy turns the dashboards you already half-trust into business intelligence you can audit —
every number traceable to its evidence — and keeps it trustworthy with a governed learning loop over
your runs and your agents.**

## What you get first: business intelligence you can defend (BI-led)

We lead with your metrics because that is the fastest value on data you already have. We rebuild your
key numbers so each one:

- declares its **denominator and the dimensions it covers** (so it cannot quietly drop what matters),
- carries a **confidence grade** (`veracidade`: high / medium / low) and a list of **evidence
  references** that justify the grade (so "proven" always comes with receipts), and
- traces to the **source events** that produced it (so a reviewer can reproduce the number).

**[proven]** GoldenQuill rebuilt grant KPIs as evidence-linked families with explicit denominators and
counterfactual receipts (source: KPI families + `CycleReceipt` in the GoldenQuill reference build).
**[hypothesis]** the per-number `veracidade` confidence grade is an adaptation we validate in your pilot.

## What keeps that BI trustworthy: three governance layers underneath

The BI stays honest over time because three layers sit beneath it:

1. **Ontology cards** — a small map of *who you serve* × *how value flows* that configures vocabulary,
   evidence schema, and reporting rhythm; matched at runtime, never hardcoded. **[proven]** (the
   customer × capital-logic card model, with the coupling-violation guard, in the reference build).
2. **Classification register** — a signed `field → authority class` table that decides where each piece
   of data may travel (local vs cloud, private vs shareable), fail-closed. **[proven]**.
3. **Promotion DAG (the governed learning loop)** — your runs become events → evidence → a candidate
   lesson → review gates → an owner decision → a promoted or retired lesson, enforced by deterministic
   rules so nothing turns into reusable "truth" too early. **[proven]** (the 11-rule validator + state
   model in the reference build).

And your **agents are governed** by the same map: routed by ontology, gated deterministically,
self-validating. **[proven]** (the routed, gated, self-validating council).

## Why this matters

A single headline number can fail in two ways one number cannot see: it can **drop a dimension** the
business actually carries (concept erasure), or its **value can be distorted** — wrong denominator,
invented rows (number hallucination). **[proven]** GoldenQuill showed a flat win-rate hid four
dimensions. **[hypothesis / interpretive]** the two-failure-mode framing is an *interpretive principle*
we borrow to name a real, separable pair of audits — it is **not** a mathematical proof that a KPI
cannot collapse. We do not claim such a proof.

## How the engagement runs (candidate shape)

Assessment → Design → Pilot (one thin slice, one real metric moved) → Handoff (this package + a
current→target transition plan). Two verticals run in parallel — **cap factory** (continuous production)
and a **logistics company** (discrete shipments) — to test whether the pattern generalizes.
**[hypothesis]** generalization is exactly what the parallel pilots must prove.
