## Promotion rule

Everything here is a **candidate**. GoldenQuill is read-only source evidence. No canonical
Arcanum promotion, no committed pricing/contracts/client names, without a separate owner-review route.

## Closure modes

- **Source-backed** — traces to a GoldenQuill artifact path.
- **Reusable-pattern** — generic form that holds beyond GoldenQuill.
- **Hypothesis** — plausible but unproven; carries a validation step.
