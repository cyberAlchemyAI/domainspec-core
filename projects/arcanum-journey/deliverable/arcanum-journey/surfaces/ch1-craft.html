Craft — the control surface

The project's memory and dashboard: a queryable ledger every other tool plugs into.

The puzzle: how does a long job not fall apart halfway through? Not a smarter model — a place to write things down that the work can read back.

What it is

A file-backed recursive ledger. Source of truth = ledger.yml. The readable CRAFT.md is just a view.

What lives in it

  • contexts (what you're doing)
  • blockers & enablers
  • open decisions & gaps
  • the current next move

The one rule

Raw blockers can't be resolved directly — you must refine them into a closure condition first. No hand-wavy "it's done".

Why "control surface"

refine, invoke, task-session, goal all read from and write back to Craft. Learn it once; every chapter plugs in here.

How the tools plug into Craft

refine→ invoke→ CRAFT (ledger)← task-session← goal
