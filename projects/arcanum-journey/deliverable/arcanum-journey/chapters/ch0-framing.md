# Chapter 0 — What is Arcanum?

> Persona: The Explainer. Analogy first, then the real thing. One idea at a time.

## Hook

You've used an AI assistant. It's great for one-off questions — but the moment the
job gets *big* (a whole project, a research paper, a codebase), things slip. The
assistant forgets, repeats itself, or confidently does the wrong thing. **Why?**

Because a chat has no memory of its own decisions, no place to keep state, and no
rules about who's allowed to change what. Arcanum is what you add to fix that.

## Analogy

Think of a **professional kitchen**, not a single cook shouting orders.

- A recipe card you can reuse = a **sigil** (one capability).
- A measuring cup that always measures the same = a **formula** (a pure rule).
- Turning raw notes into a clean dish = a **transmutation** (reshape, don't reinvent).
- A full plated course that combines several recipes = a **spell** (a workflow).
- "We always wash hands before plating" = a **discipline** (a house practice).

Arcanum is the kitchen's *operating system*: it keeps the recipes, the rules, the
state of every dish, and who's allowed to touch what.

## The real thing: the five tiers

| Tier | One line | "How much thinking?" |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formula | a pure rule — same input, same output | rule-following |
| Transmutation | reshape messy input into structure | judgment, no orchestration |
| Sigil (Arcana) | one capability that reasons in steps/gates | stitch voices into a decision |
| Spell | a workflow that composes several sigils | guided path |
| Discipline | a practice that spans many capabilities | a house rule |

## The method underneath: Cyber Alchemy

Arcanum runs on one idea — **governed synthesis, not prompt-and-pray.** Every piece
of work keeps five things visible (the *five anchors*):

1. **Objective** — what are we solving?
2. **Artifact** — what should exist when we're done?
3. **Discovery** — what must we learn first?
4. **Tension** — what could break this?
5. **Route** — who owns the next step?

And it moves through a loop: **Orient → Discover → Shape → Stabilize → Evolve.**
(That's the public "Cyber Alchemy method"; you'll feel it in every chapter.)

> Where the analogy breaks: a kitchen throws away its notes; Arcanum *keeps* them —
> that durable memory is the whole point.

## Probe (try it)

Pick a real task you care about. Say it out loud as the five anchors:
"My objective is ___. The artifact is ___. I need to discover ___. It could break
if ___. After me, ___ owns the next step." Notice how much sharper it already feels.

## One-sentence recap

**Arcanum is an operating system for serious AI work: reusable capabilities
(sigils), composed into workflows (spells), all governed so nothing gets lost.**
