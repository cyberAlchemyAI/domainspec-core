# Glossary

- **Accepted event**: a semantic event that passes admission and advances revision/head exactly once.
- **Correction state**: independent axis `none | open | resolved`; resolution is guarded, not inferred from a neighboring phase.
- **Lifecycle phase**: the process position produced by the reducer.
- **Pending gate**: the exact next gate category required by the proposed fixture, including deterministic validation gates.
- **Outcome ceiling**: the strongest current claim allowed by accumulated valid evidence; it begins `not_assessed` and may change only by listed reducer rules.
- **RS26**: the proposed canonical state after EV26, reduced from closure plus the accepted event log.
- **RP26**: a read-only projection derived from RS26 and the event head; it is not state or authority.
- **Research checker**: candidate-local proof harness for this acceptance contract, not the Whisper runtime.
- **Research completion**: passing state-contract receipt plus independent non-vacuity review, findings, audit, closure receipt, and RESULT synchronization.
- **Authority effect none**: the evidence grants no ontology, runtime, product, publication, deployment, promotion, or production authority.
