# CyberAlchemy Product-Cognition Writing Substrate Strategy

Status: closed; audited research accepted and parent-owned Whisper preparation complete.

## Preliminary Answer

The useful delta is not another list of fourteen projects and not another filter bar.
It is a writing substrate for one task-first lens over the existing truth model:
what a person is trying to do, what the tool makes easier to perceive or carry,
which judgment remains human, what evidence exists, where the capability stops, and
what bounded action comes next.

The research must test that frame rather than assume it. It must also repair the
current discrepancy between auditor-approved claim ceilings and live wording that
still uses unsupported generic `agentic writer studio` and `knowledge graph`
language. It may not silently relabel all fourteen entries as finished products.

## P1 Trigger Decision

This is a `research` dispatch. All four P1 triggers apply:

- synthesis across fourteen source surfaces, reader tasks, information architecture,
  human-machine philosophy, public/private authority, and Whisper composition;
- context protection because raw product and precedent evidence would overwhelm the
  parent and later writing context;
- isolation so candidate taxonomies, terms, and card grammars remain discardable;
- parallelism because source truth, cognitive ergonomics, and systems framing can be
  investigated independently before synthesis.

The scope is intentionally narrower than the resolved 2026-07-25 Co-Op portfolio
research and the unconfirmed 2026-07-26 showcase/frontend strategies. It researches
only the product-worldview and writing-substrate delta.

## Objective And Evidence Boundary

The governed research result must provide an exact-binding foundation for:

1. a reader-usable account of how CyberAlchemy sees tools augmenting complex human
   work without replacing domain judgment;
2. a tested task-first, augmentation-move, or hybrid product view over one source
   truth model;
3. complete recomposition across all fourteen portfolio entries;
4. a shared product-content grammar with explicit project-voice exceptions;
5. a public/private claim map and forbidden-language register; and
6. a complete Whisper intake candidate that can later be materialized into
   `text-intent-substrate.yaml` and `composition-plan.md`.

Research may inspect, compare, and recommend. It may not approve public copy, change
`projects/co-op-engine/index.html`, alter infrastructure, register product truth,
promote definitions, publish, deploy, release, or claim comprehension or production
readiness.
