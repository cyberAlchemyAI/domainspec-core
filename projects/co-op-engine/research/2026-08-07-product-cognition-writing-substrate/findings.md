## Shared grammar, without flattening project voice

Every candidate record uses ten distinct fields:

1. **Single projection record** — prevents duplicates and orphans; never substitutes a candidate identity for canonical truth.
2. **Candidate human objective** — tells the reader whether the work is recognizable; never substitutes an editorial task for measured need or fit.
3. **Bounded support operation plus state** — says exactly how support occurs; recommendation is not decision, preparation is not execution, retrieval is not review, routing is not acceptance.
4. **Named work output** — provides an inspectable object; existence is not usefulness, approval, or maturity.
5. **Named human authority** — identifies who decides, interprets, consents, approves, refuses, promotes, or stops; “human in the loop” is insufficient.
6. **Observed evidence** — says what directly exists; a test pass proves only its exact fixture and assertion.
7. **Project status** — records owner admission or unresolved state; `Built | Designed | Planned | Open` are writing labels, not project maturity.
8. **Ceiling and gap** — keeps the nearest material unsupported claim visible rather than hiding it in a generic disclaimer.
9. **Next step and authorization** — distinguishes a responsible proposal from approval, execution, completion, or an open invitation.
10. **Claim metadata and exposure disposition** — binds source, authority, type, writing state, truth effect, and public/private handling.
