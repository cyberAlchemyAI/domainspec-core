## Delivery Flow

```text

  -> scan inclusion reasons
  -> compare evidence states and limits
  -> inspect one stable record
  -> check source and authority
  -> take one bounded route or reset to All 14
```

Presentation or guided flow must reuse the same records and facts. It may change density and order, but every project moment still exposes job, identity, evidence/status, and limit. Presenter notes may not introduce hidden project facts.

Compression rules:

1. Give each visible block one job: meaning, consequence, action, or transition.
2. Remove repeated thesis statements before sentence-level editing.
3. Do not paraphrase a heading in the first body sentence.
4. Explain a mechanism once per reading path, then link back to the stable record.
5. End with a route or limit, not a restatement of the opening.

## Three-Moment Language Audition Brief

The audition is planned and unexecuted. It must run before full page drafting and cannot prove the transport or general comprehension.

1. `orient_and_choose`
   - Records available: all proposed jobs plus `All 14`.
   - Test: can each reader choose one job and one entry without facilitator explanation?
   - Capture: rationale, uncertainty, abandoned routes, and facilitator repair.
   - Fail when: the reader interprets a job as recommendation, cannot find `All 14`, or chooses from project-name familiarity alone.

2. `compare_and_inspect`
   - Representative records: Whisper, Poker Agent Arena, Sonar Loop.
   - Test: can each reader distinguish an observed workflow, implemented local harness, and designed specification while seeing the human authority and denied claims?
   - Capture: comparison language, status mistakes, hidden-limit discoveries, and incorrect autonomy inferences.
   - Fail when: all three are retold as finished products or evidence state disappears behind visual parity.

3. `remove_and_retell`
   - Remove the view after one complete path.
   - Ask for: human job, support operation, named output, retained authority, what exists, what was not claimed, how a second entry differs, and which next route was actually authorized.
   - Add one transfer case with the same domain/different job or same job/different domain.
   - Fail when: the reader substitutes recommendation for decision, preparation for execution, artifact for maturity, or route for acceptance.

Run both a technical-builder and domain-practitioner path. ## Validation Plan

Automatable checks may establish:

- exactly fourteen stable IDs and zero orphans;
- one source-bound record per ID;
- an inclusion reason for every membership;
- identical record resolution across views;
- present evidence, status, ceiling, authorization, and source metadata;
- visible critical limits;
- correct result count, reset, anchors, focus, reflow, keyboard behavior, and no-JS truth access;
- absence of forbidden phrases.

Human evidence is required for:

- accurate novel-word retell;
- separation of evidence, ambition, and limit;
- correct retained-authority account;
- meaningful distinction between entries;
- justified transfer without invented fit;
- recognition that the lens is editorial, not a recommendation;
- calibrated confidence and captured misconceptions.

Clicks, time, route completion, schema validity, accessibility mechanics, browser reachability, or two successful readers do not establish general comprehension.

## Kill And Fallback Conditions

Fall back to the project-first annotated atlas if any of these occur:

- job labels add no reader decision value;
- project voice is erased;
- stable inclusion reasons cannot be maintained;
- multi-membership implies matching or duplicates;
- any project becomes orphaned;
- evidence status or ceilings become less visible;
- formative retell performs worse than the atlas control.

Kill any implementation that profiles, ranks, matches, hides critical limits, silently resolves source conflicts, changes product truth, or treats writing status as maturity.
