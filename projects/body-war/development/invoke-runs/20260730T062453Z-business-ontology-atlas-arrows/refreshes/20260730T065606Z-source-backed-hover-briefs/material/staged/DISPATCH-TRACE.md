- Full Dispatch Spec: not required.
- Reason: the implementation route has one sequential Task Session owner and

  reusable dispatch graph is admitted.

## Considered And Rejected

- Parallel fan-out: rejected because 002/003 share renderer/test ownership and
  003/004 share generated artifact evidence.
- General graph-layout route: rejected because the known six-lens topology is a
  bounded prototype and layout generalization is deferred.
- Authority split gate: considered; no authority mutation crosses the route, so
  the owner-boundary check is sufficient.
