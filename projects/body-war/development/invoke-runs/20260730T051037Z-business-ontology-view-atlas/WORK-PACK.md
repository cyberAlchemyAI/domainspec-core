## Task Session closeout sync contract

For every SWU:

- lifecycle owner route: `invoke:refresh:apply-approved`;
- terminal source receipt:
  `projects/body-war/development/task-sessions/<SWU>/TASK-SESSION-RESULT.md`
  plus `EXECUTION-RECEIPT.json`;
- exact synchronization targets: this `WORK-PACK.md` and
  `CONTINUATION.json`;
- baseline: digest both targets at Task Session preflight and reject
  post-start drift;
- allowed owner deltas: current SWU status, evidence links, blocker/residue,
  and next cursor only;
- forbidden deltas: scope, acceptance, source digest lock, authority ceiling,
  or automatic successor authorization;
- expected owner receipt:
  `work-pack/closeout/<SWU>.receipt.json`;
- successor: the next manifest row only after both terminal and owner receipts
  pass.

The Task Session result and owner closeout receipt are separate artifacts.
Closeout cannot rewrite execution evidence or promote ontology/release state.
