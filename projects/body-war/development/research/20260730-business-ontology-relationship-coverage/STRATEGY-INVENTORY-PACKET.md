## Design Consequences

The research uses three explorer angles:

1. exact computational enumeration of the explicit graph and secondary
   reference surfaces;
2. formal interpretation of the CAV2 and Body War model contracts; and
3. adversarial product-path classification of every zero-degree node.

The handoff uses `exact-binding`: every count and classification must identify
the exact node file, relation pointer or absence test, and any lifecycle,
residue, branch, source, or challenge reference used to justify it.

Inventory remains a non-authority discovery surface. It cannot create a
relation, require universal connectivity, classify an isolate as a defect,
promote an ontology node, or authorize ontology mutation.
