# Body War Track Challenge Rules And Geometry — Decision-Ready Research Candidate

## Goal

Synthesize the competition, geometry, and economy-risk lanes from this one
dispatch into an owner-facing research candidate, while preserving every
evidence limit and downstream blocker.

## TL;DR

The bounded candidate replaces the paid-point rolling pot with an equal free
competition path, earned-only gameplay credits that cannot affect prizes, and
fixed precommitted fulfillment-backed sponsor vouchers in a separate ledger.
It organizes competition around Speed and fixed-duration Distance, three
comparison views plus a separate all-time record domain, immutable geometry
releases, and separate result-finality and reward-fulfillment state machines.

This is an informed candidate direction, not legality, adoption, accepted
Volta do Taquaral geometry, or authority to execute. Exact competition
identity, parameters, predicates, geometry bytes, safety/privacy operations,
sponsor backing, accountable authorities, and specialist classification
remain owner-selected or absent. Named-course execution, reward settlement,
and prize-bearing launch remain blocked.

## Context

The initiating concept combined daily, weekly, and monthly track challenges,
ability-sensitive competition, a named Volta do Taquaral course, and a
participant-paid point mechanism whose failed record attempts would enlarge a
rolling pot. That coupling made an inline answer insufficient: competition
design, exact geospatial provenance, trace verification, reward economics,
safety/privacy, and Brazilian specialist classification each needed a bounded
lane and adversarial review.

The dispatch therefore used three explorers, a synthesizer, three skeptics,
and three confrontation checks. The result is concrete enough for owners to
select, revise, or reject hypotheses. It is deliberately incomplete wherever
the evidence did not establish an exact rule, quantity, predicate, artifact,
authority, or approval.

## Plain-Language Candidate Game Loop

**Classification:** informed design hypothesis. This loop is decision material,
not legality, adoption, or permission to run an event.

1. A participant opts into one declared window through an **equal free path**.
   There is no purchase or point payment for competitive attempts, and a paid
   action cannot improve eligibility, attempt count, verification, ranking, or
   reward odds.
2. The participant chooses one candidate mode: **Speed**, minimizing wall-clock
   elapsed time over a completed course, or **fixed-duration Distance**,
   maximizing verified course progress during an owner-selected duration.
3. The participant records activity evidence. A recording does not consume an
   allowance. A submission targets one versioned competition identity. It
   consumes one admitted attempt only after deterministic preflight passes;
   the allowance, recovery interval, deadline, and system-fault reversal rules
   are all `TBD / owner-selected`.
4. Verification binds the evidence to one immutable geometry release and one
   ruleset. A quality anomaly can place the result on review hold; it cannot
   by itself establish fraud. Appeal and finality occur before standings or
   reward eligibility become final.
5. Only the participant's best **final-valid** result in a compatible
   comparison domain contributes. Owners must still decide whether that one
   result projects into the open, frozen-division, and Personal Breakthrough
   views or whether the views use separate competition identities.
6. A separately scoped all-time record can compare only compatible,
   predesignated record-eligible windows. Results never silently migrate
   across geometry versions.
7. Gameplay credits may be earned for non-economic play, but they are
   non-purchasable, non-transferable, non-cashable, and have **no prize-sizing,
   reservation, or settlement role**.
8. Any reward comes only from fixed, precommitted, fulfillment-backed sponsor
   vouchers held in a separate ledger. Participant value never enters the
   inventory. Only unused inventory whose backing remains valid may roll under
   owner-selected disclosed caps and expiry.
9. Window standings reset under declared interval rules. Personal baselines,
   ratings, records, and sponsor inventory change only through their separate
   owner-selected policies and state transitions.

The no-payment boundary is directly supported as a candidate exclusion by
`receipts/explorer-economy-risk.json#/model_comparison/0` and
`receipts/explorer-economy-risk.json#/model_comparison/2`; proof
status: explorer-reported risk comparison, not legal classification. The
specific split-ledger loop is an informed hypothesis at
`receipts/synthesis-revised.json#/rule_derivation/18` and
`receipts/synthesis-revised.json#/rule_derivation/19`.

## Illustrative Worked Scenarios

These scenarios explain candidate semantics only. They are not fixtures and do
not resolve any `TBD` policy.

### Scenario 1 — Daily Personal Breakthrough

Ana has an eligible compatible Speed baseline of 14:00 and records 13:52 and
13:40 in a candidate daily window. Recording alone consumes nothing. If the
13:40 submission becomes an admitted attempt and later reaches `final_valid`
under owner-selected verification and precision policies, it may become her
Personal Breakthrough. It does not by itself establish an open record, a
division win, or reward eligibility.

### Scenario 2 — Weekly Shared Tie

Bruno and Carla enter a weekly window with Division B frozen. Each obtains a
final-valid 26:55 Speed result in the same compatible comparison domain. If
the versioned precision policy cannot distinguish them, they share placing.
Submission or verification time never breaks the tie. Shared-voucher behavior
remains `TBD / reward-owner-selected`.

### Scenario 3 — Failed Admitted Record Attempt, No Participant Pot

A participant's record submission passes preflight, consumes one admitted
attempt, and then becomes `final_invalid` after review and appeal. The attempt
does not refund unless a prepublished system-fault rule applies. No payment or
gameplay point entered with the attempt, and its failure adds nothing to any
pot or sponsor voucher inventory.

### Scenario 4 — Final-Valid Record With Fixed Sponsor Inventory

A submission in a predesignated record-eligible window reaches `final_valid`
and is best in its compatible all-time record domain. Reward evaluation is
still separate. Only if a fixed precommitted fulfillment-backed sponsor unit
was bound before opening and the participant meets the owner-selected policy
may fulfillment move from `eligible` to `inventory_reserved`. A valid record
does not itself prove voucher issuance.

### Scenario 5 — Geometry Revision

A gate endpoint or corridor coordinate changes. The revised canonical bytes
must receive a new geometry identity/version and digest. Old results stay
bound to the old release and do not migrate into the new record domain. Until
the new bytes, provenance, calibration, safety review, and acceptance close,
the new geometry remains only a candidate.

### Scenario 6 — Anomaly To Hold Without Automatic Fraud

Elisa submits a Distance result whose evidence contains a vehicle-like speed
segment. The quality policy may place the result in `review_hold`. The anomaly
does not itself become a fraud finding or final invalidation. A named verifier
must apply owner-selected evidence and reason policies, preserve an appeal
path, and reach a separate provisional and final decision.
