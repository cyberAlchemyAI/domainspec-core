## Current-source gap

No direct Inventory item covers
`projects/domainspec-v2/development/comprehension-novice-test/`.
That is an explicit `no_inventory_match`, so the review must inspect its current
source bytes rather than treating an Inventory summary as a substitute.

The source instrument is a useful pattern, not a ready-made Body War test:

- its task measures ontology authoring, while the Body War surface is for
  reading, tracing, retelling, and locating boundaries;
- its agent novice is a structural pre-test, not human-comprehension evidence;
- its answer-key leakage incident shows that reviewer-only coverage material
  must not appear in the learner-visible stimulus;
- its blindness, identical-task, contamination, stall-class, and severity
  controls are reusable candidates;
- its Saga-specific baseline is historical and must not be copied as a current
  semantic model.

## Design consequences to test

1. Determine whether the HTML should contain a learner-visible comprehension
   prompt, a separate reviewer-only instrument, both, or neither.
2. Define fixed cold-reader tasks for the Body War view without embedding their
   answers. Candidate task families are locate, trace, retell, distinguish a
   sibling branch, and identify an authority boundary; the review must verify
   or reject them.
3. Adapt or replace the original stall classes
   (`selection`, `discrimination`, `edge`, `target-state`, `vocabulary`) for a
   reading surface instead of silently reusing them.
4. Keep learner and navigation projection fields outside ontology nodes and
   relations. The existing source of generated views remains
   `authoring/view-catalog.mjs` plus validated ontology records.
5. Keep the learner-visible task separate from the answer key, task-coverage
   audit, and scoring rubric. Any contamination invalidates the run.
6. Use agent-novice execution only as an L1 structural pre-test. Human
   comprehension requires a later, separately authorized human witness.
7. Require deterministic generation checks plus browser, keyboard, responsive,
   reduced-motion, and semantic-structure evidence before making UX claims.
8. Preserve the package ceiling: `candidate-local`, `authority_effect: none`,
   `release_admission: not-granted`.
