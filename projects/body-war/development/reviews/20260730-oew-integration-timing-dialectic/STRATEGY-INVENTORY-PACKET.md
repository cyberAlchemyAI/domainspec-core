## Design Consequences

1. Both lanes attack the same exact corpus; they differ by methodology, not by
   receiving different facts.
2. The integrate-now lane must seek the smallest no-duplication slice and name
   its exact validation surface.
3. The defer lane must identify a concrete blocker or dependency whose closure
   changes the integration—not merely prefer tidier governance.
4. A split recommendation is admissible: add a low-coupling launcher or
   reusable projection now while deferring deeper OEW focus-scene replacement.
5. The final answer must choose `integrate-now`, `defer`, or
   `split-now-and-later`, list disconfirming evidence, and preserve the
   candidate-local authority ceiling.
