# Hypotheses

## Product Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Signal | Expected Effect | Disconfirming Outcome | Status |
| -- | ---------- | ------ | --------------- | --------------------- | ------ |
| H-001 | Runners will return to defend owned territory if the game creates a clear loss event. | Share of runners who run again within 48h after invasion notice. | More than 30% of invaded runners attempt defense in pilot. | Less than 10% attempt defense despite receiving notice. | draft |
| H-002 | Territory ownership creates stronger retention than ordinary running badges. | Week-2 retention for runners with at least one owned zone. | Owners retain at materially higher rate than non-owners. | Ownership does not predict return behavior. | draft |
| H-003 | Clan obligations increase activity without making solo runners feel punished. | Clan-member run frequency and solo-runner drop-off. | Clan members run more often while solo retention remains stable. | Solo runners churn or clan members report pressure/fatigue. | draft |
| H-004 | A manual WhatsApp + Strava pilot can validate the core loop before mobile implementation. | Pilot completion rate and repeat defense behavior. | Manual loop produces enough evidence for MVP scope decisions. | Manual operations are too slow or confusing to test behavior. | draft |

## Game-Design Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Signal | Expected Effect | Disconfirming Outcome | Status |
| -- | ---------- | ------ | --------------- | --------------------- | ------ |
| H-005 | Composite Zone Score is fairer than fastest-time-only conquest for mixed runner abilities. | Complaint rate, repeat attempts, distribution of winners by pace level. | More runners believe they can compete through consistency. | Fast runners still dominate or rules feel opaque. | draft |
| H-006 | A 48h defense window creates urgency without requiring immediate response. | Time-to-defense and frustration feedback. | Most defenses occur within 48h and users perceive the window as fair. | Users miss defenses because window is too short or lose interest because it is too long. | draft |
| H-007 | Zone caducity after 14 days prevents stale maps and encourages revisits. | Share of ghost zones, revisit rate, neutralization events. | Active maps stay contested without excessive churn. | Users feel ownership is too fragile or inactive maps stay locked. | draft |
| H-008 | Sponsored zones can add real-world motivation without compromising fairness. | Sponsored-zone activity and fairness sentiment. | Sponsored zones increase visits without feeling pay-to-win. | Users perceive sponsor rewards as unfair or irrelevant. | draft |

## Risk Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Signal | Expected Effect | Disconfirming Outcome | Status |
| -- | ---------- | ------ | --------------- | --------------------- | ------ |
| H-009 | Anti-cheat can start with low-cost validation layers before full sensor fusion. | Cheat reports, false positives, manual review volume. | Basic speed/mock-location/coverage checks catch obvious abuse. | Cheating dominates pilot or legitimate runs are rejected often. | draft |
| H-010 | Notification urgency can drive premium conversion later, but should not be monetized before trust exists. | Willingness-to-pay interviews and trust feedback. | Users understand premium value after loop proves fun. | Delayed notifications feel exploitative or damage trust. | draft |
