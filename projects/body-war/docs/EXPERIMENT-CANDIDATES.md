# Experiment Candidates

## Candidate Experiments

| ID    | Experiment                | Purpose                                                                                                  | Method                                                                                                                                                                | Primary Signal                                     | Expected Effect                                                             | Disconfirming Outcome                                               | Status             |
| ----- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| E-001 | Park Track Control Pilot  | Validate whether runners care about controlling and defending named tracks before hex zones or app code. | Select one park, delimit 2-3 tracks, accept Strava screenshots/manual evidence, publish current controller per track, and open a 48h defense window after challenges. | Defense attempts within 48h of challenge.          | Meaningful repeat activity after a runner's controlled track is challenged. | Users treat it as a casual leaderboard and do not return to defend. | selected candidate |
| E-002 | Score Formula Simulation  | Compare composite score vs fastest-time-only outcomes.                                                   | Use sample run data or synthetic runs to compute ownership under both models.                                                                                         | Winner distribution and perceived fairness review. | Composite score supports more viable player types.                          | Formula feels opaque or produces unintuitive winners.               | candidate          |
| E-003 | Zone Size Walkthrough     | Test if ~400m hexes feel achievable and meaningful.                                                      | Map candidate pilot areas and ask runners to perform sample routes.                                                                                                   | Completion clarity and route friction.             | Runners understand how to capture zones without awkward routing.            | 60% perimeter rule is confusing or unsafe in real streets.          | candidate          |
| E-004 | Clan Defense Trial        | Test if small groups coordinate defense naturally.                                                       | Split pilot runners into small clans and announce invasion events.                                                                                                    | Number of allied defense attempts.                 | Clan members coordinate without heavy admin prompting.                      | Coordination adds confusion or social pressure.                     | candidate          |
| E-005 | Anti-Cheat Review Drill   | Estimate minimum validation needs.                                                                       | Review real pilot submissions for speed, coverage, route plausibility, and screenshot integrity.                                                                      | Flag rate and manual review time.                  | Simple checks catch obvious invalid runs.                                   | Admin review becomes too costly or unreliable.                      | candidate          |
| E-006 | Sponsored Zone Smoke Test | Test sponsor/reward motivation without building sponsor tooling.                                         | Add one manually sponsored zone with a small reward.                                                                                                                  | Activity lift near sponsored zone and feedback.    | Reward increases activity without fairness concerns.                        | Users ignore it or view it as unfair.                               | candidate          |

## Recommended First Experiment

Start with `E-001 Park Track Control Pilot`. It validates the central behavioral bet before hex maps, app implementation, monetization, advanced anti-cheat, or full scoring balance.
