`ide-assisted-arcanum-routing` spell candidate.

The repository already has the hard parts of the workflow:

- Necronomicon has active interaction, side note, candidate, gap, and handoff
  models.
- Dispatch Spec has bounded route menus, frames, handles, subagent strategy, and
  observability grouping.

- DomainSpec product evidence already wants action-bearing signals, graph
  navigation, agent runtime, and session contribution back to knowledge.

  observer envelopes.

The missing piece is the IDE bridge: a user-facing action card over a
conversation span or artifact handle that names the owner, preview, authority
class, and approval state.
