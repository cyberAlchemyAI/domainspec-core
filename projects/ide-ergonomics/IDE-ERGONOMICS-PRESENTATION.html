IDE Ergonomics

Where human attention and trusted AI route work together

A governed action-surface strategy for DomainSpec and Arcanum

Before

The user has to hold the whole workflow in their head.

A productive AI session creates useful residue: a term to define, a tangent to save, a handoff to prepare, and a route choice to make. Without an IDE surface, that all lives in memory.

Takeaway

The product is flow with receipts.

The end user does not buy a governance model. They get a cleaner workbench at the exact moment a chat turns into work.

One card. A concrete result. A visible boundary. The next session can continue without archaeology.
