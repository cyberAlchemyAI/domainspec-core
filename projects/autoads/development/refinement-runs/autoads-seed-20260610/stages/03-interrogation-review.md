# Stage 3 — Interrogation (refine-review)

- **Capability:** `interrogation` · mode `refine-review` · receipt: native-stage · **verdict: flag**

## Findings on the Define

1. **PASS — unit of value is concrete.** The "creative learning cycle" is a clean minimum unit and
   recomposes into the ontologize promotion DAG.
2. **PASS — claim discipline present.** [SB]/[adapt]/[H] labels used; cross-vertical claim correctly
   marked [H].
3. **FLAG — the "better" in "better creatives" is not yet operationalized.** Define says outcomes are
   measured but not against what baseline, with what confidence, or over what attribution window. The
   Design stage must specify the comparison method (a metric without a response window and confound
   control is the exact "metric≠authority" trap ontologize warns about).
4. **FLAG — auto-research direction is undefined.** Define names an auto-research candidate pool but not
   *what it researches* (competitor creatives? trend signals? prior winning patterns?) or how candidates
   are governed before generation. Design must scope it.
5. **FLAG — named external gap.** How real ad networks expose outcomes (metric names, attribution
   windows, API/data export, identifier sensitivity) is external to the repo. → routed to research decision.

## Verdict

`flag` — Define is sound but three items (comparison method, auto-research scope, ad-network outcome
shape) must be resolved in Design. No blockers.
