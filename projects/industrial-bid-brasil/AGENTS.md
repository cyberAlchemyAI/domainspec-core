# Industrial Bid Brasil Agent Notes

This project is a standalone Brazil industrial bidding product seed. Do not
assume GoldenQuill grant-writing rules apply here.

## Local Domain Rules

- Treat Wladimir Cardoso de Lima's seed intelligence as expert source material,
  not as a complete product specification.
- Separate public market data from private organization data.
- Do not scrape or automate private buyer portals without explicit operator
  approval and lawful credentials.
- Do not store confidential client bid files in public or shared paths.
- Keep scoring formulas explainable. Estimators must be able to inspect why a
  bid was flagged, blocked, or scored.

## Build Posture

- Prefer local-first prototypes before connectors.
- Keep data templates versioned and reviewable.
- Treat pricing recommendations as confidence-scored decision support, not as
  autonomous final price authority.
