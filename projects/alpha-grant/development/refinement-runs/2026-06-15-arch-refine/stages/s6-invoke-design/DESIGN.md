## Refined architecture (synthesis)

The Decentralized Living Library is redefined from a seven-lane prose network
into a **spine-and-extensions architecture** with an explicit ownership/authority
graph and boundary gates.

### 1. The spine (keystone)

**N1 Library** is the single public-benefit-collection node. It owns the
mission-locked asset class (accessions, DNA vouchers, host cultures, accession
DB, backup, public-access interface, governance policy). The spine is funded by
**A1 Bridge + A2 Infrastructure** and lives in a single **entity-home**
(recommended: fiscal sponsor now → new nonprofit as the bridge-funded
destination — see `entity-route-menu.md`, pending operator approval at g7).

### 2. Typed nodes, separated ownership

Five nodes typed by what they *may own* (`architecture-graph.md`). The decisive
structural rule (resolves review flag F1):

> **Authority ≠ ownership.** N5 Funding-OS (Inklovich / FIT Farms / Golden Quill)
> holds coordination authority (routing, QC, partner memory, funder comms) and
> **never holds title** to N1 public assets, **never directs** the N1 board, and
> **never binds a partner or pitches a funder** without N1 owner sign-off. Any
> operational involvement is a written service agreement, not ownership.

### 3. Extension lanes recompose through three connectors

Every non-spine lane (field N3, Houston N4, co-op crop, MycoFest, endowment)
attaches through exactly: (C1) shared accession/verification standard, (C2) a
declared asset-ownership lane, (C3) an entity-home relationship (same home /
fiscal-sponsored sub-lane / licensed-services / MTA). A lane that cannot declare
all three does not recompose and is flagged. This is the architecture's
extensibility contract.

### 4. Boundary gates encoded structurally (resolves F2; from boundary-auditor)

Each boundary is attached to a specific place, not left as cautionary text:

| Boundary | Architectural attachment | Fail-action |
|----------|--------------------------|-------------|
| O1 KU/INVAM | Lane-2 charter = concept-only; KU refs = factual-public-context only; written-permission checklist in A1 | flag |
| O2 unverified partners | `verification_status` field on every partner & opportunity; no name in a public ask unless status ≥ written | **block** |
| O3 Houston framing | "Houston Research Node / fungal systems research" label is invariant on every Lane-5 artifact; research questions precede any equipment line | flag |
| O4 public/private separation | N1 asset-ownership rules + service-agreement/ownership matrix; no MycoSymbiotics IP / FIT fee / co-op revenue line in the N1 budget | **block** |
| O5 claims review | claims-review sign-off on every outward-claim asset; unreviewed → `claims_status: unreviewed` | flag |
| O6 funder exposure | Top-50 queue only until entity-home + ask + owner + go/no-go locked; never the 368-row workbook to funders | **block** |

### 5. Required design constraints (carried into the plan)

1. `verification_status: [pending|written|confirmed|rejected]` on every partner
   and opportunity; public ask requires ≥ written.
2. Entity-home locked (g7) before any ask is drafted; appears as a header on
   every ask.
3. Lane-5 framing rule: fungal-systems-research-first; no automation/sensor
   language in the first two paragraphs or budget header.
4. Lane-2 stays concept-only until written KU/INVAM authorization is on file.
5. Service-agreement + asset-ownership matrix before any cross-node service ask.
6. Claims-review checklist on every outward-facing asset.
7. Funder-communication gating: Top-50 only until the four locks are set.
8. "Houston Research Node" phrase invariant across all Lane-5 documents.

## Dialectic outcome

The public-benefit vs fundability tension **converged** on five resolutions
(R1–R5 in `tension-residue.md`): asset-level mission-lock in writing first;
owner ≠ operator ≠ IP-holder; staged entity-home; O1/O2/O4/O5 as blocking gates;
extension via three connectors. Three residues remain with owners (Z1 outreach
timing → Plan; Z2 endowment timing → post-spine; Z3 contribution threshold →
N1 board). No residue silently promoted.

## Design verdict

`pass` — the architecture is now legible (graph), tension-resolved-or-ledgered,
boundary-gated, and recomposable. The one operator decision it surfaces (the
entity-home route) is bounded and approvable rather than open.
