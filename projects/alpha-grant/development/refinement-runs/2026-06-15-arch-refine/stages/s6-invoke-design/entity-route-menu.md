## Recommended route (both roles converge here)

**Stage the entity-home:** adopt **`fiscal-sponsor`** now as the Stage-1 home
(speed + immediate public-charity credibility + O4 enforced by the sponsor's
mission envelope), with a **written conversion plan to `new-nonprofit`** as the
A1-Bridge-funded Stage-2 destination (durability + strongest collection-funder
credibility). **`fit-farms-service-lane`** is the operator relationship (service
agreement, not ownership). **`coop-program`** is the home for the crop (Lane 4)
and festival (Lane 6) extension lanes only.

Disagreement that remains: the advocate would tolerate beginning *only* once a
fiscal sponsor's mission terms are signed; the pragmatist would open parallel
concept-note prep during the bridge period. Both accept asset-level mission-lock
in writing before the first dollar. Recorded in `tension-residue.md`.
