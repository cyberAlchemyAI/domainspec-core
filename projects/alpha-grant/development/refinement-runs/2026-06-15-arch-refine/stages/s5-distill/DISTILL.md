## Goal

Reduce the seven-lane / five-ask / four-node network to the **smallest coherent
architecture unit** that (a) is fundable on its own, (b) honors all boundaries,
and (c) recomposes into the full network. This is the `scu_swu_reduction`.

## Selected coherent unit: **C1 — the Library Spine**

The minimum viable spine is:

1. **One node** with public-benefit ownership rights (the entity-home, to be
   selected from the route menu).
2. **One asset class**: sequence-vouchered accessions + DNA vouchers + host
   cultures, mission-locked.
3. **Two asks**: the Bridge + governance seed (`$75K–$250K`) to stand up the
   entity/board/templates, and the Public living-library infrastructure ask
   (`$300K–$900K`) for curator/database/voucher/backup capacity.
4. **Governance**: accession policy, verification-status field on every partner
   and opportunity, and the O1–O6 boundary gates.

Everything else (field network, co-op, Houston node, MycoFest, endowment)
**attaches to** this spine as a typed extension lane with its own owner and
asset class — proving recomposition.

## Recomposition target (required by `recomposition_proof`)

The full network = Library Spine (C1) **+** extension lanes, where each extension
lane plugs into the spine through three connectors only: (a) a shared accession/
verification standard, (b) a declared asset-ownership lane, (c) an entity-home
relationship (same home, fiscal-sponsored sub-lane, or licensed-services
contract). If a proposed extension cannot declare all three connectors, it does
not recompose and is flagged.
