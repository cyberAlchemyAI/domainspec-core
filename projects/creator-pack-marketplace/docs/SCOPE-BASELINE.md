# Scope Baseline

## Intent

Explore a multi-creator marketplace for randomized collectible packages. A
creator may propose package contents, rarity tiers, probabilities, price, and
fulfillment terms. Buyers must be able to understand the package before paying
and verify what happened after opening.

The motivating stress case is a package containing an item valued at R$10,000
with a very small disclosed probability. That example is a calibration case,
not an approved product mechanic.

## Research Questions

1. Which mechanics on GradedShop are publicly evidenced, and which are only
   inferred from its presentation?
2. Which package models are coherent: finite inventory without replacement,
   replenished weighted pools, or another form?
3. Which formula makes odds, expected value, RTP, variance, fees, creator
   margin, reserves, and remaining inventory understandable?
4. What collateral, custody, or escrow is required before a creator can sell
   openings?
5. What must be committed immutably before the first sale, and what may change?
6. How should the system prevent self-dealing, wash trading, inventory
   substitution, deceptive valuation, and unfulfilled prizes?
7. Which Brazilian legal classifications or regulator boundaries could block
   or constrain the product?
8. Which mechanics remain interesting without relying on obscured odds or
   exploitative loss patterns?

## Required Distinctions

- random-selection integrity is not economic fairness;
- economic fairness is not legal permissibility;
- marketplace listing value is not guaranteed cash value;
- item custody is not the same as a promise to acquire an item later;
- a configurable creator tool is not permission to publish every configuration.

## Current Non-Decisions

- application architecture and technology stack;
- payment provider;
- custody and shipping partner;
- definitive Brazilian legal classification;
- allowed creator margin or minimum RTP;
- age and jurisdiction policy;
- launch scope or implementation work package.

## Readiness

Needs governed research. No implementation is authorized by this baseline.
