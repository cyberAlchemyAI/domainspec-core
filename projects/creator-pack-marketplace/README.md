# Creator Pack Marketplace

Research-first project for a marketplace where independent creators can publish
randomized collectible packages with configurable contents, tiers, odds, price,
and fulfillment terms.

The first question is not which application stack to use. It is whether the
package mechanics can be made simultaneously understandable, auditable,
solvent, abuse-resistant, and legally supportable.

## Current Stage

The project is in governed research. GradedShop is the initial reference surface,
but the target differs materially: third-party creators would be able to define
their own packages and economic parameters.

No implementation, launch, or legal-readiness claim has been approved.

## First Research Route

- Reference behavior: public GradedShop product, FAQ, and provably-fair surfaces.
- Probability and economics: odds, expected value, RTP, variance, reserves,
  inventory exhaustion, creator margin, and high-value prizes.
- Trust: committed package configurations, verifiable selection, fulfillment,
  anti-self-dealing, and auditability.
- Brazilian boundary: consumer, promotion, gambling, payment, marketplace,
  minors, and disclosure questions requiring primary-source research and later
  qualified legal review.

Research artifacts live under
`research/2026-07-15-gradedshop-and-fair-drop-mechanics/`.

## Custody And Settlement Direction

The current candidate direction is allocated physical custody: a professional
custodian holds each specific card, while an NFT represents the claim to redeem
that card. The customer-facing settlement is card-only: no platform buyback,
cash-out, internal wallet, or guaranteed monetary return. This is a candidate
design, not legal or implementation approval. The detailed research addendum is
in [Blockchain Card Custody And Card-Only Settlement](research/2026-07-15-gradedshop-and-fair-drop-mechanics/blockchain-card-custody-and-card-only-settlement.md).

## Boundary

“Provably fair” randomness is not treated as proof of fair economics. The
research must separately evaluate:

1. selection integrity;
2. probability disclosure and comprehension;
3. expected economics and creator solvency;
4. custody and fulfillment;
5. abuse resistance;
6. legal permissibility.
