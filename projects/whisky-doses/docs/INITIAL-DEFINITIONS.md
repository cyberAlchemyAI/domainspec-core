# Initial Definitions

## Glossary Seed

| Term                   | Working Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Category      | Status | Evidence Type |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------ | ------------- |
| Bottle Listing         | A posted offer for doses from a specific whisky bottle, including alcohol percentage, dose size, price per dose, total available dose slots, payment rule/deadline, and status.                                                                                        | business term | draft  | stated        |
| Dose                   | A fixed-volume portion of a bottle sold as one unit in a listing, such as 30 ml.                                                                                                                                                                                       | business term | draft  | stated        |
| Dose Slot              | A numbered position in a bottle listing that can be open, assigned to a buyer, or otherwise unavailable.                                                                                                                                                               | system        | draft  | stated        |
| Buyer                  | A group participant who requests doses. Name, WhatsApp mention, and CPF are required before reservation; full shipping address is required before first shipment.                                                                                                      | role          | draft  | stated        |
| WhatsApp Mention       | The group-visible identifier used to associate a buyer with one or more numbered dose slots.                                                                                                                                                                           | business term | draft  | stated        |
| Payment Opening Rule   | The policy that determines when dose payment should be requested, such as after a listing fills or at another configured point.                                                                                                                                        | workflow      | draft  | stated        |
| Payment Link           | A buyer-facing payment URL/identifier generated for a payment run. The operator selects one or more listings; the system opens one combined buyer obligation per buyer across the selected listing allocations, with line-item traceability to covered listings/doses. | system        | draft  | stated        |
| Payment Reconciliation | The process of matching payment evidence or provider results back to a buyer's payment obligation and updating paid/unpaid truth.                                                                                                                                      | workflow      | draft  | stated        |
| Payment Deadline       | The date by which buyers are expected to pay for allocated doses after payment opens.                                                                                                                                                                                  | business term | draft  | stated        |
| Reminder               | A buyer-facing follow-up message for payment, freight payment, or bottle listing activity.                                                                                                                                                                             | workflow      | draft  | stated        |
| Reminder Trigger       | A condition that makes a reminder eligible to be sent or rendered, such as payment opened, deadline approaching, buyer unpaid, freight quoted, or listing still open.                                                                                                  | system        | draft  | hypothesized  |
| Closed Listing         | A listing that no longer accepts new dose requests or slot changes under normal operation.                                                                                                                                                                             | business term | draft  | stated        |
| Waiting Dose           | A buyer-owned dose that has not yet been shipped and may be included in a future shipment batch.                                                                                                                                                                       | system        | draft  | hypothesized  |
| Shipment Batch         | An operator-managed shipping wave containing per-buyer packages. Each package may include that buyer's paid waiting doses across multiple listings.                                                                                                                    | workflow      | draft  | stated        |
| Freight Payment        | A later payment requested after freight is calculated for a buyer package. Freight payment is outside v1 scope.                                                                                                                                                        | business term | draft  | stated        |
| Transparency Snapshot  | A group-facing rendered view of listing, slot, payment, or shipment status intended to keep buyers informed.                                                                                                                                                           | system        | draft  | hypothesized  |
| Excel Control Sheet    | The current spreadsheet source used by the operator today. Excel migration/import is outside v1 scope.                                                                                                                                                                 | system        | draft  | stated        |

## Rules And Policies

| Name                              | Type   | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Source   | Status   |
| --------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------- |
| Slot Availability Rule            | rule   | A dose request can only be allocated when enough open slots exist in the listing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | operator | draft    |
| Listing Open Rule                 | rule   | Buyers can request doses only while the listing is open for allocation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | operator | draft    |
| Per-Person Dose Limit Rule        | rule   | If a listing has a buyer limit, allocation must not exceed that quantity for the buyer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | operator | draft    |
| Payment Opening Policy            | policy | Payment opens when a listing fills by default, or by manual operator action. Immediate-on-allocation payment is outside v1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | operator | selected |
| Payment Deadline Policy           | policy | Each listing can expose a due date such as `PAGAMENTO EM 20/06`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | operator | draft    |
| Payment Link Policy               | policy | Operator selects one or more listings for a payment run. The system defaults to all unpaid buyers in the selected listings, calculated owed amounts, listing-based descriptions, payment deadline/expiry, and no regeneration unless requested. Operators may customize buyer selection, description/message, deadline/expiry, and cancel/replace regeneration. Each buyer receives one combined obligation across selected listings. | operator | selected |
| Payment Reconciliation Policy     | policy | Uses Pix API webhook callbacks as the primary reconciliation path with a required provider correlation key, implemented for Pix as a system-generated `txid` or equivalent tied to the generated payment link. Processing is idempotent and saga-like, with cron retries for technical failures and UI notifications for unresolved exceptions. Operator manual paid marking is the simple fallback.                                  | operator | selected |
| Reminder Cadence Policy           | policy | V1 supports dose payment reminders and listing availability/fill reminders. Dose payment reminders occur when payment opens, 24h before deadline, on deadline day, and when overdue; reminders stop when payment is reconciled or suppressed by the operator.                                                                                                                                                                         | operator | selected |
| Shipment Eligibility Rule         | rule   | A dose should only be shipped after required dose payment is satisfied; freight calculation/payment is outside v1 scope.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | inferred | draft    |
| Batch Composition Policy          | policy | Shipment batching uses both levels: an operator-managed shipping wave contains per-buyer packages, and each package may include the buyer's waiting paid doses across multiple listings. Freight remains outside v1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | operator | selected |
| Excel Authority Policy            | policy | No Excel migration/import in v1. The system is authoritative for new v1 listing, allocation, and payment-run work.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | operator | selected |
| Buyer Identity Requirement Policy | policy | Buyers must have name, WhatsApp mention, and CPF before reserving doses; full shipping address is required before first shipment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | operator | selected |
| Buyer Data Retention Policy       | policy | Operator manages buyer data in v1; buyer self-service is deferred. Buyer data is kept while active and deleted or anonymized when the operator manually selects the buyer as inactive.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | operator | selected |
| Public Payment Status Policy      | policy | Copyable public listing output shows buyer-level paid/unpaid status next to each buyer/slot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | operator | selected |
| Excel Migration Scope Policy      | policy | Excel migration/import is out of scope for v1. The system should model new operations directly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | operator | selected |

## Metrics And Definitions

| Metric                          | Definition                                                                                               | Decision It Supports                                               | Status |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| Fill Time                       | Time from listing publication to all available slots filled or listing closed.                           | Whether allocation and public rendering reduce operator friction.  | draft  |
| Allocation Error Count          | Count of duplicate, oversold, or incorrect slot assignments per listing.                                 | Whether listing allocation should be prioritized.                  | draft  |
| Payment Delay                   | Time from payment opening to buyer dose payment completion.                                              | Whether payment tracking and reminders are needed early.           | draft  |
| Payment Reconciliation Accuracy | Share of payment evidences/results matched to the correct buyer, listing, and amount without correction. | Whether payment links can safely update paid/unpaid truth.         | draft  |
| Unmatched Payment Count         | Number of payments or evidences that cannot be matched to an obligation.                                 | Whether reconciliation rules/provider data are sufficient.         | draft  |
| Payment Link Click Or Use Rate  | Share of payment obligations where the buyer uses the provided link/instruction.                         | Whether payment links improve follow-through.                      | draft  |
| Reminder Conversion Rate        | Share of reminded unpaid buyers who pay within the observation window.                                   | Whether reminders are valuable and how cadence should work.        | draft  |
| Reminder Noise Rate             | Share of reminders judged duplicate, stale, irrelevant, or sent to the wrong buyer.                      | Whether reminder automation is too risky or needs stricter gating. | draft  |
| Freight Batch Age               | Age of oldest waiting paid dose included or not included in a shipment batch.                            | Whether shipment batching needs stronger prioritization.           | draft  |
