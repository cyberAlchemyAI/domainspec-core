01

Educator

You run a charter school. The PACSP RFA just dropped.

Tilth reads the RFA, drafts your sections from your own enrollment data, board materials, and prior submissions, scores the result against the PA Coalition's rubric, and tracks every required attachment. It writes in your school's voice — because your school's documents are what trained the working memory. Last year's reviewer feedback informs next year's draft.

02

Farm & Food Systems

You run a small farm. VAPG opens in March. The feasibility study is overdue.

Tilth pulls your business plan, your prior production records, and the latest USDA guidance. It identifies what's missing, drafts the work plan and budget narrative section by section, flags the SAM.gov registration steps, and runs the result against the FY26 scoring rubric before you submit. You stay the author. Tilth does the labor.

03

Municipal Steward

You're a borough manager. EPA brownfield funding closes in six weeks.

Tilth maps your project against the program's eligibility rules, drafts the application from your existing site assessments, council resolutions, and budget documents, and produces the procurement-friendly cost narrative your finance director needs to sign. Compliance lives next to the draft, not in a separate spreadsheet.

Arts & Culture

You run an arts nonprofit. The NEA Grants for Arts Projects window opens.

Tilth holds your program history, your artist roster, your audience data, and your prior LOIs. The institutional memory carries from one application to the next.
