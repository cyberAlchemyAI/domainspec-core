# GOLDEN QUILL — Tilth Console — Image-Gen Prompt Set

## Concept anchors (use these as preamble framing for any prompt)

The Tilth Console is the **operator's tactile hand on Tilth's cognitive field**. Where MyCel renders as a soft-synth panel (schematic, dark-navy, blueprint-style), the Console renders as a real brass instrument — a piece of furniture you'd find on a serious knowledge worker's desk. The hardware idiom is intentional: the Console is something you *touch*. Knobs you turn. A slider you push. A button you press. A microphone you speak into.

Aesthetic anchors:
- **Existing Tilth Console product photo** is the primary visual reference. Match its brass-on-black palette, its photographic-render quality, its "GOLDEN QUILL · Tools for thoughtful work." brand mark, its filigree-corner ornament, its three round mode buttons (sprout / community / lab beaker glyphs), its two large brass dials (Ambition / Certainty), its horizontal Depth slider, its etched microphone Voice control.
- **High-end audio gear meets watchmaker's plate** — the Console feels like a Bricasti or Universal Audio outboard unit cross-bred with a 1920s scientific instrument. Solid brass, polished walnut darkness, gold filigree, no plastic, no flat-UI affordances.
- **Hardware product render with technical-cross-section accents** — the Console plates can show controls in three-quarter product-photography view OR as brass-engraved cross-sections (gear teeth, dial mechanism internals, slider rail) where the close-up demands it.
- **Tagline of the Console**: *"Tactile control for governed thought generation."*
