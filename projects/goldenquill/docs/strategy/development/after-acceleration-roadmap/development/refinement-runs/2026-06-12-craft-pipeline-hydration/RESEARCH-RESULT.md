## Findings (claim → local source path)

1. **The roadmap is explicitly post-capital, not a build gate.** "WHAT COMES NEXT — AFTER STARTUP CAPITAL / ACCELERATION" — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (Sheet 2 title, line 84); reinforced by DESC-AAR-001 in `.craft/ledger.yml`.
2. **The eight themes are enumerated but not sequenced.** The workbook lists themes with roles but assigns no order, threshold, or trigger between them — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (Sheet 2 table, lines 88–97). This is the substance of `GAP-AAR-SEQUENCING`.
3. **Two themes already carry their own internal triggers in the source.** Team build-out is "sequenced against deployed-node count" and Service line validation hires an admin lead "only if pilots prove it" — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (lines 94, 97). These are the only explicit triggers present; the other six themes have none.
4. **Repeatability has a concrete, testable done-condition.** "Deployment playbook hardened until a non-founder runs an install end-to-end" — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (line 90). This is `GAP-AAR-REPEATABILITY` (treatment: validate; owner: customer-success-field-tech).
5. **Next financing names its own evidence set.** "Data room, measured unit economics, support-load evidence, renewal data, channel plan" — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (line 96). These five evidence classes are produced by instrumentation/measurement workstreams, tying financing readiness to QA/Measurement output (Victor, line 110).
6. **Instrumentation is the upstream source of the trigger metrics.** Sheet 1 task "Instrumentation: support hrs/node, usage metrics, baselines, renewal-intent capture" (BUILD then ONGOING, QA/Measurement) — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (line 61); and "Day 60 use review: metrics, support load, renewal signal" (line 76). The triggers GAP-AAR-SEQUENCING needs (deployed-node count, support load, renewal data) are generated by existing planned v0.1 instrumentation — meaning the sequencing evidence is a *downstream read* of v0.1 work, not new research.
7. **Service-line pilot risk dimensions are named.** "Grant-administration pilots at controlled scope; measure labor & reputational risk; hire admin lead only if pilots prove it" — `docs/golden-quill-task-role-breakdown-handoff.md` (line 94). This is `GAP-AAR-SERVICE-PILOTS` (treatment: pilot; owner: founder). The two measured dimensions are labor and reputational risk.
