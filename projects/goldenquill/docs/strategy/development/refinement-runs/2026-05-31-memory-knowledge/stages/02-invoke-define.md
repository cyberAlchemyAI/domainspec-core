# Stage 02 - Invoke Define

Status: pass, locally materialized.

Definition:

GoldenQuill Memory/Knowledge Mesh is the coordination layer that lets agents retrieve, cite, propose, approve, and reuse knowledge without violating Law 3, owner boundaries, or canonical-source authority.

Core distinction:

- Memory is operational residue from sessions, decisions, outcomes, and lessons.
- Knowledge is reusable domain or product content with provenance and scope.
- Authority is the rule that decides whether a retrieved item can affect a draft, task, gate, or future memory.
- Promotion is the governed transition from candidate evidence to canonical reusable memory/knowledge.

The layer must be local-first, provenance-bearing, fail-closed on org boundaries, and compatible with Arcanum Inventory/Ontology rather than replacing them.
