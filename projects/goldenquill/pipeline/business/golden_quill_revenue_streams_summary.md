## Revenue Architecture Summary

The business model has four revenue layers that compound:

**Layer 1 — Immediate Services (v0.1, today):** Grant writing, strategy consulting, RFA interpretation. Revenue from Josh's existing skills amplified by Tilth. No hardware sale required. Target: $50K–$150K/year.

**Layer 2 — Hardware + Setup (v0.1):** GB10 pass-through margin ($185–$480) + White Glove Setup ($1,500–$15,000). Each deployment is a $1,700–$15,500 event. Target: 10–20 deployments in Year 1.

**Layer 3 — Recurring (v0.1–v0.2):** Managed Mode ($149–$499/mo) + Cultivator seats ($29–$149/mo per seat). This is the compounding layer. 20 clients on Standard Managed = $35,760/year recurring. With seat expansion, $60K–$120K/year ARR by end of Year 2.

**Layer 4 — Ecosystem Scale (v1.0+):** Reseller program, CDFI partnerships, white-label licensing, API access, benchmarking data. Each channel multiplies Layers 1–3 without proportional effort increase.

### Financing & Capital Facilitation (New Category — 6 streams)

This is the most important structural addition to the venture. The HaaS-priced model you surfaced ("hardware you own at SaaS prices") only works without killing cash flow if financing is third-party. Five distinct revenue events within this category:

1. **Financing Origination / Brokerage Fee** (v0.2) — 1–5% of financed amount from the financing partner. $200–$1,500 per ~$15K deal. Capital-light.

2. **Hardware-as-a-Service Lease Revenue** (v0.2) — $300–$599/month all-in (hardware + setup amortized + Managed Mode) on a 3-year term. Transforms $15K CapEx into an OpEx line. 501(c)(3)s and school districts are ideal credit risks for the financing partner.

3. **Foundation-Funded Deployment Program** (v1.0) — $10K–$25K per deployment, foundation-funded. Highest-leverage channel — sophisticated buyer funding cohorts of grantees.

4. **Capital Pool / SPV Deal Origination** (Future) — 1–3% origination + 0.5–2% AUM. The endgame "capital gateway" positioning. Do not pursue until core business is profitable and 2+ years of tracked deal performance exist.

5. **Revenue Share in Client Ventures** (Future) — selective equity or rev-share where FIT Farms contributes material operational capability, not just grant writing.

6. **Tokenized Impact Pool Management** (Future) — true DeFi, Stage 5-adjacent. Only accurate use of the "DeFi" framing.

## Strategic Frames (from the 2026-04-19 expansion discussion)

**The venture is a capital access infrastructure layer, not a SaaS or hardware business.** Revenue follows from that positioning — service fees today, recurring subscription tomorrow, financing broker after that, capital routing at scale.

**The financing broker model is the unlock.** It converts $15K CapEx into $399/month OpEx without putting FIT Farms on the hook for the hardware loan. Three separate contracts required: hardware+setup (FIT Farms), financing (partner), SaaS (FIT Farms). Never guarantee payment or FIT Farms becomes the lender. Reputational risk on client failure remains — that is not transferable.

**"DeFi with no risk" is not accurate language.** The capital-light facilitator model has real risk (reputational, operational, partner concentration). True DeFi sits at Stage 5+ and requires tokenomics, AML/KYC frameworks, and protocol design. Use "vendor finance enablement" or "capital-efficient infrastructure sales with embedded financing" for current stages. The other framing is a regulatory risk.

**The productized first-win offer is a packaging decision, not a new revenue stream.** "$2,500 or we get you a $50K–$250K grant or you don't pay" = bundling Grant Strategy Consulting + Grant Writing Full Service + Performance Fee into one SKU with guaranteed minimum. It simplifies the sales conversation but doesn't add a line item. Worth building as its own hero offer doc separate from this database.
