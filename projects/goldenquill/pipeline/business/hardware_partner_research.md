**Date:** 2026-04-19
**Purpose:** Evaluate paths for Golden Quill / FIT Farms to recommend and earn margin on GB10-class hardware (Dell Pro Max GB10, Lenovo ThinkStation PGX) sold to nonprofit/ag cooperative clients without holding inventory or handling warranty.

## 1. Dell Partner Program

### Program Structure

Dell Technologies Partner Program has four tiers: **Authorized → Gold → Platinum → Titanium**. Tier progression is based on revenue thresholds and training/certification achievements.

### Authorized Tier (Entry Level)

- **Requirements:** Submit a partner application. No publicly stated minimum revenue threshold for Authorized status — it's the entry tier. Dell reviews and approves applications.
- **Benefits at Authorized:** Access to MyRewards points program, deal registration, Cloud Partner Connect, partner portal with quoting/ordering tools, and the RISE rewards program (additional incentives when purchasing through authorized distributors like TD SYNNEX or Ingram Micro).
- **Financial model:** Base rebates on sales from dollar one (no floor). Rebate multipliers for focus product lines and new customer acquisition. Marketing Development Funds (MDF) become more available at Gold+.

### Higher Tiers (for context)

- **Gold:** Revenue threshold not publicly disclosed but estimated ~$500K-$1M annual Dell sales. Adds enhanced rebates, proposal-based MDF.
- **Platinum/Titanium:** $5M+ and $20M+ respectively. Restore Titanium incentives new for 2026.

### How It Works for a Small Operator

At Authorized tier, you'd register deals through the partner portal, quote the Dell Pro Max GB10 to your client at a partner-discounted price, and place the order through Dell or a distributor (TD SYNNEX, Ingram Micro). Dell ships direct to the client. You never touch inventory. Dell handles warranty. Your margin comes from the spread between partner cost and sell price, plus rebates.

Specific margin percentages are under NDA once you're in the program, but channel sources indicate **5-12% on workstation-class hardware** depending on deal registration, volume, and whether you're buying through distribution or direct.

### Application

- **Partner Portal:** https://www.delltechnologies.com/partner/en-us/partner/partner.htm
- **Registration Guide:** https://www.delltechnologies.com/partner/en-us/partner/partner-registration-guide.htm
- **Self-Application Guide:** https://www.delltechnologies.com/partner/en-us/partner/self-application-guide.htm

### Dell Affiliate Program (Lighter Option)

Dell also runs a consumer/SMB **affiliate program** — no inventory, no VAR status needed:

- **Commission:** 3% on home products, up to 5% on Dell Small Business products
- **Cookie:** 90 days
- **No minimum payout**
- **Managed through:** CJ Affiliate, Sovrn Commerce, FlexOffers
- **Limitation:** This is a consumer/web-referral model. Not designed for B2B consulting relationships. You'd get a tracking link. Client buys online. You get a percentage. No deal registration, no quoting, no gov/edu pricing access.

**Verdict:** The affiliate program is too thin for what Golden Quill needs (consulting + procurement facilitation for nonprofits). The Authorized VAR tier is the right fit — low barrier to entry, no inventory requirement, deal registration protects your margin.

## 2. Lenovo Partner Program (Lenovo 360 / Partner Engage)

### Program Structure

Lenovo 360 framework with tiers: **Authorized → Silver → Gold → Platinum**.

### Tier Revenue Thresholds

| Tier | Annual Revenue Requirement |
|------|--------------------------|
| Authorized | None (entry on registration) |
| Silver | $250,000 |
| Gold | $1,000,000 |
| Platinum | $10,000,000 |

### 2026 Program Changes

- **Certification requirements eliminated** for tier advancement — revenue is now the sole driver.
- **Lenovo 360 Elevate** framework helps partners move from Authorized upward.
- **Simplified tier progression** — less complexity in advancement criteria.
- Recognized as a **5-star program** in the 2026 CRN Partner Program Guide.

### Authorized Tier Details

- Entry-level status upon registration — minimal requirements.
- Access to Lenovo Partner Hub portal for quoting, ordering, deal registration.
- Partner pricing through authorized distributors (TD SYNNEX, Ingram Micro, D&H).
- Lenovo ships direct or through distribution. No inventory holding required.

### Financial Model

Like Dell, specific margins are under NDA once enrolled. Industry benchmarks for workstation-class hardware through Lenovo channel are comparable (5-10% margin on hardware, higher on attached services/support).

### Application

- **Lenovo Partner Hub (registration):** https://www.lenovopartnerhub.com/web/lenovo-global-site/getting-started
- **Lenovo Partner Network (US):** https://www.lenovopartnernetwork.com/us/
- **Reseller Benefits & Form:** https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/business/reseller-benefits/form/
- **Lenovo Partner Advantage:** https://www.lenovopartneradvantage.com/

### Lenovo Referral-Only Option

Lenovo does not appear to offer a public affiliate/referral program comparable to Dell's. The Authorized partner tier IS the lightweight entry point. Given no certification requirements and no revenue minimum, it's already designed for small operators.

## 3. Government & Nonprofit Procurement Channels

### Dell Government Contracts

**GSA Schedule:** Dell Federal Systems L.P. holds GSA MAS contract **47QTCA22D003G**. The Dell Pro Max GB10 is **TAA certified** for federal procurement and is actively marketed to federal agencies for on-premises AI workloads.

**NASPO ValuePoint:** Dell holds active NASPO ValuePoint contracts, enabling state and local government procurement across all 50 states with pre-negotiated pricing. Details at: https://www.dell.com/en-us/dt/dellnaspovp/program-and-contracts/index.htm

**PEPPM:** Dell holds 3 PEPPM Product Line contracts available nationally (all 50 states). PEPPM is run by Central Susquehanna Intermediate Unit (CSIU) in Pennsylvania — relevant for PA-based nonprofits and educational institutions. Details at: https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/dt/industry-state-and-local-government-contracts-peppm

**State contracts:** Dell maintains individual state contracts in addition to NASPO. Full list: https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/dt/industry-state-and-local-government-contracts

**Carahsoft:** Dell products also available through Carahsoft's GSA Schedule for government buyers. https://www.carahsoft.com/dell/contracts

### Lenovo Government Contracts

**NASPO ValuePoint:** Lenovo holds contracts **#23013** (Personal Computing Devices — Windows & Non-Windows OS) and **#23014** (Servers and Storage). ThinkStation PGX would fall under #23013. Details at: https://techtoday.lenovo.com/us/en/naspo and https://solutions.lenovo.com/naspo/

**GSA Schedule:** Lenovo maintains GSA MAS schedule presence. Products available through GSA Advantage.

**State/local:** NASPO contracts extend to agencies in participating states, local government units, and other authorized entities (which can include nonprofits in some states depending on local cooperative purchasing rules).

### Can a VAR Facilitate Gov/Cooperative Purchasing?

**Yes, with caveats.** As an Authorized partner with either vendor:

- **Federal (GSA):** You'd typically work alongside or through the vendor's federal sales team or a master government aggregator like Carahsoft or CDW-G. Small VARs can register deals and earn margin on federal sales, but the procurement vehicle (GSA Schedule) runs through the prime contractor (Dell Federal Systems, Lenovo's federal entity).
- **State/Local (NASPO, PEPPM):** These are cooperative purchasing contracts. State/local/edu/nonprofit buyers can purchase directly through the contract. As a VAR, your value-add is configuration consulting, needs assessment, and pointing the buyer to the right contract vehicle. You may or may not be in the purchasing chain depending on how the specific state implements the contract.
- **Nonprofits:** Most cooperative purchasing contracts allow eligible nonprofits to buy through them. PEPPM is particularly nonprofit-friendly for PA entities. NASPO eligibility varies by state.

**The realistic play for Golden Quill:** Position as a **consultant/integrator** who helps the client identify the right configuration, maps it to the right procurement vehicle, and earns margin either through VAR deal registration or a consulting fee for procurement facilitation.

## 5. Recommendations for Josh / Golden Quill

### Preferred Path: Dual Authorized VAR Status

1. **Register as Authorized partner with both Dell and Lenovo.** Both programs have minimal entry requirements — fill out the application, get approved. No revenue minimums at Authorized tier. No inventory. No certification requirements (Lenovo eliminated these; Dell's are minimal at Authorized).

2. **Why both?** Different clients may have vendor preferences or existing relationships. Gov/edu buyers may be locked into one vendor's contract vehicle. Having both gives you flexibility.

3. **Revenue model per sale:** On a ~$3,700 system, expect $185-$444 margin (5-12%) through deal registration + partner pricing. Add consulting/configuration fees on top for the Tilth setup, training, and ongoing support — that's where the real margin lives.

### Procurement Facilitation Value-Add

For nonprofit/cooperative clients who can access NASPO ValuePoint or PEPPM contracts, your value is:
- Helping them identify which contract vehicle they're eligible for
- Specifying the right configuration
- Navigating the procurement process
- Setting up Tilth on the hardware after delivery
- This is a **consulting engagement** that sits alongside the hardware margin

### What to Skip

- **Dell Affiliate Program:** Too thin (3-5% on consumer web orders, no B2B quoting, no gov pricing). Not worth the effort for your model.
- **Higher VAR tiers:** Don't chase Gold/Platinum. The Authorized tier gives you everything you need for the volume you'll do in Year 1. Tier up organically if volume grows.
