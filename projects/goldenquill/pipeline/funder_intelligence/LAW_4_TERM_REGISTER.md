# Law 4 Term Register

This register governs active admin-risk term scanning. Judge and Red Team agents use these substitutions to ensure apolitical, funder-preferred dialects are used in all federal and high-risk applications.

| Term / Pattern                     | Risk Context                                                                                  | Use Instead                                                                                      | When to Substitute                                                                    | When to Retain                                                                                          | Applies To             | Owner             | Notes                                                                      |
|:-----------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------|:------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| food justice                       | Federal current-administration risk; can be read as ideological rather than service delivery. | food access; nutrition access; community food security; local food infrastructure                | Federal applications, federal pass-throughs, or politically sensitive public sources. | Foundation/community funders that explicitly use justice language and reward it.                        | food_systems           | Scribe + Logician | Do not erase truth; translate to funder-native dialect.                    |
| justice-involved                   | Federal current-administration risk; may trigger politicized readings.                        | returning citizens; reentry participants; people returning from incarceration; workforce reentry | Federal applications unless solicitation uses the term.                               | Reentry-specific funders or legal-service funders that use the term.                                    | education; workforce   | Scribe + Logician | Use plain factual language and avoid euphemism that hides program purpose. |
| equity                             | Federal current-administration risk in some notices and reviews.                              | broad access; fair access; high-need communities; community benefit; opportunity access          | Federal applications when not solicitation language.                                  | Local foundations, Pittsburgh Foundation/Opportunity Fund, and funders with explicit equity priorities. | all                    | Judge + Scribe    | The workbook should preserve funder dialect, not impose one vocabulary.    |
| underserved                        | Can be acceptable in many federal notices but still should be checked.                        | high-need; low-access; limited-resource; low-income; rural; eligible population                  | Use specific population descriptors from NOFO where possible.                         | Retain when the NOFO uses the term or defines it.                                                       | all                    | Logician          | Prefer exact NOFO language.                                                |
| DEI / diversity, equity, inclusion | High federal risk under current policy climate.                                               | access; participation; representation; community-informed design; workforce readiness            | Federal applications unless required by program.                                      | Private philanthropy or education/workforce funders that explicitly request DEI.                        | education; research    | Scribe + Logician | Keep values; translate register.                                           |
| environmental justice              | Federal risk varies by agency/notice; can still appear in some official language.             | environmental health; pollution reduction; conservation outcomes; community exposure reduction   | Use cautiously in federal applications unless solicitation uses it.                   | Retain for funders that name environmental justice as a priority.                                       | food_systems; research | Logician          | Match solicitation exactly.                                                |
| community-led                      | Low risk but can become vague.                                                                | community-informed; resident-informed; partner-reviewed; stakeholder-designed                    | Use where reviewer needs operational specificity.                                     | Retain where funder values participatory design.                                                        | all                    | Scribe            | Make the governance mechanism explicit.                                    |
