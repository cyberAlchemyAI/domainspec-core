# Golden Quill

**Sovereign funding intelligence. Customer-owned hardware. Customer-owned keys. Defensible sovereignty under three explicit deployment postures.**

Golden Quill is the product. **Tilth** — named for the condition of soil prepared for growing — is the software intelligence inside it. Tilth writes, reviews, and scores the full funding stack: grant proposals, fellowship and residency applications, sponsorship proposals, business plans, and proformas. It runs on a 128 GB desktop the customer owns, with local-first storage, BYOK API keys, and explicit cloud-route classification. Confidential or operator-flagged content stays local; cloud routes are operator-curated and audit-recorded; the company that ships this could disappear tomorrow and the operator would still have their machine, their data, their keys, and their grants.

## What it is

A six-seat AI council (Scout, Scribe, Editor, Judge, Red Team, Logician) plus a small support-agent set (Intake, Uploader; Data Collector and Report Generator at v0.2; Archivist at v1.x) — running on the customer's own hardware under an operator-curated classification register that decides, per field, what is allowed to leave the machine. No seat outranks another. No prompt, skill, or model can override the Five Operating Laws (§"How it works" below). Every cloud route is explicit, classification-allowed, BYOK-backed, and audit-recorded.

The system does not invent applicants or grants. It shapes existing credentials, data, partnerships, and budgets into funder-ready form — flagging knowledge gaps, dialect mismatches, admin-risk language, and missing evidence before a draft reaches the funder.

## Why it matters

This is not a demand-invention play. The founder has already won six competitive grants in 2026  — NorthEast area, from food-systems to robotics — using openclaw and the agents in the workflow this system encodes. Tilth productizes that working substrate. The commercial wedge is sovereignty: a customer-owned 128 GB desktop, BYOK keys, fail-closed Logician gates, and three explicit deployment postures (`Frontier-Eligible`, `Split-Context Hybrid`, `Local-Only / Sovereign`) instead of the SaaS-vendor-controls-your-data default. That sovereignty is not a slogan; it is enforced by the routing substrate documented below.

Phase One — Founding 12 — targets twelve Pittsburgh-area Founder Node deployments across six customer identity categories (Food Systems, Educator, Public Agency, Arts & Culture, Cooperative, Ecosystem Intermediary) between mid-July 2026 and mid-April 2027. Phase Two (April 2027 forward) opens to tribal sovereign configurations, researchers, foundations, institutional research offices, and multi-site consortiums only after the Founding 12 prove deployment, support, card logic, and renewal behavior.
