Program Solicitation: Core Programs

The NSF Directorate for Computer and Information Science and Engineering (CISE) supports investigative research and education projects that develop new knowledge in all aspects of computing, communications, and information science and engineering, as well as advanced cyberinfrastructure.

The core programs of the CISE divisions of Computing and Communication Foundations (CCF), Computer and Network Systems (CNS), and Information and Intelligent Systems (IIS) are open to proposals in the areas described below. Proposers are encouraged to read the descriptions carefully to determine the most appropriate program for their submission.

Eligibility Constraint (Node Warning)

Organizations must be non-profit, non-academic entities with a registered DUNS holding specialized clearance for autonomous computing research (Ref: Sec 4.1.a).

Information and Intelligent Systems (IIS)

The IIS division supports research and education activities that develop new knowledge about the role of information in the context of human activities and the design of systems that can process, manage, and understand that information. Relevant areas include human-computer interaction, artificial intelligence, computer vision, natural language processing, and data science.
