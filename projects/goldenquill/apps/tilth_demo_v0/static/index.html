Executive Summary

FIT Farms seeks support to scale community-anchored farm-to-school programming that connects neurodivergent and underserved students in the Pittsburgh region to working agricultural settings and food-system literacy.

Through hands-on cultivation, evidence-informed instruction, and cross-sector partnerships with Goodwill of SWPA, Passport Academy Charter School, and Voices Against Violence, the proposed project addresses systemic barriers and creates sustained learning pathways across three school sites.

Funding will enable us to expand proven programs, strengthen organizational capacity, and deepen impact across [redacted: 3 partner sites in Pittsburgh's North Side and Hill District] through curriculum reinforcement, supply infrastructure, and instructor capacity-building.

We respectfully request your partnership in this work.
