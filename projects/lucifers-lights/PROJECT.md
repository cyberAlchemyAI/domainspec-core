# Project Brief

## Premise

Lucifer operates a service called Lucifer's Lights. People request help after ordinary doors have stopped opening; he names a price, honors exact terms, and preserves the client's ability to refuse. Whether he is truly the fallen angel or only an extraordinary human fixer remains deliberately unresolved.

Chapter 1 follows Mara Vale, who asks Lucifer to find her missing sister Elena before sunrise. The request conflicts with an earlier Light in which Elena asked to become impossible to find—especially for Mara. Mara voluntarily accepts a narrower amendment, discovers she is being tracked, and hears Elena warn that Lucifer's app has been compromised. The chapter ends when a nonexistent client named Michael offers Lucifer his true name in exchange for delivering Mara to Vesper's abandoned cathedral.

## Creative promise

- Mature dark urban fantasy with crime-thriller momentum.
- First-person Lucifer narration: intimate, charismatic, precise, funny, and unsettling.
- Mythology remains Lucifer's interested interpretation rather than neutral exposition.
- Supernatural events retain a plausible mundane reading whenever possible.
- Human choice matters; contracts constrain Lucifer instead of granting arbitrary power.
- Mara remains an active decision-maker, never cargo inside Lucifer's story.

## Recurring scene engine

1. A person makes a concrete request.
2. Lucifer tests its meaning and names compensation.
3. The client knowingly accepts, refuses, or amends the terms.
4. Performance reveals a hidden conflict or human cost.
5. The consequence creates the next request, debt, or choice.

## Editorial rule

Lucifer's interjections are part of the novel's identity, but each one must touch the live scene. An aside earns its place by reading a person, exposing a rule, changing a decision, sharpening danger, or advancing the clock. Longer meditations belong in moments where the action can carry their weight.

## Current boundary

The accepted story direction and generated assets are repository-local working material. The project does not claim publication, release, deployment, or external distribution. Chapter 1's latest revision remains a review candidate until the author accepts it.
