# Lucifer's Lights

Repository-local working project for the *Lucifer's Lights* novel and its graphic-novel and narrated-motion-comic derivatives.

## Start here

- [Project brief](PROJECT.md) — premise, creative promise, and working boundaries.
- [Craft status](CRAFT.md) — current decisions, gaps, gates, and next moves.
- [Source map](SOURCE-MAP.md) — which files are canonical, derived, or historical.
- [Characters](story-bible/CHARACTERS.md), [locations](story-bible/LOCATIONS.md), and [continuity](story-bible/CONTINUITY.md) — Chapter 1 story facts.
- [Voice guide](editorial/VOICE-GUIDE.md) and [revision log](editorial/REVISION-LOG.md) — editorial discipline and change history.
- [Whisper ontology](ontology/README.md) — candidate-local intent, story entities, knowledge states, edge policies, and validation.

The authoritative project ledger is [.craft/ledger.yml](.craft/ledger.yml). `CRAFT.md` is its human-readable view, not a second source of truth.

## Current state

Chapter 1 exists as a complete candidate draft. A first tracked editorial revision has clarified Vesper, tethered several Lucifer interjections to the live scene, and restored the sunrise countdown through the ending. That revision still needs a human read before it becomes the accepted Chapter 1 baseline.

Sixteen selected graphic-novel keyframes and an ElevenLabs-ready voiceover script already exist. They remain derivative artifacts and must be checked for drift after the revised prose is accepted.

A closed-world Whisper ontology now maps the current authoring intent, six candidate
characters, five candidate places, six candidate encounters, and twenty-three
evidence-bearing knowledge records. Its mechanical policy checks pass, but it remains
candidate-local until the author reviews its shape and deferred semantics.

Nothing in this project is committed, published, released, or production-proven.
