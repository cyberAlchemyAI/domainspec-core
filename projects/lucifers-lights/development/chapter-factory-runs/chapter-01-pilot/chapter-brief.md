# Chapter Factory Brief — Chapter 01 — Presentation Is Everything

Status: `accepted-pilot-baseline`

## Objective

Use the existing Chapter 1 candidate to prove a repeatable authoring, review, correction, and exact author-decision loop for Lucifer's Lights.

## Chapter engine

- A person makes a concrete request.
- Lucifer tests its meaning and names compensation.
- The client knowingly accepts, refuses, or amends the terms.
- Performance reveals a hidden conflict or human cost.
- The consequence creates the next request, debt, or choice.

## Preserve

- Lucifer's intimate, exact, amused, and unsettling first-person voice
- Mara's choices and agency in the agreement, escape, and tracker removal
- The priority of Elena's earlier Light and the voluntary amendment
- The mundane-supernatural ambiguity of the city, app, and white flame
- The sunrise clock and Michael cliffhanger

## Open questions

- Why did Elena need Mara excluded from finding her?
- What is the ledger, and who wants it?
- Who compromised Lucifer's app?
- Who or what is Michael?
- Who is approaching down the station stairs?
