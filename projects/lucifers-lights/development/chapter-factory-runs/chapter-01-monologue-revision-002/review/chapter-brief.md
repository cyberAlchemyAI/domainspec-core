# Chapter Factory Brief — Chapter 01 — Presentation Is Everything

Status: `accepted-pilot-baseline`

## Objective

Revise the accepted Chapter 1 baseline through the author-approved Lucifer opening audition, a more explicit but still ambiguous account of Lucifer's Lights, a compressed unfinished lock-and-key parable, selective deepening or removal of internal asides, and a project-local reader-discovery rule that keeps mechanics explicit while delaying thematic interpretation into evidence and payoff.

## Chapter engine

- A person makes a concrete request.
- Lucifer tests its meaning and names compensation.
- The client knowingly accepts, refuses, or amends the terms.
- Performance reveals a hidden conflict or human cost.
- The consequence creates the next request, debt, or choice.

## Preserve

- The accepted transaction-first Halo Café opening and immediate 2:13 clock.
- Lucifer's intimate, exact, amused, vain, and unsettling first-person voice.
- Mara's choices and agency in the agreement, escape, station decision, and tracker removal.
- The priority of Elena's earlier Light and the voluntary amendment.
- The mundane-supernatural ambiguity of Lucifer, the city, the app, and the white flame.
- The sunrise clock, Michael request, and final symmetry reversal.

## Open questions

- Why did Elena need Mara excluded from finding her?
- What is the ledger, and who wants it?
- Who compromised Lucifer's app?
- Who or what is Michael?
- What is Lucifer's true name?
- Who is approaching down the station stairs?
