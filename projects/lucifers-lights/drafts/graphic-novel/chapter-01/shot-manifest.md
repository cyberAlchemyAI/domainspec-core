## Shared prompt contract

Create a cinematic 16:9 keyframe for a mature neo-noir graphic novel: painterly ink illustration, expressive faces, controlled linework, rain-slicked urban textures, deep charcoal shadows, warm café amber, cold cyan rain, and sparing crimson supernatural light. Keep it atmospheric and grounded rather than photorealistic. Preserve character identity, wardrobe, props, spatial logic, and weather from the approved Halo meeting anchor.

- Lucifer: human-looking man with warm olive-medium skin, angular face, light stubble, shoulder-length wavy black hair, white suit jacket, and open-collar black shirt. Charismatic and unsettling; never give him horns, wings, glowing eyes, or a monster form.
- Mara: young woman with wet jaw-length black hair, charcoal-black rain jacket, guarded posture, and a pale desaturated-yellow hospital bracelet through KF14.
- Ruth: older woman with a silver low bun, burgundy blouse, charcoal cardigan, and café apron.
- Motifs: circular Halo sign, rain, train vibration, cathedral silhouettes, restrained white or red flame-like light, reflections, and contracts expressed through choices and objects.
- Do not bake in captions, dialogue balloons, logos, watermarks, or readable phone-interface text. Lettering and UI overlays belong to post-production after the narration is attached.
