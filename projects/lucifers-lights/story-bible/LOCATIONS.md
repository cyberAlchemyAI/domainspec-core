# Locations

## Vesper

Vesper is the city in which Chapter 1 takes place. Its current name is contested by older passports, implying political or governmental renaming without yet defining the complete history.

The city has two practical maps:

1. The public map of streets, monuments, tourists, taxpayers, and police.
2. Lucifer's remembered map of service tunnels, broken fences, bribed doormen, unfinished buildings, and briefly unlocked doors.

Vesper is rain-soaked, flood-prone, threaded by elevated rail, and built around overlapping official and unofficial systems.

## Mercy Avenue

The flooded neon avenue containing the Halo Café. An elevated railway runs above it. The abandoned cathedral stands across the street from Halo.

## Halo Café

- All-night meeting place beneath the elevated railway.
- Hot coffee, broken security cameras, and an owner who does not remember faces.
- Windows and lights react to trains passing every six minutes.
- Each train gives nine seconds of acoustic privacy.
- Connected to a kitchen, storeroom, rear alley, and counter hatch used during the escape.
- Its isolated blackout is deliberate rather than storm-wide.

## Old cathedral

- Abandoned and locked for eleven years.
- Located across from Halo.
- Its bell rings once during the storm despite the closure.
- Michael's request names it as the delivery destination for Mara.
- Whether it has a literal supernatural role remains unresolved.

## Saint Orison Hospital

- Mara wakes there after being drugged.
- No one can identify who admitted her.
- One of the two pursuers was present there.
- The hospital bracelet is actually a tracking device and is not hospital-issued.

## Saint Orison Station

- Reached through a flood-damaged maintenance entrance.
- Nearly empty at night, with a closed southern platform, lockers, and northbound service.
- Locker 318 contains Elena's controlled dead drop.
- Chapter 1 ends below its stairs while footsteps approach.

The shared Saint Orison name does not yet prove an operational relationship between the hospital and station.
