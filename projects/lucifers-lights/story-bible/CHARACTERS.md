# Characters

Only Chapter 1 evidence is recorded here. Unknowns remain unknown.

## Lucifer

- First-person narrator and operator of Lucifer's Lights.
- Presents as a human-looking man in a white suit with an open-collar black shirt.
- Uses Lucifer as a chosen client-facing name; his true identity remains unresolved.
- Claims an affinity with the fallen angel and gives a self-interested interpretation of Eden, knowledge, and free will.
- Demonstrates logistics, surveillance awareness, contract discipline, city knowledge, and a network of reciprocal favors.
- Values presentation, precision, choice, and exact promises.
- Fascinated by humans, sometimes tenderly and sometimes predatorily.
- Bound by his own rules: no children, no worship, no cruelty without purpose; a client may refuse a collected favor but loses access to future Lights.
- Cannot simply override an older conflicting Light.
- Does not recognize Michael as a valid client and does not understand the offered compensation of his true name.

### Voice constraints

- Intimate and conspiratorial rather than theatrically demonic.
- Humor should emerge from observation, timing, contradiction, or cost.
- Interjections must remain tethered to a person, rule, danger, choice, or clock.
- Apparent supernatural knowledge must retain a mundane explanation where the scene permits one.

## Mara Vale

- Approximately twenty-five, with wet black hair and a hospital bracelet when she reaches Halo.
- Elena Vale's younger sister.
- Requests that Lucifer find Elena and bring her home before sunrise.
- Was abducted from her apartment, drugged, and woke at Saint Orison wearing the tracking bracelet.
- Does not know where Elena hid the ledger sought by her abductors.
- Chooses the narrower amended Light after reading its terms.
- Identifies the moth charm as belonging to her mother.
- Rejects a safe train escape, chooses to hear Elena's message, and removes the tracker herself.
- Ends Chapter 1 beside Lucifer while pursuers approach.

## Elena Vale

- Mara's older sister.
- Wears their mother's silver moth necklace in the beach photograph.
- Purchased the earlier Light: make her impossible to find, especially for Mara.
- Created an emergency channel using Ruth, the moth-marked locker key, locker 318, and a recorded message.
- Scheduled the message that directed Mara to the man in white before disappearing.
- Says she is alive, hid a ledger, and believed disappearing would protect Mara.
- Warns that Lucifer's app is compromised and that Michael wants Lucifer to remember.
- Physical location remains unknown.

## Ruth

- Seventy-two-year-old owner of the Halo Café.
- Practical, formidable, and part of Lucifer's reciprocal human network.
- Holds Elena's black envelope until the white flame turns red.
- Uses the train noise, counter hatch, and a coffee-pot diversion to enable Mara and Lucifer's escape.
- The pre-project draft contained additional history concerning Ruth's son; that material was removed from the active chapter and remains reserve material rather than current revelation.

## Michael

- Named as a client by the compromised app.
- Never speaks or appears directly in Chapter 1.
- Requests delivery of Mara to the old cathedral before sunrise.
- Offers Lucifer's true name as compensation through a field Lucifer says he never designed.
- Identity, motive, and relationship to Lucifer remain unresolved.

## Pursuers

- Two men in dry coats arrive from a waiting car during heavy rain.
- One was present at Saint Orison Hospital.
- They follow Mara from Halo toward the station.
- Their employer, exact objective, and relationship to Michael remain unproven.
