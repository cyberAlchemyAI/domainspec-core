## Contract chain

1. Elena's earlier Light: make her impossible to find, especially for Mara.
2. Mara's initial request: find Elena and bring her home before sunrise.
3. The system registers but holds Mara's request because the prior Light has priority. Mara owes nothing.
4. Lucifer offers cancellation without payment or a voluntary amendment.
5. Mara accepts the amendment: confirm Elena is alive, ask whether she will speak to Mara, do not disclose Elena's location without consent, deadline sunrise.
6. Mara chooses to stay and activates the amended Light. If Lucifer proves Elena is alive, the open favor becomes his.
7. Michael's request arrives through a mechanism Lucifer says should not exist.

## Known derivative mismatch

The current manuscript says Mara cuts the bracelet with Lucifer's knife. The generated KF15 image and ElevenLabs motion-comic adaptation show the bracelet safely unclasped and opened with a tool. This is a deliberate safety/visual adaptation, not yet a manuscript change. The final motion-comic must choose whether its audio follows the manuscript action or the selected image.
