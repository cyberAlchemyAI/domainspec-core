## Synthesis Boundary

The four explorer returns converge on a narrower claim than "reuse Nimbalyst." Nimbalyst is a pinned product, protocol, failure-semantics, and packaging specimen. Local expression is the default. Literal MIT client-code reuse is a candidate only when a symbol-level comparison shows that avoided local work exceeds extraction, authority adaptation, testing, provenance, expected upgrade review, and abandonment costs. The separate collaboration server behind `wss://sync.nimbalyst.com` is not contained in the MIT client repository and is not an available reuse unit.

This dossier normalizes the converged Ashby/Hewitt matrix as `NIM-C01` through `NIM-C16`, then applies three independent reviewer gates. PRECEDENT records ownership and never kills. DEFINITIONAL-SOUNDNESS asks whether a candidate is distinct. NON-VACUITY controls terminal admission. No candidate has the required present witness, so all sixteen terminal verdicts are **KILL** even where ownership is clean or the hypothesis is definitionally sound.

The result is plain: **no immediate Nimbalyst-derived implementation or integration unit is admitted**. Nimbalyst remains pinned product, protocol, failure, and security precedent. Local L0 repair and the existing L0.5 fake/offline MCP witness proceed without a Nimbalyst dependency.

In particular:

- evidence diff is presentation-only and cannot proceed until local L0 after-state/reference, approval, attribution, persistence, and schema-enforcement repairs are independently verified;
- task/session linkage is a read-only join over owner-qualified canonical references, never a second tracker, workstream, route, or status owner;
- raw records and projection distinctions from former C06 are folded into C05; abort, cleanup, completion, and resume distinctions from C06 are folded into C12; provider parser code waits until two admitted provider formats demonstrate actual duplication;
- permission, provider, MCP custody, terminal, Git/worktree, storage, sync/mobile, telemetry, and updater implementations do not cross into the local kernel by shortcut;
- adapters may propose, observe, and produce evidence; the local kernel alone owns grants, exact approval, durable artifacts/state, outcome adjudication, and receipt append.

Cost ratings are comparative (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `very high`) and include the smallest defensible local slice, not a production estimate.

## Three Banked Reopening Hypotheses

### NIM-C05 - Evidence Projection Pair

An evidence projection pair is `(R, P_v)`, where `R` is an immutable, ordered, redacted sequence of provider-native records preserving unknown records and correlation data, and `P_v(R)` is a versioned, deterministic, disposable canonical view. `R`, never `P_v`, is evidential source material; neither adjudicates outcome. Reopen only after two admitted provider formats show duplicated parser/projection work.

### NIM-C08 - Owner-Qualified Provenance Join

An owner-qualified provenance join is a disposable, read-only relation over independently canonical identifiers whose cells retain `(owner, schema/version, id)` and whose unresolved edges remain explicit. It has no status, lifecycle, launch, promotion, or write semantics. Reopen only after a recurring query, baseline navigation cost, and resolver owner are measured.

### NIM-C12 - Observed Termination Semantics

Observed termination semantics separates requested interruption from observed process termination, resource cleanup, resumability validation, and kernel outcome. Resume succeeds only when provider identity and opaque continuation identity match; partial side effects/evidence remain residue and cannot be promoted to success. Reopen with the first real resume/process-capable admitted adapter.
